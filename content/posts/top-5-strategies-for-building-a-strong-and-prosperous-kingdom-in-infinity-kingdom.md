Building a strong and prosperous kingdom is key to success in Infinity Kingdom. In this article, we will share the top 5 strategies to help you achieve this goal.

## Strategy 1: Focus on Resource Production

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/image-153-1024x104.png)

- Building and upgrading resource-generating buildings
- Utilizing gathering marches and resource nodes
- Prioritizing research on production technology

## Strategy 2: Efficiently Manage Your Immortals

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/image-154-1024x333.png)

- Prioritizing upgrading and leveling your main march Immortals
- Utilizing your lower-level Immortals for gathering or support purposes
- Melting down unused or low-level Immortals for crystal fragments

## Strategy 3: Join a Strong Alliance

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/image-155-1024x288.png)

- Finding and joining an active and competitive alliance
- Contributing to alliance technology research and development
- Collaborating with alliance members for mutual benefits

## Strategy 4: Participate in Events and Activities

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/image-156-1024x399.png)

- Completing daily and weekly quests for rewards
- Participating in limited-time events for unique rewards
- Strategizing for Alliance Expedition battles

## Strategy 5: Plan and Execute Effective Attacks

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/image-157.png)

- Scouting enemy kingdoms before launching attacks
- Utilizing a balanced army composition for optimal effectiveness
- Coordinating with alliance members for successful joint attacks
