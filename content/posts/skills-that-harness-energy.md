### Unleash the Power: Skills That Harness Energy in Infinity Kingdom

In the ever-evolving battleground of Infinity Kingdom, managing and maximizing your immortal's energy is a vital aspect of crafting a winning strategy. Understanding the skills that revolve around energy can be the key to turning the tide of battle in your favor.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-22-1024x548.png)

In this guide, we'll delve into the skills that are intricately linked with energy management and the heroes who can master these abilities to become unstoppable forces on the battlefield.

### 1. Full Coverage

Full Coverage is a skill that truly shines when equipped on heroes like Hippolyta or other physical DPS immortals with high energy regeneration rates.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-23.png)

This skill allows them to recover energy more rapidly, ensuring that they can unleash their ultimate abilities more frequently. When combined with Adrenaline Rush (and Wind Fury), Full Coverage becomes especially effective, transforming your DPS immortals into relentless damage dealers.

### 2. Energy Burst

Energy Burst is a skill that excels with backline DPS heroes who have a high critical hit rate but suffer from low energy regeneration, such as Seondeok or Himiko (when equipped with a critical hit artifact). This skill allows these immortals to regain energy quickly, ensuring that they can frequently unleash their ultimate abilities.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-24.png)

Energy Burst becomes exceptionally efficient when paired with skills that reliably proc damage, like Nova, Chaotic, Garrote, or Death Breath. Additionally, combining Energy Burst with Weakness can further enhance your critical hit rate, making it a powerful addition to your energy-focused strategy.

### 3. Adrenaline Rush

Adrenaline Rush is a skill designed for frontline immortals with low energy regeneration rates, such as Charles, Siegfried, Kubilai, William, and others. Frontline heroes offer a higher chance to proc this skill due to their more frequent attacks, which occur every 2 seconds compared to backliners, who attack every 3 seconds. Immortals with low energy regeneration benefit significantly from Adrenaline Rush, as it allows them to cast their ultimate abilities more often, compensating for their lower energy regeneration rates.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-25.png)

Adrenaline Rush is especially efficient when paired with skills like Full Coverage or Fighting Master, creating a synergy that not only enhances energy regeneration but also boosts overall combat effectiveness.

**In conclusion**, mastering the art of energy management in Infinity Kingdom is all about understanding the skills that can optimize your immortal's energy usage. Whether you're ensuring rapid energy regeneration with Full Coverage, enabling frequent ultimate ability usage with Energy Burst, or compensating for low energy regeneration rates with Adrenaline Rush, these skills can turn your heroes into relentless powerhouses on the battlefield.

So, dive into the intricate world of energy management, refine your strategy, and watch as your immortals become the champions who shape the battlefield and lead your army to victory in the world of Infinity Kingdom.
