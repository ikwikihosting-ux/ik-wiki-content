### Introduction

The Alliance Sparring system is one of the most significant quality-of-life additions Infinity Kingdom has received in years. Until now, practicing PvP outside of Legion of Frostborne was surprisingly difficult. On many servers, Norheim has (on most servers) long been governed by non-aggression agreements or dominated by a single alliance, leaving little opportunity to test new marches or simply enjoy PvP between major events.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-7-1024x452.png)

Sparring changes that. Instead of leaving your alliance to duel other players or waiting weeks for the next KvK, alliances can now organize internal battles between two teams of up to fifteen players. The result is a flexible PvP sandbox that serves as a testing ground, a source of Honor, and a genuinely fun activity during quieter periods of the game.

---

## Creating a Spar

Starting a Spar is straightforward, it simply requires two team leaders.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-4-1024x542.png)

A player initiated the spar by clicking on an alliance friends nameplate, and hitting Spar.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-8.png)

That begins the spar selecting two players who will then become captains of the two sparring teams. Each captain can send a Spar invitation via the chat to alliance members, and members can apply to join via the tab function.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-6-1024x662.png)

Players are free to organize balanced teams, create veteran-versus-veteran matches, or deliberately split stronger players across both sides for more competitive battles. Since each team can contain up to fifteen members, there is plenty of room for large-scale fights involving most of the alliance.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-3-1024x250.png)

Once players have joined, both teams simply move onto the world map and begin fighting.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-5-1024x231.png)

---

## How Spar Battles Work

Although Sparring is friendly competition, the battles themselves use normal combat mechanics.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-1-1024x461.png)

Troops are wounded and prosperity is lost exactly as they would be during ordinary PvP. Territory resources, however, are not affected, making Sparring far less disruptive than real alliance warfare.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-2-1024x391.png)

When the session is over, either team leader can simply disband the Spar.

---

## An Excellent Environment for Testing

Perhaps the biggest advantage of Sparring is the ability to test builds under realistic conditions.

Arena battles provide only limited information, while Legion of Frostborne often carries consequences for failed experiments. Sparring removes much of that pressure and gives players a controlled environment to compare marches, dragons, artifacts, skill combinations and positioning against opponents they know.

For players who enjoy theorycrafting, this is probably the most valuable addition. Rather than relying entirely on combat reports from major events, alliances can now answer questions directly.

How does a new artifact perform against Holy? Is Blood Frenzy actually outperforming Demonic Contract? Does changing one support immortal improve consistency? Instead of guessing, you can simply test it.

---

## More Than Practice

One of the more pleasant surprises is how naturally Sparring turns into its own activity.

During downtime between KvK seasons, many alliances have little to do beyond routine PvE. Sparring fills that gap remarkably well. Players can organize internal tournaments, challenge rival groups within the alliance, or simply divide into two balanced teams and spend an evening fighting for fun.

Because Honor is earned from Spar battles, the activity also provides meaningful progression rather than existing purely for entertainment.

It creates exactly the kind of repeatable PvP many players have been requesting for years.

---

## Things to Know Before You Start

A few mechanics are worth keeping in mind before organizing your first Spar.

- Each side can field up to **15 players**.
- Sparring only works on your server's **Norheim world map**.
- Normal troop losses and prosperity loss still apply.
- Territory resources are never lost.
- Participants may attack members of the opposing Spar team.
- Honor can be earned, subject to the daily cap.
- Certain world-map activities become unavailable while participating in a Spar.
- Team leaders can disband the Spar when the event is finished.

---

## Final Thoughts

The Alliance Sparring system succeeds because it addresses a problem that has existed since Infinity Kingdom launched. Outside of Legion of Frostborne, meaningful PvP opportunities have often been scarce, particularly on mature servers where diplomacy limits open-world fighting.

Rather than forcing players to leave their alliance or wait for the next competitive event, Sparring creates a space where alliances can practice, theorycraft and simply enjoy PvP together.

For players interested in improving their marches, it may become one of the best tools in the game. For everyone else, it adds something Infinity Kingdom has arguably been missing for years: a reason to log in with your alliance and fight purely because it's fun.
