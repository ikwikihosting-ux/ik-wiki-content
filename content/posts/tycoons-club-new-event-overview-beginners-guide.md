## VIP Pass – Complete Missions and Earn Rewards

The VIP Pass works much like the familiar seasonal event passes. Players complete gameplay objectives to earn EXP and progress through **30 levels of rewards**.

There are two reward tracks:

* **Free Pass:** Provides resources and a total of 300 Tycoon Coins.
* **Premium Pass ($4.99):** Adds additional rewards, including Amethyst Stones that can be exchanged for 900 Tycoon Coins.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151255-g2yrml.png)

Objectives include training troops, defeating gnomes and gnome bosses, and completing other gameplay activities. Some require substantial resources, so check the objectives early and plan accordingly.

The premium pass offers potentially good value for players who complete all 30 levels. We'll examine that more closely in our separate spending guide.

## Max Bounty – Fight Enemies for Free Rewards

Max Bounty introduces six combat missions, including Hostile Takeover, Riot Suppression, and Asset Recovery.

These missions work similarly to Rebel encounters. Players fight enemies, earn rewards, and progress through a milestone track. Stronger opponents can be tackled with alliance rallies.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151313-dd1ar9.png)

**The important mechanic is that Max Bounty refreshes every hour.**

Completing all six missions awards 21 bounty points. The full milestone track requires 260 points, meaning approximately **13 complete rounds** are needed to finish it.

Completing the milestones rewards players with around **1,000 Tycoon Coins**, making this one of the event's most important activities for free-to-play players.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151345-hjvjsp.png)

Remember that missions consume AP and can cause significant troop losses. AP-reduction talents work on these encounters and can help reduce costs.

## Fortune Wheel – Slot Machine and Energy System

The Fortune Wheel is the casino component of Tycoon's Club.

Players spend Tycoon Coins to spin a three-reel slot machine, with different symbol combinations determining their rewards.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151403-1jop9o.png)

Winning combinations can award additional Tycoon Coins, while less fortunate outcomes may provide only resources or speedups.

A normal spin costs **10 Tycoon Coins**, and players can increase their wager using the Reward Multiplier. Higher multipliers increase the rewards received but do not appear to improve the chance of winning.

### How Does Energy Work?

The Fortune Wheel also features an Energy meter that fills as players use the machine.

When the meter is full, players receive a **10% discount on spin costs**. For example, a 10× spin normally costs 100 coins but drops to 90 with the discount.

Importantly, Energy does not appear to guarantee a jackpot or increase winning probabilities. It is a cost-reduction mechanic, not a traditional pity system.

The Fortune Wheel also includes a competitive leaderboard with additional ranking rewards.

**Be careful:** The coins used for gambling are the same coins needed to purchase guaranteed rewards in the event shop. You can lose coins without receiving anything particularly valuable in return.

## Private Treasure Gallery – Spend Your Tycoon Coins

The Private Treasure Gallery is the event's reward exchange shop. Here, players can purchase specific resources and materials using Tycoon Coins.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151427-brxp7p.png)

Some notable rewards include:

**Crimson Moonlight and Stardust are particularly interesting rewards**, offering potentially excellent value compared with other ways of obtaining these materials.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151438-net5mp.png)

For many players, saving Tycoon Coins for guaranteed shop purchases may be preferable to gambling them in the Fortune Wheel.

## Gilded Club – Additional Shop Offers

The Gilded Club is a separate event-related shop accessible through Infinity Kingdom's regular shop interface.

It contains additional resource bundles, premium purchases, and offers that can be obtained using Gems.

Some rewards are available for free, so remember to check the shop regularly and claim anything available.

## What Should You Prioritize?

For most players, our recommendations are fairly straightforward:

1. **Complete Max Bounty:** Work toward all 260 milestone points to collect the event rewards.
2. **Progress your VIP Pass:** Complete objectives and claim as many of the 30 levels as possible.
3. **Check the Gilded Club:** Don't miss the free rewards.
4. **Choose your Gallery rewards:** Crimson Moonlight and Stardust are worth particular attention.
5. **Be cautious with the Fortune Wheel:** Remember that gambling Tycoon Coins means giving up guaranteed purchasing power in the Gallery.

## Final Thoughts

Tycoon's Club offers a welcome new activity between Infinity Kingdom's regular seasonal events, with opportunities for both free-to-play players and spenders.

The most important activities for casual players are the VIP Pass and Max Bounty, which offer predictable progression and rewards. The Fortune Wheel adds a more exciting but riskier option for players interested in gambling or competing for leaderboard rankings.
