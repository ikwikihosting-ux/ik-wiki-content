The community has spoken through its creativity—now it's time to decide the winner.

After receiving a wide range of incredible submissions, the Infinity Kingdom team has narrowed the field down to **10 outstanding finalist designs** for the **6th Immortal Outfit Design Contest**. The final decision is no longer in the hands of the judges—it's in yours.

Whether you've been following the contest from the beginning or you're just discovering these amazing concepts, now is the perfect time to support the artists who help make the Infinity Kingdom community so creative and passionate.

## How to Vote

Casting your vote only takes a minute.

Simply:

1. Open the official voting form.
2. Enter your **Role ID (UID)**.
3. Select your favorite design.
4. Submit your vote.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-20-1024x557.png)

If you don't know your Role ID, open Infinity Kingdom and tap your **profile portrait** to access the **Lord Info** screen. Your unique ID is displayed at the top of the window, alongside a handy **Copy** button that lets you copy it directly to your clipboard before pasting it into the voting form.

Remember that **each UID can vote only once**, so make your choice carefully.

Voting closes on **8 September at 23:59 UTC**.
