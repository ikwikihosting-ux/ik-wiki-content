Hey there, fellow Lords! Exciting news in Infinity Kingdom—our Lightning roster just got a serious upgrade with the introduction of **Cao Cao**, a brand-new Lightning Immortal. This guy isn’t just any support unit; he brings something totally new to the table—**healing and sustain!** If you've been struggling with Lightning’s lack of sustain outside of **Tokugawa’s shielding**, then buckle up because Cao Cao might just be the answer to our prayers.

## Cao Cao’s Abilities

Cao Cao is a **backline support Immortal** with a unique healing mechanic that sets him apart. Let’s break down his ultimate ability:

### Ultimate Ability: Sustained Recovery & Enemy Damage Reduction

- **Reduces the damage dealt by all enemies by 20%**
- **When your frontline gets hit by normal attacks, they recover HP (Recovery Rate: 170%)**
- **Effects are lasting 6 seconds**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-89-1024x472.png)

This is huge. Let’s analyze the key points:

1. **Healing in a Lightning Team** – Unlike other healing abilities, this one is directly tied to **normal attacks received by your frontline**. That means **no healing from ultimate abilities or Tower of Knowledge passive damage**, but it rewards tanky frontline setups.
2. **Comparing to Shelter** – The Shelter passive recovers **40% HP on hit** with a **50% activation chance** on all damage types. Cao Cao's **170% over 6 seconds** means that his healing could be even stronger in prolonged fights, particularly against normal attack-heavy teams.
3. **Anti-Hybrid Potential** – This is where Cao Cao really shines. **Hybrid teams**, especially those relying on high attack speed, deal massive normal attack damage. **Cao Cao’s healing thrives in those conditions**, making him a prime candidate for countering Hybrid comps.
4. Debuff synergy - Because of the way the lightning dragons passive work, you will gain a damage buff on your march whenever a new debuff is applied to the enemy. Adding Cao Cao will ensure a consistent damage dealt debuff and in turn boost your own damage output through the dragon passive.
5. Providing PvE sustain - Lightning relies on damage to end battles fast and a Tokugawa shield to provide protect against troop losses while completing PvE activities. Adding Cao Cao I expect lightning to have a better time in large scale PvE grinds with the consistent sustain effect.

## Synergies & Team Combinations

Since Lightning already has powerful Hybrid counters like **Cid**, adding Cao Cao into the mix opens up some serious team-building opportunities. Here are some **potential team comps** to consider:

### Khan/Hammurabi - Cao Cao - Cid - Tokugawa /Khubilai/Peter/Louis

- **Why it works:** Khan’s raw damage and energy regeneration work well alongside Cid’s devastating thorns effect. Synergizing well with Cao Caos normal attack skill. Cao Cao is **keeping the frontline alive longer**, this setup could be a path to making lightning viable as a Hybrid killer.
- **Who to add as the 4th?** Possible options include **Peter **(extra damage), **Tokugawa** (shielding), or **a Khubilaikhan for physical damage debuffs and as a second damage dealer** for pressure.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-92-1024x484.png)

These are just initial thoughts, but as **more players max out Cao Cao**, I expect to see some incredible new strategies emerge.
