At first glance, Touch of Surprise looks like a more rewarding version of Dazzling Wish. The scratcher has several outfit jackpots, offers up to 60 Eternal Grails from a single result, and promises better winning probabilities as your Wonder Card levels up.

But how much better are those probabilities? How difficult is it to reach the higher levels? And is the scratcher actually better value than the old Dazzling Wish?

We've collected the complete Level 1 reward probabilities, examined the new mechanics, and compared them with our previous Dazzling Wish calculations.

The results are interesting. **Touch of Surprise starts with better Eternal Grail returns, but considerably worse outfit-fragment odds. Its level-up system improves those odds over time, although reaching the higher levels requires a substantial investment.**

## TL;DR – Is Touch of Surprise Better Than Dazzling Wish?

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-152522-0zxtfh.png)

These comparisons use the natural reward probabilities, without incorporating the different pity systems or Dazzling Wish's unspecified Surprise Event rewards.

Our initial conclusion is that **Touch of Surprise is worse for outfit fragments at Level 1 but offers approximately 33% more Eternal Grails than Dazzling Wish**.

However, the new leveling mechanic means this comparison changes as players continue scratching.

For players whose main objective is Eternal Grails, we still recommend the dedicated Wish Grail wheel over either event.

## How Does Touch of Surprise Work?

Touch of Surprise replaces the traditional spinning mechanic with Wonder Cards.

Each Wonder Card costs one Token of Light and contains five hidden symbols. Players scratch the card to reveal a combination of symbols and receive the corresponding reward.

The rewards include Eternal Grails and Fu Fei's Night of Terror outfit fragments.

Unlike Dazzling Wish, Touch of Surprise features several different outfit jackpots rather than a single 20-fragment reward.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-152755-irssrv.png)

The three major outfit prizes are:

* Five Turkeys: 45 Fu Fei outfit fragments
* Five Meat Legs: 30 outfit fragments
* Five Cakes: 20 outfit fragments

The remaining outcomes primarily reward Eternal Grails.

The event also includes daily ranking rewards, with different symbol combinations contributing different numbers of points.

But the most important new feature is the **UP system**, which allows players to increase their Wonder Card level and improve their chances of receiving grand prizes.

## The UP System – How Do Wonder Card Levels Work?

This is the most important difference between the two events.

Every time you receive the **Five UP** result, your Wonder Card increases by one level.

The maximum level is 10.

The game explicitly states that higher levels increase the probability of obtaining grand prizes while decreasing the probability of receiving smaller rewards.

At Level 1, the chance of receiving Five UP is 1%.

This means players need an average of approximately 100 scratches to obtain one level-up.

Unlike the 1,200-scratch guaranteed prize, **leveling up does not require winning an outfit jackpot**.

The two systems are separate.

## How Much Do Outfit Odds Improve at Higher Levels?

This remains the biggest unknown in our complete value calculation.

The game confirms that grand-prize probabilities improve with each level, but the complete probability tables for Levels 2–10 are not currently available to us.

However, community reports from players investigating the new system suggest that the individual outfit-fragment probabilities may increase in small increments as the Wonder Card levels up.

One player reported maximum-level outfit probabilities in the region of **0.10% to 0.21%** across the three different fragment rewards.

That would represent a meaningful improvement over the starting values of 0.01%, 0.02%, and 0.03%.

Unfortunately, we don't yet have a complete screenshot of the Level 10 reward table confirming the exact percentages or their progression across every intermediate level.

For that reason, we won't publish a supposed average outfit return across 900 or 1,200 scratches based on guessed upgrade rates.

What we can say confidently is that the level-up mechanic improves outfit odds, but the overwhelming majority of players will spend much of their event participation below the highest levels.

This makes Touch of Surprise much harder to evaluate than Dazzling Wish.

## The 1,200-Scratch Guaranteed Prize

Touch of Surprise also features a guaranteed grand-prize system.

The in-game counter begins at 1,200 scratches and decreases as players participate.

The rules state that after the required number of scratches, players receive a guaranteed reward randomly selected from the three major outfit outcomes:

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-152624-rgx2ql.png)

This is quite different from Dazzling Wish, where 700 draws guaranteed 20 fragments.

The new event requires considerably more participation to reach its guarantee, but the prize can potentially contain more fragments.

However, we don't know whether the three pity outcomes are equally likely or whether their ordinary reward probabilities determine the selection.

We also haven't established how receiving a natural outfit jackpot interacts with the pity counter.

Therefore, the expected outfit-fragment value of the 1,200-scratch guarantee cannot yet be calculated precisely.

One point is nevertheless clear: **a single guaranteed prize does not necessarily unlock Fu Fei's outfit**.

The outfit requires 80 fragments, meaning players may still need additional jackpots or fragments from other sources.

### Which Event Has the Better Pity?

Dazzling Wish required 700 draws to guarantee a fixed 20-fragment reward.

Touch of Surprise requires 1,200 scratches for a reward of 20, 30, or 45 fragments.

That means the new pity threshold is approximately 71% higher, but the potential reward is also larger.

If the new event consistently awarded 45 fragments, the increased requirement could be attractive.

If players frequently receive just 20 fragments, however, the guarantee becomes substantially worse.

Without the selection probabilities, we cannot fairly assign an exact winner to the pity comparison.

## What Should Players Do?

The practical recommendations depend on how many Tokens of Light you're willing to spend and what you're trying to obtain.

### Free-to-Play and Low Spenders

For these players, Touch of Surprise is generally not an attractive outfit investment.

At Level 1, the natural outfit probability is only 0.06%, and reaching the higher levels requires hundreds of scratches.

A few lucky players will obtain fragments, but most should expect to receive ordinary Eternal Grail rewards.

If your main objective is long-term account progression, saving Tokens for Wish Grail remains the stronger choice.

### Moderate Spenders

For players spending approximately 100–300 Tokens, the UP system begins to become relevant.

You'll likely reach at least Level 2 and potentially progress a few levels further.

However, you remain far away from the 1,200-scratch guarantee and are unlikely to reach Level 10.

This means much of your participation will occur at relatively low Wonder Card levels.

Unless you're specifically collecting Fu Fei's outfit or competing for ranking rewards, I'd generally recommend spending those Tokens on Wish Grail instead.

### Heavy Spenders and Outfit Collectors

This is the group where Touch of Surprise becomes more interesting.

Players willing to spend 900–1,200 Tokens can realistically reach the higher Wonder Card levels, benefit from improved outfit probabilities, and potentially trigger the guaranteed grand prize.

However, the event still contains substantial uncertainty.

Without the complete upgraded probability tables, we cannot determine exactly how many outfit fragments these players should expect.

And because the pity reward is selected from three possible outcomes, reaching the guarantee doesn't provide a fixed number of fragments.

For players determined to collect every outfit, this may simply be the cost of participating.

But for players comparing the purchase against Chaos Immortal fragments, artifacts, and other valuable progression systems, it's difficult to recommend such a large investment without better information.

## Final Thoughts – Is the New Scratcher Actually Better?

Touch of Surprise is an interesting replacement for Dazzling Wish.

Its new mechanics offer more variety, larger potential outfit jackpots, and a progression system that gradually improves the chances of obtaining rare rewards.

The starting Eternal Grail returns are also noticeably better than those of the old Dazzling Wish.

However, that doesn't automatically make Touch of Surprise a better event.

The starting outfit probabilities are considerably worse, and the strongest improvements are locked behind a leveling system that requires hundreds of scratches to develop.

The 1,200-scratch guarantee is also a major investment, particularly when the guaranteed outfit reward can vary between 20 and 45 fragments.

For players who primarily want Eternal Grails, Wish Grail remains the more attractive option.

For outfit collectors, Touch of Surprise may become competitive at higher levels, but the exact value remains uncertain until we have the complete upgraded probability tables.

**Our recommendation: Treat Touch of Surprise as a specialized outfit lottery rather than your main source of seasonal value. Use Wish Grail for Eternal Grails, and only invest heavily in the scratcher when the featured outfit is worth the potentially enormous cost to you.**

We'll update this analysis as more players share the Level 2–10 probability tables and clarify the guarantee mechanics.
