In Infinity Kingdom, honor buffs play a crucial role in enhancing your gameplay by providing significant advantages through the honor ranking system. Each of the three kingdoms of Norheim has its own set of honor ranks, meaning there are three positions in total per server for each rank, making the competition for these spots fierce.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-21-1024x570.png)

### Understanding Honor Buffs

Honor buffs are obtained as you climb the honor rankings within your kingdom. These rankings are not only a measure of prestige but also a source of powerful buffs that can increase your troop count, strength, intelligence, agility, physique, and aptitude by a percentage. The higher you are on the ranking, the more substantial the buffs you receive, which can significantly impact your power and performance in the game.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-22-1024x558.png)

### Competition and Strategy

The competition for honor rankings is intense, as each rank has a limited number of spots. For instance, there can only be one Prince, one Archduke, and one Duke, with the number of spots increasing as you go lower in the ranking list. Achieving a higher rank requires not just accumulating honor but also outmaneuvering other players who are vying for the same positions.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-23-1024x435.png)

### Maximizing Honor Buffs

To maximize the benefits of honor buffs, players should adopt strategic approaches, such as allocating their strongest fighters across the honor ladders of different kingdoms. This strategy ensures access to a broader range of buffs, providing strategic advantages in battles and wars. Key steps include evaluating your alliance's strengths, planning for kingdom allocation, and securing and maintaining honor rankings through active participation in kingdom activities.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-24-1024x532.png)

The power of honor ranking buffs lies in their ability to make your armies more resilient and versatile. Achieving high honor rankings and strategically utilizing the buffs can tilt closely contested battles in your favor, making your force a formidable opponent on the battlefield.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-26.png)
