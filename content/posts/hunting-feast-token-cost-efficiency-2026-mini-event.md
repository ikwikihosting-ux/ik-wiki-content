## Introduction

The Hunting Feast is arguably the most economically important system inside the May 2026 Hunting Festival. While the event contains large amounts of Soul Crystal Chests, Philosopher’s Offer Chests, speedups, and insignia rewards, the real question for most experienced players is much simpler:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-84.png)

> Is buying Meat for Hunting Feast more cost efficient than directly purchasing Tokens of Light?

This guide focuses specifically on that comparison.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-85.png)

Rather than looking at the event emotionally or from a completionist perspective, we will break the system down mathematically by comparing:

- Meat cost per Feast level
- Token rewards per level
- Dollar cost per Token
- Direct Token purchase efficiency through Gtarcade bundles

An important detail is that Hunting Feast is fundamentally a 2-player cooperative event. If both players contribute equally, both players receive the full rewards while effectively sharing the progression burden. That cooperative structure is one of the reasons the event can become surprisingly efficient.

The goal of this guide is therefore not simply to say whether Hunting Feast is “good” or “bad,” but instead to determine exactly where the event outperforms direct Token purchases and where the value begins falling off.

---

## Meat Pricing and Hunting Feast Level Costs

To evaluate Hunting Feast properly, we first need a consistent Meat-per-dollar benchmark.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-87.png)

Using the efficient recurring Gtarcade bundles available during the event, the daily Meat shop looks like this:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-88.png)

This establishes the core benchmark used throughout the guide: **24 Meat per Dollar**

Or:

- 1 Meat ≈ $0.0417
- 10 Meat ≈ $0.42
- 100 Meat ≈ $4.17

The Hunting Feast itself scales upward every few levels. Levels become increasingly expensive as progression continues, while Token rewards rise much more slowly.

The event follows this structure:

- Completing levels 1, 2, 3, and 4 costs 2000 points total, meaning 500 points or 5 Meat per level.
- Completing levels 5, 6, and 7 costs 1000 points each, or 10 Meat per level.
- Completing levels 8, 9, and 10 costs 2000 points each, or 20 Meat per level.
- Completing levels 11, 12, and 13 costs 3000 points each, or 30 Meat per level.
- Completing levels 14, 15, and 16 costs 4000 points each, or 40 Meat per level.
- This pattern continues upward, increasing by 1000 points, or 10 Meat, every three completed levels.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-86-1024x461.png)

This distinction matters because the reward bands and cost bands do not always line up neatly. Token rewards increase every 10 levels, while Meat costs increase every 3 levels.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-89.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-90.png)

The early levels are extremely efficient, then the value settles into a repeating pattern where the first few levels after each Token increase are stronger, and the last levels before the next Token increase are weaker.

This is why levels such as 11, 21, 31, 51, 61, 71, 81, and 91 look better than the levels immediately before them. The Token reward has just increased, while the Meat cost has not yet fully caught up.

## Direct Token Purchase Pricing

To compare Hunting Feast fairly, we also need to establish the efficiency of buying Tokens directly.

To avoid distortions from inefficient whale bundles, we only use the most efficient lower-tier Gtarcade Token packs:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-91.png)

This gives us the baseline direct-purchase value:

## Direct Token Purchase Efficiency

- 1.46 Tokens per Dollar
- **$0.685 per Token**

This number becomes the benchmark Hunting Feast must beat.

If Hunting Feast provides Tokens below $0.685 each, then the event is mathematically more efficient than buying Tokens directly.

## Comparing Hunting Feast Versus Direct Token Buying

Now, at this stage it will be obvious for everyone that if you do indeed have a friend who contributes to the event, Hunting Feast surpasses direct token purchasing by MILES! And as we shall see just below, we are actually getting superior value even doing this even solo (while also boosting someone else!). Amazing!

Once we compare the numbers directly, the early and middle Hunting Feast levels clearly outperform standard Token purchases - and that is even not counting any of the other rewards like the thousands of philosopher stones and million or two in Soul Crystals you will be getting in addition.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-92.png)

The most important conclusion is that Hunting Feast remains more efficient than direct Token buying for a surprisingly large portion of the event.

Even many of the later levels remain mathematically competitive despite the rising Meat costs.

For developing accounts, the Soul Crystal Chests and Philosopher’s Offer Chests massively increase the overall value.

For long-term whales, however, many of those resources are already heavily stockpiled after several abundance-style events. In that situation, the event becomes much more of a pure Token-per-dollar calculation.

Another major factor is free Meat generation. Players also earn Meat through:

- Ingredient Hunt
- Glorious Challenge
- Pinnacle Trial
- Daily event participation

That free Meat significantly improves the effective efficiency of Hunting Feast in practice since you are doing this event together with someone else. Even doing this event as a spender looking to complete all 100 levels with a free-to-play player, your real dollars spent per level across the 100 levels will be lower than above, since you will be getting some "free" meat from your friend. That it NOT included in the above meaning that as the event already favors token purchasing via Meat over Directly, the participation of a friend in the event will make it much much more efficient.

## Additional Rewards and Final Verdict

While this guide primarily focuses on Token efficiency, Hunting Feast also generates enormous secondary value through:

- Philosopher’s Offer Chests
- Soul Crystal Chests
- Speedups
- Festival Insignias

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-93-1024x461.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-94-1024x461.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-95-1024x461.png)

For mid-game accounts especially, these rewards dramatically increase the event’s real progression value beyond what the Token calculations alone suggest.

This is why many active players consider Hunting Feast one of the strongest seasonal spending systems Infinity Kingdom has introduced recently.

The early and middle levels are particularly efficient because they combine:

- Excellent Token-per-dollar value
- Valuable secondary rewards
- Cooperative progression advantages
- Large amounts of free supplemental Meat

The event only begins losing efficiency near the extreme late levels where Meat scaling starts outpacing Token growth.

Even then, the event may still remain worth pushing for players who:

- Need abundance resources
- Want the insignia
- Care about collection completion
- Have a strong cooperative partner
- Generate large amounts of free Meat

## Final Takeaway

Compared purely as a Token conversion system, Hunting Feast is significantly more efficient than direct Token purchases throughout most of the event.

The strongest value sits in the early and middle progression tiers, where Token costs are substantially lower than standard shop pricing.

For coordinated 2-player teams, especially active mid-game accounts, Hunting Feast is arguably one of the best spending opportunities available during the entire Hunting Festival.

The Hunting Feast is one of the most strategically interesting seasonal systems Infinity Kingdom has introduced recently because its value depends heavily on coordination and account progression.

For developing accounts, the event is extraordinarily strong due to the combined value of:

- Tokens of Light
- Soul Crystals
- Philosopher’s Stones
- Speedups
- Free Meat generation

And if your alliance coordinates properly, the efficiency improves dramatically beyond what the raw numbers initially suggest.
