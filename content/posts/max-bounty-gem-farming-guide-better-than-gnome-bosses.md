## How Max Bounty Gem Farming Works

Max Bounty features six combat missions, ranging from one to six stars. Each mission rewards **5 Gems per star**, meaning the six-star mission provides 30 Gems.

| Difficulty        | Gem Reward   |
| ----------------- | ------------ |
| 1 Star            | 5            |
| 2 Stars           | 10           |
| 3 Stars           | 15           |
| 4 Stars           | 20           |
| 5 Stars           | 25           |
| 6 Stars           | 30           |
| **Full Rotation** | **105 Gems** |

Completing all six missions provides 105 Gems, and a new set becomes available every hour.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261018-195826-uyndo5.png)

Importantly, completing the final milestone at 260 points doesn't stop the activity. Players can continue earning Gems from missions throughout the event, even after collecting all milestone rewards.

## Max Bounty vs. Gnome Bosses – Gem Efficiency

To determine whether Max Bounty is worthwhile, we compared it with farming Level 20 Gnome Bosses, which consistently provide **5 Gems per successful attack**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261018-195808-ng2fgc.png)

Based on our testing, the average AP cost is approximately 77 per Max Bounty mission and 83 per Level 20 Gnome Boss attack.

| Metric                 | Max Bounty | Level 20 Gnome Boss |
| ---------------------- | ---------- | ------------------- |
| Gems per attack        | 5–30       | 5                   |
| AP per attack          | ~77        | 83                  |
| Gems per 100 AP        | **22.7**   | **6.0**             |
| AP needed for 100 Gems | ~440       | ~1,660              |

This means that Max Bounty provides approximately **3.8 times more Gems per AP** than regular Level 20 Gnome Bosses.

For example, spending 1,000 AP on Max Bounty would theoretically yield around 227 Gems, compared with just 60 Gems from regular boss farming.

That's a significant difference, particularly for players with large AP reserves.



## How to Improve Your Farming Efficiency

One useful trick is to **activate AP-reduction talents before starting Max Bounty missions**.

Testing has confirmed that these talents work on Max Bounty encounters, reducing the AP cost of individual battles.

Players can also rally with alliance members to tackle difficult missions, potentially reducing the burden on weaker formations.

Remember that you don't necessarily need to complete every mission each hour. If particular encounters cause excessive casualties, it may be better to focus on easier missions rather than repeatedly losing troops for a few additional Gems.

## Is Max Bounty Worth Farming?

**Yes, particularly for players who can defeat the missions without suffering excessive troop losses.**

Based purely on AP efficiency, Max Bounty is an excellent source of Gems, providing approximately 3.8 times the Gem rewards of Level 20 Gnome Boss farming.

However, that advantage must be weighed against the potentially higher troop casualties, especially for players with weaker marches.

For stronger players, the ability to earn 105 Gems every hour makes Max Bounty one of the more interesting repeatable farming opportunities introduced with Tycoon's Club.

**Our recommendation:** Complete the 260-point milestone track first, then continue farming Max Bounty if your AP reserves and troop losses make it worthwhile. With significantly better Gem returns per AP than regular Gnome Bosses, there's good reason to keep participating even after collecting the final milestone reward.
