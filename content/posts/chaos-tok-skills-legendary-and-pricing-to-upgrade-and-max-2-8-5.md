## Introduction

With the rise of Conquest seasons and Chaos elemental content, Infinity Kingdom players now have access to a brand-new set of **Tower of Knowledge (ToK) skills** exclusive to Chaos immortals. These powerful skills can dramatically enhance your team's capabilities, offering everything from better sustain and control to explosive burst potential.

But while the value is undeniable, the **cost to unlock and upgrade these Chaos ToK skills is significant**. In this guide, we break down everything you need to know: how to unlock the skills, what they do, how to acquire the materials, and what it costs to max them out.

---

## What Are Chaos ToK Skills?

Chaos ToK skills are a special category of active and passive abilities available exclusively in Conquest servers. Unlike standard ToK skills, these cannot be purchased with purple crystals or unlocked via standard gameplay mechanics.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-35-1024x477.png)

Instead, they require a new material called **Skill Stones**, obtained from the **Wisdom Well** event. Once unlocked, Chaos skills function just like traditional ToK skills, except they offer some of the most potent effects in the game, often supporting high-end strategies or enabling unique Chaos-exclusive setups.

---

## How to Unlock Chaos Skills

To unlock a Chaos ToK skill, you need:

- **60 Skill Stones** for the initial unlock
- These are acquired through the **Wisdom Well** event
- The well requires **Wishing Well Coins**, a special currency you earn via bundles and potentially limited events

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-36.png)

Each Wisdom Well pull costs a number of Coins and has a chance to give you a 4-fragment drop of a Chaos skill (the "jackpot"). Once you collect 60 fragments, the skill unlocks.

### Upgrade costs to Chaos/Legendary Skills:

## The Cost to Unlock: Real-World Money Estimate

There is currently one primary bundle available for Skill Coins:

- **$5 USD bundle** = **8 Skill Coins**
- Limit: **10 purchases per week** = **80 Skill Coins per week**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-38.png)

Based on this:

### Unlocking a Skill (Worst Case RNG)

If your luck is bad, you may need up to **600 coins** to unlock a skill:

- That’s 240 Skill Stone fragments needed (60 for unlock, 180 for level-up)
- You get **4 fragments per jackpot hit**
- Assuming one jackpot per 40 pulls: 240 / 4 = 60 jackpots * 40 coins = **2400 coins** for full level 8
- **2400 Skill Coins ÷ 80 per week = 30 weeks**
- **30 weeks × $50/week = $1500 USD** (worst-case to fully level a skill)

### Unlocking a Skill (More Realistic Average)

In practice, many players report a more common rate of 4 fragments per **25 coins**:

- That would bring your total required to **around 1500 coins** for full level 8
- Which equals **1500 / 80 = ~19 weeks**
- **19 weeks × $50 = $950 USD**

You can expect to spend **between $400 (just to unlock)** and **$1500 (to max out)** a single Chaos ToK skill depending on RNG and spending pace.

## Are They Worth It?

For top-level PvP? Absolutely. Chaos ToK skills provide powerful effects that often do not exist in standard skill sets. Many high-end whales and top arena players are already starting to build new meta strategies around these.

But for low-to-mid spenders, the cost is steep. Instead of rushing every skill, we recommend:

- Prioritizing 1 or 2 Chaos skills that support your main march
- Waiting for event bundles or discounted weeks

---

## Conclusion

Chaos Tower of Knowledge skills bring exciting new tools to Infinity Kingdom's strategic landscape—but they don’t come cheap. Whether you're looking to fully invest or just dip your toes, understanding the cost and strategy behind unlocking these skills can help you make smarter, more effective decisions.

Unlocking just one of these powerhouse skills can take **weeks** or even **months**, so choose wisely, spend smartly, and plan ahead.
