### Unleash the Auras: Enhancing Your Immortals in Infinity Kingdom

In the ever-evolving battlefield of Infinity Kingdom, auras hold the power to tip the scales of battle in your favor. Understanding the auras that can empower your immortals is crucial for crafting a winning strategy. In this guide, we'll delve into the auras designed to enhance your heroes and the heroes who can best utilize these effects to become unstoppable forces on the battlefield.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-59-1024x582.png)

### 1. Sacrifice

Sacrifice is an aura that shines when assigned to heroes like Trajan, Qin, Dido, or any other immortals who don't rely on dealing damage. These heroes can thrive even if they aren't the primary damage dealers on the field.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-50.png)

Sacrifice becomes especially effective in marches featuring numerous DPS immortals, such as fire-focused teams. It allows your support immortals to take on a critical role by absorbing damage, protecting your more damage-oriented heroes.

### 2. Berserk

Berserk is an aura designed for heroes like Alex and Hannibal. It particularly excels in marches where there are numerous immortals relying on normal attacks or Adrenaline Rush.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-51.png)

The aura boosts the damage output of your immortals, turning them into relentless attackers capable of swiftly dealing with opponents.

### 3. Malice

Malice is an aura that thrives when assigned to heroes like Charles and Theodora. It finds its true potential in sustain-focused marches that aim to avoid sudden spikes of damage.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-52.png)

By reducing the enemy's damage output, Malice ensures your team can outlast your opponents, gradually chipping away at their forces.

### 4. Misleading

Misleading is an aura that shines in wind or holy marches due to their naturally high dodge rates. It becomes especially effective when combined with other evasion-enhancing skills like Afterimage and Wind Protection.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-53.png)

Misleading makes it challenging for opponents to land critical hits, ensuring your immortals remain resilient and hard to defeat.

### 5. Weakness

Weakness is an aura that is particularly effective in fire marches due to their high critical hit rates. It becomes challenging for abilities like Malice, Dido's epic artifact, or the increased resilience from a high-level dragon to negate the aura's effect.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-54.png)

Weakness ensures that your opponents' attacks are less likely to deal critical damage, increasing your immortals' survivability.

### 6. Coercion

Coercion is an aura that excels in full physical marches, such as holy or wind-earth-hybrid teams.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-55.png)

By reducing the enemy's physical damage, Coercion ensures your immortals can endure prolonged battles and outlast your opponents.

### 7. Shelter

Shelter is an aura that finds its place in sustain-focused marches, like earth or holy teams. It shines against opponents whose attacks are consistently low in damage.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-56.png)

Shelter provides additional protection, ensuring that your immortals remain resilient and can withstand enemy onslaughts.

### 8. Paralysis

Paralysis is an aura that becomes especially effective against wind-earth hybrid teams.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-57.png)

It can disrupt the flow of the battle, impairing the enemy's immortals and giving your team a crucial advantage.

### 9. Spring of Life

Spring of Life is an aura that thrives when assigned to heroes like Theodora and Zenobia. It becomes especially potent when combined with other healing skills like Vitality Touch and Purification.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-58.png)

Spring of Life ensures that your immortals receive continuous healing, allowing them to endure longer and maintain their combat effectiveness.

In conclusion, mastering the art of auras in Infinity Kingdom is essential for constructing a resilient and potent team. Whether you're sacrificing for the greater good, enhancing the berserk rage of your attackers, sowing malice among your enemies, or misleading opponents with elusive tactics, these auras can turn your heroes into unstoppable forces on the battlefield.

So, embrace the power of auras, refine your tactics, and witness your immortals become the champions who lead your army to victory in the world of Infinity Kingdom.
