### Fortify Your Defense: Skills that Shield Against Normal Attacks

In the ever-evolving world of Infinity Kingdom, the ability to defend against normal attacks is just as crucial as launching them. Understanding which skills can protect your heroes from the relentless onslaught of foes can make the difference between victory and defeat. In this guide, we'll explore skills that provide a shield against normal attacks and the heroes who can best utilize them.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/image-45.png)

### 1. Fighting Will

Fighting Will is a skill tailor-made for frontline immortals who don't rely on their HP to be effective in battle. Heroes like Richard, Bjorn, and Trajan are prime examples of characters who can embrace the power of Fighting Will. These heroes are known for their remarkable durability and battlefield prowess. What sets this skill apart is its ability to allow these heroes to drop and, if necessary, even meet their demise swiftly.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/image-46.png)

For heroes with Fighting Will, the battlefield isn't a place where they cling to life desperately; it's where they unleash their full potential, undeterred by the fear of falling in combat. This skill ensures that they can make their presence felt, even if they are the first to face the enemy's onslaught.

### 2. Toxin Barrier

Toxin Barrier is a skill that shines in the hands of heroes like Charles and Tokugawa. These heroes have a unique talent for sustaining high HP over extended periods, making them formidable forces on the battlefield. Toxin Barrier takes their already impressive durability to the next level by allowing them to endure even more damage while simultaneously protecting them from normal attacks.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/image-47.png)

In essence, heroes with Toxin Barrier can become nigh-invincible on the front lines. Their high HP ensures they can soak up immense damage, while the barrier itself acts as a shield against normal attacks. This combination makes them exceedingly difficult to defeat and allows them to deal out substantial damage in return.

### Conclusion

In the Infinity Kingdom, the ability to defend against normal attacks is paramount to your success in battles. Skills like Fighting Will and Toxin Barrier offer unique advantages to specific heroes, turning them into unyielding bastions of defense. Whether you're using heroes like Richard, Bjorn, and Trajan to embrace the swift fall of Fighting Will or entrusting Charles and Tokugawa with the power of Toxin Barrier, these skills can turn the tide of battle in your favor.

So, as you embark on your conquests in the Infinity Kingdom, remember the importance of a solid defense. These skills and the heroes who wield them can provide the protection you need to weather the storm of normal attacks and emerge victorious on the battlefield. Fortify your defenses, choose your heroes wisely, and let these skills be your shield in the ever-expanding world of Infinity Kingdom.
