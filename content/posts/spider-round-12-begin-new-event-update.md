***Announcing the Revamped Cross-Server Spider Competition in Infinity Kingdom!***

Attention, Lords and Ladies of Infinity Kingdom! Get ready to embark on a thrilling new chapter in one of our most beloved events—the Cross-Server Spider Competition! After eleven intense seasons, we're shaking things up to bring you an even more exciting and competitive experience.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/image-9.png)

## What's New?

This time around, we're introducing **four distinct brackets** based on your march's Strategic Troop Power (STP). This innovative change aims to level the playing field, ensuring that battles are matched more fairly and focus on player strategies and builds. Say goodbye to the days of David versus Goliath matchups and hello to competitions where tactics truly shine!

### Bracket Breakdown:

- **Bracket A:** 500K+ STP
- **Bracket B:** 400K-500K STP
- **Bracket C:** 300K-400K STP
- **Bracket D:** 0-300K STP

Your placement in these brackets will be determined by your march's STP, verified through screenshots submitted via our official submissions channel.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/image-10-1024x542.png)

## Exciting Rewards Await!

Competing in these brackets not only brings glory but also gems! Here's what's up for grabs in each bracket:

- **1st Place:** 1200 gems
- **2nd Place:** 800 gems
- **3rd Place:** 500 gems
- **4th & 5th Places:** 300 gems each
- **6th - 10th Places:** 100 gems each

**And for our aspiring conquerors below level 100, a special lower bracket ensures everyone gets a slice of the action:**

- **1st Place:** 500 gems
- **2nd**: 300 gems
- **3rd - 5th Places:** 200 gems each

### Important to Note:

For this inaugural revamped competition, players are allowed to compete in **one bracket only**. Keep your eyes peeled, though, as this may evolve in future competitions.

**Mark Your Calendars!**

The 12th round of the Spider Competition kicks off on **Sunday, 17th of March at 00:01 UTC**, running through to the **28st of March at 23:59 UTC**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/image-59-1024x558.png)
