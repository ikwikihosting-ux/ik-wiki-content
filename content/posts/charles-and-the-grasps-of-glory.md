### Player Guide: Maximizing Charles with the Grasps of Glory Unique Artifact in Infinity Kingdom

**Introduction to Charles’ Unique Artifact:** Charles, a formidable tank in Infinity Kingdom, becomes even more robust with his unique artifact, **Grasps of Glory**. This artifact not only boosts his inherent defensive capabilities but also offers a unique protective mechanism against critical hits, making it a pivotal addition to any team where Charles is a key player.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-65-1024x470.png)

**Understanding Grasps of Glory:** The Grasps of Glory unique artifact enhances Charles’ utility by linking to his shield-generating ability, **Terra Shield**. When this ability activates and creates a shield, it also grants the immortal with the lowest troop count a significant reduction in the likelihood of receiving critical hits.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-64-1024x428.png)

The exact mechanics of this crit-defense aren't explicitly detailed in the game, such as the duration of the effect or the exact reduction percentage, but the protective impact against potentially devastating critical strikes is invaluable.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-66.png)

## Attribute rolls to aim for

**Ideal Attribute Rolls for Grasps of Glory:** When it comes to optimizing this artifact, the primary attribute you should aim for is:

- **Physical Defense (Preferably as a Percentage)**: This enhances Charles’ ability to absorb physical attacks, crucial for his role as a frontline shield.

Secondary attributes that are beneficial include:

- **Resilience**: Adds an extra layer of survivability.
- **HP Growth or HP Guard Bonuses**: While both are beneficial, HP Growth is often favored for its overall increase in health, providing broader benefits against all types of damage.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-67.png)

## How to get it?

**Acquisition of Grasps of Glory:** Grasps of Glory can be obtained through two primary methods:

- **Stellar Events**: Participate in these events where you can directly aim to collect this artifact by exchanging event-specific currency like Crystal Balls for artifact fragments.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-68.png)

- **5-Day Skill Stone Bundles**: These bundles offer unique artifacts as part of their reward structure, allowing players to accumulate necessary skill stones for their immortals while also snagging powerful artifacts.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-69.png)

**Strategic Usage of Grasps of Glory:** Incorporating Grasps of Glory into your strategy provides Charles with an enhanced role beyond just tanking. With this artifact, Charles not only becomes a barrier against physical damage but also a guardian against critical strikes which are prevalent in high-level play. This makes him exceptionally useful in protecting key immortals who might be vulnerable due to low troop counts.

**Conclusion:** Charles’ Grasps of Glory elevates his status from a mere frontline tank to a crucial tactical asset capable of safeguarding important allies from critical hits. This unique artifact's value in mitigating critical damage cannot be overstated, especially against opponents who rely heavily on crit-based strategies. By strategically enhancing Charles with this artifact, players can ensure their teams are better equipped to handle the diverse threats posed by various battle formations in Infinity Kingdom. Embrace this powerful tool and reshape your battlefield strategy around a more resilient and protective Charles.
