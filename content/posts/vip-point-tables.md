You unlock VIP ranks as you play the game. You gain VIP points from: (1) Daily login, (2) Ingame Purchases and (3) Spending Gems.

## Why is VIP rank important?

The **rewards**from VIP ranks are many. A couple different stand out:

1. The first milestole is reaching level 10. As a VIP 10, you will get **1x Philosophers**stone every day in the daily login chest. This is VERY important for your growth and you should prioritize reaching VIP 10 (even if you need to gem it).
2. The **lootboxes**you can purchase upon unlocking a new rank hold some very good rewards. If you are willing to spend on the game, these have solid loot - especially if you run an earth team relying on Zenobia. The later boxes are a must if you aim to run shadow.
3. Each rank gives you unique **buffs**. These buffs are increasingly strong and as you mature towards late game, the buffs give huge power bonuses to you marches.
4. Completing VIP rankings and reaching VIP 15 unlocks the VIP shop. Here you can purchase the **holy dragon egg** (costs 100k VIP points).
