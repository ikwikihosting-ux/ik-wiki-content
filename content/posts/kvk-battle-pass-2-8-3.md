## ❄️ Introducing the Expedition Battle Pass

In the upcoming update, the Legion of Frostborne event will feature a new **Expedition Battle Pass**, accessible through a third tab in the KVK Chronicle interface. This system is designed to enhance your engagement by rewarding active participation in personal quests throughout the KVK period.

### 🔹 How It Works

- **Earning Battle Pass Experience (EXP):** Complete personal Legion Achievement tasks during the KVK to accumulate EXP.
- **Progression:** As you gain EXP, you'll unlock successive tiers of rewards in the Battle Pass.
- **Duration:** The Battle Pass is active throughout the Legion of Frostborne war period. If the war ends early, the Battle Pass concludes accordingly.
- **Server Transfers:** Be cautious—transferring to another server during the Battle Pass period will reset your progress, including paid unlocks and task completions.[infinitykingdom.gtarcade.com](https://infinitykingdom.gtarcade.com/en/news/7022562.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 🏆 Reward Tiers: Free vs. Paid

### Free Tier

While accessible to all players, the free tier offers modest rewards, primarily consisting of resources and random immortal fragments.

### Paid Tier

Investing in the paid tier significantly boosts your rewards, making it highly worthwhile for dedicated players. **The paid tier will cost 50$ for a seasonal pass.** Key benefits include:

- **Holy/Shadow Random Fragments:** Enhance your immortal collection with a substantial number of these valuable fragments.
- **Tower of Knowledge Skill Unlock:** Gain a full 60-stone unlock of a top-tier Tower of Knowledge skill, providing a significant advantage in battles.
- **Castle skin**: Gain the new Corridor of Ghostflame skin adding 2% Troop HP and 5% honor.
