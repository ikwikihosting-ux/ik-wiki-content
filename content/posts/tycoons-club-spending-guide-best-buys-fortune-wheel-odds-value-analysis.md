## VIP Pass – Is the $5 Purchase Worth It?

The premium VIP Pass costs $4.99 and offers one of the best returns in the entire event.

Completing all 30 levels rewards players with 450 Amethyst Stones, which can be converted into **900 Tycoon Coins**, alongside 500 Gems and various resource chests.

Combined with the free pass and Max Bounty rewards, the total potential currency looks like this:

That's already almost enough for 100 Crimson Moonlight, which costs 2,500 Tycoon Coins in the Private Treasure Gallery.

For comparison, a free-to-play player completing the available activities can collect approximately 1,370 coins, enough to purchase 100 Stardust for 1,000 coins.

**Our verdict: The $4.99 VIP Pass is an excellent purchase for active players who can complete all 30 levels.** The currency alone makes it attractive, even before accounting for Gems and additional resources.

## Gilded Club – The Best Way to Buy Additional Coins

One easily overlooked mechanic is that the Gilded Club shop uses a tiered purchase system.

Buying one offer unlocks the next, and these tiers include free rewards and purchases made with Gems. This makes the shop much better value than simply comparing the prices of individual bundles.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151648-zs584h.png)

Here are the first nine tiers:

The combined cost of reaching tier 9 is **$10.96 plus 1,000 Gems**, rewarding 120 Amethyst Stones from tiers 2–9. These convert into 240 Tycoon Coins.

Because the shop offers free and Gem-purchased rewards between paid bundles, progressing through the tiers gives players considerably more value than purchasing Amethysts from standalone bundles.

### Getting 100 Crimson Moonlight for $15.95

![](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151438-net5mp.png)

This is where the event becomes particularly interesting.

Players who complete their free activities and purchase the VIP Pass can obtain 2,270 Tycoon Coins. They need just 230 more to purchase 100 Crimson Moonlight.

![](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151255-g2yrml.png)

By progressing through the first nine Gilded Club tiers, they obtain another 240 coins.

The total monetary cost is:

* VIP Pass: $4.99
* Gilded Club paid tiers: $10.96
* Gilded Club Gem purchases: 1,000 Gems

**Total: $15.95 + 1,000 Gems for 100 Crimson Moonlight**, assuming all event activities are completed.

Players also receive the VIP Pass rewards, additional resources from Gilded Club purchases, and other items along the way.

That makes this one of the most interesting low-spender opportunities in the event.

The calculation assumes seven daily free claims, full Max Bounty completion, and completion of all 30 VIP Pass levels.

## Is It Worth Spending More in the Gilded Club?

Beyond the first few tiers, the Gilded Club introduces increasingly expensive purchases, but also larger rewards.

For example, the later tiers include free Amethyst rewards after paid purchases, alongside additional Gem-based offers.

This means each purchase should be evaluated based on the cumulative rewards it unlocks, not just the Amethysts included in that particular bundle.

However, there are diminishing returns. Once you've obtained enough coins for your target Gallery purchase, additional spending should be justified by the next specific reward you want.

The more expensive Chaos-related rewards require substantially greater investment. For example, a 34,000-coin reward requires the equivalent of 17,000 Amethyst Stones before accounting for event rewards.

![](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-151427-brxp7p.png)

For most players, pursuing these premium rewards through direct purchases will be considerably more expensive than obtaining Crimson Moonlight or Stardust.

**Our recommendation is to take advantage of the early Gilded Club tiers, particularly when they unlock free or Gem-purchased Amethysts, but avoid buying large bundles without a clear reward target.**

## Fortune Wheel – Is Gambling Your Coins Profitable?

The Fortune Wheel allows players to wager Tycoon Coins for a chance to win more coins and additional prizes.

Unfortunately, the expected returns are not particularly attractive.

These are statistical averages, not guarantees. Individual players can win substantially more or lose almost everything they wager.

The key consideration is that **the Fortune Wheel uses the same Tycoon Coins needed for guaranteed Gallery rewards**.

If you gamble 1,000 coins, your expected coin balance after the resulting payouts is approximately 898 coins. You may receive other resources and leaderboard points, but your purchasing power in the Gallery has decreased on average.

### Does Energy Make Gambling Better?

The Fortune Wheel features an Energy meter that provides a 10% discount when fully charged.

For example, a regular spin costing 10 coins drops to 9 coins when the discount is active. A 10× spin costing 100 coins drops to 90.

At the discounted price of 9 coins, the expected return of approximately 8.98 coins is almost break-even.

However, players must first accumulate the Energy required to activate the discount, meaning the overall activity still involves regular-price spins.

The Reward Multiplier also increases both the wager and potential rewards without appearing to improve winning probabilities.

**Our verdict: Gambling is not a reliable way to increase your Tycoon Coin balance.**

The Fortune Wheel can still be worthwhile for players competing for leaderboard prizes or seeking its other rewards, but players targeting guaranteed Gallery purchases should generally save their coins.

## What Are the Best Rewards to Buy?

The Private Treasure Gallery contains a wide range of rewards, but two stand out for ordinary players.

**100 Crimson Moonlight – 2,500 Tycoon Coins**

This is arguably the most attractive purchase for players who need Crimson Moonlight. The relatively low coin requirement, combined with the VIP Pass and early Gilded Club tiers, makes it accessible without enormous spending.

**100 Stardust – 1,000 Tycoon Coins**

Stardust is an excellent alternative for free-to-play players or those who need the resource for their current Immortal progression.

The larger artifact and Chaos-related rewards can be valuable, but their much higher currency requirements make them substantially less attractive for most low and moderate spenders.

## Final Recommendations

Tycoon's Club is an unusual event because its best value appears concentrated at the lowest spending levels.

For just $4.99, the premium VIP Pass supplies substantial currency and additional resources. With a relatively small investment in the Gilded Club, players can reach the 2,500 coins required for 100 Crimson Moonlight.

Beyond that point, the cost of obtaining additional high-end rewards increases significantly.

Meanwhile, the Fortune Wheel offers exciting potential jackpots but is not an efficient method of accumulating coins over time. Its negative expected return makes guaranteed Gallery purchases the more dependable choice for most players.

**Our recommendation: Buy the VIP Pass if you can complete it, take advantage of the early Gilded Club tiers, and prioritize guaranteed rewards rather than gambling your currency.**

For low spenders especially, 100 Crimson Moonlight for $15.95 plus 1,000 Gems looks like one of the standout opportunities in this new event.
