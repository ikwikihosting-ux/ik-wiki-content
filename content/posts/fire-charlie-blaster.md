This guide is part of my article series on Prime League Illusion Builds. If this build is not to your likeing, have a look at the rest of my builds on the main Prime League page.

**This build is made for:**Round 3 of Pre-league (150 Violets)

This article is on fire with Charles. From running fire for round 1 and round 2 on my main account, I find that the removal of tech and VIP boosts means crit is simply too low to resonably run chase setups like the one I did for round 1 with Baldwin. Instead, relying on Charles strong shields and boosting the base damage of Immortal Ultimate abilities seems the best way to go. Therefore, I have narroewed down my previous two fire builds into a new version updated for round 3. Noticably we boost Wu ultimate moer, continue to focus on physical damage and building a superior crit-environment with both weakness, malice and AD on William.

## Setup

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-7.png)

**Empress Wu**: Annihilation + Concentration + Weakness

**Ashoka**: Death Breath + Cleave + Anger

**Charles**: Oaken Guard + Assist + Malice

**William I**: Adrenaline Rush + Fighting Master + Coercion

## Strengths and weaknesses

This setup is a solid wind-lightning killer. With the high damage nuke it eats through slow-sustain and no-sustain setups quickly. Moreover, since we are running Charles, we are not weak to the wounds debuff from Qin / Khan.

**In conclusion, a strong march that will kill most things. Great for rallying!**
