## Countering Energy Regeneration Skills in Infinity Kingdom

In the dynamic world of Infinity Kingdom, mastering the art of countering enemy skills is an indispensable skill set for any commander seeking victory in PvP battles. One category of skills that often takes center stage in these confrontations is energy regeneration skills. In this guide, we will delve into the intricacies of countering energy regen skills, focusing on three specific abilities: Energy Burst, Adrenaline Rush, and Blessing of Rage. These skills enhance your immortals' energy regeneration, providing them with a significant advantage in the heat of battle.

### 1. Countering Energy Burst (EB): Nullifying the Power of Critical Hits

Energy Burst is a skill that thrives on critical hits to provide energy regeneration. It is often utilized by immortals like Hippolytta or Khubilaikhan, whose ultimate abilities involve multiple smaller damage hits. The more hits per second, the greater the chance of landing critical hits and, consequently, energy regeneration from EB.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-68.png)

To counter this skill effectively, consider the following tactics:

- **Control Effects**: Limit the enemy's opportunities to cast ultimates by using control effects such as stun, silence, or other crowd control methods. By doing so, you reduce the enemy's chance to land those critical hits and regenerate energy.
- **Dodge and Misdirection**: Skills like Misleading and After-image can enhance your dodge chance against the enemy. A physical attack cannot land a critical hit if it doesn't hit at all. These skills provide a strategic way to reduce the effectiveness of Energy Burst.
- **Energy Suppression**: This potent skill directly counters enemy energy regeneration. It is especially effective against opponents who heavily rely on energy regen.

### 2. Countering Adrenaline Rush: Disrupting Normal Hits for Energy Gain

Adrenaline Rush operates differently, relying on normal hits for a chance to activate energy regeneration.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-67.png)

Countering Adrenaline Rush involves minimizing the enemy's opportunities to hit you with normal attacks. Here are some strategies to thwart this skill:

- **Dodge and Misdirection**: Utilize skills like Misleading and After-image to increase your dodge chance against the enemy's normal attacks, which are always physical. If they can't land their hits, they can't trigger Adrenaline Rush.
- **Control Effects**: Employ stun, silence, or root effects to reduce the enemy's attack speed and limit their normal attacks. These controls play a pivotal role in diminishing the impact of Adrenaline Rush.
- **Energy Suppression**: As with Energy Burst, Energy Suppression is an excellent counter to Adrenaline Rush, especially against opponents heavily invested in energy regeneration.

### 3. Countering Blessing of Rage: Silencing the Energy Surge

Blessing of Rage poses a unique challenge as it provides energy regeneration to three immortals for the initial 12 seconds of a battle, without relying on critical hits or normal attacks.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-66.png)

To counter this effect, consider the following approaches:

- **Silence Effects**: Employ skills that silence the enemy, removing their ability to regenerate energy. Silencing disrupts Blessing of Rage's energy surge effectively.
- **Energy Suppression**: As an overarching counter to energy regeneration, Energy Suppression can counteract the effects of Blessing of Rage by limiting the enemy's ability to build energy.

In the world of Infinity Kingdom, countering energy regeneration skills is a strategic imperative. By mastering these tactics, you can mitigate your opponents' advantages and emerge as a commanding presence on the battlefield. Remember that every counter has its own strengths, and the key to success lies in choosing the right approach for each situation.
