***Announcement: Cross-Server Spider Competition - Round 10!***

Greetings, fearless commanders!

As we emerge from the festive holiday break into the first event of 2024, get ready for the adrenaline-pumping return of the Cross-Server Spider Competition! It's time to reignite the excitement and prove your mettle against the formidable Titantula Spider.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-15.png)

### Event Schedule:

- Start Date: November 29th, 00:01 (UTC)
- End Date: December 14th, 23:59 (UTC)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-18-1024x567.png)

**Reward Pool:**
A whopping **15,800 gems** await the champions across both the upper and lower brackets of this thrilling tournament!

**Upper Bracket Rewards (Level 100 Spiders):**
1st Place: 2000 gems
2nd Place: 1200 gems
3rd Place: 800 gems
4th to 10th Place: 500 gems each
11th to 20th Place: 300 gems each

**Lower Bracket Rewards:**
1st Place: 500 gems
2nd to 10th Place: 300 gems each

### How to Participate:

1. Screenshot your biggest hit on the spider boss
2. Send it via the official Submissions Channel on IK-Official Discord (link below)
3. Screenshot MUST BE of a hit within the timeframe above
4. Multiple submissions allowed (please do not spam)

![](blob:https://ik-wiki.com/5bae5784-ea6e-45e5-bc48-31eec58f2d5b)

### Submission Details:

- All submissions must be made via the official Infinity Kingdom Discord.
- Head to the event-submissions channel to share your conquests and be part of the epic saga.
