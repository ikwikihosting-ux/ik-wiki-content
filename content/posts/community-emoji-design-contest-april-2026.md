## A New Creator Event Begins – Community Emoji Design Contest

Infinity Kingdom has moved straight from the success of the Creator Frame event into its next community-driven challenge, and this one leans even further into everyday player interaction. The **「Community Emoji Design Contest」** is now live, and it’s one of the more practical and engaging creator events we’ve seen so far.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-65-1024x577.png)

Where the frame event focused on identity and presentation, this contest shifts toward something players actually use constantly—communication. The goal is simple on paper, but it opens up a lot of room for creativity: design emojis that feel like they truly belong to the Infinity Kingdom community.

---

## What This Event Is About

At its core, this event asks players to create a **set of emojis based on Infinity Kingdom’s Immortals**, designed for regular social use. That distinction matters. These are not just decorative assets—they’re meant to function in real conversations across Discord and community spaces.

There are a few key requirements to keep in mind:

- Your emojis should be **based on IK Immortals**
- They need to be **clear, readable, and stylistically consistent**
- A set of **3–5 emojis is considered optimal**, though more are allowed
- You can use **Photoshop, AI tools, or other software**, but the work must be **original**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-67.png)

This naturally pushes designs toward something that works at small scale, similar to how frame readability mattered in the previous event. Overly detailed designs tend to lose clarity, while strong silhouettes and expressions stand out immediately.

---

## How to Participate

Joining is straightforward:

- Create your emoji set following the guidelines
- Submit your work through the [**official Discord event channel**](https://discord.gg/kps7gK9gxY)
- Ensure your submission follows the required format

The event runs **until April 29**, which gives enough time to iterate if you want to refine your work after seeing other submissions.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-66.png)

### Design requirements:

- Create a set of emojis based on the Immortals of IK, suitable for everyday social use.
- Ensure clear visuals and a consistent style, with no limit on the number of emojis in the set (3-5 is optimal).
- Support PS creation or AI assistance, must be original and compliant, with no restrictions on form.

---

## Rewards and Recognition

Like previous creator events, this one balances accessibility with meaningful incentives:

- **All participants** receive in-game item rewards
- High-quality entries may be featured in the official **Community Emoji Selection**, with proper credit
- The **most interesting emoji** will be voted on by players and added to the Discord server

There’s also long-term value tied to participation:

- Taking part in **three creator theme events** earns a **creator-exclusive avatar frame**

That structure makes it worthwhile even if you’re not aiming for the top spot.

---

## Why This Event Is Worth Joining

From a player perspective, this is one of the more approachable creator events. Designing emojis doesn’t require the same level of structural precision as frames, but it does reward a good understanding of expression and clarity.

In practice, the strongest entries will likely focus on:

- **Recognizable Immortal traits** exaggerated into simple expressions
- Clean, bold visuals that remain readable at small sizes
- A consistent style across the entire emoji set

If you’ve spent time in alliance chat or Discord, you already know how quickly certain reactions become part of daily communication. This event is essentially giving players the chance to define that “emoji language” for the community.

---

## More Than Just a Contest

Just like the frame event, a big part of the value here comes from browsing the submissions themselves. These creator channels tend to develop into a mix of:

- Well-crafted, highly polished designs
- Creative or unexpected interpretations of Immortals
- Lighthearted or humorous entries that reflect community culture

Even if you don’t plan to submit anything, it’s worth joining the channel just to see what others are creating.

---

## Final Thoughts

The **Community Emoji Design Contest** builds directly on the momentum of the previous creator events, but shifts the focus toward something more interactive and widely used. It’s not just about creating something that looks good—it’s about creating something players will actually use.

If you want to see more events like this continue, participation matters. Submit a design, experiment with ideas, or at the very least follow the event and engage with the community. These creator contests are starting to define a new layer of Infinity Kingdom, and this is exactly the kind of event that benefits from broad player involvement.
