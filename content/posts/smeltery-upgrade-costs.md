Unlocking at Castle level 4, the Smeltery can provide you with Iron. Iron is an important material for construction, troop training and fortification manufacturing. Iron is needed for most War related technologies. So if you plan to research those technologies first, make sure you have enough Iron.

![](https://oss.gtarcade.com/forum/jpg/2020-08-28/25_a153b2f9-dfbd-4ae8-b55b-5b44c945c908_193240.jpg)
