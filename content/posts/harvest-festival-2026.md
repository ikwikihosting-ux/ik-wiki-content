## Introduction

The **Harvest Festival 2026** follows Infinity Kingdom's familiar seasonal-event format, with several returning activities alongside a few important changes to the rewards and progression.

If you played through the previous seasonal festivals, most of the event will feel familiar. **Lucky Koi, Summer Passion, Fishing Contest, Dazzling Wish, Grail Shop, Pinnacle Trial, and Glorious Challenge** all return, while **Fruit Frenzy** introduces a 2048-style minigame to the rotation.

The interesting part of Harvest Festival is not simply that these activities are back. It is how the rewards have changed.

Fishing Contest continues the progression of **Alexander the Great – Summer Passion**, while Dazzling Wish once again focuses on **Athena – Summer Passion**. Fruit Frenzy provides another source of Magic Fishing Nets and introduces its own seasonal insignia.

The biggest change, however, concerns **Eternal Grails**.

The large Grail jackpot previously found in Dazzling Wish has been removed, but it has effectively been replaced by a dedicated **Grail Wheel**. In practice, this is a very good change: considerably more Grails can now be obtained than through the old jackpot system. At the same time, the Grail Shop has received one of its most interesting updates yet, including much cheaper access to **Apollo and Medusa**, as well as their artifact progression.

Rather than listing every reward available during the festival, this guide focuses on what each event tab is for, what has changed, and where I think players should concentrate their effort.

---

## Lucky Koi

**Lucky Koi** returns in essentially the same format as previous seasonal events.

Purchasing the Koi Gift Pack provides entry into the Lucky Koi lottery alongside the resources contained in the pack. Players who participate repeatedly without winning also progress toward the guaranteed consolation reward, so there is at least some protection against walking away empty-handed after several unsuccessful draws. Note that this 5-purchase bonus reward alone is the highest efficiency Grail Item available in the game. So make sure you get it!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-43-1024x527.png)

There is also a free daily gift attached to Lucky Koi. Even if you have no intention of purchasing the paid pack, remember to open the tab and collect it each day.

## Summer Passion

**Summer Passion** is the main progression battlepass for Harvest Festival and acts as one of the links between the different event activities.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-44-1024x562.png)

Progressing the pass supplies resources used elsewhere during Harvest, including **Tokens of Light** for Dazzling Wish and **Magic Fishing Nets** for Fishing Contest.

It also continues the seasonal progression surrounding **Alexander the Great – Summer Passion**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-45.png)

Alexander's outfit requires 60 fragments for the initial craft, with additional fragments used afterward to increase its Glamour Level. Players who started the outfit during the previous festival can therefore continue developing it during Harvest rather than starting an entirely separate outfit progression.

Summer Passion is also connected to the **Judgment Realm** castle skin.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-46.png)

Judgment Realm provides permanent account bonuses and is significant enough that its fragment requirements and progression deserve a separate article rather than a small subsection here.

## Fishing Contest

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-47-1024x566.png)

**Fishing Contest** is once again particularly important for Alexander players.

The event uses **Magic Fishing Nets**, with Fishing Contest progression providing **Alexander the Great – Summer Passion Outfit Fragments** at its major catch milestones.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-50.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-49-1024x556.png)

There is also a **unique Harvest Festival insignia** available through Fishing Contest, giving collectors a separate reason to pay attention to the event.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-48-1024x369.png)

Fishing Contest has enough going on that it makes more sense as a standalone guide. Catch progression, milestone planning and the various ways of acquiring Nets all influence how far it is actually worth pushing. To complete the 15 level each day you need around 57K nets each (two players).

Harvest Festival offer Nets through several other activities. Fruit Frenzy, Summer Passion and the combat events all contribute toward your total. There is little reason to buy your way toward an Alexander milestone on the first day when free Nets earned later in the festival might have completed the same threshold.

## Fruit Frenzy

**Fruit Frenzy** is the new minigame in Harvest Festival, although the underlying game will be immediately recognizable to a lot of players. It is basically **2048 with fruit**. You combine matching fruits to create progressively higher-level fruits, while trying to keep enough usable space on the board to continue merging. If you already know how to play 2048, there is very little new to learn mechanically. If you do not, this video provides a useful explanation of the underlying strategy: [Watch this 2048 strategy guide on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kQhkkqjGkFA&utm_)

The most important habit is to keep your largest fruit toward one corner and build the board around it. Randomly chasing every available merge tends to scatter your high-level pieces, and once that happens it becomes increasingly difficult to combine them before the board fills.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-51-1024x532.png)

Prioritize **board structure over short-term merges**. Sometimes declining an attractive-looking movement pattern is better than disrupting the corner you have spent the entire game building.

Fruit Frenzy is also worth doing even if you are not particularly interested in the minigame itself. The event provides **Magic Fishing Nets**, which means Fruit Frenzy progression feeds directly into Fishing Contest and Alexander's outfit progression. There is also a **unique Harvest Festival insignia** to obtain here.

For Alexander players, that makes Fruit Frenzy effectively part of Fishing Contest progression. Every useful batch of Nets obtained here is one less batch you potentially need to purchase elsewhere.

## Dazzling Wish

**Dazzling Wish** returns as the main event associated with **Athena – Summer Passion**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-52-1024x538.png)

Tokens of Light collected throughout Harvest Festival can be spent here, with Athena outfit fragments forming the major long-term target.

The core event is therefore familiar, but there is one important change compared with previous seasonal versions.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-53-1024x499.png)

The big **Eternal Grail jackpot is gone from Dazzling Wish**.

Initially, I expected this to be a fairly substantial reduction in the value of the event. Eternal Grails have become increasingly useful, particularly as the Grail Shop continues to improve, so removing a large Grail payout would normally hurt.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-54-1024x573.png)

Fortunately, that is not really what happened. Instead of keeping Grail acquisition tied to a lucky Dazzling Wish result, Harvest Festival introduces a separate **Grail Wheel**. And the new system is considerably better.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-55-1024x508.png)

The dedicated Grail Wheel provides a much stronger opportunity to accumulate Eternal Grails, to the point that players can obtain **even more Grails than were available through previous versions of the festival**. I think this is an excellent change to the event structure. Dazzling Wish now has a clearer identity: **you are primarily here for Athena**. Eternal Grail progression has its own system instead of being dependent on hitting the right Dazzling Wish reward.

## Grail Shop

The **Grail Shop is arguably the biggest winner of Harvest Festival 2026**.

There are still plenty of familiar resources and progression materials available, but those are not what makes the current version noteworthy. The big change is access to **Apollo and Medusa**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-56-1024x528.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-57-1024x568.png)

Both are now available through extremely attractive Eternal Grail options compared with the investment historically required to develop these Immortals. For players who have wanted to build either Immortal but could never justify the previous cost, this is a significant change.

Apollo and Medusa have traditionally sat in a category where deciding to build them represented a serious investment. Harvest's Grail Shop substantially lowers that barrier quite significantly, enabling slow-build strategies by low- to mid-spenders.

The choice between Apollo and Medusa will depend on your account and where you are in progression. Apollo remains extremely strong during Conquest, while Medusa becomes a much more interesting long-term proposition once **Legendary Tower of Knowledge skills** enter the picture. **[Read more here.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/new-player-guides/season-progression-everything-you-need-to-know-before-a-new-season/which-chaos-immortal-carry-to-get-going-from-conquest-into-legendary/)**

I would therefore make sure to buy fragment chests simply because they are cheap. Decide which Immortal actually fits your account later, and use the seasonal discount to accelerate a build you genuinely intend to finish.

The other important part of the Grail Shop update is **artifact progression**. Cheap Immortal fragments are only half the equation if the corresponding artifact remains prohibitively difficult to develop. Having artifact options alongside the Immortals makes the current shop much more useful because players can work toward a complete build rather than merely unlocking another Immortal.

For developed accounts, I would pay particularly close attention here. If you already have the Immortal progression you need, artifact fragments may provide considerably more practical combat value than adding another unfinished Immortal to the roster.

## Pinnacle Trial

**Pinnacle Trial** returns as one of Harvest Festival's combat activities. The basic format has not changed enough to require another lengthy explanation. Work through the available stages, collect the festival progression attached to them, and participate in the ranking component if your account is strong enough to compete for it.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-58-1024x551.png)

What matters for the wider Harvest Festival is that Pinnacle Trial contributes resources that feed into the other event systems.

---

## Glorious Challenge

**Glorious Challenge** fills a similar role. Progress through the stages, collect the available festival resources, and move into the harder content as it becomes available and your account allows.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-59-1024x557.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-60-1024x336.png)

Again, I would not overcomplicate this section. Glorious Challenge is familiar content, and the strategic decision is less about how the activity itself works and more about remembering to finish it before calculating how many Nets, Harvest Coins you require each day for the daily stock in the Challenge Shop.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-61-1024x484.png)

Free progression should always be exhausted before paid progression.

---

## Conclusion Summary

Harvest Festival 2026 keeps the familiar seasonal format, but several reward changes make this version particularly worthwhile. Fishing Contest remains the main route for **Alexander – Summer Passion**, Fruit Frenzy provides additional Fishing Nets and a seasonal insignia, while Dazzling Wish once again focuses on **Athena – Summer Passion**.

The biggest improvement is the combination of the new **Grail Wheel and updated Grail Shop**. More Eternal Grails are available, and they are considerably more valuable now that the shop offers cheap access to **Apollo and Medusa**, alongside useful artifact progression. Clear Pinnacle Trial and Glorious Challenge, collect all the free resources first, and only afterward decide which Alexander, Athena or Grail Shop milestones are worth pursuing.
