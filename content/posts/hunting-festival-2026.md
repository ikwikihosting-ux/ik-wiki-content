## Introduction

The Hunting Festival has returned to Norheim for May 2026, bringing another seasonal event built around Token of Light progression, Eternal Grails, and cooperative milestone rewards. Structurally, the event is very similar to previous seasonal festivals, though this version introduces the new **Throne of Blood** castle skin and a cooperative progression system through the **Hunting Feast** tab.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-69-1024x543.png)

For experienced players, the event is mainly about efficient grail acquisition and long-term Chaos immortal progression. For newer or mid-game accounts, the value comes from maximizing free Token of Light income, coordinating feast participation properly, and avoiding inefficient spending traps.

The event includes eight primary tabs:

1. Lucky Koi
2. Throne of Blood
3. Ingredient Hunt
4. Hunting Feast
5. Dazzling Wish
6. Grail Shop
7. Glorious Challenge
8. Pinnacle Trial

Below is a full breakdown of each section, including where the real value sits and which areas deserve priority.

---

## 1. Lucky Koi

The Lucky Koi tab works almost identically to previous festival versions. Purchasing a **Koi Gift Pack** enters you into the recurring lottery for a large Eternal Grail jackpot every two days.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-70-1024x554.png)

### Koi Gift Pack Rewards

Each purchase includes:

- 5 Tokens of Light
- Gems
- VIP Points
- Participation in the 4,250 Eternal Grail lottery

In addition, purchasing five Koi packs during the event grants an extra **850 Eternal Grails**.

From a pure efficiency perspective, the Koi pack remains one of the strongest direct grail-value purchases in the game. If your account is actively progressing Chaos immortals, especially expensive immortals like Tengu or future artifact upgrades, the grail-per-dollar efficiency is difficult to beat.

Players also receive one free Token of Light daily from this tab. It is minor individually, but over the full event duration it contributes meaningfully toward additional Dazzling Wish spins.

## 2. Throne of Blood

This is the event battle pass section.

Like previous festivals, there is both a free reward track and a premium reward track.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-71-1024x528.png)

## Free Track Rewards

The free track mainly provides:

- 30 Tokens of Light
- Standard progression resources

## Premium Track Rewards

The premium pass includes:

- 120 additional Tokens of Light
- Gems
- Large quantities of Gem Meat
- The Throne of Blood castle skin

The important detail here is the amount of Gem Meat included. Compared to earlier seasonal passes, the raw Token of Light count may look similar, but the Hunting Feast system dramatically**increases the total effective value.**

If your feast group contributes evenly, the extra Gem Meat effectively converts into significantly more Tokens of Light, Soul Crystal Chests, and Philosopher’s Offer Chests.

## Throne of Blood Castle Skin Analysis

The new skin provides:

- 5% Troop Attack
- 5% Technology Speed

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-72.png)

The troop attack bonus immediately places this among the strongest combat-oriented castle skins currently available.

In practice, troop attack scales better than immortal stats because it scales off of troop count to much greater values.

### Comparison to Abyssal Throne

Many late-game players will compare this skin directly with Abyssal Throne.

- **Abyssal Throne** offers troop attack plus reduced death rate.
- **Throne of Blood** offers troop attack plus technology speed.

From actual gameplay experience, Abyssal Throne still has slightly better wartime sustainability because reduced death rate saves resources during prolonged fighting.

However, the 5% technology speed bonus on Throne of Blood is extremely relevant because technology tends to remain one of the final long-term progression bottlenecks even for highly developed accounts.

For players still heavily progressing research, this skin has real long-term value beyond appearance alone.

## Battle Pass Progression

Progression is handled through standard daily quests.

Quests include:

- Troop training
- Arena battles
- Well of Time
- Market purchases
- Pinnacle Trial
- Glorious Challenge
- Gnome bosses

You can afford to miss a small number of tasks, but consistent daily completion is strongly recommended if you want full value from the pass.

## 3. Ingredient Hunt

Ingredient Hunt is the event’s 2048-style minigame.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-73-1024x542.png)

Mechanically, it works almost identically to previous puzzle events. Players merge ingredients into higher-tier dishes while progressing through increasingly valuable reward tiers.

## Main Rewards

The most important rewards are:

- Gem Meat
- Festival Insignia progression
- Small amounts of Eternal Grails at higher tiers

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-74-1024x520.png)

The real value here is the Gem Meat. Because Hunting Feast rewards scale so well, every free piece of Gem Meat matters. Even fully free-to-play players can accumulate meaningful feast progression simply by maximizing their Ingredient Hunt attempts.

The Eternal Grail rewards at the highest tiers are useful, but noticeably less generous compared to some earlier 2048 event versions.

## Should You Buy Extra Attempts?

In most cases, no.

If you are exceptionally good at this game, you can max it without and if you are not, you are most likely not going to see the big lobster rewards anyway.

For most players, the better approach is:

1. Use all free attempts
2. Maximize Gem Meat income
3. Convert that value through Hunting Feast progression

If you play the mode consistently every day, the cumulative rewards become surprisingly solid even without spending.

## 4. Hunting Feast

Hunting Feast is the most important tab of the entire event.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-75-1024x530.png)

Players invite two partners and collectively spend Gem Meat to progress through shared milestone rewards.

## Major Rewards

The reward pool includes:

- Large quantities of Tokens of Light
- Soul Crystal Chests
- Philosopher’s Offer Chests
- Speedups
- Additional progression resources

The overall value here is extremely high.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-76.png)

## Coordination Matters

This event heavily rewards balanced participation.

The best setup is usually:

- Three players with similar spending levels
- Similar activity levels
- Shared expectations before starting

One of the biggest mistakes players make is joining random feast groups where contribution expectations are unclear.

## Spending Strategy

Buying Gem Meat can be relatively efficient compared to directly purchasing Tokens of Light, but only if:

- Your group is active
- Both parties contributes
- You actually reach meaningful milestone tiers

Note that for some, you may have a whale friend who does not mind carrying you. In that case, you naturally do not need to buy anything but instead thank the kind person for carrying your feast! Efficiency of bundles assume equal participation...

This is the event section I would prioritize above almost everything else during the event.

Even lower-spending accounts can gain substantial value simply by coordinating properly with alliance members. In previous cooperative seasonal events, the players who planned participation ahead of time consistently gained far more value than players spending impulsively.

## 5. Dazzling Wish

Dazzling Wish remains the standard Token of Light spin system.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-77-1024x545.png)

Players spend Tokens of Light for chances at Eternal Grails.

## Grail Drop Rates

- 0.45% → 300 Grails
- 1.55% → 50 Grails
- 3.5% → 25 Grails
- 12% → 5 Grails
- 82.5% → 1 Grail

## Expected Value

Over large sample sizes, the average value remains approximately: **1 Token of Light ≈ 4.2–4.3 Eternal Grails**

## 6. Grail Shop

The Grail Shop is where event currencies convert into permanent account progression.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-78-1024x524.png)

## Eternal Grail Costs

### Immortals

- Tengu → 750 Grails
- Fu Fei → 450 Grails
- Athena / Wukong / Loki / Poseidon → 250 Grails

### Artifacts

- Tengu Artifact → 1050 Grails
- Most Chaos Artifacts → 1000 Grails
- Fu Fei Artifact → 600 Grails

## General Priority Advice

Since there is no major new immortal release pressure this event, most accounts should focus on efficient long-term Chaos development.

### Mid-Spender Perspective

For many mid-spenders:

- Daily 250-grail immortals
- Fu Fei purchases

...often provide the best long-term efficiency.

Moving into the 750-grail tier is a larger commitment because those immortals generally require continued investment through future events and artifact progression.

## Challenge Shop

The Challenge Shop uses Feathers earned through Glorious Challenge.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-79-1024x547.png)

Important purchases include:

- Stardust
- Unique Artifacts
- Tokens of Light

## Smart Feather Management

Do not spend all gem-ticket resources immediately.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-80.png)

Hard Mode unlocks later during the event, and many players regret exhausting their ticket economy too early.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-81.png)

## Player Perspective

Personally, I almost always prioritize Stardust because opportunities to accumulate meaningful quantities remain relatively limited.

After that, remaining currency usually goes toward Tokens of Light for additional grail progression.

## 7. Glorious Challenge

Glorious Challenge consists of:

- Normal Mode
- Hard Mode

Normal Mode opens immediately, while Hard Mode unlocks later during the event.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-82-1024x556.png)

## Rewards

Players earn:

- Feathers
- Gem Meat
- Gems
- Tokens of Light

This mode is simple mechanically, but skipping daily clears significantly hurts event efficiency (you get 5 daily free tickets that cannot carry over into other days).

Raiding gives smaller returns than first clears, but you will need to raid a few Normal camps to make sure you use the daily stock in the feather-shop for the items you most desire based on current account progression (e.g. artifacts vs stardust vs tokens).

Again, careful management of gem-ticket purchases matters if you want enough currency available for Hard Mode progression.

---

## 8. Pinnacle Trial

Pinnacle Trial features stronger enemies with one-time daily attempts.

## Main Rewards

- Random Chaos immortal fragments
- Tokens of Light
- Gem Meat
- Festival Insignia progression

## Do Not Miss Daily Attempts

Missing days here is one of the easiest ways to lose free event value.

Even accounts that cannot fully clear every stage should attempt progression daily.

## Difficulty Advice

Some later stages can become surprisingly difficult depending on account development.

If you struggle:

- Adjust skill compositions
- Experiment with march positioning
- Ask alliance or Discord communities like the ik-wiki community for tested setups

Often small composition adjustments matter more than raw power.

---

## Conclusion Summary

The May 2026 Hunting Festival is one of the stronger seasonal events we have seen recently because multiple systems overlap efficiently.

The combination of:

- High-value cooperative rewards
- Strong Token of Light generation
- A genuinely useful castle skin
- Consistent Chaos progression

...makes this an event worth taking seriously even for players who normally spend conservatively.

For free-to-play players, the key is maximizing daily participation and finding reliable feast partners.

For spenders, the best value comes from combining Koi efficiency, battle pass rewards, and coordinated Hunting Feast progression.

Most importantly, avoid wasting resources early in the event without planning around Hard Mode unlocks and cooperative milestones. Good coordination matters just as much as spending during this festival.
