For players who enjoy PvP, Fighting Master is the most straightforward profession. Its talent tree provides powerful bonuses to Troop Attack, Troop Defense, Marching Speed, and survivability, along with additional bonuses for Immortal damage and rallies.

However, upgrading these talents is expensive, and not all of them are equally valuable. In this guide, we'll go through what Fighting Masters do, how the talent tree works, the costs of upgrading it, and which bonuses are most useful.

## TL;DR – Is Fighting Master Worth Choosing?

Fighting Master is designed for active combat players who want to maximize their effectiveness during Legendary KvK.

* **Best for:** Strong, active PvP players and alliance rally leaders.
* **Main advantage:** Up to +10% Troop Attack, +10% Troop Defense, +10% Marching Speed, and additional combat bonuses.
* **Upgrade cost:** 3 million Gold per talent point, with five points available in each of seven talents.
* **Maximum theoretical cost:** 105 million Gold to fully upgrade all 35 talent points.
* **Our recommendation:** Prioritize Troop Attack and Troop Defense for general PvP performance, while considering survivability and specialized bonuses depending on your battlefield role.

Importantly, Fighting Master doesn't unlock exclusive combat activities. Craftsmen and Alchemists can still fight normally. The difference is that Fighting Masters gain additional bonuses that make them more effective in battle.

## What Does a Fighting Master Actually Do?

Fighting Master is the combat-focused profession in Legendary KvK. Unlike the other two professions, Fighting Masters don't have any production responsibilities. Instead, their specialization is designed around fighting enemy armies, attacking objectives, defending territory, and participating in alliance operations.

Their most important contribution is helping the alliance win the battles that allow Altar Stones to be deployed and protected.

Even though Altar Stones are needed to destroy the new Energy Cores, ordinary armies remain essential for defeating defending garrisons, destroying walls, fighting other players, and protecting strategic positions.

In practice, Fighting Masters are especially useful for powerful players who regularly participate in these activities.

For alliance leadership, however, it's important to remember that Fighting Master isn't automatically the best choice for everyone. An alliance with too many Fighting Masters and insufficient Craftsmen or Alchemists may struggle with the production needed to capture cities.

## The Fighting Master Talent Tree

Fighting Masters have seven talents, each with five upgrade levels. Every talent provides a different combat bonus, and developing the full tree requires a substantial investment.

The talent tree follows a progression system in which players must invest points to unlock additional talents.

Here are all seven talents and their maximum bonuses.

These are the maximum bonuses displayed in the current Fighting Master talent tree, based on in-game screenshots.

### 1. Superhuman Speed – Up to +10% Marching Speed

Superhuman Speed is the first talent in the Fighting Master tree and provides additional marching speed.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-090702-noag1e.png)

Each upgrade increases Marching Speed by 2%, reaching a maximum of 10% at five points.

While this doesn't directly improve combat damage, mobility is incredibly important during KvK. Faster marches allow players to reinforce threatened positions, move between objectives, and respond more quickly to changing battlefield conditions.

This is also the starting talent, meaning players must develop it to progress further through the tree.

### 2. Mighty Forces – Up to +10% Troop Attack

Mighty Forces increases Troop Attack when entering battle.

Each point provides 2% Troop Attack, reaching a maximum bonus of 10%.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-090710-7g4fv5.png)

This is arguably the strongest general-purpose talent available to Fighting Masters.

Troop Attack is valuable across virtually every march composition and directly improves offensive combat performance, making it particularly attractive for players who regularly attack other Lords or participate in contested sieges.

Unlike specialized bonuses that only benefit certain situations, Troop Attack is relevant to every single battle and is the single best modifier to obtain in terms of damage scaling. That makes this talent node particularly important and players want to try and complete enough achievements to unlock 5/5 Mighty Forces BEFORE the gates open and PvP begin.

**In essence:** One of the highest-priority talents for Fighting Masters. Once unlocked, maximizing Mighty Forces should be a major objective for offensive PvP players.

### 3. Rock Steady – Up to +10% Troop Defense

Rock Steady provides additional Troop Defense when entering battle.

Like Mighty Forces, it increases by 2% per point, reaching +10% at maximum level.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-090718-xtzk74.png)

Troop Defense improves survivability and can be particularly valuable during long engagements, defending objectives, or fighting opponents with stronger marches.

For competitive players, defensive bonuses can also help preserve troops and reduce the frequency with which armies need to be replenished.

The in-game talent tree shows that Rock Steady requires a total investment of 10 talent points before it becomes available.

**In Essence**: Another extremely valuable talent, particularly for frontline fighters and players responsible for defending important objectives. Together with Mighty Forces, this forms the core of a strong Fighting Master build.

### 4. Shelter – Up to −5% Soldier Death Rate

Shelter reduces soldier casualties, with each point providing a 1% reduction in Death Rate. At maximum level, the bonus reaches −5%.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-090725-e6few0.png)

This is an interesting talent because it doesn't necessarily make your march stronger during an individual battle. Instead, it helps reduce permanent troop losses.

During Legendary KvK, where players may spend weeks fighting over territory and participating in repeated battles, reducing deaths can have considerable long-term value.

For players who fight constantly, even a relatively modest reduction in casualties may help preserve troop reserves and reduce rebuilding costs.

### 5. Gladiator – Up to −5% Critical Damage Received

Gladiator reduces the critical damage received by all Immortals.

Each upgrade provides a 1% reduction, reaching −5% at maximum level.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-090732-jky4pz.png)

Unlike Rock Steady, which increases Troop Defense, Gladiator specifically targets critical damage dealt against Immortals. This is a super important node to reach, as crit damage is extreemly powerful in Infinity Kingdom and most top tier builds center around consistent critical strike chance and high critical strike damage.

Against teams that rely heavily on critical damage, the effect may be useful. Against opponents with limited critical damage, its value will naturally be lower.

### 6. Wild Nature – Up to +5% Immortal Damage

Wild Nature increases damage dealt by Immortals.

The bonus starts at +1% and increases to +5% when fully upgraded.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-090741-l09bh8.png)

This provides an offensive improvement that is separate from Mighty Forces' Troop Attack bonus.

For marches relying heavily on powerful damage-dealing Immortals, additional Immortal damage can be useful, particularly when combined with other offensive buffs.

This is the single strongest talent in the tree giving raw damage output. However it is also quite far to reach and requires you to be consistently and actively engaged in the season in order to complete enough achievements to unlock 5/5 here. 

When in a highly competitive season, you want to help your main key strongest players to complete their achievements in order to unlock Wild Nature before fighting for zone 3.

### 7. Team Worker – Up to +5% Rally Damage

Team Worker is the final specialized talent, increasing damage dealt when attacking through a rally.

Each upgrade provides +1% damage, reaching +5% at maximum level.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-090748-vmrfer.png)

There is one important restriction: **the bonus does not activate if the rally contains only one player**.

This makes Team Worker particularly interesting for alliance rally leaders and players who regularly participate in coordinated attacks.

However, players who primarily fight through individual marches will receive considerably less value from this talent.

An attractive specialization for dedicated rally players, but lower priority for players who spend most of their KvK fighting independently.

## How Much Does It Cost to Upgrade Fighting Master?

Every Fighting Master talent has five upgrade levels.

Each point costs **3 million ordinary Gold**, alongside one Contract Crystal earned through Legendary KvK achievements or other available sources.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-090759-db5qbz.png)

This means fully upgrading a single talent costs 15 million Gold.

Fully upgrading all seven talents therefore requires a theoretical total of **105 million Gold and 35 Contract Crystals**, assuming every upgrade costs one Crystal and 3 million Gold as shown in the current tree.

However, having enough Gold isn't the only requirement.

Players must also unlock talents progressively by investing points in earlier parts of the tree. Contract Crystals must be earned through the relevant achievements, and some talents require a minimum number of points to be invested before becoming available.

For example, Rock Steady requires 10 total invested points.

This means players cannot simply choose to maximize the strongest late-tree talents immediately.

The exact unlock requirements for every later talent should be checked in-game as the tree progresses.

## How to Progress Your Talents Efficiently

Fighting Master talents are developed using Contract Crystals obtained through Legendary KvK achievements.

For example, the Legion Achievements menu includes objectives such as constructing Thermal Towers, destroying enemy Thermal Towers, damaging city walls, and defeating gnome garrisons.

Some of the demonstrated achievements award two Contract Crystals upon completion.

Players looking to maximize their Fighting Master talents should therefore pay attention to these achievements throughout the season.

An important practical point is that progressing your talent tree isn't simply a matter of spending Gold.

You also need to actively participate in the tasks that reward the required Contract Crystals.

If you're planning to maximize your Fighting Master talents, preparing 105 million Gold is useful, but you'll also need to complete enough achievements to unlock all the required upgrades.

For active players, the best approach is to review your available achievements early and incorporate them into your normal KvK activities instead of leaving them until the final days.

## How to Use Fighting Master Effectively in Legendary KvK

The biggest advantage of Fighting Master is that its bonuses improve activities players are already performing.

Strong PvP players benefit from additional Troop Attack and Defense, frontline fighters can reduce casualties, and rally participants can gain additional offensive power.

However, Legendary KvK introduces an important strategic consideration.

Capturing cities now requires destroying an Energy Core using Altar Stones produced by Alchemists. Fighting Masters cannot replace this requirement, regardless of their combat strength.

Fighting Masters should focus on clearing defending enemies, contesting enemy positions, protecting friendly Altar Stones, and preventing opponents from interfering with important sieges.

For alliance leadership, selecting Fighting Masters should make sure to include the strongest and most active fighters you have. 


## Final Thoughts – Is Fighting Master the Best Contract?

Fighting Master is undoubtedly the strongest of the three Legendary KvK professions when considering direct personal combat bonuses.

Fully developed, the talent tree provides +10% Troop Attack, +10% Troop Defense, +10% Marching Speed, reduced soldier deaths, additional Immortal damage, and specialized defensive and rally bonuses.

That's a substantial collection of improvements for players who spend much of their time fighting.

However, Fighting Master isn't necessarily the most valuable profession for every alliance member.

Craftsmen and Alchemists are essential to producing and deploying Altar Stones, and without these structures an alliance cannot capture Legendary KvK objectives.

For strong, active PvP players, Fighting Master is an excellent choice. For players who are less active in combat, another profession may offer considerably more value to the alliance.

**My recommendation:** Choose Fighting Master if you expect to spend much of your Legendary KvK fighting, reinforcing, or participating in alliance attacks. Develop the universal combat bonuses first where the talent prerequisites allow, and invest in the more specialized talents according to your battlefield role.

In order to keep up with Alter Stone production, you need a lot of actives to NOT be Fighting Masters. However at the same time you want your most powerful fighters to BE Fighting Masters. Hence, alliance leadership must figure out enough active players to fill Craftsman and Alchemist roles first, then filling the rest info Fighting Master. 

While that is true they must ALSO make sure that the most powerful fighters ARE Fighting Master no matter what.

And remember to prepare your Gold. With a potential 105 million Gold required to maximize the tree, Fighting Master is not a cheap profession to develop.
