Upgrading your dragon is the most expensive action in the game but also one of the biggest power upgrades you can get your army. Upgrading both dragon level and its skills is paramount to a strong army. Below you see a table for upgrading dragon.

## Domesticate: Upgrade Dragon Skills / Abilities

| 10 | 5670 |
| --- | --- |
| 9 | 4480 |
| 8 | 3430 |
| 7 | 2520 |
| 6 | 1750 |
| 5 | 900 |
| 4 | 375 |
| 3 | 150 |
| 2 | 50 |
| 1 | - |

In total we need 3 abilities maxed on each dragon. That means 19,325 Dragon Souls x 3 abilities = 57,975 Dragon Souls!

Needless to say, you have to be efficient about your Dragon Soul grind if you want to have any chance of a competitive army. Have a look here for more info on obtaining Dragon Souls and killing Bosses: **[Combat/dragons/obtain-dragon-soul-crystals/](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/combat/dragons/obtain-dragon-soul-crystals/)**

Upon eaching 10-10-10 you will unlock the new Dragon Talent Trees. These are an expansion of the Dragon abilities as you can choose between three specializations and upgrade your Dragon's abilities even further.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/image-5-1024x526.png)

The total count of Dragon Soul Crystals required is MASSIVE for filling out this tree. [For more information on advanced Dragon Talents please see the seperate guides here.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/dragon/dragon-talents-patch-2-3/)
