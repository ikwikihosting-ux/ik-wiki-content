### Infinity Kingdom v2.8.3 Update Guide

Patch 2.8.3 brings a wealth of improvements and exciting new features to *Infinity Kingdom*. From a comprehensive **season merger** to the introduction of a **brand-new Expedition Battle Pass**, this update is set to significantly enhance the *Legion of Frostborne* gameplay and progression experience. Here's everything you need to know.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-17-1024x358.png)

---

## 🌀 What's New in v2.8.3

### 🔁 1. Season Merger and Progression Overhaul

To streamline the player experience and reduce downtime between seasons, Infinity Kingdom is implementing a **new season structure** and **merging existing seasons**.

#### 📊 New Season Format:

- **Preparation Season** (Level Cap: 40)
- **Season 1** (Level Cap: 50)
- **Season 2** (Level Cap: 50)
- **Conquest Season** (Level Cap: 55)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-18-1024x866.png)

#### 🔄 Server Progression Flow:

- **Original Preparation and Season 1 servers** → will enter new Season 1.
- **Original Season 2 servers** → will merge and jump directly to Conquest Season.
- **Servers just entering Season 2** → will go directly to Conquest after finishing current season.
- **Season 3 servers** → will complete current Expedition and then enter Conquest.

#### 🛠 Balance and Display Adjustments:

- Season naming and color-coded server icons improved for clarity.
- Seasonal resource compensation and buffs (e.g., building/research speed) added for new servers.
- Matchmaking improvements for new Conquest Season entrants.
- Legion rewards and mission difficulties have been rebalanced across level caps.

---

### 🚀 2. Server Transfer Updates

With the restructured season format, **server transfer rules** have also been adjusted:

#### 📌 Key Transfer Rules:

- Only allowed **within the same season**.
- **Target server must be in the Preparation Zone.**
- **Item cost increases** with each transfer during a season.

## 🔁 Server Transfer Portal Cost Changes – What You Need to Know

One of the most impactful changes in Patch 2.8.3 is the **new scaling cost system** for **server transfer portal items**. Previously, transferring between servers required a fixed number of portals. However, starting with this update, the **cost of transferring servers will now increase based on how many times you’ve transferred within the current season**.

### 📈 How the New System Works:

- **First transfer** of the season: costs the standard number of portals.
- **Subsequent transfers**: each additional jump during the same season will require **more portal items** than the last.
- The increase is **progressive**, meaning the cost ramps up quickly if you attempt multiple transfers.

### 🔒 Why This Matters:

This update is designed to **discourage excessive server hopping**, especially from veteran players trying to **stay permanently in lower-tier seasons (e.g., Season 1)** with fully maxed-out marches and artifacts.

Previously, players could manipulate matchmaking and progression systems by constantly **resetting their server environment**, creating unfair advantages over newer or less developed players. With the new system:

- Jumping back and forth between S1 and early servers becomes **resource-prohibitive**.
- Strategic, **long-term server investment** is now more important than ever.
- Server environments will feel **more balanced**, especially in preparation and early seasons.

### 🔔 Key Takeaways:

- **Plan your server transfers carefully** — multiple moves in a season will be costly.
- **Stay informed about your current season status** (icon color and rules) to avoid surprises.
- This change brings more **stability** and **fairness** to the Legion of Frostborne experience for everyone.

If you’re managing multiple accounts or alt strategies, consider **committing to your main server** for the season to avoid burning precious portals.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-19.png)

#### 🔴 Special Handling:

- Servers in S1/S2 at the time of the update will be marked **red**.
- **Red → Yellow** transfers allowed; **Yellow → Red** are blocked.
- Free transfers allowed between servers of the **same icon color**.

### 🎯 3. Expedition Battle Pass – New KVK Progression Feature

A **major addition** to the Conquest Season is the new **Expedition Battle Pass**, designed to reward activity in the *Legion of Frostborne* battlefield. **[See more read.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/legion-of-frostborne/kvk-battle-pass-2-8-3/)**

#### 🏅 Battle Pass Details:

- **Access via:** Legion Achievements tab.
- **Earn EXP:** Complete personal Legion tasks to level up.
- **Duration:** Lasts for the full war period; ends if war ends early.
- **Important:** Progress (including paid unlocks) resets if you transfer servers during an active war.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-20-1024x487.png)

#### 🎁 Reward Tiers:

- **Free Tier:** Basic resources and random immortal fragments.
- **Paid Tier:**  - Holy/Shadow Immortal Fragments
- **FULL 60-stone unlock** of a powerful Tower of Knowledge skill

This makes the paid pass incredibly valuable for players looking to build top-tier marches and maximize their KVK rewards.

- **Inner City UI**: Resource panels grouped by resource type for improved clarity.

Whether you're strategizing your next Conquest or preparing your march in Season 1, now is a great time to dive into Norheim and make your mark.
