### Conquering With Homer's Epic: A Guide to Alexander the Great's Unique Artifact in Infinity Kingdom

Embark on a journey to elevate Alexander the Great, one of the most formidable Immortals in Infinity Kingdom, with the acquisition of his Unique Artifact, Homer's Epic. This artifact isn't just a piece of equipment; it's a game-changer that transforms Alexander into an unstoppable force on the battlefield. This guide will navigate you through the power of Homer's Epic, how to obtain it, and strategies to maximize its potential.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-149-1024x644.png)

#### The Power of Homer's Epic

Homer's Epic does more than enhance Alexander's capabilities; it revolutionizes his role in combat. By converting his ultimate ability into a passive skill, it ensures that Alexander enters the fray at his maximum potential, with no energy cost required for activation. The artifact bestows a monumental 45% increase in damage dealt and attack speed, coupled with immunity to all control effects. The implications are profound: Alexander can consistently apply pressure, disrupt enemy lines, and sustain his offensive momentum throughout the battle, making him an indomitable force.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-152-1024x411.png)

#### Acquiring Homer's Epic

**Stellar Event & Mysterium Labyrinth:**
Homer's Epic can be obtained through participation in the Stellar event or by navigating the challenges of the Mysterium Labyrinth.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-150-1024x539.png)

These avenues provide a relatively cost-effective path to not only acquire but also maximize the artifact, offering a tangible goal for both free-to-play and investing players. Engaging in these events with a focused strategy enhances your chances of securing Homer's Epic, turning Alexander into a cornerstone of your Immortal lineup.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-151-1024x587.png)

#### Strategies for Maximizing Homer's Epic

1. **Prioritize Alexander's Placement:** With the transformation of his ultimate into a passive ability, positioning Alexander strategically becomes crucial. Place him where he can immediately impact the battle, preferably where his enhanced damage and attack speed can disrupt key enemy targets from the get-go.
2. **Build Around Immunity:** Alexander's immunity to control effects allows for more aggressive playstyles. Tailor your team composition to exploit this, pairing him with Immortals who can benefit from the chaos he creates, or those who can amplify his damage further.
3. **Optimize Gear and Companions:** Complement Homer's Epic with gear and companion choices that further boost Alexander's attack speed, damage, or survivability. This synergy ensures he remains a consistent threat throughout the battle.
4. **Focus on Artifact Enhancement:** Engage actively in the Stellar event and Mysterium Labyrinth to gather the necessary resources for enhancing Homer's Epic. Maximizing this artifact not only scales Alexander's power but also significantly impacts your overall battle performance.

#### Conclusion

Homer's Epic is not merely an artifact; it's a testament to Alexander the Great's enduring legacy and a key to unlocking his full potential in Infinity Kingdom. By transforming his ultimate ability into a relentless passive force, it sets the stage for Alexander to lead your Immortal army to glory. Engage in the Stellar event and Mysterium Labyrinth with determination, for Homer's Epic awaits those ready to wield its power. Equip Alexander the Great with this artifact, and watch as he carves a path of victory, making history once more on the battlegrounds of Infinity Kingdom.
