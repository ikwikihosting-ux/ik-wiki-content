## 🏩 VIP System Guide – Infinity Kingdom

The **VIP system** in *Infinity Kingdom* is a long-term progression feature designed to reward consistent logins, purchases, and gem investments with powerful bonuses. It is one of the most important systems to optimize if you want to get stronger over time — whether you spend money or not.

This guide will cover what VIP does, how to upgrade it, what rewards you get, and why hitting **VIP 15** should be a top goal.

---

## 📈 What Is VIP?

VIP is a **player progression system** with 15 levels in total. Each level offers permanent benefits ranging from extra healing speed to bonus troop stats and exclusive shop items.

There are **three main ways to increase your VIP level**:

1. **Daily logins** – Free VIP points each day
2. **Spending gems** – Exchange gems for VIP points (1 gem = 1 point)
3. **Buying packs** – All purchases include VIP points

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-111.png)

At certain VIP levels, you unlock:

- **Daily VIP chests**
- **Special shop access**
- **Powerful combat stat boosts**
- **Gifting options** (send items to allies and alts)

---

## 🍜 VIP Rewards Overview

### 🎁 Daily VIP Chest

Once you reach VIP Level 10, you start receiving a **daily reward chest**, which includes valuable items like:

- **Field of the First Stone** (from VIP 10+)
- Resource boosts
- Speedups
- Scrolls and potions

These add up over time and provide excellent passive progression — especially helpful in early and mid-game.

### 🍭 VIP Shop

At **VIP 15**, you unlock the **VIP shop**, which lets you spend VIP points you’ve earned over time. Some highly desirable items include:

- **Shadow Dragon Crystals**
- **Holy Dragon Egg** – Extremely valuable
- **Troop consumables**, boosters, and utility scrolls

This shop rotates and evolves, giving ongoing value even after you’ve hit max VIP level.

---

## 🧙 Immortals from VIP Packs

As you progress through VIP levels, you unlock **exclusive chests** that offer strong immortals:

- **Merlin** – Very early game (VIP 1+)
- **Zenobia** – VIP 5 chest (fantastic early support immortal)
- **Elizabeth Báthory** – Available in mid-to-late VIP chests

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-108-1024x411.png)

While once powerful, these immortals are no longer meta-defining. However, unlocking Zenobia early (for ~$5) is still a **smart investment** because it enables you to find her fragments in the market and build her quickly.

## ⚔️ VIP Combat Bonuses

VIP isn’t just cosmetics or convenience — the **combat bonuses** it grants are game-changing:

| VIP Level | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 11 | +4% Immortal Defense |
| 12 | +4% Immortal Attack |
| 13 | +5% Crit and Resilience |
| 14 | +5% Accuracy and Dodge |
| 15 | +500 Troop Capacity |

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-107-1024x407.png)

The **+500 troop capacity at VIP 15** is especially powerful:

- More damage output
- Greater survivability
- Better scaling in rallies and boss fights

These are **permanent buffs** that apply to all battles, making VIP progression one of the best long-term investments in the game.

## 💎 How to Level Up VIP (Even Free-to-Play)

VIP points are earned through:

1. **Daily Login** – +150 VIP points every day for showing up
2. **Gems** – 1 Gem = 1 VIP point
3. **Event Rewards** – Like relic donations and special packages
4. **Alliance Spending Rewards** – Sometimes you get small VIP boosts when others in your island or alliance make purchases

### Free-to-Play Strategy

Even as a **100% free-to-play** player, you can reach **VIP 15** over time If you are free-to-play I would recommend progressing atleast to level 13 for the major combat buffs:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-109-1024x452.png)

- **Stack your gems** – Save for a major spend push during a **Wonder Journey** or **Spending Reward** event
- Use the gems to buy **VIP levels** and **trigger reward milestones** in parallel

For example:

- VIP 15 costs **1 million total VIP points**
- If done with only gems, that’s **1 million gems**
- You can do this gradually over months, spending during events for **double value**

On F2P alts, this is **extremely efficient**.

---

## 🏰 Gifting Unlocks

At higher VIP levels, you unlock the ability to **gift alliance members and alts**:

- **Attack/Defense/Speed Buffs**
- **Teleport scrolls**
- **Misc. consumables**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-110-1024x572.png)

This makes your **VIP 15 main account** incredibly useful to your other accounts or friends, especially during alliance wars or IB preparations.

Not all items are giftable at lower VIP tiers — the higher your VIP level, the more valuable items you can send.

---

## 🔥 Why You Want VIP 15

VIP 15 is **one of the most powerful milestones** in the game:

- +500 Troop Count is **massive**
- Full access to the **VIP Shop**
- Permanent **combat buffs**
- Maxed **gifting power**
- Passive resource growth from daily chests

If you’re aiming for late-game competitiveness, **VIP 15 is not optional** — it's a core progression goal.

---

## 📌 Final Tips

- **Buy Zenobia at VIP 5** if you're a new spender. Great early support.
- **Don’t waste gems randomly** — wait for **Spending Events** (like Wonderful Journey) to maximize value
- **VIP Shop items rotate** – Check regularly for rare items
- **Prioritize VIP growth over cosmetic purchases** early on

---

## 🧠 Summary

The VIP system in Infinity Kingdom is more than just fluff — it’s one of the **most impactful features** in terms of long-term growth, combat strength, and utility. Whether you’re a spender or a grinder, hitting **VIP 15** will significantly boost your power and unlock important features that give you the edge in both PvP and PvE.

**Free-to-play** players: It’s a marathon, not a sprint. Plan your gem spending wisely, log in daily, and save for key events.

**Spenders**: You’ll hit VIP 15 faster, but still maximize your value by syncing upgrades with reward events.
