The Hospital can heal wounded troops. **The higher the Hospital level, the more wounded troops it can treat**. The Hospital cannot treat the troops exceeding its max capacity. Unfortunately, you will lose these troops forever.

![](https://oss.gtarcade.com/ucms/fe7145ad-bf88-4fe7-b8cf-6cc31493aa94_2020-05-13.jpg)
