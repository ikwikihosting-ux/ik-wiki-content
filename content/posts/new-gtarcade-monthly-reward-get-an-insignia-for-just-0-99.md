GTarcade has improved its monthly first-purchase reward by adding an **Official Supporter - Common Insignia**. The important part is that this reward does not require a large recharge. Any qualifying purchase through the official GTarcade platform appears to activate the gift, including the **$0.99 Voucher**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-60-1024x528.png)

For players willing to spend even a single dollar, this is one of the most efficient purchases currently available.

## What Do You Receive?

Purchasing the $0.99 Voucher activates the GTarcade Platform First Purchase Gift, which includes:

- **Official Supporter - Common Insignia**
- **King Arthur**
- **100 Gems**
- **9 Philosopher’s Stones**
- **3 AP Recovery items**
- **3 SP Recovery items**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-61-1024x590.png)

The ordinary resources are already respectable for such a small purchase, especially the AP and SP. The Insignia is what makes the offer stand out, however.

## Why the Insignia Matters

Insignias contribute to the Immortal Affinity system and can provide meaningful account buffs as your collection develops. They are therefore not merely cosmetic collectibles. Even a Common Insignia can be worth obtaining when it is available this cheaply, particularly because opportunities to expand your Insignia collection remain limited.

For $0.99, you are effectively obtaining a new Insignia while also receiving Arthur, Gems, Philosopher’s Stones, AP and SP as additional value. There are very few purchases in Infinity Kingdom that provide this much permanent progression for approximately one dollar.

Now, you may be wondering what all the fuzz is about, why is a single Insignia so important that I need to read this? Well because these collectables unlock incredibly powerful buffs that directly improves your combat ability. Buffs that if they where a Castle Skin would be hugely expensive to unlock. **[Read much more about Insignias here](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/lord-lady/insignias/basics-how-insignias-work-in-infinity-kingdom/)**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-56.png)

## How to Claim It

So back to this incredibly cheap Insignia - and how to claim it!

Visit the official GTarcade shop while logged into the correct account and purchase the **$0.99 Voucher**. Because the first-purchase gift accepts a purchase of any price, the voucher should activate the reward without requiring you to buy a larger bundle.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-57-1024x411.png)

The Insignia is delivered as the **Official Shop First Top-Up Reward** and can then be claimed in-game.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-58-1024x590.png)

As always, confirm that the correct Infinity Kingdom account and character are selected before completing the purchase.

## Multiple shop insignias

The Official Supporter Insignia actually comes in multiple forms, however the main interesting one is the Common version because it is so incredibly cheap to collect! But just for the sake of covering all the bases, you should know that there are multiple versions - but they get expensive!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-59.png)

The **Official Supporter - Common Insignia** is the one included with the first-purchase reward. It should not be confused with the **Official Supporter - Leader Insignia** shown at the $999.99 Monthly Cumulative Recharge milestone.

**The low-cost purchase gives the Common Insignia, which is an excellent reward considering the price.**

## Is It Worth Buying?

If you are completely free-to-play, these 1 dollar rewards are probably the ones ones I would consider breaking your F2P-playstyle for. This is so incredibly efficient. And for anyone who already spends occasionally, however, this is about as easy a recommendation as we get.

Buy the **$0.99 Voucher**, activate the monthly first-purchase reward and collect the Insignia alongside the accompanying items. At approximately one dollar, it is an extremely efficient way to add another Insignia to your collection while picking up some useful AP, SP, Gems, Philosopher’s Stones and Arthur at the same time.
