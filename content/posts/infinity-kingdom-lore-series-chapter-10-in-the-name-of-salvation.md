## 💠 The First Stone and Its Three Fragments

The episode opens with a flashback to an ancient catastrophe.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-223839-rtjyty.png)

A temple burns as people fight over a powerful artifact known as the **First Stone**. To prevent further disaster, its keeper divides the stone into three fragments and sends them to distant lands.

The keeper believes that separating the fragments will prevent anyone from reuniting them and unleashing another catastrophe.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-223821-tfl93f.png)

However, we learn that the final fragment was entrusted to the palace of Vitas.

This is a significant revelation. Throughout the series, we've encountered references to mysterious stones, ancient powers, and the Council's attempts to manipulate forces beyond ordinary human understanding.

Now we know that at least one important artifact was deliberately divided to keep its power out of anyone's hands.

Naturally, that raises an obvious question: what happens if someone manages to bring all three fragments together again?

## 💠 A Dangerous Return to Vitas

We then follow Kael and a mysterious older man wearing a monocle as they prepare to enter the royal palace.

Kael is carrying a small chest containing two glowing red fragments. These appear to be the same ancient stones introduced in the opening sequence.

Meanwhile, Vitas is facing a serious military threat.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-223910-53p9hy.png)

An army of dwarves has gathered outside the city, deploying enormous cannons and preparing to attack its defenses.

The situation inside the palace is understandably tense.

When Kael and his companions enter the throne room, they are greeted by the king and a mysterious woman carrying a staff.

The woman clearly knows Kael and remarks that he has finally returned.

There is an immediate sense that these characters share a history we haven't yet been told about.

## 💠 The King's Desperate Plan

During the confrontation, the king reveals that the royal family has been safeguarding the final fragment of the First Stone.

With Kael bringing the other two fragments to Vitas, all three are now within reach.

The king is desperate. The dwarven army is approaching, and the city may soon fall.

He asks the mysterious woman to help save Vitas.

At first glance, this sounds like a reasonable request from a ruler trying to protect his people.

But given everything we've learned about ancient stones and dangerous experiments throughout the series, bringing together three fragments that were deliberately separated to prevent catastrophe seems like a remarkably bad idea.

And things are about to become even more suspicious.

## 💠 The Worldheart and a Terrifying Revelation

The man with the monocle reveals that he was responsible for writing a binding spell.

However, when the ancient fragments are brought together, he realizes something is wrong.

Rather than simply saving Vitas, the ritual appears connected to a much larger and more dangerous plan.

He reveals that the King's sorcerer is in fact **draining the Worldheart to summon heroes**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-224205-ipcghq.png)

This is perhaps the most fascinating revelation in the chapter.

In Infinity Kingdom, Immortals are legendary heroes summoned to fight alongside players. Until now, the animated lore series has largely focused on the consequences of alchemical experimentation and the manipulation of souls.

The mention of summoning heroes through the Worldheart potentially connects those mysteries to one of the fundamental mechanics of Infinity Kingdom itself.

Could the power required to summon Immortals come at a terrible cost to the world?

And could the Alchemical Council's experiments be connected to this process?

These are still theories, but the implications are fascinating.

## 💠 Salvation at Any Cost?

As the ritual begins, glowing red energy spreads through the city.

The mysterious woman channels the power of the fragments, and figures begin to materialize around her.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-224101-iy6v3o.png)

What initially appears to be an attempt to protect Vitas takes on a much darker appearance.

The woman believes that she is acting in the name of salvation, but the monocled man's warning suggests that the ritual may sacrifice far more than anyone realizes.

It's an interesting continuation of one of the series' recurring themes: the pursuit of extraordinary power by people who believe they are serving a greater good.

We've seen similar justifications surrounding the Alchemical Council's experiments, and now the same ideology appears to have reached the heart of Vitas itself.

## 💠 Brynhild Confronts Kael

The episode ends with a dramatic confrontation.

As the mysterious woman commands those around her to kneel, Brynhild draws her sword and turns toward Kael.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-224340-d867ru.png)

And with that, the chapter ends.

It's a rather excellent cliffhanger.

For much of the series, Brynhild and our Hangover Knight have been traveling together, uncovering the Council's secrets and investigating the mysterious stones.

Now, however, we have to wonder whether Kael has been telling us everything.

He clearly has connections to powerful people in Vitas, and his arrival with two fragments has helped make this dangerous ritual possible.

Was he aware of what would happen? Has he been manipulated? Or is there something more complicated going on?

Whatever the answer, Brynhild certainly doesn't appear willing to stand quietly by.

## 💠 My Take – Are We Finally Learning How Immortals Are Summoned?

Chapter 10 might be one of the most important episodes of *Resonance of the Sacred Emblem* so far.

The revelations about the First Stone and its three fragments give us a much clearer understanding of the ancient power everyone seems to be pursuing.

But what really caught my attention was the reference to the Worldheart.

The possibility that summoning legendary heroes requires draining an ancient source of power opens up some interesting questions about Infinity Kingdom's wider mythology.

We've already seen the Alchemical Council experimenting with warrior souls, attempting to create enhanced soldiers, and pursuing the power to forge a false god.

Could all these experiments ultimately be different approaches to controlling the same source of power?

I'm also very interested in Kael. After following the Hangover Knight for so many chapters, we're finally learning more about his identity and his connections to Vitas. He may turn out to be considerably more important than his occasionally ridiculous behavior has suggested.

And then there's Brynhild, who once again finds herself standing against forces that seem willing to sacrifice others in pursuit of power.

The chapter leaves us with plenty of questions, but one thing seems increasingly clear: **the greatest threat to Norheim may not be the armies fighting its wars, but the people claiming they can save it.**

## 💠 Follow the Story

*Resonance of the Sacred Emblem* continues to expand Infinity Kingdom's lore, introducing new mysteries surrounding the Immortals, ancient artifacts, and the Alchemical Council.

We'll continue following Brynhild and Kael as the story unfolds, particularly now that their journey has brought them face to face with the powers controlling Vitas.
