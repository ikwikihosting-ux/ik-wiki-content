## TL;DR – The Best Ways to Obtain Holy & Shadow Crystals

For most players actively developing Lucasta or Melanthios, **Theia's Roulette and Rune Factory should be the main events to watch**. Both offer recurring opportunities to obtain crystals, either through Gems or real-money purchases.

## Theia's Roulette – A Major Improvement in August 2026

Theia's Roulette has become one of the most interesting recurring sources of Holy and Shadow Dragon Crystals since Patch 2.9.8.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-094823-lrl7eu.png)

Players can now select either **350 Holy Dragon Crystals or 350 Shadow Dragon Crystals** as their featured reward.

The wheel also contains smaller crystal rewards, meaning you can obtain crystals without necessarily hitting the jackpot.

The important mechanics are:

* The 350-crystal jackpot has a natural 3% drop chance.
* The jackpot is guaranteed by the 25th spin if it hasn't appeared earlier.
* Five additional crystal rewards provide 45 crystals each, with a combined 5.2% chance.
* The jackpot counter resets whenever the grand prize is obtained.

Taking the jackpot guarantee and smaller crystal rewards into account, the expected return is approximately **22 Dragon Crystals per spin**.

This is a considerable improvement over earlier versions of the event.

### What Can You Expect?

These are long-run statistical averages. Individual results will vary, particularly during smaller sessions.

The event also awards milestone chests at certain spin thresholds. These are valuable additional rewards, although they shouldn't be counted as extra Dragon Crystals.

For players purchasing tickets with Gems, each ticket costs 1,000 Gems. This means approximately **4,540 Gems per 100 expected Dragon Crystals**.

Theia's Roulette also has one free spin each event day, making it worth visiting even when you aren't planning to spend.

For the complete odds, pity calculations, and milestone analysis, see our [Theia's Roulette Guide](https://ik-wiki.com/posts/theias-roulette---pricing-chance-calculations-and-more/).

## Rune Factory – The Other Major Recurring Source

Rune Factory replaced the older Sparks of Galaxy event and remains an important source of Holy and Shadow Dragon Crystals.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-094908-4f7c15.png)

Players use Energy Cores to make draws and obtain Rune Insignias, which can then be exchanged for rewards in the Factory Shop.

The relevant exchange rate is straightforward:

**20 Rune Insignias = 100 Holy or Shadow Dragon Crystals.**

This produces an average of approximately **1.31 Rune Insignias per Energy Core**, equivalent to 6.55 Dragon Crystals per draw when exchanging all Insignias for crystals.

Rune Factory still has a pity mechanic, but because the counter resets whenever you receive any Rune Insignias, reaching the full guarantee is extraordinarily unlikely.

### Buying Crystals with Gems

Rune Factory also provides three Energy Cores for 1,000 Gems.

At the published average drop rate, this means approximately 19.65 Dragon Crystals per 1,000 Gems, or roughly 5,090 Gems per 100 expected crystals.

That is slightly less efficient for Dragon Crystals alone than the current Theia's Roulette calculation.

However, Rune Factory remains attractive because its shop contains other valuable rewards, including Dragon Essences, Holy and Shadow Immortal progression materials, and other rare items.

Players who need several different progression materials may therefore find Rune Factory particularly useful.

## VIP Shops, GTArcade Rewards & Dragon Bundles

Outside recurring events, there are several additional opportunities to collect premium Dragon Crystals.

**VIP Points Shop:** Holy Dragon Crystals have historically been available at VIP 15, with 20 crystals costing 3,000 VIP Points. This can be useful for players with accumulated VIP Points, although the opportunity cost is substantial.

**Mystery Pearl Shop:** Shadow Dragon Crystals have historically been available for 15 Mystery Pearls per 50 crystals, subject to the relevant account requirements. Mystery Pearls are valuable, so this isn't necessarily an efficient purchase.

**GTArcade Rewards:** Certain monthly rewards and Points Gift Packs have offered batches of 500 Holy or Shadow Crystals. These are worth checking, particularly for players who already participate in GTArcade reward programs. Availability and eligibility may vary.

**Dragon Knight and Light & Shadow Bundles:** Both provide direct crystal purchases. The Light & Shadow bundles have also included Dragon Essences, which can improve their overall value for players developing their Talent Trees.

These sources are useful for supplementing progression, but their prices, stock limits, and availability should be checked against the current shop before purchasing.

## Can You Collect Holy and Shadow Crystals for Free?

Yes, although free progression is extremely slow compared with the quantities needed for complete upgrades.

The most reliable recurring opportunities are the free spins in Theia's Roulette, free Rune Factory entries, and purchasing additional attempts using Gems earned through ordinary gameplay.

If both events continue on their published weekly schedules, three free Roulette spins provide approximately 66 Dragon Crystals per week on average. Three free Rune Factory draws add an expected equivalent of about 20 more crystals, assuming the Rune Insignias are exchanged for crystals.

That works out to approximately **86 Dragon Crystals per week from free event entries**, before Gem spending, promotions, or other rewards.

These averages assume that the event schedules, free-entry quantities, and reward tables remain unchanged.

Over time, that adds up. But compared with a requirement approaching 300,000 crystals, it becomes obvious why completing a premium dragon solely through free entries is an extremely long-term project.

For F2P players, the most practical strategy is to combine free entries with carefully planned Gem spending, while taking advantage of any additional crystal rewards that become available.

## Which Dragon Should You Prioritize?

The choice between Holy and Shadow should depend primarily on the march you're developing.

**Lucasta, the Holy Dragon**, offers healing, defensive support, and additional utility that can work well in a broad range of formations.

**Melanthios, the Shadow Dragon**, provides offensive pressure and disruption, including its distinctive Shadow mechanics, making it particularly interesting for certain PvP compositions.

Both can be powerful, but neither is a good investment simply because you happen to have some spare crystals.

Because upgrades require such enormous quantities, players should decide which dragon contributes most to their main march before investing heavily.

When spending your crystals, develop the important basic skills first, then balance Talent Tree progression against affordable Portrait upgrades. Early Portrait levels can be cost-efficient, while key Talent nodes may offer more impactful combat bonuses.

## Final Thoughts – Make Every Crystal Count

Holy and Shadow Dragon Crystals remain among Infinity Kingdom's most difficult progression materials to collect.

Fully developing a premium dragon requires hundreds of thousands of crystals, so completing one is a major long-term investment even for regular spenders.

Fortunately, the acquisition options have improved considerably.

Theia's Roulette now provides a direct route with an expected return of around 22 crystals per spin, while Rune Factory offers regular access to crystals through Rune Insignias and a flexible selection of alternative rewards.

For ordinary players, the best approach is to claim all available free entries, save Gems for efficient event opportunities, and avoid spending scarce currencies on crystals that won't support their primary march.

For spenders, **start with the most efficient recurring event purchases before considering expensive direct bundles**, but always check the current prices and purchase limits.

Above all, remember that these dragons are long-term investments. You don't need to max every skill, talent, and Portrait level immediately to gain meaningful combat benefits.

Choose your dragon, prioritize the upgrades that matter most, and make every crystal count.
