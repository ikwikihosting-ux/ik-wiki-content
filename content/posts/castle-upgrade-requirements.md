As of patch 2.6 released july 2023, we have an increased building cap of level 55. Among the numerous elements of Castle Level 55, one aspect that demands attention is the building costs. This crucial information sets the stage for players' resource management skills to shine. The infographic reveals the intricate details of wood, iron, stone, and the newly introduced resource, aether, required for constructing and upgrading buildings. Mastering these costs will be the key to unlocking the full potential of your castle. Moreover, what we need to know are the**Building Requirements which is what this page is all about!**Quite interestingly the new builds seem to follow the logic of the older tiers.
