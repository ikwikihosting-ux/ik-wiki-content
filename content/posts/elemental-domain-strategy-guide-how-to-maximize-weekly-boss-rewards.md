## TL;DR – How Should You Progress Elemental Domain?

There are two main approaches, depending on how developed your account is.

**Strategy 1: Build for kill-days**

If you have access to the right Immortals, upgraded Tower of Knowledge skills, Soul Crystals, artifacts, and other progression resources, prioritize one elemental boss until you can consistently defeat it.

A full kill provides the highest rewards, and a weekday boss can be challenged on its regular weekday and again on Saturday and Sunday.

That means **six-to-nine maximum-reward attempts every week from one formation** (depending on weather you can beat both saturday and sunday).

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-113226-hi14ns.png)

**Strategy 2: Progress through multiple elemental bosses.**

If defeating a boss isn't realistically achievable yet, focus on reaching the next reward milestone across several different elements.

The important thresholds are 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%. Each provides additional rewards, so even going from 50% to 75% is worthwhile.

However, whether an improvement is worth investing in depends on how expensive it will be to achieve.

The goal is to maximize your total weekly rewards, rather than investing enormous resources into a single formation that remains far from its next milestone.

## How Elemental Domain Works

Elemental Domain becomes available in Legendary Season for players with Castle level 55 or higher.

It contains eight elemental dungeons: Water, Fire, Wind, Earth, Lightning, Holy, Shadow, and Chaos.

Each dungeon restricts players to Immortals and Dragons belonging to the corresponding element. This means your strongest PvP march may not necessarily be available, and even if it is, it might not be particularly effective against the boss.

The event follows a weekly schedule:

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-114813-hplchu.png)



Players receive three reward-bearing attempts each day.

During weekdays, those attempts are restricted to the available element. On Saturdays and Sundays, however, you can choose between all eight elemental bosses.

This weekend flexibility is central to developing an efficient Elemental Domain farming strategy.

The event also offers Simulation Challenges, allowing players to test their formations without consuming their daily reward attempts.

Use these simulations extensively when experimenting with new Immortals, adjusting Tower of Knowledge skills, or determining whether an upgrade is likely to improve your rewards.

## Understanding Elemental Domain Rewards

Elemental Domain rewards are determined by how much damage you deal to the boss.

The in-game completion screen lists five milestones:

* 10% damage
* 25% damage
* 50% damage
* 75% damage
* 100% damage, defeating the boss

One important detail is how the rewards are presented.

The completion screen displays rewards separately for each milestone. When these milestone rewards are added cumulatively, the difference between reaching 50%, 75%, and defeating the boss becomes substantial.

Reaching 75% instead of 50% provides 50% more of each listed reward. Fully defeating the boss provides another 33% improvement over 75%.

And compared with stopping at 50%, a full kill effectively **doubles your rewards**.

### Why Every Milestone Matters

The increase from 50% to 75% adds another six Forging Chests, ten Forging Hammers, and fifty Tempering Elixirs per attempt.

Exactly the same additional quantities are obtained when progressing from 75% to a full kill.

Across three daily attempts, reaching the next milestone therefore adds:

Consequently, players should no longer think of 50% damage as a stopping point unless reaching 75% would require disproportionately expensive investments.

The objective is to keep improving your reward milestones, but to do so efficiently.

## Strategy 1 – Prioritize One Boss Until You Can Kill It

For developed accounts, one of the best ways to improve Elemental Domain rewards is building a dedicated formation capable of consistently defeating a weekday boss.

This strategy has a significant advantage over spreading investments across multiple elements.

![](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-105759-sqkcxb.png)

Once you establish a reliable full-kill formation, you can use it repeatedly on weekends, rather than needing separate high-performing formations for Saturday and Sunday.

### Why One Full Kill Is So Valuable

Let's imagine you've developed a Fire formation that consistently defeats the Fire boss.

You can use that formation on Tuesday, Saturday, and Sunday.

Since you receive three reward attempts per day, that's nine full kills every week.

Using the cumulative reward figures above:

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120639-m2vxm4.png)

That's a considerable amount of equipment progression material from just one properly developed formation.

For comparison, consistently reaching 50% damage on those same nine attempts would provide only 108 chests, 180 hammers, and 900 elixirs.

In other words, upgrading from a 50% formation to a full-kill formation doubles the rewards available across those three days.

**This is why getting one reliable boss-killing formation should be a major objective for developed players.**

It creates a dependable source of maximum rewards every week, allowing you to gradually improve other elements without sacrificing your strongest farming days.

### Are You Ready to Build a Boss-Killing Formation?

Not every account should immediately invest everything into achieving a full kill.

Before committing, consider whether you have the resources necessary to develop a specialized PvE formation.

The most important requirements include:

**Suitable Immortals:** You may need to upgrade characters that aren't part of your regular PvP march. Strong single-target damage dealers and Immortals with powerful control effects can be exceptionally valuable against bosses.

**Tower of Knowledge skills:** Boss-killing formations often use specific skill combinations, including control, Energy suppression, damage amplification, and long-term scaling. These skills need to be unlocked and sufficiently upgraded.

**Soul Crystals:** Fully developing the necessary Tower of Knowledge skills can be expensive, especially when several important Legendary skills are involved.

**Immortal development:** High boost levels, experience levels, and appropriate talent development are important for both damage and survivability.

**Artifacts and equipment:** Strong artifacts with the correct attributes can provide meaningful improvements to the performance of your specialized damage dealers.

**Dragon progression:** A developed elemental Dragon with relevant upgrades can make a significant difference.

If you already possess most of these components, investing in one dedicated boss-killing build may be an excellent use of your resources.

If you're missing several of them, however, the cost of reaching 100% damage could be substantial.

That's when expanding your performance across multiple elements may be more efficient.

## Strategy 2 – Improve Your Reward Milestones Across Multiple Bosses

For many players, fully defeating an Elemental Domain boss is still a long-term objective.

That doesn't mean you should ignore the event or invest everything into one element.

Instead, focus on improving the reward thresholds you can consistently reach across the week.

The most important consideration is **how much additional investment is required to reach your next milestone**.

### Step 1 – Establish a Reliable 50% Formation

Start with the element where you have the strongest existing team.

Your first major objective should be reaching 50% damage consistently.

This provides a meaningful reward level and creates a foundation for further progression.

Because weekends allow you to choose any available boss, even one 50% formation can provide useful rewards on three different days.

For example, a Fire formation reaching 50% can be used on Tuesday, Saturday, and Sunday.

That's already nine attempts at the 50% reward tier every week.

### Step 2 – Decide Whether 75% Is Realistic

Once you're consistently reaching 50%, evaluate whether pushing to 75% would require a reasonable investment.

If you're already reaching 65–70%, a few skill adjustments, better artifacts, or upgrading an important Immortal might be enough to reach the next milestone.

In that situation, pursuing 75% makes excellent sense.

It provides 50% additional rewards compared with stopping at 50%.

However, if you're barely reaching 50% and the next milestone would require multiple expensive upgrades, it may be better to improve another element first.

This is where individual account progression becomes important.

The objective isn't automatically to push the same boss as far as possible. It's to identify where your next investment will produce the greatest reward improvement.

### Step 3 – Expand Into Additional Weekdays

Once you've established a strong formation for one element, begin improving another.

For example, suppose you can consistently reach 75% on Fire but only 35% on Earth.

Improving Earth to 50% may be a much more affordable investment than attempting to push Fire from 75% to a full kill.

You would then have two reliable weekdays with good rewards, alongside the ability to use your strongest formation during weekends.

Continue applying this principle to other elements.

Eventually, your goal should be to reach meaningful reward milestones across all five weekdays while maintaining one particularly strong formation for weekend farming.

### The Most Efficient Approach Depends on Your Next Milestone

Consider two possible upgrades:

**Option A:** Improve your Fire formation from 50% to 75%.

Across Tuesday, Saturday, and Sunday, that improvement could affect nine reward attempts. Each attempt would award six additional chests, ten hammers, and fifty elixirs.

That's an additional **54 chests, 90 hammers, and 450 elixirs per week**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-114913-bic2pd.png)

**Option B:** Improve your Earth formation from 25% to 50%.

Assuming Earth is only used on Thursday, that improvement affects three attempts, adding eighteen chests, thirty hammers, and 150 elixirs per week.

Both upgrades are useful, but the Fire improvement has three times the weekly reward impact because the formation is used on three days.

Of course, if upgrading Earth is substantially cheaper, it could still be the better investment.

This is why players should evaluate both the cost of an upgrade and the number of weekly attempts that benefit from it.

## Elemental Domain Builds Are Completely Different From PvP

One of the biggest lessons from Elemental Domain is that the strongest PvP formation isn't necessarily the strongest boss-killing formation.

In ordinary PvP, you're fighting several Immortals with different abilities, roles, and synergies.

In Elemental Domain, you're facing a single boss with enormous HP and unique defensive and offensive mechanics.

This creates an entirely different environment.

Some Tower of Knowledge skills that are rarely used in competitive PvP become extremely powerful, while certain Immortals that aren't particularly popular in the current PvP meta can become essential for maximizing boss damage.

Two mechanics are especially important.

### Control Effects and Energy Suppression

Control effects can be incredibly powerful in Elemental Domain.

Depending on the boss, skills applying Silence, Root, or other disabling effects may prevent dangerous abilities from activating or interrupt mechanics that would otherwise significantly reduce your damage.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-114942-7xkxae.png)

This can make some of the older blue Tower of Knowledge skills worth using again.

Skills such as Thump, alongside appropriate Silence or Root effects, may provide far more value than their rarity suggests.

Energy suppression is another important tool.

Hippolyta is a particularly good example. Her ultimate attacks repeatedly reduce the target's Energy and Attack Speed.

Against a single boss, every hit is directed at the same target, allowing her to interfere with the boss's ability to cast ultimates.

With an effective setup, Hippolyta can help create significantly longer and safer battles.

However, every Elemental Domain boss has different mechanics and immunities. Some are resistant or immune to particular control effects, meaning the same approach won't work equally well everywhere.

This is why dedicated builds are so important.

### Long Battles Make Scaling Skills Extremely Powerful

One particularly interesting aspect of Elemental Domain is that successful control setups can extend battles considerably.

In ordinary PvP, many encounters are decided relatively quickly.

But when you're fighting a boss with enormous HP, delaying its abilities and keeping your formation alive can create much longer encounters.

This gives certain Tower of Knowledge skills time to reach their full potential.

Two notable examples are **Demonic Contract and Battle Stance**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-115002-1kvzmb.png)

Demonic Contract initially penalizes damage but gradually increases offensive output as the battle continues, eventually providing an extremely powerful damage bonus.

Battle Stance similarly rewards formations that can survive beyond its initial period of reduced effectiveness.

These skills may be less attractive in short PvP encounters, but in prolonged boss fights they can become particularly valuable.

The key is survival.

A scaling skill doesn't help much if your formation dies before its bonuses become effective.

Successful boss-killing formations often establish control and survivability first, then use damage amplification and scaling effects to maximize their performance over time.

## Which Immortals Are Best for Elemental Domain?

While the ideal formation differs between bosses, certain Immortals are particularly interesting because their abilities work exceptionally well against single targets.

### Water – Yoshitsune

Yoshitsune is a strong option for Water boss encounters because of his concentrated single-target damage.

His ultimate can direct multiple attacks against the same target, making it particularly effective when there's only one enemy on the battlefield.

For players already developing a Water formation, Yoshitsune is an important Immortal to consider.

### Earth – Arash and Alexander

Arash is extremely valuable against bosses because of his single-target damage and offensive support.

Combined with Alexander, he can provide the foundation for a very strong Earth boss-killing formation.

This is an excellent example of why your regular PvP lineup might need adjustments.

Replacing a PvP-oriented Immortal with someone better suited to prolonged single-target damage can produce substantial improvements.

### Fire – Hippolyta With Wu Zetian or Seondeok

Fire has some particularly interesting options for Elemental Domain.

Hippolyta's Energy suppression can make an enormous difference, potentially delaying dangerous boss ultimates and allowing your damage dealers to survive longer.

Pairing her with offensive Immortals such as Wu Zetian or Seondeok creates several interesting possibilities.

However, the correct setup depends on the boss's defensive mechanics, your available Tower of Knowledge skills, and how developed your Immortals are.

These are starting points, not complete builds.

## How to Decide What to Upgrade Next

Once you've established several working formations, the most efficient progression method is relatively straightforward.

First, look at your current damage results against each boss.

Next, identify which formations are closest to reaching their next reward milestone.

Then evaluate how difficult it would be to achieve that improvement.

For example, if you're reaching 72% damage against Earth and believe a small artifact upgrade could push you to 75%, that's likely an attractive investment.

If you're dealing 52% against Lightning but would need to develop an entirely new Immortal to reach 75%, the investment may be considerably less efficient.

Finally, remember that your strongest weekday formation can also be used twice during the weekend.

This means improvements to that formation can provide three times as many weekly reward opportunities as an improvement to a formation used only on its weekday.

A particularly good long-term progression plan might therefore look like this:

1. Develop your strongest element to a reliable 50% or 75%.
2. Evaluate whether reaching a full kill is realistic.
3. If it is, prioritize that formation and secure three days of maximum rewards.
4. If it isn't, expand your reward coverage across additional weekdays.
5. Once your main formation is established, continue improving other elements to 50%, 75%, and eventually 100%.

The exact order will differ between accounts, but the principle remains the same: **spend resources where they provide the greatest improvement to your weekly rewards**.

## Final Thoughts – Maximize Weekly Rewards, Not Just Damage

Elemental Domain should be approached as a long-term progression activity where your objective is maximizing the total rewards you collect every week.

Every reward milestone matters.

Reaching 75% instead of 50% provides considerably more rewards, and defeating the boss increases them further.

However, the most important consideration is how much investment each improvement requires.

For players with the resources to develop a dedicated boss-killing team, focusing on one weekday element can be extremely effective. A reliable full kill provides nine maximum-reward opportunities every week thanks to the weekend schedule.

For players who aren't ready to defeat a boss, the best approach is to gradually improve several elemental formations, targeting whichever reward milestones are realistically achievable.

And remember that Elemental Domain isn't normal PvP. Control effects, Energy suppression, sustained damage, and long-term scaling can be far more important than simply fielding your strongest Immortals.

**Our recommendation: Establish one strong formation for weekend farming, work toward a reliable full kill when realistic, and use your remaining resources to improve reward milestones across the other weekdays.**

Over time, this creates a steady and increasingly valuable source of materials for Mythic equipment progression.
