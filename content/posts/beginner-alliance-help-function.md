Alliances are unlocked and you are now able to take full advantage of the community aspects of Infinity Kingdom. Do spend time on finding a solid and ACTIVE community. For now, the power, skill and even levels of kindness of your fellow alliance friends is completly irrelevant. You need activity before all else, since you want to take full advantage of the **Alliance "Help" function** .

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/image-78-1024x570.png)

This is extreemly important to understand, as you **can upgrade your buildings much more efficient with a little help** from your friends (as they sang in the 60's...).

## What is the "help" function?

When you are in an alliance, you can ask your alliance friends to help you build your buildings. This works in the same way as using speed-up items. Each "help" speeds along your building with a fixed **1% build** time plus an additional speed based on your alliance technology level.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Skaermbillede-2022-08-24-165234-1024x530.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Skaermbillede-2022-08-24-165225-1024x525.png)

So, for a level 1 alliance each "help" gives you **1% build time + 30 seconds in speedup**. As you level up "Brothers in Arms" alliance technology, the help function adds another incremental 30 seconds to the speedup boost.

Hence, a capped alliance help speeds 1% + 300 seconds. This is why leveling an alt up is much faster in a high level alliance, since 300 seconds per help is a lot for the lower level builds that dosen't take a month each.

| Brothers-in-Arms Lvl | Speedup |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 % + 30s |
| 2 | 1 % + 60s |
| 3 | 1 % + 90s |
| 4 | 1 % + 120s |
| 5 | 1 % + 150s |
| 6 | 1 % + 180s |
| 7 | 1 % + 210s |
| 8 | 1 % + 240s |
| 9 | 1 % + 270s |
| 10 | 1 % + 300s |

## How do I increase the amount of "helps" I can get?

You can recieve more help from your alliance friends the higher the level of your castle. The precise amount in not certain, however this I know for sure:

Castle level <10 = 8 helps
Castle level 17 = 13 helps
Castle level 44 = 27 helps
Castle level 45 = 27 helps
Castle level 50 = 30 helps

As such it follow logically that the level 10-20 bracket is around 13 helps and the 20-30 bracket around 23 helps. This is my best guess on complete brackets - please reach out and help me fill it in with any corrections!
