This guide is part of my article series on Prime League Illusion Builds. If this build is not to your likeing, have a look at the rest of my builds on the main Prime League page.

**This build is made for:**Round 3 of Pre-league (150 Violets)

This article is on water with Khan. Water is a strong element because of the strong dragon skills, Atillas control/damage mix and Merlins insanely high damage. What it needs it to be able to break earth teams and wind teams in their healing and physical damage output - here is the solution! Wounds!

## Setup

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/image-3.png)

**Genghis Khan**: Adrenaline Rush + Fighting Master + Anger

**Merlin**: Coercion + Annihilation + Concentration

**Attila**: Malice + Death Breath + Cleave

**Peter**: Weakness + Oaken Guard + Life Link

## Strengths and weaknesses

This setup is a solid earth killing march. With the high damage and wounds it eats through sustain setups quickly. Moreover, since we are running water ourselves, we have quite strong sustain and control which is very important versus other marches such as wind and fire.

Lightning setups with Khan can be a problem if built right, since we do still rely on our sustain. But generally I am confident this will do well versus anything you meet in Prime League round 3.

**In conclusion, a balanced march easy to use and strong against most things you will meet.**
