### Gift of Bondings – November Edition (Thanksgiving Theme)

The November rotation of **Gift of Bondings** brings a full Thanksgiving theme to Norheim. This month introduces a new profile frame, a tropical-themed teleportation effect, and a festive feast-inspired castle skin. As usual, the bundles follow the standard three-tier progression ($5 → $50 → $100), and higher tiers can only be purchased after the $5 bundle is bought.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-81.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-79.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-80.png)

While the cosmetics this month are visually appealing, the gameplay buffs remain minor and are not significant sources of power progression.

---

## $5 Bundle – “A Warm Dinner” Profile Frame

### Contents

- **“A Warm Dinner” Profile Picture Frame**
- **Elemental Artifact Selection Chests** (standard quantity)
- **Dragon Essence**
- **VIP Points**
- **Basic Resources**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-81.png)

### Overview

The $5 bundle sets the theme for the month with a Thanksgiving-style avatar frame. This tier provides the usual set of minor progression items and is the most cost-efficient bundle for collectors or casual spenders.

---

## $50 Bundle – “Singular Realm” Teleportation Effect

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-79.png)

### Contents

- **Singular Realm Teleportation Effect**
A tropical, Hanging-Gardens-inspired teleport animation.
- **3% Gathering Speed** bonus
- **16 Elemental Artifact Selection Chests**
- **16 Philosopher Stones**
- **Resources**
- **VIP Points**

[Embedded media](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Screenrecorder-2025-11-25-20-44-03-660.mp4)

### Overview

The Singular Realm teleportation effect is the visual highlight of this tier. The 3% Gathering Speed bonus is minimal and not impactful for competitive players. This bundle is primarily a cosmetic purchase.

---

## $100 Bundle – “King’s Feast” Castle Skin

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-80.png)

### Contents

Brining back another of the old-school castle skins. The King's Feast was initially released during the very first seasonal thanksgiving event years ago. Now it is back to celebrate another thanksgiving!

- **King’s Feast Castle Skin**
A Thanksgiving banquet-themed castle appearance.
- **3% Resource Production Speed**
(Wood, Food, Iron, Stone — *no Gold bonus*)
- **20 Elemental Artifact Selection Chests**
- **24 Philosopher Stones**
- **Resources**
- **VIP Points**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-82.png)

### Overview

The King’s Feast castle skin offers a festive, seasonal design and a small production buff. The 3% boost is minor, making this bundle largely cosmetic and aimed at collectors or players seeking themed visuals.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-83.png)

---

## Conclusion

The November edition of Gift of Bondings delivers strong seasonal cosmetics but very limited gameplay value.

- The **$5 bundle** is accessible and provides a themed profile frame.
- The **$50** and **$100** tiers offer attractive visual effects and skins, but their stat bonuses are too low to matter for progression.

### Bottom Line:

This month’s Gift of Bondings is best suited for collectors and players who enjoy themed cosmetics. Competitive players can safely skip the higher-tier bundles without losing meaningful power.
