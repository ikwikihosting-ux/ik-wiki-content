## Introduction: Why Test Drop Rates?

While the in-game Lucky Spin displays theoretical drop rates, any seasoned player knows that *actual results can vary significantly in practice*. So, how can you know what to expect for your pearls and dollars spent?

That’s exactly what we’ll break down here: **real-world test results from nearly 500 pearls spent**, what kind of **stardust return** you can realistically expect, and why **Season 1 is the best time to invest** in this wheel.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-84-1024x424.png)

---

## Lucky Spin – Stardust Edition: What Makes It Unique?

The Stardust Edition of the Lucky Spin is arguably one of the most generous resource events *Infinity Kingdom* has ever introduced.

Key features:

- Every slot on the wheel contains **Stardust** (season 1).
- **Jackpot prize**: 400x Stardust (0.3% drop chance).
- **Mini jackpot**: 250x stardust (0.4%)
- **Micro jackpot** 200x stardust (0.9%)
- Tons of low-stardust fields (8x per slot, 12.3% each).
- A bonus bar granting additional Stardust every few spins.
- Accessible using **Pearls of Finding**, acquired via event bundles.

Most importantly: **In Season 1, the wheel is *pure stardust***. In later seasons, **chaos portrait items** are added, diluting drop rates considerably—especially for non-chaos players.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-85.png)

## The Real Drop Test: 494 Pearls Used

To test actual value, we spun the wheel **494 times** (using Pearls of Finding) and tracked the total **Stardust gained only from wheel drops** (not including bonus bar rewards).

### 🔍 Results:

- **494 Pearls spent**
- **6,714 Stardust gained**
- **Average Stardust per Pearl: 13.59**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-86-1024x301.png)

This is a much more useful figure than the theoretical 0.3% jackpot math—because it gives you a clear expectation when budgeting pearls.

---

## Value Per Dollar: Incredible ROI

Now, let’s translate that into spending efficiency.

### 📦 The Best Bundle: $5 = 18 Pearls

- At 13.59 Stardust/Pearl → **18 Pearls = 244.62 Stardust**
- That’s **49 Stardust per $1 spent**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-89-1024x538.png)

For context: Stardust in the **Marketplace costs 120 Gems per 1 Stardust**. So...

### 💎 Gem Value Comparison:

- 244.62 Stardust × 120 Gems = **29,354 Gems worth**
- For **$5** spent!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-87-1024x493.png)

That’s an **incredible gem-to-dollar conversion**—outclassing nearly every other bundle in the game.

## Why Season 1 Players Should Prioritize This Event

This guide and test were conducted on **Season 1 servers**, where the **wheel contains only Stardust**. That means:

- **No chaos items** to dilute drops
- **Maximum average Stardust return**
- **Better long-term value** if you're building non-chaos teams (which most players are in Season 1-3)

In later seasons, chaos portrait upgrade materials (like Crimson Ashes) are added to the wheel. For players not investing in chaos immortals, those drops are **dead slots**, which significantly **lowers your stardust-per-pearl average**.

So if you're in Season 1 and ever considered buying pearls, **now is the time**.

---

## What If I’m In a Chaos-Enabled Server?

Even if you're on a Chaos-enabled server (Season 4+), the wheel still provides **decent value**, though significantly less than in Season 1.

Assuming drop efficiency is halved due to dilution:

- ~6.79 Stardust per Pearl
- 18 Pearls = ~122.31 Stardust
- That’s still **24.46 Stardust per $1 spent**

While not as amazing as Season 1, it's still a viable option—especially during periods of high resource need (e.g., when preparing for immortal portrait upgrades).

If you’re looking to stockpile Stardust—especially for **portrait upgrades**, **boss builds**, or future events—**this is the most cost-efficient method available**. Testing confirms it.
