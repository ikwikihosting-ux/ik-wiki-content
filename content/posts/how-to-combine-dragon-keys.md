## Why This Update Is a Big Deal for Dragon Key Management

In Infinity Kingdom, dragon crystals are one of the most valuable upgrade materials in the game—fueling your elemental dragons, boosting their combat stats, and ultimately powering your main march to compete at higher levels. But for many players, **opening Dragon Crystal Chests has always been gated by one thing**: the right kind of key.

You earn Dragon Chests from boss fights across Norheim, the Mysterium, and Spider events. But to open them, you need specific keys: green (3-hour), blue (6-hour), purple (12-hour), or legendary (24-hour). The longer the duration, the better the chest quality. Until now, most players were sitting on mountains of 3-hour and 12-hour keys while *never* seeing the rarer blue or legendary ones—unless they spent real money.

That all changed with the latest patch.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-41.png)

**As of the June update, Infinity Kingdom now allows you to combine Dragon Keys directly from your inventory.** No crafting timers, no hidden building requirements, no alchemy needed. You can now convert two 12-hour keys into one 24-hour key *for free*. This small but crucial quality-of-life change gives players—especially Free-to-Play and low spenders—much better access to premium dragon rewards.

This guide breaks down what changed, how it works, and why smart Dragon Key management just became an essential part of your daily routine.

Let’s dive in.

## 🗝️ Why Dragon Keys Matter

Dragon Chest Keys are crucial for unlocking Dragon Crystal Chests earned from boss fights (Norheim, Mysterium, Spider Boss). These chests contain:

- Elemental dragon crystals (Fire, Water, Earth, Lightning)
- Gems, speed-ups, and rare resources

Before the patch, **3-hour (green)** and **12-hour (purple)** keys were common, but **6-hour (blue)** and **24-hour (legendary)** keys were rare and typically **only obtainable via paid bundles**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-42.png)

---

## 🔥 What Changed in the Latest Patch

With the update released yesterday, a long-requested feature arrived: **dragon key combining**! Now you can:

- Combine **two 12-hour keys** → **one 24-hour key**
- Later on, hopefully combine 6-hour keys into 12-hour ones

**No cost** in resources or time—it's purely a UI convenience applied instantly.

---

## 🛠️ How to Combine Keys: Step-by-Step

A lot of players asked how to do this—there is **no Alchemy Lab recipe** or special button in the Dragon Chest building. It’s simple:

1. Go to your **inventory** → **Others** tab
2. Select a key (e.g. a purple 12-hour key)
3. Hit **Combine**
4. TWO keys merge into the next-tier key
5. No spend, no timer—just instant upgrade

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-39-1024x525.png)

This works on **all servers** and **all players**, not limited by castle level or server type.

## 🎯 Why This Is a Game-Changer

- **Maximize your rare 24-hour keys**: Instead of spending money, you can simply combine paid-earned 12-hour keys.
- **Free players benefit too**: Use surplus 12-hour keys to create a powerful 24-hour key—no spending required.
- **Efficiency in chest opening**: Longer-duration keys are better value per use and easier to align with your play schedule.
- **Streamlined inventory**: Fewer key types to track and fewer UI elements to manage.

---

## ⚙️ Strategic Use of Keys

- **Stay stocked**: Save green & purple keys until you've built enough 24-hour keys for when you'll be at your strongest dragon assortment.
- **Best use timing**: Combine keys when you have at least two of the same tier to avoid wasting dormant keys.
- **Gauge chest value**: 24-hour chests hold the most crystals—so prioritizing these provides better long-term value.
