## Infinity Kingdom Portrait Levels Guide

In Infinity Kingdom, portrait levels play a crucial role in enhancing the power of your Immortals. These portrait levels offer various attribute gains as you level up your Immortals, and understanding how to strategically invest in them can significantly impact your gameplay. In this guide, we will delve into the details of portrait levels, including costs, rewards, and the best strategies to follow.

## Immortal Type-Specific Progression

As you embark on the journey of upgrading portrait levels in Infinity Kingdom, it's vital to understand how each type of Immortal evolves uniquely at different levels. The cost associated with each level increases, and so do the attribute bonuses. Let's delve into the specific progression for each Immortal type:

### Tank Immortals

Tank Immortals are the frontline protectors of your team. They focus on absorbing damage and maintaining the stability of your formation. Here's how they progress as you invest in portrait levels:

- Attribute gains are Physical Attack, Magical Defense and Physical Defense.
- As you continue upgrading, you'll unlock a 5% Damage Resistance bonus at level 20. This bonus provides them with additional resilience against various forms of damage.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/image-38-1024x518.png)

### Attack Immortals

Attack Immortals specialize in dealing physical damage to enemies. Their progression is vital for boosting your team's offensive capabilities. Here's how they evolve with portrait level upgrades:

- Attribute gains are Physical Defense, Magical Defense and Physical Attack.
- The first % bonus at level 10 provides a 5% Accuracy Rate, improving their precision in combat.
- At level 20, you unlock a 5% Physical Damage bonus, significantly amplifying their damage output.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/image-42-1024x545.png)

### Ranged Immortals

Ranged Immortals provide crucial support from a safe distance, making them valuable for controlling the battlefield. Their progression is as follows:

- Attribute gains are Physical Defense, Magical Defense and Physical Attack.
- The first % bonus at level 10 grants a 5% Accuracy Rate, making their ranged attacks more precise.
- Reaching level 20 rewards them with a 5% Physical Damage bonus, enhancing their ranged damage output.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/image-40-1024x529.png)

### Magic Immortals

Magic Immortals unleash the power of spells and magical attacks. They play a pivotal role in your team's ability to deal magical damage. Here's how they progress:

- Attribute gains are Physical Defense, Magical Defense and Magical Attack.
- The first % bonus at level 10 provides a 5% Crit Rate, increasing their chances of landing critical hits.
- At level 20, they receive a 5% bonus to Magical Damage, significantly amplifying their magical damage potential.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/image-41-1024x514.png)

### Support Immortals

Support Immortals are the backbone of your team, providing valuable assistance and healing. Their unique progression is as follows:

- Attribute gains are Physical Attack, Physical Defense and Magical Defense.
- The first % bonus at level 10 grants a 5% Dodge Rate, making them more evasive and challenging to hit.
- At level 20, they unlock a 5% Damage Resistance bonus, making them more resilient and capable of enduring damage.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/image-43-1024x542.png)

It's essential to consider these unique progressions and the associated costs as you plan your portrait level investments. Understanding the strengths and weaknesses of each Immortal type and prioritizing the right ones for your strategy can make a significant difference in your overall performance in Infinity Kingdom.

## The Power of the Second % Bonus

Reaching level 20 in portrait levels unlocks the second % bonus, which provides substantial boosts to your Immortals:

- **Attack and Ranged Immortals**: Receive a 5% increase in physical damage.
- **Magic Immortals**: Gain a 5% increase in magical damage.
- **Tank and Support Immortals**: Acquire a 5% damage resistance bonus.

These upgrades are incredibly potent and can make a significant difference in your Immortals' performance on the battlefield.

## Managing Costs

One important factor to consider is that the cost of leveling up portrait levels increases as you progress. This means that, while you keep receiving attribute bonuses, they come at a much higher cost. For example, at portrait level 4, you gain 56.7 physical attack for just 7 Stardust, but at level 21, you get the same 56.7 physical attack for the price of 85 Stardust.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/image-39-1024x524.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/image-42-1024x545.png)

This highlights the importance of strategic management when upgrading portrait levels. Here's what you should keep in mind:

1. **Different Upgrade Paces**: Avoid upgrading all your Immortals at the same pace. As the levels get more expensive, the returns diminish until you reach the major boost % slot.
2. **Low-Level Investments**: Take advantage of the extremely low cost at lower levels. The attribute boosts at these levels are still valuable. Consider stopping at either level 4 or 6, depending on your resources.
3. **Focus on Key Immortals**: Invest heavily in one or a few Immortals to unlock the major upgrades at level 10 and 20. Choose the most valuable Immortals for your strategy, such as Alex in a hybrid team or Wu in a Fire-focused lineup.

By following these strategies and understanding the value of portrait levels, you can optimize your Immortal's power and dominate the battlefield in Infinity Kingdom.

Remember, the journey to the second % bonus is where your Immortals truly shine, so plan your portrait level investments wisely and lead your team to victory!
