Update **2.9.8** isn't a massive content expansion filled with new immortals or balance changes, but it introduces two features that could have a lasting impact on how *Infinity Kingdom* is played. Alongside a brand-new PvE mode, the long-requested Alliance Sparring system finally gives players a way to enjoy meaningful PvP outside of Kingdom vs Kingdom.

The remainder of the update focuses largely on progression improvements and quality-of-life features, including changes to Artifacts, Dragon Souls, the Zuma Shop and several interface improvements. None of these are game-changing on their own, but together they make progression a little smoother, particularly for newer players.

Let's take a closer look at what Update 2.9.8 brings.

---

## Alliance Sparring Finally Fills a Long-Standing Gap

For many servers, meaningful PvP has been limited for years. Outside of Legion of Frostborne and Kingdom vs Kingdom, there often isn't much opportunity to fight.

On one-alliance servers, there simply isn't another alliance of similar strength to compete against. Even on servers with two or three large alliances, Norheim usually settles into an unofficial peace because everyone wants to focus on growth rather than constant troop losses. The result is that many players only experience large-scale PvP during KvK.

The new Sparring System looks set to change that.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-130-1024x491.png)

From the patch notes, it appears alliances will be able to divide into two teams and fight one another during dedicated sparring sessions. Assuming that's how it works in practice, this could become one of the most valuable additions the game has received in years. It gives alliances a way to organise PvP training, test marches and rally compositions, practise coordination, and even earn Honor without asking players to leave the alliance to create temporary enemies.

The fact that Sparring still causes troop losses and triggers Bloody Rage also suggests it is intended to feel like genuine world-map combat rather than a simplified duel mode. That should make it a much better environment for testing real marches before taking them into KvK.

It's a feature the community has been asking for since the early days of the game, and it's great to finally see it become a reality.

---

## Endless Tower Looks Promising

The second major addition is **Endless Tower**, a new PvE progression mode unlocked after 15 days on a server once players reach Castle Level 20.

The mode follows a familiar formula, with players climbing increasingly difficult floors while adapting to unique Floor Traits that change the challenge from one level to the next. Five attempts are available each day, rewarding players with progression materials and fragments for Holy, Shadow and even Chaos immortals.

Whether Endless Tower becomes a favourite will probably depend on what kind of player you are.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-132-1024x104.png)

Players who enjoy solving PvE encounters and experimenting with different teams will likely appreciate another challenge mode, especially if Floor Traits encourage creative team building rather than simply rewarding higher Combat Power. Players who already feel there are enough daily PvE activities may be less enthusiastic about another mode to complete every day.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-131-1024x172.png)

Personally, I hope the difficulty comes from mechanics rather than raw statistics. If success depends on choosing the right immortals, adapting your skills and responding to each floor's traits, Endless Tower could become one of the more engaging PvE modes in the game. If progression simply comes down to increasing your Combat Power, it risks becoming another routine grind.

The rewards are what make Endless Tower particularly interesting. A new source of **Holy, Shadow and Chaos Immortal fragments** has the potential to improve long-term progression, especially for low and mid-spending players building around Holy and Shadow marches instead of full Chaos teams. Making those immortals more accessible should encourage greater variety in team building across the game.

Chaos fragments are also listed as rewards, although I wouldn't expect them to be especially generous. Much like the Harbour, they will likely provide slow, steady progress rather than replacing the existing premium acquisition methods.

## Other Notable Changes

Although Endless Tower and Alliance Sparring are clearly the headline features, the remainder of the patch contains several welcome improvements.

**Artifact progression** has become much more accessible. Removing the Chronicle requirement means every new server can begin using Artifacts immediately instead of waiting for progression milestones. At the same time, Artifact Potential has been adjusted so Season 0 players can reach Silver quality while Season 1 players can progress to Gold. It's a sensible change that allows newer servers to engage with the Artifact system much earlier.

**Theia's Roulette** has also been updated with selectable Holy and Shadow Dragon Soul rewards. We still need to see exactly how these rewards are distributed, but giving players more control over Dragon Soul progression is certainly a positive step.

The **Zuma Shop** receives a smaller update, adding new Rare Fragments while removing the option to exchange Pearl of Findings for Energy Cores. Depending on what fragments become available, this may change purchasing priorities for players who regularly use the shop.

Several quality-of-life improvements round out the patch. Immortal Outfits can now be previewed immediately, the Appointment interface has been simplified with static portraits, Insignias now use clearer colour coding based on rarity, and chat finally gains the ability to quote messages. None of these changes transform the game, but they make the overall experience a little cleaner and easier to navigate.

---

## Final Thoughts

Update 2.9.8 may not introduce flashy new immortals or sweeping balance changes, but it addresses several areas that have needed attention for a long time.

The **Alliance Sparring System** has the potential to give players meaningful PvP outside of Kingdom vs Kingdom, solving a problem that has existed on many servers since the game launched. If it works as advertised, it could become one of the most useful social features ever added to *Infinity Kingdom*.

Meanwhile, **Endless Tower** adds another avenue for long-term progression while introducing a valuable new source of Holy and Shadow Immortal fragments. Whether it becomes an engaging strategy mode or simply another daily activity will depend largely on how challenging and varied its Floor Traits prove to be.

Overall, this feels less like a major expansion and more like a quality-of-life update that strengthens the game's core systems. It may not be the most spectacular patch on paper, but if these new features deliver on their potential, Update 2.9.8 could have a lasting impact on how players spend their time in *Infinity Kingdom*.
