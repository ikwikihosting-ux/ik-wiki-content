The release of patch 2.6 has ushered in a thrilling challenge for players with the increase in Immortal level cap from 50 to 55. As the level cap expands, players face the daunting task of obtaining more experience points (EXP) to propel their Immortals to the pinnacle of power. In this comprehensive guide, we will delve into the actual EXP requirements for levels 1 to 55, equipping you with valuable insights to navigate this epic journey.

Experience points (EXP) are the lifeblood of a character's growth in our cherished game. Accumulating EXP allows Immortals to level up, unlocking new abilities and augmenting their combat capabilities. Each level presents a greater challenge, demanding a progressively higher amount of EXP to reach the next stage of mastery.
