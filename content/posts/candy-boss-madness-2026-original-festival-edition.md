## Introduction

The Candy Defenders system looks simple on the surface: throw candy at a boss, get rewards. But once you break it down properly, it becomes clear that this is one of the most efficient progression systems we’ve had in a seasonal event.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-53-1024x563.png)

To understand its real value, you need to look at three things:

1. What you actually get from candy (on average)
2. How much candy it takes to kill a boss
3. What that translates to if you decide to spend

I’ll walk through each of these the same way I would evaluate it in-game—starting from the raw drop rates and turning that into practical expectations.

---

## Rewards per 1000 Candy

The first step is converting all the probabilities into something usable. Instead of thinking in percentages, we scale everything to **1000 candy**, which gives a stable baseline.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-54.png)

### Boss Damage

From the drop table:

- 40% → 1 bomb (15 damage)
- 20% → 2 bombs (30 damage)
- 12% → 4 bombs (60 damage)
- 8% → 6 bombs (90 damage)

If you average that out:

- Each candy deals **26.4 damage**
- Per 1000 candy → **26,400 total damage**

That’s important because it ties directly into boss kills later.

---

### Purple Soul Crystals

You have a 3% chance per candy to get an epic spirit chest.
Each chest averages out to **607 crystals** when you factor in all outcomes.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-55-1024x648.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-56-1024x495.png)

So over 1000 candy:

- ~30 chests
- → **18,210 purple crystals**

This alone is already a serious amount. In practice, this is enough to noticeably move immortal progression even on mid-late accounts.

---

### Philosopher Stones & Extras

Again, 3% drop rate → ~30 chests per 1000 candy.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-57-1024x640.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-58-1024x447.png)

From the internal probabilities, each chest averages:

- **1.77 Philosopher Stones**
- **3,500 EXP**
- **1.95 hours of universal speedups**
- **15 gems**

So over 1000 candy:

- **~53 Philosopher Stones**
- **~105,000 EXP**
- **~58.5 hours universal speedups**
- **~450 gems**

This is a very efficient conversion into Tower of Knowledge progress and general account acceleration.

---

### Speedup Chests

At 4% drop rate:

- ~40 chests per 1000 candy

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-64-1024x497.png)

The exact value depends on how the chest works in your version:

- Minimum case → **~40 hours total**
- Best case (one of each type) → **~160 hours total**

Either way, this stacks on top of the universal speedups, which is why the total speedup gain feels so high in practice.

---

### Resources

At 10% drop rate:

- ~100 procs per 1000 candy

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-59-1024x557.png)

Each gives a large bundle, resulting in:

- **10M Food / Wood / Stone / Iron**
- **2M Gold**

Not the main reason to farm candy, but it removes a lot of friction when pushing upgrades.

---

### Summary – 1000 Candy

What you are effectively getting:

- **26,400 boss damage**
- **18,210 purple crystals**
- **53 Philosopher Stones**
- **105,000 EXP**
- **58.5+ hours universal speedups**
- **40 speedup chests**
- **450 gems**
- **10M resources + 2M gold**

From experience, this is already above what most seasonal side systems provide—even before considering scaling.

---

## Candy Required per Boss Kill

Now that we know how much damage candy produces, we can convert that into boss kills.

- Boss HP: **100,000**
- Average damage per candy: **26.4**

So:100,000÷26.4≈3,788100,000 \div 26.4 \approx 3,788

### Practical Result

- **~3,800 candy per boss kill**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-61-1024x357.png)

Note that there are also rewards for killing the bosses! You have 10x bosses maximum per day, yielding Tokens of Light as well as a few speed ups upon kill. Token of Light drops scale from 4 on first three kills to 5 on the next three, 6 on two more and 7 tokens on the last kill.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-62.png)

This is quite good value! But the main attraction of this event is in the speedups, philosopher stones and soul crystals which are in absolute abundance!

---

### What This Means in Practice

Every ~3,800 candy you spend gives you:

- ~69,000+ purple crystals
- ~200+ Philosopher Stones
- Hundreds of hours of speedups

When I ran the numbers against actual gameplay, this lined up very closely with what you feel in-game: boss kills come steadily, and the reward flow is consistent rather than spiky.

## Cost Analysis (If You Choose to Spend)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-60.png)

- **22,500 candy**
- **$55 total cost**

### Total per day (both platforms)

- **45,000 candy**
- **$110 total cost**

The bundles are straightforward, which makes the math unusually clean for an event like this.

### Daily Purchase (One Platform)

- Total cost: **$55**
- Total candy: **22,500**

This gives a cost of: **~$0.00244 per candy**

## Conclusion Summary

- **1000 candy = extremely high, stable resource output**
- **~3800 candy = 1 boss kill**
- **~$9.30 per boss kill if spending**

If you treat candy as a primary resource and push it from all available sources, this event will outperform most previous seasonal formats in terms of raw account progression.
