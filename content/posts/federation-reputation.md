## Introduction

Federation Reputation is one of the central progression systems in the modern Legion of Frostborne battlefield. Since the Federation system replaced the older Alliance structure, coordination between alliances now matters far more than individual alliance performance alone.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-56-1024x588.png)

A Federation can contain up to five alliances from the same faction, and all participating alliances contribute toward shared battlefield progress. In practice, Federation Reputation acts as the combined measure of your coalition’s activity, battlefield control, and long-term expedition performance. Legion of Frostborne rewards organization more than raw power once the expedition enters the mid and late stages.

---

## What Is a Federation?

According to the in-game rules:

- A Federation can include up to 4 alliances.
- Only alliances from the same faction may join.
- The alliance leader who creates the Federation becomes the Federation Leader.
- The Federation Leader can invite alliances, approve applications, and disband the Federation.
- Alliances that leave a Federation must wait 8 hours before joining another one.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-57-1024x546.png)

The system effectively creates a battlefield coalition. Instead of alliances operating independently, Federations share strategic goals and contribute toward faction-wide progress.

One important detail many newer players miss is that Federation structure affects both coordination and rewards. Poorly organized Federations often lose efficiency because reputation generation becomes fragmented across disconnected objectives. Managing high Alliance Reputation and Legion Marks throughout all members of your Federation is to the benefit of all!

---

## How Federation Reputation Works

Federation Reputation is tied directly to overall battlefield contribution during the expedition.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-60-1024x356.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-62-1024x579.png)

The game evaluates performance through several connected systems:

- Personal Legion’s Marks
- Alliance Reputation
- Federation Reputation

These layers build upward from individual activity into alliance performance, then finally into Federation-wide progression.

---

## Personal Legion’s Marks

Players earn Legion’s Marks through battlefield activity, including:

- Earning Honor
- Gathering resources
- Defeating Gnomes and Gnome Bosses
- Building Thermal Towers
- Destroying enemy Thermal Towers
- Reducing enemy Territory Prosperity
- Expelling enemy territories
- Attacking cities and checkpoints
- Donating resources for repairs

In actual gameplay, active participation matters more than pure troop power. A mid-powered player constantly building towers, gathering, reinforcing, and contributing to objectives often generates more useful marks than a high-powered inactive player.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-58.png)

During several Frostborne seasons, I noticed that Federations with disciplined activity schedules climbed reputation rankings much faster than alliances relying only on whales fighting frontline battles.

---

## Alliance Reputation

Alliance Reputation is built from the collective Legion’s Marks generated by alliance members.

The game tracks:

1. Total Legion’s Marks earned by alliance members
2. Additional battlefield contribution metrics tied to expedition objectives

This creates a cumulative progression system where alliance-wide participation becomes extremely important.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-59-1024x579.png)

An alliance with 70 consistently active players will usually outperform a stronger but less organized alliance with low participation rates.

Frostborne was designed to reward sustained activity and strategic coordination over isolated combat wins.

---

## Victory Conditions and Expedition End

The expedition lasts a maximum of 32 days.

However, Humanity can achieve an early victory if an alliance occupies the Gnome King Capital for more than 24 hours.

Once the expedition ends:

- Players withdraw from the Frozen Realm
- Rewards are distributed
- Federation and Alliance Reputation determine progression payouts

Importantly, players must have entered the battlefield during the season to receive rewards.

---

## Player Perspective

From several Frostborne seasons, one pattern becomes obvious: activity density matters more than raw alliance power.

The strongest Federations are rarely the ones with the biggest individual spenders alone. They are usually the groups with:

- Consistent participation
- Reliable leadership
- Coordinated objective timing
- Clear battlefield structure

For instance, when our Federation split tower responsibilities between alliances instead of letting everyone expand randomly, overall progression speed improved noticeably. We wasted less stamina, secured cities earlier, and hit higher reputation thresholds well before the final week.

The Federation system rewards organization at every level.

---

## Conclusion Summary

Federation Reputation is the core progression system behind Legion of Frostborne coalition play. It reflects the combined battlefield contribution of all alliances within a Federation and directly determines rankings and seasonal rewards.

The system heavily rewards:

- Active participation
- Strategic expansion
- Alliance coordination
- Long-term battlefield efficiency

Players who understand how Personal Marks scale into Alliance and Federation Reputation generally perform much better across an entire Frostborne season than players focused only on combat power or kill counts.
