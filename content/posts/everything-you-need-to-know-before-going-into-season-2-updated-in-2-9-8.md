Season 2 is still an important transition in Infinity Kingdom, but it is no longer the huge wall of newly unlocked systems that it used to be. Several mechanics that were previously tied directly to Season 2 have been moved earlier in account progression, while old events such as Sparks of Galaxy and the Wheel of Stardust are no longer part of the game.

The more useful way to prepare for Season 2 today is to understand **what actually changes, what you should finish before leaving Season 1, and where your resources and spending give you the most value**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-110-1024x461.png)

There are still plenty of opportunities to improve your account during the transition. The Hall of Summoning changes, Golden Path rewards rotate, your dragon development becomes increasingly important, and your longer-term march direction should start becoming clearer. Season 2 is therefore less about suddenly unlocking everything and more about making sure the investment you made during Season 1 continues in the right direction.

---

### Immortal Talents — No Longer a Season 2 Unlock

One of the biggest corrections to the old version of this guide is **Immortal Talents are no longer unlocked simply by entering Season 2**. They now become available as you reach the required Castle progression, so depending on how quickly you develop your account, you may already be working on talents before the season transition.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-113-1024x433.png)

The main progression materials are still **Marks and Insight Books**, with the Elite Well of Time being an important regular source. If Elite Well of Time is already available to you, make sure you are using your attempts consistently rather than waiting for Season 2.

There is also a very good spending option tied to this system. The monthly $10 shop purchase gives you **five additional attempts per day and six Insight Books per day**, making it one of the better recurring purchases for someone actively developing Immortal Talents.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-114-1024x491.png)

- **Priority tip**: Always do your daily Elite Well of Time attempts.
- **[Read the full Immortal Talent and Elite Well of Time guide here.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/immortals/immortal-talent-specializations/upgrading-immortal-talents-and-well-of-time-elite/)**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-111-1024x512.png)

For most marches, I start with the main damage dealer but develop the important backline support alongside it rather than pushing the DPS far ahead on its own. For example, when I am developing Wu and Qin together, I generally keep their progression reasonably close.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-112.png)

The reason is that DPS talents give slightly more immediate offensive value per rank, but the cost increases considerably at higher levels. Using the strongest mage route such as **Stormeye**, a rank can provide around **5.5% additional damage and 2.5% Critical Rate**, while a support rank can provide around **4% damage and 20 Initial Energy**. If every rank cost the same amount, pushing the mage would usually be the obvious choice. Once the increasing Insight Book cost is taken into account, developing the support alongside the carry becomes much more efficient.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-115-1024x498.png)

Frontline tanks are usually my lowest priority. I still like getting the **first talent level** relatively early because that activates the important talent effect and, just as importantly, lets you begin spending Marks on the minor nodes. Otherwise you can end up sitting on a huge pile of Marks while Insight Books remain the actual bottleneck.

The main principle is therefore not to max one immortal while ignoring everyone else. Get the important talent effects online, prioritize the immortals that determine how your march actually functions, and spend your expensive later ranks where they have the most impact.

### Dragon Specialization Trees

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-116-1024x525.png)

Dragon development remains one of the systems you should be preparing for well before the Season 2 transition. Even if you are not yet able to complete the specialization tree you want, **Dragon Crystals are worth farming continuously** because you will eventually need a very large amount of them.

**[Detailed guide on Dragon Talents can be found here.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/dragon/dragon-talents-patch-2-3/)**

For most dragons, I still follow the same broad development route. Start by pushing the initial progression to **2/2**, then bring **Claw and Armor to level 3**. From there, work toward the dragon's important specialization skills and bring the useful damage and defensive nodes to **3/3** before spending heavily on lower-priority stats.

I generally leave things such as Resilience and Accuracy until later unless a particular dragon or build gives me a specific reason to prioritize them. Once the important specialization effects are established, I return to Claw and Armor, push them to **5/5**, and continue through the tree toward the second Claw and Armor nodes.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-118-1024x536.png)

The old version of this guide recommended **Sparks of Galaxy** as a major source of Dragon Essences. That advice is obsolete because Sparks of Galaxy is gone. Dragon Essences now need to be collected through the current events, shop and bundle sources, including options such as the Illusion Battle League shop and weekly progression sources and are available in loads in this modern version of the game.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-117-1024x758.png)

The important preparation advice has not changed, though: **do not neglect Dragon Crystals during Season 1**. Even if Essences temporarily limit how quickly you can move through the specialization tree, you will eventually need the Crystals so bank them!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-153-1024x342.png)

### Check Your Season 1 Golden Path Immortals Before Moving On

The **Golden Path rotation changes in Season 2**, and this is one of the things I would actually pay attention to before your server transitions.

**Charles, Baldwin and Genghis Khan disappear from the Golden Path when you move into Season 2.** If one of those immortals is important to the march you are building, check your progress before leaving Season 1 rather than assuming the same fragments will continue being available afterward.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-157-1024x461.png)

Season 2 we are seeing these replaced again with Immortals **Seondeok, Mathilda, Trajan, Ragnar, Mehmed, Darius, etc. who also** become available through the **$4.99 Golden Path**.

For spenders, the $4.99 Golden Path remains one of the better-value purchases in the game. The immortal progression is useful, you can obtain artifacts for elemental and seasonal immortals, and the AP and SP help maintain the pace of account development.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-158-1024x461.png)

Random Immortal Summon Fragments are another useful part of the reward pool because they can potentially provide fragments for immortals that are more difficult to obtain through normal summoning, including Khan.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-156-1024x491.png)

The old reason for recommending Golden Path was often its supply of **Purple Soul Crystals**. Those are still useful, but I would no longer buy the pass because of them. Modern seasonal events provide so many Soul Crystals and Philosopher's Stones that these resources are nowhere near as restrictive as they used to be.

Golden Path is still excellent value. **The reasons for buying it have simply changed.**

## Battle Passes Are Now Tied to Castle Progression

Another major change is that the different Battle Passes are no longer something you should describe as specifically unlocking because you entered Season 2.

Once your account reaches **Castle 40**, the Battle Pass options become available together. Your Castle progression is what matters here rather than the season transition itself.

That means the old distinction of "Season 1 has Golden Path, then Season 2 introduces Legendary Battle Pass" should be ignored.

For spenders, the passes can still provide excellent value, particularly once you have a clear direction toward Holy, Shadow or eventually Chaos development. The important thing is to buy them because their rewards fit your account, not because a guide tells you they are mandatory simply because you entered Season 2.

For most light and mid spenders, I would still put the **$4.99 Golden Path** very high on the list because its cost is low enough that the combination of immortal fragments, artifacts, AP/SP and general progression is difficult to beat.

### Chaos Immortals Arrive

Chaos Immortals become an increasingly important part of account planning around this stage, but they should not convince a normal spender to suddenly throw away the march they spent Season 1 developing.

Chaos is expensive.

A Chaos immortal requires substantially more fragments than a normal Epic immortal, and additional fragments are eventually required for talent development as well. Building one quickly can therefore become extremely expensive if you rely on premium events rather than gradual progression.

**[You can see the Immortal fragment requirements here.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/immortals/specific-immortal-deepdives/star-upgrade-costs-immortal-fragments/)**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-120-1024x475.png)

For whales, immediately developing new Chaos immortals can make sense because they are paying for the time advantage. For most other players, I would treat Chaos as a **long-term project** and continue making the strongest march possible with the account you already have.

Battle Pass progression and seasonal rewards make gradual Chaos development much more realistic than it once was. There is rarely a need for a mid spender to rush the entire project at premium event prices.

## Sparks of Galaxy and Wheel of Stardust Are Gone

If you have been reading older Season 1 and Season 2 guides, this is an important correction.

**Sparks of Galaxy is gone.**

The old recommendation was to use Sparks heavily in Season 2 for Holy and Shadow fragments, Dragon Crystals and Dragon Essences. That used to be a major part of Season 2 spending strategy, but it no longer applies to the current version of the game.

The same is true of the **Wheel of Stardust**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-122.png)

Older advice told Season 1 players to buy as much Stardust as possible before entering Season 2 because the Season 1 wheel offered unusually good value. That system has now been replaced by **Zuma and the current seasonal-event structure**, so there is no reason to hoard Stardust for a transition that no longer exists.

If you see an old guide telling you to spend heavily on Sparks or bank Stardust before Season 2, you are reading advice written for an older version of Infinity Kingdom.

## Pearls and Immortal Fragments

Pearls have become more important in the modern progression economy, particularly now that some of the older event systems have disappeared. They can be used toward Immortal Fragments and other useful progression, which makes them worth considering when evaluating daily purchases and seasonal spending.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-154-1024x542.png)

I would still avoid blindly spending Pearls simply because you have them. By this stage of the game, you should be starting to form an idea of which immortals you intend to continue developing through Conquest. Saving flexible currencies until you know what your account actually needs is usually better than spending them immediately for a small short-term improvement.

This is especially true for light and mid spenders, where every premium currency has an opportunity cost.

### Artifact Quality and Your Long-Term March

Artifact development becomes increasingly important as your account progresses, but I would approach it differently from the old advice of simply buying every available artifact material each day.

The important question is **which immortals you are actually going to keep using**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-126-1024x769.png)

Season 1 marches can change substantially by Conquest. Spending heavily on artifact empowerment for an immortal you replace shortly afterward is one of those purchases that feels good immediately and looks much worse a few months later.

For players who already know their long-term carry — perhaps Merlin, Wu, Alexander or another immortal they are committed to — artifact investment makes considerably more sense. If your final march is still uncertain, I would be conservative and keep flexible resources available until the direction becomes clearer.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-127-1024x545.png)

Materials for these are limited — the **Mysterious Pearl shop’s daily materials** are a must-buy if you’re spending. They’re worth it all season long, and events won’t hand them out often.

## Best Value Purchases Around the Season 2 Transition

If you are spending, I would prioritize purchases that either permanently improve the account or give unusually good value for the money rather than trying to buy every new Season 2 mechanic.

The **second permanent builder** is one of the easiest purchases to justify because the benefit stays with your account permanently. Faster development matters throughout the early seasons, and unlike a temporary event bundle, you never outgrow the extra building capacity.

The **$4.99 Golden Path** is another purchase I would rate very highly. The combination of seasonal immortals, artifacts, AP, SP and general progression makes it one of the better low-cost purchases available, particularly now that Soul Crystals themselves are no longer the main reason to buy it.

**Divine Coins** can also be attractive when the **20× limited event** is running. This is the sort of purchase where timing matters considerably; buying the same resource during the correct event can produce much better value than purchasing it whenever it happens to appear in the shop.

Useful **seasonal-event currency** is another place where a small spender can get good value, provided you know what you are targeting in the event. I would rather buy enough currency to reach a meaningful reward breakpoint than spend randomly across several events without completing anything important.

The **$4.99 King of the Hill packs**, bought up to five times when appropriate, can also be a strong option when the immortal being offered fits your account. As always, the value depends on whether you are actually going to use that immortal.

## What to Do Before Leaving Season 1

The most important preparation for Season 2 is no longer hoarding one specific event currency. It is making sure your account is ready for the things that actually change.

Before the transition, check your **Golden Path immortals**, particularly Charles, Baldwin and Genghis Khan, because their availability changes once you enter Season 2. Continue farming **Dragon Crystals** rather than waiting until you urgently need them. Keep progressing **Immortal Talents and Elite Well of Time** as soon as your Castle progression makes them available rather than thinking of them as something that begins in Season 2.

I would also take a serious look at your main march before spending heavily on artifacts or specialized resources. By now you should have enough experience with the account to start deciding which element and carry you want to bring toward Conquest. The clearer that plan becomes, the easier every spending decision becomes.

Most importantly, ignore old preparation advice built around **Sparks of Galaxy and Stardust**. Neither should influence how you prepare for the current Season 2.

## Main Recommendations

Going into Season 2 today is much more about continuing efficient account development than preparing for a huge list of season-locked systems.

Keep doing your **Elite Well of Time attempts** once they are available and develop your main DPS together with the support immortals that enable it. Get the first talent level on important frontline immortals so their talent effect and minor-node progression are active, but do not sink expensive Insight Book levels into tanks before your damage core is developed.

Keep farming **Dragon Crystals** throughout Season 1. Dragon progression is one of those systems where having a stockpile before you need it feels much better than discovering the bottleneck afterward.

Before the season changes, check whether you still need **Charles, Baldwin or Genghis Khan from Golden Path**. Their Season 1 availability should be treated as something you may want to finish rather than assuming they will remain there indefinitely.

For spenders, prioritize purchases with lasting value. The **second permanent builder and $4.99 Golden Path** are excellent starting points, while Divine Coins during the 20× limited event, useful seasonal-event currency and relevant KoH packs can provide additional value depending on your budget.

Finally, do not build your Season 2 plan around outdated events. **There is no Sparks of Galaxy strategy to prepare for and no Stardust stockpile you need to carry across the transition.**

---

## Conclusion

Season 2 is still where your account starts becoming more specialized, but the transition is much smoother than it used to be. Immortal Talents and Battle Pass access are now tied more closely to Castle progression, seasonal events provide far more basic development resources, and several of the old events that once defined Season 2 spending have disappeared.

The players who prepare well are therefore not necessarily the ones who hoard the most currency. They are the ones who understand what their account is becoming.

Develop your main DPS and its supporting immortals together. Keep your Dragon Crystal farming moving. Check the Season 1 Golden Path before its immortal selection changes. Spend on permanent or unusually efficient progression before chasing expensive short-term advantages, and start thinking seriously about which march you intend to carry into Conquest.

Season 2 is not really the point where the game suddenly begins anymore. It is the point where the decisions you made in Season 1 start turning into a long-term account.
