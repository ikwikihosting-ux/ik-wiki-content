## A Castle level 30 guide to Fire

At Castle level 20, you have limited access to Tower of Knowledge passives. Moreover, you will quite quickly reach level 20 and will not have the most amazing stable of Immortals nor many stars ranks on the Immortals in play. As such, this guide attempts to zero in on the average player and where he or she is at in terms of Immortals and Passives around Castle level 20. It is based on an average player accounts and experiances with where we expect to be at around this progress level. Special thank you to Jessica who has been the primary driver, inspiration and brains behinds all of this!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/image-1.png)

## Immortals

In between Castle 20 and Castle 30, there is no middle point worth mentioning as most of the skills we will be using are in the Castle 27 to Castle 30 range, I have excluded a middle point.

I will be assuming that you are using **Merlin, Cleopatra, Hippolyta, William**

The main question people may have, why **Cleopatra?** The main cause of this, the low star level of most immortals we have access to & the high star level of Water Immortals as they are vastly easier to obtain this early on, and as such, pose a much larger threat, so, we use **Cleopatra** for both her Earth elemental bonus, and to negate the enemy **Merlin's** damage.

The other option is **Gilgamesh**, however, I feel that Castle 30 is spread too thin on physical skills at this moment in time and it is not viable to run 3 physical damage dealers.

I will be assuming that **Merlin** has 3 stars, **Cleopatra** has 3 Stars, **Hippolyta** has 3 Stars, **William** has 3 Stars,

**Merlin:**Concentration (3),

**Cleopatra:**(Fire / Toxic) Nova (3),

**Hippolyta:**Adrenaline Rush (3),

**William:** Fighting Master (3)*

![](https://oss.gtarcade.com/forum/png/2022-10-01/771066_35b7eb9c-20f5-4179-a597-a57391a60e35_141608.png)

*I believe the minor survivability boost this provides, via CC immune from water dragon, allowing William to use his active skill for Merlin is of greater benefit than Anger at this moment in time.

From this point, most of the questions I can foresee have been answered, all but one "Why no Wu?".

The simple answer, there is just no way that anyone this early has a Wu worth using, her star level will be next to 0.

**If you have a Wu at this stage of the game, that is equal to your Merlin in terms of skill level, just replace Cleo and continue with the skill guides**
