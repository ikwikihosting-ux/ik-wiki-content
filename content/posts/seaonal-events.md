## Introduction

Since Infinity Kingdom launched more than five years ago, seasonal events have become one of the game's defining progression systems. I've been playing since the first day of global release and, aside from a short break during 2025, have logged in every day while documenting the game's mechanics, events, and updates for the community. Over the years, I've spent countless hours collecting data, testing new features, and writing guides, which has made it possible to see how seasonal events have evolved across multiple years.

In the early days of Infinity Kingdom, seasonal events were far less predictable. They appeared irregularly, each with its own structure, rewards, and mechanics. Some were built around real-world holidays, while others introduced entirely unique activities that never returned. Although this variety made each event feel distinct, it also meant players had very little idea what was coming next or how best to prepare.

Today, the system has matured into a much more consistent annual cycle. Almost every month now features a seasonal event built around the same core framework, including a Battle Pass, Tokens of Light, the Grail Shop, daily missions, and other familiar progression systems. While each festival introduces its own theme, cosmetics, and limited-time rewards, the underlying mechanics remain largely the same. This makes it much easier for players to plan their resources and decide when to spend—or save—their gems, speedups, Action Points, and other valuable items.

Over time, a clear seasonal calendar has also emerged. Most months now feature the same recurring festival each year, with the occasional special event or major content update taking the place of a traditional holiday theme. Although GTarcade does not publish an official annual roadmap, the patterns have become consistent enough that experienced players can anticipate what is likely to arrive next.

This guide brings together those observations into a single annual calendar, helping players understand the current seasonal rotation, plan ahead for future events, and make the most of one of Infinity Kingdom's most rewarding progression systems.

## Infinity Kingdom Seasonal Event Calendar

Infinity Kingdom follows a fairly predictable seasonal event schedule throughout the year. While GTarcade does not publish an official annual roadmap, recurring festivals have established a clear pattern that players can use to plan their resources, Tokens of Light, speedups, and premium currency.

Most seasonal events share the same core framework, including daily missions, a seasonal Battle Pass, Glorious Challenge, Pinnacle Trial, Dazzling Wish, and an event shop. The theme, rewards, cosmetics, and featured gameplay vary from event to event, while the overall progression system remains largely consistent.

## Planning Ahead for Seasonal Events

Seasonal events are among the most rewarding recurring activities in **Infinity Kingdom**. Although each festival has its own theme, cosmetics, and limited-time rewards, the underlying progression system remains largely the same throughout the year. This consistency allows players to plan ahead and maximise the value of their resources.

Every seasonal event includes a dedicated **Battle Pass**, offering free and premium reward tracks filled with valuable items, exclusive cosmetics, and event currencies. Players can also earn **Tokens of Light** through daily activities, challenges, and event missions. These tokens can be spent in the seasonal shop on rewards that are often far more valuable than those available through standard weekly events.

Another major feature of seasonal events is the **Grail Shop**. Seasonal Grails provide one of the most efficient ways to strengthen **Chaos Immortals**, allowing players to purchase Chaos Grails and other progression materials that are otherwise limited or significantly more expensive to obtain. For anyone building a Chaos march, seasonal events are one of the best opportunities each year to accelerate long-term progression.

Because the seasonal framework repeats every month, experienced players rarely approach each event unprepared. Saving **Gems**, **Tokens of Light**, speedups, Action Points, and other valuable resources before a festival begins can significantly increase the rewards earned over the event's duration. Knowing what event is coming next also helps players decide whether to spend resources immediately or save them for a seasonal event that better matches their current goals.

While the exact themes and featured rewards may change from year to year, the monthly seasonal rotation has become one of Infinity Kingdom's core progression systems. Understanding the annual calendar allows players to plan more efficiently, spend resources more wisely, and make steady progress throughout the year.
