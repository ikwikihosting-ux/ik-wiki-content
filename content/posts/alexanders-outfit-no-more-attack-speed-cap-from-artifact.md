## Introduction

***Alexander's Outfit Guide: How It Works and Is It Worth Unlocking?***

Alexander's outfit is one of the first Immortal Outfits introduced in Infinity Kingdom, and it's also one of the easiest to misunderstand.

Many players assumed the outfit completely changed how Alexander's Exclusive Artifact works, while others believed it turned him into a traditional Energy-based immortal again. Neither is true.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-64-1024x532.png)

Instead, the outfit improves the mechanic already introduced by his Exclusive Artifact, removing its biggest drawback while leaving everything else exactly as it was.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-65.png)

If you're already using Alexander as a frontline damage dealer, understanding this interaction is essential before deciding whether the outfit is worth investing in.

---

## What Does Alexander's Outfit Change?

Alexander's outfit replaces his original ultimate with a stronger version. On paper the differences look small, but the interaction with his Exclusive Artifact is what makes the outfit valuable.

Without the Exclusive Artifact equipped, the only direct improvement is a longer duration of Control Immunity.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-66.png)

For players who don't own Alexander's Exclusive Artifact, that's the only gameplay difference.

The real upgrade only appears once the Exclusive Artifact is equipped.

---

## How Alexander's Exclusive Artifact Works

Normally, Alexander's ultimate is an active skill that grants:

- 50% Damage Dealt
- 50% Attack Speed
- Control Immunity

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-67.png)

These effects last for a limited time after casting his ultimate.

His Exclusive Artifact completely changes that mechanic.

Instead of consuming Energy and casting his ultimate during battle, the buffs become permanently active from the start of combat.

To balance permanent uptime, two of the bonuses are reduced:

- Damage Dealt falls from **50% to 35%**
- Attack Speed falls from **50% to 35%**

In exchange, Alexander gains:

- Permanent Damage Dealt
- Permanent Attack Speed
- Permanent Control Immunity
- No Energy requirement

Once the artifact is equipped, Alexander no longer builds Energy or casts his ultimate during battle.

---

## What Changes With the Outfit?

This is where most of the confusion comes from.

The outfit does **not** remove the Exclusive Artifact mechanic.

Alexander still:

- Does not generate Energy.
- Does not cast his ultimate.
- Keeps permanent Control Immunity.
- Keeps permanent buffs from the artifact.

The only difference is that the outfit removes the penalty applied by the Exclusive Artifact.

Instead of reducing his bonuses to 35%, Alexander returns to the full values:

- **50% Damage Dealt**
- **50% Attack Speed**

Everything else remains exactly the same. In other words, the outfit doesn't introduce a new playstyle. It simply upgrades the one players are already using.

---

## Is the Outfit Worth It?

If you're already running Alexander with his Exclusive Artifact, the answer is generally yes.

The artifact's permanent buffs have always been powerful, but the reduction from 50% to 35% Damage Dealt and Attack Speed was the price paid for that consistency.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-68-1024x656.png)

The outfit removes that limitation without asking you to give up any of the artifact's advantages.

The result is a permanent increase in both offensive stats while keeping:

- Permanent Control Immunity
- Permanent buff uptime
- No reliance on Energy generation
- No interruption from crowd control

For players who use Alexander as a primary frontline carry, it's a meaningful improvement rather than a complete redesign.

## Player Perspective

After testing the outfit, the biggest takeaway is that Alexander still plays exactly the same. His role in a march doesn't change, and you won't suddenly see him behaving like a conventional Energy-based immortal.

The improvement comes from removing the compromise built into his Exclusive Artifact. Before the outfit, players accepted reduced offensive stats in exchange for permanent uptime. Now you keep the permanent buffs while regaining the full 50% Damage Dealt and Attack Speed bonuses.

If you've already invested in Alexander and his artifact, the outfit feels like the final upgrade that lets him perform at his intended potential rather than introducing an entirely new version of the immortal.

---

## Conclusion

Alexander's outfit is less about changing his gameplay and more about refining it.

Without the Exclusive Artifact, the upgrade is fairly modest, offering only longer Control Immunity.

With the Exclusive Artifact equipped, however, the outfit removes the reduced offensive values while preserving every other benefit. Alexander continues to fight with permanent buffs, permanent Control Immunity, and no Energy requirement, but now does so with the full offensive bonuses originally found on his ultimate.

For players building around Alexander, that's what makes the outfit valuable. It doesn't reinvent the immortal—it perfects the version that was already working.
