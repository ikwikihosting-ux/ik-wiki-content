Chapter 4 of the *Infinity Kingdom Lore Series* continues directly from *The Hangover Knight*, and this time we get a familiar face from the game. Our still-unnamed hero finds himself at the Arena, where **Brynhild**, the Water Immortal, is fighting for her life.

The official title is **“The Broken Blade and the Shattered Pact,”** released alongside the following description:

> “Beneath the Broken Blade, a bloody battle in the Arena kicks off. It is a fated reunion, and also the beginning of an escape.”

The “fated reunion” is an interesting choice of words because Brynhild and our hero clearly know each other. We aren't told exactly what happened between them in the past, but he quickly gets involved in her fight and the two eventually escape together, taking us even further beneath the streets of Vitas.

The final few seconds are where things get particularly interesting, though. We were promised a journey towards the Abyss in Chapter 3, and after falling deep beneath the city our characters appear to have found something very old waiting for them down there.

## Brynhild Enters the Story

Brynhild's appearance is probably the biggest immediate reveal in Chapter 4. She is the first character in this part of the story whom players can immediately identify as one of the Immortals from the game.

We find her fighting in an underground Arena beneath Vitas against a huge stitched-together monster. It isn't exactly the Arena we're used to clicking through every day, but seeing an actual fighting Arena within Norheim is another nice bit of world-building. It also fits quite well with the darker Vitas we started seeing in Chapter 3, where the peaceful green kingdom turned out to have a fairly substantial black market and criminal underworld operating beneath the surface.

Our Hangover Knight is there too, and it doesn't take long before it becomes obvious that Brynhild isn't a stranger to him. He recognizes her and eventually joins the fight, helping her against the creature before the pair make their escape.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-132-1024x582.png)

The official description calls this a “fated reunion,” so there is clearly some history here that we haven't been told yet. Exactly what their relationship was, why they were separated and what has brought them both to this particular Arena are questions the series is keeping for later.

For me, this is also where the lore series starts connecting much more directly with the *Infinity Kingdom* we actually play. Brynhild isn't simply an Immortal portrait or another unit standing in one of our marches. We're seeing her as a character within Norheim, interacting with people and apparently having her own history in this world.

## The Broken Blade and the Shattered Pact

The title itself gives us another mystery.

We know there has been some kind of reunion between Brynhild and the Hangover Knight, but **“The Broken Blade and the Shattered Pact”** suggests there is considerably more history between these characters than Chapter 4 is ready to explain.

The Shattered Pact in particular caught my attention. It could refer to something personal between Brynhild and our hero, but it could just as easily be part of a larger story that hasn't been revealed yet. The same goes for the Broken Blade. There are several ways to interpret the title after watching the episode, but nothing that I think we can confidently call an answer yet.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-133-1024x569.png)

For now, the important part is simply that these two characters have met before. Our hero didn't randomly decide to save somebody from an Arena fight. He recognized Brynhild, helped her and then escaped alongside her, which leaves us with another piece of his past to figure out.

That is now becoming almost as interesting as the mystery surrounding the sacred emblem he picked up in Chapter 3.

## Escaping Beneath Vitas

After the Arena fight, Brynhild and the Hangover Knight are forced to run. They are chased through Vitas, but Brynhild appears to know another way out and leads them into the passages beneath the streets.

This continues something I've really enjoyed about the last two chapters. We're seeing much more of Vitas than the version we know from the world map. Chapter 3 gave us markets, taverns, docks, back alleys and the black market, while Chapter 4 takes us into an underground Arena and then into the tunnels beneath the city.

Brynhild seems comfortable enough down there and appears to know where she is going. Unfortunately, knowing the tunnels doesn't prevent their escape from going rather badly. At some point the two fall. And they don't just fall down into another tunnel. They fall **far**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-134-1024x510.png)

The episode gives us a sense that they are dropping much deeper beneath Vitas than either of them intended, and considering what Chapter 3 told us, that immediately raises an obvious question: have they just fallen into the **Abyss**? We don't know yet. It could be the Abyss, an entrance leading towards it, some ancient underground part of Vitas, or perhaps even a dragon den. The episode doesn't give us an answer before ending.

It does, however, show us what is waiting below.

## Two Dragons — And a Possible Clue to Our Hero?

Shortly after Brynhild and the Hangover Knight disappear into the depths, we are shown **two dragons**. One is blue, the other is green. Those colours are difficult to ignore in *Infinity Kingdom*. Blue represents **Water**, while green represents **Wind**, meaning we appear to be looking at a Water Dragon and a Wind Dragon deep beneath Vitas. One half of that pairing already makes perfect sense: Brynhild is a **Water Immortal**.

She has just fallen into this place alongside our mysterious hero, and the episode immediately shows us Water and Wind Dragons below. That may be nothing more than the elements of the dragons living there, but it could also be a fairly deliberate clue.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-135.png)

We still don't actually know who the Hangover Knight is. Could he perhaps be **Arthur**, a Wind Immortal?

For now, that is only a theory. The series hasn't identified him as Arthur, and there isn't enough evidence yet for us to treat it as anything more than speculation. Still, showing a Water and Wind Dragon immediately after a Water Immortal and an unidentified hero fall into their domain is interesting enough that I think it is worth remembering.

If our Hangover Knight eventually turns out to be a Wind Immortal, the ending of Chapter 4 is going to look considerably less coincidental.

There is another possibility as well. Perhaps the dragons aren't clues about the characters at all and instead tell us something about the place they have fallen into. We know dragons are deeply connected to the elemental system of *Infinity Kingdom*, and finding two of them hidden far beneath Vitas could have its own importance.

Hopefully Chapter 5 will give us a better idea of what we're looking at.

## The Lore Series Is Becoming an Actual Story

Four chapters in, I think we're starting to see what the developers are doing with this series.

Chapters 1 and 2 gave us the background. We learned about Norheim, the Empire of Light, the war with the Dwarves and the events that eventually divided the continent into the three kingdoms players know today.

Chapter 3 was where things really started moving. We entered Vitas, met the Hangover Knight, explored its black market and discovered the mysterious sacred emblem. We were also told that all of this was leading towards the Abyss.

Chapter 4 doesn't stop to explain another piece of Norheim history. Instead, it simply continues that adventure. We meet Brynhild, discover that she and our hero share some kind of past, escape through the tunnels beneath Vitas and end up falling into somewhere neither of them presumably intended to go.

I think that format works much better. We're still learning about Norheim, but we're doing it by travelling through the world with the characters rather than having its history explained to us.

Having Brynhild join the story makes that even more interesting. *Infinity Kingdom* has an enormous collection of historical and legendary Immortals, and there has always been plenty of written material about them individually. What we haven't seen much of is how those Immortals actually fit together as characters living within Norheim.

If Brynhild is the first of several familiar Immortals we're going to meet during this journey, there is a lot the series can do with that.

## My Take on Chapter 4

I enjoyed Chapter 4, although I think the final part of the episode is more interesting than the Arena fight itself.

Seeing Brynhild was a great surprise and immediately connects the story more closely with the game, while her apparent history with the Hangover Knight gives us another mystery surrounding our protagonist. I'm also enjoying the way Vitas continues to be expanded. We've gone from knowing it mainly as the green kingdom on the world map to seeing its streets, black markets, taverns, fighting Arenas and now the tunnels running beneath it.

The ending is what really has me waiting for Chapter 5, though.

Brynhild and our hero have fallen somewhere very deep beneath Vitas. Chapter 3 already told us that we were heading towards the Abyss, so perhaps we have finally arrived. More interestingly, there are a blue and a green dragon waiting somewhere below them.

We know Brynhild represents Water. Could the Wind Dragon be our first real clue that the Hangover Knight is a Wind Immortal, perhaps even Arthur? Or are the dragons telling us something completely different about the place they have just fallen into?

For now, we don't know, and I would rather keep it that way than try to turn every clue into an answer before the series gives us one. Half the fun of following these chapters is spotting the little details and then seeing a week or two later whether we were anywhere close.

One thing is certain: escaping the Arena wasn't the end of Brynhild and the Hangover Knight's problems. Judging by what is waiting underneath Vitas, it may have been the easy part.
