## Introduction

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-101-1024x529.png)

Now that Asteria has returned in patch 2.9.5, the new Immortal Affinity system is starting to make a lot more sense. At first glance the shop looks fairly straightforward, but once you actually test the numbers, you realize the system is less about finding “better” items and more about understanding how the bonus affinity mechanics work for different immortal factions.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-96-1024x540.png)

After going through the values from every gift item in the shop, the biggest thing that stands out is how differently elemental immortals are treated compared to Chaos, Holy, and Shadow immortals. That distinction is probably going to matter quite a bit long term, especially for players investing heavily into premium factions.

---

## How Affinity Gifts Actually Work

Every gift in the Asteria shop gives a base amount of affinity points that can be used on any immortal. Some gifts also provide bonus affinity when used on the correct elemental immortal.

For example, elemental gifts like Abyssal Pearl or Zircon Ring give a smaller base value to everyone, but provide a large additional bonus when matched to the proper element. In practice, that means Water immortals benefit much more from Water-themed gifts, Fire immortals from Fire gifts, and so on.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-97-1024x635.png)

The system is actually cleaner than it first appears. Once the bonus is applied correctly, all properly matched gifts end up landing at essentially the same crystal efficiency.

That was honestly a bit surprising to me. I expected there to be obvious “best value” purchases, but after testing the numbers, the developers seem to have balanced the entire shop around a very consistent ratio.

---

## The Hidden 10-to-1 Efficiency Pattern

Once you calculate the affinity gained per crystal spent, almost every properly used gift lands at roughly the same efficiency.

The universal gifts:

- Spiritfont Nectar
- Starweave Silk
- Letter of Blessing

all convert at 10 affinity per crystal.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-98.png)

*Special thanks to VictorSeven for sharing the above table with us all!*

The elemental gifts do the same thing, but only if they are used on the correct immortals.

For instance, the purple elemental gifts cost 50 crystals and provide a total of 500 affinity when the elemental bonus activates. The blue elemental gifts cost 30 crystals and provide 300 total affinity under the same conditions. Both still end up at the same 10-to-1 conversion rate.

So the system is not really about hunting for mathematically superior items. The optimization comes from using the right gifts on the right immortals and avoiding wasted bonus potential.

---

## Why Elemental Immortals Have an Advantage

This is where the system gets more interesting. Chaos, Holy, and Shadow immortals do not appear to receive any elemental bonus affinity at all. They only gain the base value from elemental gifts.

That creates a pretty massive efficiency gap.

Take a purple elemental gift as an example. A Water immortal receives the full 500 affinity from an Abyssal Pearl, while a Chaos immortal only gets the base 200 points. The crystal cost stays the same either way. That means elemental immortals effectively gain affinity more than twice as efficiently from those items.

The same thing happens with the blue elemental gifts. An elemental immortal receives the full 300 points, while Chaos, Holy, and Shadow only receive 100.

It is pretty clear that elemental gifts are not worth using on non-elemental immortals unless you have absolutely nothing else available. Especially since you want to unlock bonuses by getting as many insignias completed (affinity lvl 30) as possible. More about that in another guide!

---

## The Best Gifts for Chaos, Holy, and Shadow

Because they cannot trigger elemental bonuses, Chaos, Holy, and Shadow immortals are heavily dependent on the universal gifts instead.

That means:

- Spiritfont Nectar
- Starweave Silk
- Letter of Blessing

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-99-1024x626.png)

are currently the best-value affinity items for those factions.

All three maintain the full 10 affinity per crystal ratio regardless of immortal type, which makes them dramatically more efficient than elemental gifts for universal factions.

I suspect this is going to create a long-term bottleneck eventually. Players building Chaos, Holy, or Shadow marches are all pulling from the exact same affinity material pool, while elemental players are spread across separate elemental gift categories.

On larger or older servers especially, I would not be surprised if Nectar and Silk become extremely contested resources later in the patch cycle.

---

## What This Means for Long-Term Progression

Right now the Affinity system still feels new, but the resource economy behind it already tells us quite a lot.

If you mainly run elemental teams, your progression path looks fairly smooth. You can specialize into your own elemental gift pool and maintain strong efficiency throughout.

Chaos, Holy, and Shadow players probably have a rougher road ahead. Since they cannot benefit from elemental bonuses, they are locked almost entirely into universal gifts, which means every player using those factions is competing for the same limited resources.

That does not necessarily make those immortals weaker, but it does make them more expensive to optimize over time.

And honestly, that fits Infinity Kingdom pretty well historically. Premium factions have almost always carried heavier progression costs somewhere in the system.

---

## Final Thoughts

The Affinity system initially looked more complicated than it really is. After testing the numbers, the structure is actually fairly straightforward once you understand how the bonus affinity mechanic works.

At the moment, the most important thing is not finding secret high-value purchases. It is understanding which gifts properly match your immortals and recognizing that Chaos, Holy, and Shadow factions currently operate under very different efficiency rules than elemental teams.

That difference will probably end up shaping long-term account progression much more than people realize during these first few days of the patch.
