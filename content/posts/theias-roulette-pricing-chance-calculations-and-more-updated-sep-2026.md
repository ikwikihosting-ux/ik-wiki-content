Patch 2.9.8 significantly changed the value of **Theia’s Roulette** by adding selectable **Holy and Shadow Dragon Crystal** rewards. The event can still be used to target Holy and Shadow Immortal fragments, but it is now also one of the most direct ways to collect the crystals needed to develop the two premium dragons.

This guide focuses on the new Dragon Crystal wheel: how the rewards work, what the pity system actually does, how many crystals you can expect per spin, and what those crystals cost through the different ticket options.

## 1. What Is Theia’s Roulette?

Theia’s Roulette is a recurring three-day event that currently runs from Thursday through Saturday. Players choose a featured grand prize and spend **Raffle Tickets** to spin the wheel. One free spin is available each day, while additional spins require tickets obtained with Gems or purchased through bundles.

The selectable grand prizes include Holy and Shadow Immortal fragments, Exclusive Artifact fragments, and, following Patch 2.9.8, **350 Holy Dragon Crystals or 350 Shadow Dragon Crystals**.

The reward table changes according to the grand prize you select. The calculations in this article apply specifically when **Holy or Shadow Dragon Crystals** are selected. They should not be used to estimate the fragment or artifact versions of the wheel.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-113-1024x573.png)

Players can make up to 9,999 spins per day, so the daily spin limit will not matter to anyone outside the most extreme spending levels.

## 2. How the Dragon Crystal Wheel Works

When Holy or Shadow Dragon Crystals are selected, the roulette contains 12 reward slots:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-109-1024x160.png)

If you select the Holy Crystal reward, both the 350-crystal jackpot and the five 45-crystal rewards provide Holy Dragon Crystals. Selecting the Shadow version changes those rewards to Shadow Dragon Crystals.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-114.png)

The wheel therefore has an **8.2% displayed chance to award Dragon Crystals on an ordinary spin**. Most spins still produce resource packages, but the short jackpot guarantee greatly improves the long-term crystal return.

## 3. Drop Rates and the 25-Spin Guarantee

The 350-crystal grand prize has a natural drop rate of 3%. Without any protection, a 3% reward would appear once every 33.3 spins on average.

Theia’s Roulette does not make players wait that long. If the grand prize has not appeared earlier, it is guaranteed by the 25th spin. Whenever the 350-crystal reward is won, including an early natural hit, the counter immediately resets and a new 25-spin cycle begins.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-115-1024x193.png)

This is the important correction to the previous guide. The system is not a fixed block in which players make 25 spins and then receive a separate bonus. An early jackpot ends the current cycle immediately. You might win the jackpot on spin five, reset the counter, and win another one several spins later.

With the 3% natural rate and the 25-spin guarantee combined, the average jackpot cycle lasts approximately **17.8 spins**. The effective long-term jackpot rate is therefore much better than the displayed 3% chance alone suggests.

## 4. Expected Dragon Crystals per Spin

The expected return comes from two parts.

### The 350-Crystal Jackpot

The average jackpot cycle is approximately 17.8 spins: **350 ÷ 17.8 = approximately 19.7 Dragon Crystals per spin**

### The Five 45-Crystal Slots

The five smaller crystal slots have a combined drop rate of 5.2%: **45 × 5.2% = 2.34 Dragon Crystals per spin**

### Total Expected Value

Adding the two sources together gives: **19.7 + 2.34 = approximately 22.04 Dragon Crystals per spin**

For practical planning, it is easiest to use a rounded figure of:

> ### Approximately 22 Holy or Shadow Dragon Crystals per Raffle Ticket

This is an average, not a promise. The 25-spin guarantee prevents an extremely long jackpot drought, but results can still vary because early jackpots and the five smaller crystal slots are random.

One recorded 180-spin run produced **3,330 Holy Dragon Crystals**, equal to 18.5 crystals per spin. The theoretical return from 180 spins is approximately 3,967 crystals, so that result was around 16% below the expected average. A different run could finish above the average if several jackpots arrive early.

## 6. Raffle Ticket Prices

Raffle Tickets can be obtained with Gems, through the in-game shop, or through GTArcade bundles.

### Gem Tickets

One ticket costs **1,000 Gems**. The current event offers up to 50 Gem tickets per day, and players also receive one free spin on each of the event’s three days.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-117-1024x526.png)

At the expected rate of 22.04 crystals per ticket: **1,000 Gems = approximately 22 Dragon Crystals**

### Approximately 4,537 Gems = 100 expected Dragon Crystals

### GTArcade Bundles

The three smaller GTArcade packs provide the strongest direct ticket value:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-118-1024x576.png)

> ### $2.27 per 100 Holy or Shadow Dragon Crystals

The $5, $10 and $20 GTArcade packs should therefore be purchased before the larger packs when they are available.

### In-Game Cash Bundles

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/image-112-1024x194.png)

The first three in-game packs cost approximately $1 per spin, twice the price of the equivalent smaller GTArcade packs. At that rate, 100 expected Dragon Crystals cost approximately **$4.54**.

Prices can vary slightly by region, tax and platform, so always check the live shop before purchasing.

## 7. Which Milestones Are Worth Reaching?

The best stopping point depends on whether you mainly want Dragon Crystals or whether the Selection Chest II milestones also matter to your account.

### Free and Low-Spending Players

Always use the three free spins. Beyond that, only spend Gems if you are actively developing the Holy or Shadow Dragon and are comfortable committing a meaningful amount of Gems to it.

The **50-spin milestone** is the first sensible target. It produces approximately 1,100 Dragon Crystals on average and awards 10 Selection Chest II rewards. Because three spins are free, reaching it requires 47 purchased tickets if you complete all three free spins first.

The **100-spin milestone** is also efficient because the additional 50 spins award another 10 selection chests. For players making a controlled investment, 50 or 100 spins are the cleanest stopping points.

### Medium Spenders

The 200-spin milestone provides approximately 4,400 Dragon Crystals and 30 cumulative selection chests. It is a reasonable target when the Dragon Crystals themselves are the priority, but the milestone value becomes weaker after the first 100 spins.

The 350, 500 and 700 milestones each add only 10 selection chests. They are not especially attractive stopping points on milestone value alone. Continue into this range because you need the crystals, not because the reward bar is offering an exceptional bonus.

### Heavy Spenders

If you are already planning to pass 700 spins, the **900-spin milestone** is worth completing. The final 200-spin segment awards 60 Selection Chest II rewards, compared with only 10 at the preceding milestones.

A full 900-spin run produces approximately **19,836 Dragon Crystals** and all 120 selection chests on average. Using the smaller GTArcade packs at roughly $0.50 per ticket, the ticket value is approximately **$450**, before allowing for the three free spins and any tickets bought with Gems.

This does not make 900 spins appropriate for everyone. It simply means that stopping between 700 and 900 is poor milestone planning if you already intend to spend at that level.

## 8. Final Thoughts

Patch 2.9.8 has given Theia’s Roulette a much clearer purpose. Players can now directly target the extremely scarce Holy or Shadow Dragon Crystals instead of relying on less focused reward sources.

The key number is approximately **22 Dragon Crystals per spin**. That makes the smaller GTArcade packs the best cash option at roughly $2.27 per 100 expected crystals, while Gem tickets offer a no-cash route at 1,000 Gems per spin.

For most players, 50 or 100 spins provide the best combination of manageable cost, Dragon Crystal progress and milestone rewards. The middle milestones are mainly worthwhile when the crystals themselves are urgently needed. The 900-spin target is reserved for major investment, but its final 60-chest reward makes it the logical finish for anyone already pushing beyond 700.

Most importantly, choose the Dragon Crystal reward that supports a dragon you are actually building. Theia’s Roulette is much better after the update, but 1,000 Gems or real money spent on the wrong dragon is still wasted progression.
