## Introduction

The 2026 Midsummer Festival follows the familiar structure used by recent Infinity Kingdom seasonal events. Players complete challenges, collect Tokens of Light, exchange event currencies, progress through a battle pass, and spend Eternal Grails in the seasonal shop.

The underlying structure has not changed dramatically. What has changed is the purpose of several major reward tracks.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-45-1024x461.png)

Previous festivals primarily converted participation into broad account resources such as Eternal Grails, Philosopher’s Stones, Soul Crystals, speedups, and castle skins. The Midsummer Festival redirects much of that value toward the new Immortal Outfit system. Athena replaces the traditional Dazzling Wish jackpot, while Alexander fragments is available in addition to the large resource rewards previously found in similar event tabs, e.g. Candy Defenders.

As a result, the event is much more account-specific than earlier festivals. Players using Alexander or Athena may see considerable value. Players using neither immortal lose some of the flexibility that made earlier seasonal events broadly rewarding.

The event accompanies Version 2.9.7, which officially introduced Immortal Outfits and new time-limited activities.

---

## Event Structure

The main festival tabs are:

- Koi Gift Pack
- Summer Passion Battle Pass
- Fishing Contest
- Dazzling Wish
- Challenge Shop
- Grail Shop
- Glorious Challenge
- Pinnacle Trial

Most of these are returning seasonal systems with adjusted rewards. The Fishing Contest is the main themed activity introduced for Midsummer.

---

## Koi Gift Pack

## Structure

The Koi Gift Pack retains the usual low-cost seasonal bundle structure.

The first bundle costs approximately $5 and contains:

- Lucky Koi participation
- Tokens of Light
- Magic Fishing Nets
- Access toward the recurring Koi consolation and grand-prize system

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-47-1024x461.png)

After purchasing the initial bundle, players can unlock an additional bundle costing $10. This provides more Tokens of Light and Magic Fishing Nets.

## What Changed

Previous festivals generally limited players to the standard daily Koi purchase. Midsummer allows additional bundle purchases.

Mechanically, this does not improve the original bundle. It simply increases the amount of event currency available for purchase.

The important point is that buying the additional bundle is not required to qualify for the normal Koi reward structure. Purchasing the regular $5 bundle on the five required days still provides the usual consolation-prize progression and the top grail efficiency purchase available anywhere.

## Player Perspective

For most players, very little has changed.

The initial bundle remains one of the more efficient seasonal purchases for players who already buy it. The extra bundles mainly provide additional stock for heavy spenders who intend to clear the Fishing Contest, make large numbers of Dazzling Wish pulls, or empty substantial portions of the Grail Shop.

Light and medium spenders should not interpret the additional bundles as mandatory event progression. They are simply another way to buy more currency.

## Summer Passion Battle Pass

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-46-1024x461.png)

## Structure

The seasonal battle pass returns with the usual combination of free and paid rewards.

Expected contents include:

- Tokens of Light
- Magic Fishing Nets
- Gems
- Event resources
- Alexander Outfit Fragments

The paid track awards 60 Alexander Outfit Fragments at Level 30.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-59-1024x629.png)

## What Changed

Previous festival passes usually awarded a castle skin or an evolution copy of an existing castle skin.

Midsummer replaces that castle reward with a complete first-stage Alexander outfit unlock.

This is one of the most meaningful changes in the event.

## Player Perspective

For an account using Alexander, the paid pass offers unusually strong value. Players who would normally buy the seasonal pass receive the regular Tokens, Gems, and event resources while also obtaining enough fragments to unlock the outfit directly.

That is considerably more useful than receiving an unwanted castle skin or a minor evolution increase on a skin that rarely sees use.

For players who do not use Alexander, the pass remains a normal event bundle with good currency value, but its headline reward is much less important.

For free-to-play players, the change is less favourable. A castle skin could provide account-wide attributes or collection progress regardless of march composition. Alexander fragments have little immediate value unless the player is actively building him.

## Fishing Contest

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-48-1024x461.png)

## Overview

Fishing Contest is the main Midsummer activity.

Mechanically, it resembles Candy Defenders and similar paired seasonal systems. The presentation has changed from attacking candy creatures or seasonal bosses to catching fish, but the underlying progression loop remains familiar.

Players spend Magic Fishing Nets to participate and may pair with another player. Pairing must be renewed daily, so players should verify that they have an active partner before committing large amounts of currency. That being said, partnering up is not as important as in past events. While getting the Token rewards from the 15 daily levels is great, the main reward here is the personal "Smiley-sharks" cought progression found under My Catch - this awards Alexanders outfit fragments and is the real prize here.

The activity contains two separate reward tracks:

1. Daily fishing-level progression
2. Cumulative My Catch milestones

Understanding the difference between them is important.

---

## Fishing-Level Progression

The daily portion contains 15 levels.

Players spend Magic Fishing Nets to progress through the levels, with rewards improving as they advance. The activity appears to reset daily, including its Token of Light rewards, while cumulative catch progression remains for the duration of the event.

Reaching Level 15 awards only a small number of Alexander Outfit Fragments—reported as three fragments per complete clear.

Clearing all 15 levels each day may still provide Tokens of Light and other useful rewards, but the final level itself is not the main source of Alexander fragments.

---

## My Catch

The real outfit progression sits under **My Catch**.

Fishing produces different catches, including Smiley Sharks. Catching large quantities of the required fish advances cumulative milestones containing substantially more Alexander Outfit Fragments.

This is the equivalent of the deeper reward track seen in previous Candy Defenders iterations. Rather than receiving most of the value from individual attacks, players gradually progress through a larger event-wide milestone ladder.

The difficulty is volume. Players need a very large number of Smiley Sharks, and the outcome is partly affected by fishing results rather than only by a fixed currency threshold.

## What Changed from Candy Defenders

Previous versions of this event type rewarded large quantities of broadly useful resources, including:

- Philosopher’s Stones
- Soul Crystals
- Speedups
- Resource boxes
- General development materials

Midsummer shifts the premium milestones toward Alexander Outfit Fragments.

This makes the reward track narrower but potentially more valuable for the correct account.

A player relying on Alexander may prefer permanent outfit progression over another pile of expendable resources. A player who does not use Alexander will generally find the fishing rewards less compelling than the old Philosopher’s Stone and Soul Crystal packages.

---

## Fishing Strategy

Players should not judge the activity only by the three fragments shown at Level 15.

The main questions are:

- How many Smiley Sharks are obtained per amount of Fishing Nets?
- How far can the account progress through My Catch?
- How many fragments are available at reachable milestones?
- Does the player intend to use Alexander long-term?

For mid-spenders running Alexander, concentrated investment can make sense because the outfit is a permanent combat upgrade. The seasonal pass also supplies the first 60 fragments, allowing fishing progress to build on an outfit that is already unlocked.

For free-to-play players, converting large quantities of Eternal Grails into Fishing Nets is much harder to justify. Grails remain one of the primary routes toward Chaos Immortals and artifacts, while Fishing Nets provide uncertain progress toward one specific immortal.

Until the complete catch rates and milestone requirements are documented, players should avoid assuming that a partial fishing push will reach a meaningful Alexander threshold.

## Dazzling Wish

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-49-1024x461.png)

## Structure

Dazzling Wish still consumes Tokens of Light.

Players make individual draws and receive smaller Eternal Grail rewards, while a pity counter guarantees the featured final reward after enough attempts.

However, both the jackpot and pity structure have changed.

---

## Removal of the 300-Grail Jackpot

Previous seasonal events included a chance to obtain a 300 Eternal Grail jackpot from an individual draw.

That jackpot is no longer part of the standard Midsummer reward table.

The final pity reward has also changed from general Grail value to Athena Outfit Fragments.

This sharply reduces the flexibility of Tokens of Light. Previously, almost every account benefited from converting Tokens into Grails because Grails could be spent on multiple Chaos Immortals, artifacts, and seasonal resources. Athena fragments only benefit players investing in Athena.

---

## New Pity Requirement

The pity threshold has reportedly increased from 300 to 700 draws.

At 700 pulls, players receive 20 Athena Outfit Fragments.

This is a much heavier commitment than previous Dazzling Wish systems. Players should not begin chasing the pity reward unless they understand how many Tokens they can obtain and whether unfinished progress carries into a future event.

---

## Mystery Bonus

Dazzling Wish also includes a new mystery or special reward.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-50.png)

Reported options include:

- A refund of one Token of Light
- A reward of two Tokens of Light
- A Cleopatra crying emoji
- Access to a discounted Token of Light bundle that still requires payment

In practical terms, the mystery reward is extremely weak.

A special draw should normally provide additional progression value. Here, several outcomes either return a negligible amount of currency, award a cosmetic emoji, or merely unlock another purchase opportunity.

The Cleopatra crying emoji is unintentionally appropriate, because it accurately reflects the value of receiving the “special” reward.

---

## Player Perspective

For Athena users, Dazzling Wish now has a clear long-term purpose. The outfit fragments are scarce, and the outfit itself provides meaningful progression.

For everyone else, Dazzling Wish has become substantially worse.

The removal of the 300-Grail jackpot reduces average excitement and broad account value. The increased pity threshold requires far more stored currency, and the featured reward is tied to one immortal.

Players who do not use Athena should strongly consider preserving Tokens of Light for a later seasonal event. Future versions may feature a different immortal, introduce selectable outfit fragments, or restore a more flexible Grail-based reward structure.

Tokens of Light should no longer be spent automatically simply because Dazzling Wish is available.

## Challenge Shop

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-53-1024x461.png)

## Structure

The Challenge Shop follows the established seasonal format.

Players use currency earned from Glorious Challenge to purchase daily and limited-stock rewards.

Important purchases usually include:

- Tokens of Light
- Stardust
- Progression resources
- Event-specific materials

## Strategy

Tokens of Light and Stardust remain the safest general purchases for most accounts.

Beware the shop is on daily stock meaning you need to make sure you buy the items you most need, e.g. Tokens or Stardust or Artifacts each day and make sure you have enough currency to do so. Early days it is worth it buying additional gem-hits on Glorious Challenge to take full advantage of the daily stock resets.

Hard Mode becomes available later in the event and improves the amount of currency that can be earned.

---

## Grail Shop

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-55-1024x441.png)

## Structure

The Grail Shop remains the primary destination for Eternal Grails.

Its established rewards include:

- Chaos Immortal fragments
- Chaos Immortal artifact fragments
- Triss or Token Skill-related progression
- Seasonal materials
- Magic Fishing Nets

The exact Immortal selection depends on server progression and seasonal availability.

## New Fishing Net Exchange

The relevant Midsummer addition is the ability to exchange Eternal Grails for Magic Fishing Nets.

A reported exchange offers:

- 5,000 Magic Fishing Nets
- Cost: 200 Eternal Grails

This connects the traditional Grail economy directly to Fishing Contest progression.

## Opportunity Cost

The exchange is not automatically good or bad. Its value depends on the account.

Buying Fishing Nets means giving up progress toward:

- Chaos Immortal fragments
- Chaos artifacts
- Future discounted Chaos chests
- Other limited Grail Shop rewards

For a committed Alexander player who is close to an important My Catch milestone, purchasing Nets may be reasonable. For a developing account still missing important Chaos Immortals, spending hundreds of Grails on uncertain fishing outcomes is much harder to defend.

The correct comparison is not “Are more Fishing Nets useful?” They obviously are. The correct comparison is whether the expected Alexander fragments are more valuable than the Chaos progression sacrificed to obtain them.

## Glorious Challenge

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-51-1024x461.png)

## Structure

Glorious Challenge remains one of the main sources of seasonal shop currency.

The usual progression pattern is expected:

- Complete rotating objectives
- Earn event currency
- Unlock later difficulty stages
- Spend currency in the Challenge Shop

Hard Mode traditionally becomes available around Day 4 and is the main breakpoint for efficient progression.

## Strategy

During the early days:

- Complete objectives normally
- Buy important daily stock
- Avoid unnecessary spending merely to force low-value tasks
- Preserve resources for Hard Mode requirements

Once Hard Mode opens, players can commit more heavily because later objectives generally provide better event progression.

Unlike the Original Festival and Children’s Day versions, Glorious Challenge is no longer primarily feeding a huge Candy resource engine. Its importance remains substantial, but the broader event value is now more concentrated in Fishing Nets, Tokens of Light, and outfit progression.

## Pinnacle Trial

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-52-1024x461.png)

## Structure

Pinnacle Trial returns as the festival’s staged combat challenge.

Rewards generally include:

- Immortal fragments
- Tokens of Light
- Event resources
- Additional seasonal progression materials

Difficulty increases through successive stages, making march depth and formation quality increasingly important.

## Player Perspective

Pinnacle Trial remains worth pushing as far as the account can manage without wasting large quantities of premium resources.

Tokens of Light are particularly important during Midsummer because they are more difficult to value correctly. Athena players may want every available Token, while non-Athena players can stockpile them for future events.

Even players skipping Dazzling Wish should complete Pinnacle Trial. Saving the Tokens does not make them less valuable.

## Main Changes from Previous Festivals

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-56-1024x680.png)

## Dazzling Wish

Previous events:

- Included a 300-Grail jackpot
- Converted Tokens of Light into broadly useful Grail progression
- Used a much lower 300-draw pity structure

Midsummer:

- Removes the 300-Grail jackpot
- Features Athena Outfit Fragments
- Raises pity to 700 draws
- Adds a poor-value mystery reward

This is the largest negative change for general account progression.

---

## Seasonal Side Activity

Previous events:

- Used Candy Defenders or similar themes
- Awarded large quantities of Stones, Soul Crystals, speedups, and resources

Midsummer:

- Uses Fishing Contest
- Requires Magic Fishing Nets
- Focuses cumulative milestones on Smiley Sharks
- Awards Alexander Outfit Fragments as the premium reward

This is a major improvement for Alexander accounts but a reduction in universal resource value.

---

## Battle Pass

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-57-1024x532.png)

Previous events:

- Awarded a castle skin or skin evolution copy

Midsummer:

- Awards 60 Alexander Outfit Fragments
- Provides a complete initial outfit unlock

For players using Alexander, this is arguably the strongest improvement in the event.

---

## Koi Gift Pack

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-58-1024x561.png)

Previous events:

- Offered the standard limited Koi bundle

Midsummer:

- Retains the original bundle
- Unlocks additional bundles after purchase
- Provides more Tokens and Fishing Nets for players willing to spend further

For ordinary spenders, the practical value is unchanged. Whales simply have more stock available.

## Spending Perspectives

## Free-to-Play

The event is less generous in broadly usable progression materials than earlier festivals.

Best priorities:

- Complete Glorious Challenge
- Push Pinnacle Trial
- Collect free Tokens of Light
- Buy important daily Challenge Shop stock
- Participate in Fishing Contest with free Nets
- Avoid chasing Athena pity without a realistic Token reserve
- Be cautious about exchanging Grails for Fishing Nets

Free-to-play Alexander users can collect fragments gradually, but should not sacrifice core Chaos progression without knowing that a meaningful outfit milestone is reachable.

---

## Light Spenders

The regular Koi Gift Pack remains efficient if it was already part of the player’s seasonal spending.

The paid battle pass becomes highly attractive for Alexander users because the 60-fragment reward provides an immediate unlock.

Additional Koi bundles are optional. They should not be treated as necessary simply because they are available.

---

## Mid-Spenders

Mid-spenders running Alexander are the clearest winners.

The battle pass gives the first outfit level, while Fishing Contest offers a path toward further development. Spending on Nets can be worthwhile when it secures a known My Catch fragment milestone.

Athena users should calculate Dazzling Wish carefully. A 700-draw pity is too large to approach casually, even for a moderate spender.

---

## Heavy Spenders

Heavy spenders can purchase the additional Koi stock, clear daily fishing progression, push My Catch deeply, and pursue Athena fragments through Dazzling Wish.

For these accounts, Midsummer offers a large amount of permanent combat progression.

However, the event does not necessarily provide better general value than previous festivals. It simply provides more purchasable stock and more character-specific progression.

---

## Player Perspective

The Midsummer Festival looks familiar when viewed from the main event screen, but its reward philosophy has changed considerably.

The Original Festival was memorable because Candy Defenders produced enormous piles of resources that helped almost every account. Children’s Day made the same system more expensive, but Stones, Soul Crystals, and speedups still had universal value.

Midsummer replaces much of that flexible progression with Alexander and Athena fragments.

For an Alexander player, the trade can be excellent. The battle pass alone provides a far more meaningful headline reward than many previous castle skins, and Fishing Contest gives mid-spenders a realistic reason to invest beyond the pass.

For an account using neither featured immortal, the event is notably weaker. Dazzling Wish has lost its 300-Grail jackpot, the new mystery reward is poor, and Fishing Contest no longer showers the account with the same quantity of universal development materials.

The event is therefore not simply better or worse than previous festivals. It is more specialized.

---

## Conclusion Summary

The 2026 Midsummer Festival retains the standard seasonal framework but redirects its most valuable rewards toward Immortal Outfits.

The key points are:

- Koi Gift Pack now offers additional purchasable stock, mainly benefiting heavy spenders.
- Summer Passion replaces the usual castle skin with 60 Alexander Outfit Fragments.
- Fishing Contest functions like Candy Defenders with a fishing theme.
- Smiley Shark milestones are the main source of Alexander fragments.
- The daily Level 15 fishing reward is much less important than My Catch progression.
- Dazzling Wish no longer contains the 300-Grail jackpot.
- Athena fragments replace the old flexible jackpot progression.
- The pity requirement has increased from 300 to 700.
- The new mystery bonus offers extremely poor value.
- Eternal Grails can be exchanged for Fishing Nets, creating a direct choice between Alexander and Chaos progression.
- Glorious Challenge, Pinnacle Trial, Challenge Shop, and the usual Token economy remain important.

For Alexander users, particularly mid-spenders, this can be one of the more valuable seasonal passes and side events released so far. For players without Alexander or Athena, the correct approach is more conservative: collect the general rewards, preserve flexible currencies, and avoid being pulled into expensive outfit tracks that do not support the account’s actual marches.
