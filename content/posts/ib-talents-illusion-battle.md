## Talents

You want to swap into your combat talent set for Illusion Battlefield matches.

There are two very important talents you must make sure you have on for any IB match: **Riding Fast + Working Hard.**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Skaermbillede-2022-02-23-215028.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Skaermbillede-2022-02-23-215010.png)

You want both of these because you want to quickly move around the map, speed is extreemly important both for your attacks and for your relocations. Moreover, with Working Hard you can strategize your first relocation and [cancel enemy relos right out of the gates!](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/pvp/illusion-battlefield-ib/combat-strategies-tips-n-tricks-to-win/)
