For players wondering whether Tycoon’s Club had replaced the usual monthly seasonal, the additions are encouraging. The collection entries point to several familiar festival activities alongside the new Witch Forest event, making a **full seasonal lineup look increasingly likely**.

Ghost Fiesta itself was already named in the official 3.0.0 patch notes. What today’s additions help reveal is the festival’s activity lineup, while a Discord reply from IKVIP points to **Thursday, October 22** as a possible start date. The complete schedule and reward routes still need confirmation.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261020-190036-crmu0h.png)

## What Today’s Update Reveals

The clearest information comes from the new Ghost Fiesta entries in the Festival collection. Their acquisition descriptions identify several activities directly.

These descriptions give us more to work with than the artwork alone. **Pinnacle Trial, Dazzling Wish, Alchemical Secrets and Witch Forest** are all named as sources within the festival collection.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261020-190029-sdy0w1.png)

The Halloween presentation is equally clear. Pumpkins, ghostly imagery, eerie backgrounds and the Night of Terror outfits all fit the Ghost Fiesta theme.

## A Full Seasonal Alongside Tycoon’s Club?

The main question this month has been whether Tycoon’s Club would take the place of the traditional seasonal festival. The new entries strongly suggest that October will still receive a broader event lineup.

Pinnacle Trial and Dazzling Wish are particularly useful clues because they fit the familiar seasonal format: a festival built from several activities, progression rewards and rankings rather than a single event tab.

A **battle pass, Glorious and an event exchange shop** would also fit that pattern. However, those remain expectations based on previous seasonals; the insignias listed above do not confirm every part of the usual structure.

Taken together, the additions point to Ghost Fiesta as October’s main Halloween seasonal, with familiar components and at least one new activity.

## Alchemical Secrets Looks Set to Return

The Alchemists insignia explicitly lists **Alchemical Secrets** as its acquisition source, pointing to the return of the gem-alchemy activity.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261020-190046-w3tlys.png)

Its presentation matches the version seen during September’s Pirate Festival. That suggests a familiar format, although the Halloween edition’s costs, rewards and limits still need to be checked once the event opens.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261020-190106-qpxyu4.png)

For players who participated last month, Alchemical Secrets should therefore be one of the more recognisable parts of the upcoming festival. The important detail will be whether its value remains the same.

## Witch Forest Brings a New Board Event

Witch Forest is the most distinctive addition to the lineup, and its basic gameplay has already been outlined in the [official 3.0.0 notes](https://ik-wiki.com/posts/patch-3-0-0-jadwiga-gilded-club-and-ghost-fiesta-arrive?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

Players use **Magic Wands** to move around a board, landing on spaces that award resources, generate candy or trigger special events. Candy contributes points toward progression chests, while special effects can increase rewards or grant another move.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261020-190216-t9lmxo.png)

That establishes Witch Forest as a board activity. Whether it takes over an outfit reward previously associated with Fishing Contest is a separate question that the available information does not settle.

The new insignia confirms its place in the Ghost Fiesta collection. Its reward track and any connection to Louis IX’s outfit remain details to watch when the event becomes available.

## Fu Fei and Louis IX Receive Night of Terror Outfits

The update also brings the Halloween appearances for **Fu Fei and Louis IX** into view. Both belong to the **Night of Terror** series, which the official patch notes link to Ghost Fiesta.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261020-190153-0e72h6.png)

The individual acquisition routes are less certain. Dazzling Wish is the most obvious candidate for Fu Fei, following the route previously used for Athena’s outfit. However, an insignia tied to Dazzling Wish rankings does not by itself establish where her outfit fragments will be available.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261020-190203-gbtsm5.png)

Louis IX’s outfit may be distributed through a separate festival activity. Witch Forest is a possible candidate, but we should wait for the reward lists before treating it as the replacement for Fishing Contest.

The distinction matters for anyone planning their event spending: the featured outfits are known, while their exact reward sources and development costs still need confirmation.

## Could Ghost Fiesta Begin on October 22?

A Discord reply from IKVIP indicates that the **“Holy Grail Event” will begin on Thursday, October 22**. That makes October 22 a plausible date for the seasonal launch, especially with the related content now appearing in-game.

However, the reply uses the Holy Grail Event name. It does not on its own establish the full Ghost Fiesta timetable or confirm that every activity begins on the same day.

A launch on October 22 would place the festival comfortably within the Halloween period. Exact activity durations and the closing date of any exchange shop should be taken from the event timers once they appear.

For now, **October 22 is a date to watch**, rather than an officially established full festival schedule.

## What We Can Expect

Today’s additions make a full Halloween seasonal look much more likely, even with Tycoon’s Club already running this month. The collection entries point to Pinnacle Trial, Dazzling Wish, Alchemical Secrets and Witch Forest, while Fu Fei and Louis IX provide the featured Halloween outfits.

The remaining questions are the complete schedule, the outfit reward routes and whether the usual battle pass and Glorious activities return alongside them. Ghost Fiesta is taking shape; the next step is seeing how its rewards and costs compare with previous seasonal events.
