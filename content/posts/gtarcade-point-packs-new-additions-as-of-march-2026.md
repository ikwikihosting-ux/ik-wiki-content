### Introduction

The latest update to the GTarcade point shop adds a mix of free rewards, low-cost bundles, and a few higher-tier options that are actually worth a closer look. On the surface, it’s a standard refresh—but when you break it down in terms of in-game value and resource scarcity, a few of these stand out more than they first appear.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-109-1024x572.png)

I’ll go through them the same way I evaluate any bundle: looking at long-term usefulness, opportunity cost, and how they fit into actual progression.

---

## Free Bundles – Quietly High Value

The addition of new free daily, weekly, and monthly bundles is easily the most impactful part of this update.

### Daily Free Bundle

- Gems ×40
- Heart of Norheim ×1
- Small SP Recovery ×1
- Small AP Recovery ×1

Individually small, but over time this adds up. The Heart of Norheim is the key item here—those are always useful, especially for players still stabilizing immortal progression.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-110.png)

### Weekly Free Bundle

- Gems ×100
- 30m Speedup ×4
- March Speedup ×1
- Troop Recall ×1

The **Troop Recall** is the standout. These are hard to stockpile and incredibly valuable in both PvP and event play. Having a consistent weekly source, even at low volume, is a meaningful upgrade.

### Monthly Free Bundle

- Gems ×500
- 4h Protection Barrier ×1

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-111.png)

Simple but effective. The barrier alone justifies the claim, especially during active kingdom conflict periods.

## Low-Cost Point Packs

### IK Mini Pack (1000 points) - the gem bundle!

- Gems ×400
- Superior EXP Rolls ×6
- Market Orders ×6

#### Point-per-Gem Efficiency Overview

Looking strictly at **points per gem**, the IK Mini Pack stands out as the only bundle with strong raw efficiency at **2.5 points per gem**, while all other paid bundles sit at a much weaker **10 points per gem baseline**. This creates a clear divide: the Mini Pack is efficient if you only care about gems, whereas the 5k and 10k bundles are effectively charging a premium where the **real value must come from the extra items**, not the gems themselves. In practice, this means you should never justify the higher-tier packs based on gem value alone—they only make sense if you actively need things like Kingdom Contracts, Alchemy Stones, or Stardust. Otherwise, you’re overspending points for what is, at its core, a worse gem conversion rate.

#### Point-per-Gem Table

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-112.png)

Obviously the free bundles are best value... but the mini pack for just 1000 points giving 400 gems with a daily stock of 1 is a very good way to efficiently earn Gems every day!

### Lite Pack (5000 points) - skipable

- Gems ×500
- 3h Speedups ×5
- 8h Enhanced Gathering ×5
- Superior EXP Rolls ×5

These look fine on paper, but the issue is efficiency. You can get similar or better gem conversion rates from other point bundles that include more impactful items.

From experience, these are the kinds of packs you end up buying early and later regret once you realize how tight points become over time.

## Mid-Tier Packs

### Basic Pack (10,000 points)

- Gems ×1000
- Alchemy Stone ×5
- Territorial Teleport ×5
- Kingdom Contract ×1

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-113.png)

This one is genuinely useful.

- **Kingdom Contract** alone replaces a 3000 gem cost
- **Alchemy Stones** without using Cinders is significant for many players
- Teleports are always relevant in active kingdoms

If you’re planning a kingdom transfer or actively progressing alchemy, this is one of the better uses of points.

---

### Plus Pack (10,000 points)

- Gems ×1000
- Ultimate EXP Rolls ×10
- Stardust ×10

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-114.png)

This one is more situational.

The **Stardust** is the key factor—it’s one of the few consistent ways to obtain it outside limited events. That said, 10 Stardust for 10k points is not particularly efficient unless you’re specifically bottlenecked there.

In most cases, I would only take this if Stardust is actively slowing your progress.

## High-Tier Pack – Long-Term Investment

### Shadow & Holy Dragon Crystal Bundles (High-Tier Situational)

These bundles follow a very different value logic compared to the rest of the shop:

- Dragon Essence ×15
- Shadow *or* Holy Dragon Crystals ×500
- Epic Immortal Spirit Chest ×3
- Market Orders ×100

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-117.png)

On paper, these have **poor general efficiency**, especially since there are **no gems included at all**. If you evaluate them the same way as standard bundles, they fall behind immediately.

However, the moment you are actively progressing a **Holy or Shadow Dragon**, the equation changes completely.

Dragon Crystals—especially for these two elements—are one of the more restrictive progression materials. You don’t get them in meaningful quantities from regular play, and farming them passively is slow. Getting **500 crystals in a single purchase** is a substantial acceleration.

From experience, once you reach the stage where your dragon development starts gating your march performance, these kinds of bundles become one of the few ways to push forward at a reasonable pace.

If you are **not** building a Holy or Shadow Dragon, this bundle has almost no practical value and should be skipped without hesitation.

---

### IK 10 Pack (50,000 points)

- Equipment Forging Stone Random Chest ×20

This is where things become more context-dependent.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-115.png)

In early and mid game, this won’t feel impactful. But once you reach **Legendary Seasons**, forging materials become a real bottleneck.

I’ve run into this myself—gear progression slows down not because of design, but because you simply can’t generate enough forging materials consistently.

This bundle helps offset that.

## Cosmetic Bundles – Purely Optional

A range of new profile frames has been added. These are expensive and offer no gameplay advantage.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-116-1024x542.png)

If cosmetics matter to you, they’re there. Otherwise, they’re easy to ignore without missing anything meaningful.

## Player Perspective

From a long-term standpoint, the real winners here are:

- The **free bundles** (especially weekly troop recall)
- The **Basic 10k pack** (practical and flexible value)
- The **50k forging chest pack** (late-game relevance)

The lower-cost packs are tempting, but they don’t hold up when you compare them against how limited GTarcade points actually are over time.

For instance, when I started prioritizing long-term progression—alchemy, gear, and mobility—I found myself consistently skipping the smaller packs. The immediate gain just didn’t justify the opportunity cost later.

---

## Conclusion Summary

This update is stronger than it first appears, mainly because of the added free rewards and a few well-placed mid/high-tier bundles.

- **Always claim the free bundles** – they add up significantly
- **Prioritize the 10k Basic Pack** for practical utility
- **Consider the 50k forging pack** if you’re entering Legendary Seasons
- **Skip most low-cost packs** unless you have excess points

In short, the update rewards patience. The best value comes from saving points and spending them where progression bottlenecks actually exist.
