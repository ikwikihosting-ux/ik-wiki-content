Upgrading your barracks level requires specific buildings to have been constructed first. Moreover, the ressources needed for the different barrack buildings are in sum the same, but the distribution across rss types is very different. e.g. Spears rely on more iron, and Bows on more wood.

**The list below is a comprehensive list of all requirements for cavalry barracks and every level.**

Cavalry barracks always **require**you to have **Smeltery**leveled up.
