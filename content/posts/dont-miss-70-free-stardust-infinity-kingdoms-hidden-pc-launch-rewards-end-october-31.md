To celebrate the GTArcade PC Launch, players can collect additional daily rewards, including **70 Stardust, 70 hours of Speedups, 60 Random Immortal Fragments, and several other useful freebies**.

And considering how difficult Stardust is to obtain in Infinity Kingdom, this is one of those opportunities you really don't want to miss!

The event runs from **October 21 to October 31, 2026**, so the deadline is here. Players who have already participated should make sure to collect any outstanding rewards before it ends.

## 70 Stardust Just for Logging In!

The most interesting part of the event is the GTArcade Desktop Exclusive Daily Benefits.

Players who log in through the GTArcade desktop client can claim rewards across seven daily check-ins.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261031-204949-lyg8ih.png)

Here's what you get for completing the entire reward track:

That's 70 Stardust, 70 hours of Speedups, and 60 Immortal fragments without spending any money!

The Stardust is particularly valuable. Players upgrading their Immortal Portraits already know how difficult this resource is to collect, especially at higher portrait levels where upgrade costs become increasingly expensive.

While 70 Stardust might not sound like much compared with the thousands required to fully upgrade a portrait, it's still a meaningful amount of an otherwise scarce resource. And unlike many seasonal rewards, collecting it doesn't require competing in rankings or purchasing expensive bundles.

You simply need to log in through GTArcade Desktop and claim the available daily rewards.

**Important:** This is a seven-day check-in track, not an instant 70-Stardust gift. With the event ending October 31, players starting today should not expect to complete all seven days, but those who have already participated should collect everything they've unlocked.

## More Free Rewards for Everyone

There's also a separate three-day Daily Sign-In Event that isn't restricted to desktop players.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261031-204942-t0xybj.png)

All eligible players can visit the event page and claim:

* **Day 1:** 3 Major SP Recovery Potions
* **Day 2:** 3 Major AP Recovery Potions
* **Day 3:** 5 Theia's Roulette Raffle Tickets

These are useful extras, particularly the Raffle Tickets, which provide additional free chances at Theia's Roulette.

Unlike the seven-day desktop rewards, these can be claimed through the promotional event page without needing to play Infinity Kingdom on PC.

## Extra Rewards for Inviting Friends

The event also includes invitation rewards for players who bring friends to the GTArcade desktop client.

Successful invitations unlock additional milestones, including Gems, teleport items, and Dragon Essences.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261031-205110-aqb1xa.png)

Players completing all three invitation milestones can receive up to **1,100 Gems** alongside the other rewards.

There's also a Newbie Gift Pack for eligible newcomers, including additional resources and progression items.

These rewards are less universally accessible than the daily check-ins, since they require eligible friends to complete the invitation process, but they're worth checking if you know other players interested in trying the desktop version.

## A Chance to Win 2,000 GT Points

There's one more bonus for players who have completed all seven desktop check-ins.

GTArcade is running a separate Halloween giveaway in which **20 players will each win 2,000 GT Points**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261031-205003-cnou9b.png)

To participate, players must submit a screenshot showing their completed seven-day desktop check-in record in the comments of the official giveaway announcement.

Completing the check-ins alone does not automatically enter you into the giveaway.

You can find the official giveaway announcement here:

<https://forum.gtarcade.com/topic/262511>

## How to Claim the Rewards

Visit the official PC Launch event page:

**<https://event.gtarcade.com/micro_invite3/ik2026_microinvite>**

Log in with your Infinity Kingdom account and check the available reward sections.

The regular Daily Sign-In rewards can be claimed through the event page. For the seven-day exclusive track, you'll need GTArcade Desktop installed and must access the event through the desktop client.

If you've already been using the PC client during October, check whether you have any outstanding rewards to claim.

## Final Thoughts – Don't Leave Free Stardust Behind!

Infinity Kingdom frequently distributes free Gems, Speedups, and resources through events, but **Stardust is much harder to come by**.

That's why these PC Launch rewards are worth highlighting separately from Ghost Fiesta.

A complete seven-day check-in provides 70 Stardust, 70 hours of Speedups, and 60 Random Immortal Fragments. Those are genuinely useful rewards for something requiring very little effort.

With the event scheduled to end on **October 31**, this is your final reminder to claim whatever you've already unlocked and enter the giveaway if you've completed all seven check-ins.

Don't leave valuable Stardust unclaimed!
