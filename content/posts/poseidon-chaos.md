### Introduction

Poseidon sits in an unusual position in the current Infinity Kingdom landscape. As a **Chaos-element spearmen immortal**, he adapts to any elemental lineup while benefiting from a troop type that many players underinvest in during early and mid progression.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-85.png)

While most players are not naturally building into Spearmen, the meta has quietly shifted in a way that favors him as more cavalry-centered builds—especially those revolving around Alexander—are becoming more common again, and spearmen naturally counter cavalry. If you prepare for Poseidon early in seasons or reach a point where your tech is broadly developed, he becomes one of the most threatening damage dealers available.

---

### Ultimate Ability – Giant Wave

### Official description:

> Inflicts Physical Damage (400%) to all enemies, and has a 50% chance of Biting them, inflicting physical damage (240%) every 3s for 9s. Bitten targets are unable to gain shields.

#### Damage Breakdown

- Base AoE hit: **400%**
- DoT (Bite): **240% × 3 ticks = 720% per target**
- Total potential per cast (5 targets): **3760%**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-86-1024x480.png)

This places Poseidon among the **highest raw ultimate damage dealers in the game**.

From testing and compiled data:

- **Ultimate value ranking:** ~Top 4 (realistically Top 2–3)
- **Base DPS ranking:** ~10th
- **Base energy regen:** 53 (very low)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-87-1024x265.png)

The discrepancy is important. His raw output is elite, but his **low energy regen limits frequency**, which is why builds that accelerate his casting cycle dramatically increase his effectiveness.

---

### Energy Scaling & Skill Synergies

Poseidon is one of the clearest examples of an immortal who benefits disproportionately from energy acceleration.

- **Energy Master value:** Disregard, Poseidon unable to wield it as a frontline attack immortal.
- **Corrosive Power value:** 752%

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-95.png)

These are among the highest returns in the game and directly tied to:

1. High ultimate scaling
2. Poor base regen

#### Why This Matters

When you increase Poseidon’s cast frequency:

- You’re not just increasing uptime—you’re multiplying one of the strongest ultimates in the game.
- This is why he dominates environments like Open Arena where optimized skill setups are common.

From experience, once you give him consistent access to faster ult cycles, the difference is immediate. In one of my own test setups, shifting from a standard DPS configuration to a Corrosive Power-focused build increased total damage output by a noticeable margin even before artifact optimization.

---

### Utility – Anti-Shield Mechanic

The most understated part of Poseidon’s kit is this line:

> *“Bitten targets are unable to gain shields.”*

This is extremely impactful in the current meta:

- Hard counters **shield-based sustain**
- Disrupts **dual tank setups**
- Weakens **Charles-centered compositions**
- Combines well with **Wound effects** (Qin / Khan)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-89-1024x346.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-90-1024x483.png)

#### Practical Impact

When paired with Wound application:

- No shields
- Reduced or nullified healing

This effectively **turns off sustain mechanics entirely**, forcing fights into pure damage races—where Poseidon excels.

---

### Core Weakness – Nine Tails Counter

Poseidon’s viability is directly tied to one major problem: **Nine Tails**.

Her ultimate:

- Removes **Bite**
- Removes **Serious Wound**
- Removes **all DoTs**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-92-1024x626.png)

#### What This Means

Against Nine Tails:

- No DoT damage
- No shield denial
- Reduced overall output to just base hit
- Low regen becomes a real liability

In practice, this doesn’t just weaken Poseidon—it **invalidates his core design**.

After her recent buffs, Nine Tails has re-entered the meta in a meaningful way. You will encounter her regularly in competitive environments, especially in main marches.

---

### Strategic Role – Where Poseidon Fits

Given his strengths and hard counter, Poseidon is best positioned as:

#### 1. Open Arena Specialist

- Optimized energy builds
- High uptime on ultimate
- Punishes non–Nine Tails teams heavily

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-91-1024x607.png)

#### 2. Second-Team Carry (Recommended)

This is where he shines the most strategically.

### Reasoning:

- Forces opponent to commit Nine Tails to counter him
- Most players run Nine Tails in their **main team**
- Your Poseidon team:  - Destroys secondary teams
- Loses predictably to main
- Your main team:  - Fights without facing Nine Tails

This sequencing advantage is extremely valuable in multi-march formats.

From experience, running Poseidon as a second march creates a consistent pattern: clean wins across weaker lines, followed by a controlled loss into the enemy’s main—setting up favorable main vs main engagements.

### Unique Artifact & Build Priorities

#### Artifact Effect

- Increases Bite chance to **75%**

This is **essential**, not optional. All meaningful damage calculations assume this is active.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-94-1024x694.png)

#### Recommended Stats

- **Primary:** Physical Attack %
- **Secondary:**  - Physical Attack
- Surge (high priority)
- Crit (secondary consideration)

#### Why Surge Matters

Poseidon’s entire kit revolves around ultimate casts. Surge directly increases:

- Frequency
- Consistency of damage output

### Skill Synergy – Building Around Energy and DoT Scaling

Poseidon’s skill setup is very straightforward once you understand his limitation: **low base energy regen**. Everything revolves around compensating for that while amplifying the value of his already high-impact ultimate.

#### Core Foundation: Corrosive Power

If you’re building Poseidon properly, **Corrosive Power is non-negotiable**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-96.png)

- Offsets low energy regen through additional triggers
- Scales extremely well due to his high ultimate value
- Provides consistent supplemental damage tied to skill usage

In practice, Corrosive doesn’t just “help”—it stabilizes his entire damage profile. Without it, his output becomes too dependent on slow natural casts.

---

#### Primary Damage Synergy: Executioner

Executioner pairs naturally with Poseidon because of how his kit applies damage over time.

- Amplifies **DoT ticks from Bite**
- Converts sustained damage into meaningful burst over time
- Synergizes especially well when Bite uptime is high (artifact assumed)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-97.png)

From testing, this combination is where Poseidon starts to feel oppressive against teams lacking cleanse. The longer fights go, the more value Executioner extracts from each Bite application.

---

#### Flexible Third Slot (Epic Builds)

This slot depends on what you need in the matchup:

- **Fighting Master**  - Adds **control resistance**
- Useful into stun-heavy or control-based teams
- Stabilizes consistency in real fights
- **Blade Vortex**  - Adds **additional DoT instances**
- Increases Executioner value
- Best when committing fully to damage-over-time stacking
- **Time to Shine**  - Provides **damage amplification**
- Strongest in shorter fights or burst-oriented setups
- Less consistent than Vortex, but higher ceiling

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-98.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-99.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-100.png)

There isn’t a single correct choice here. It depends on whether you need reliability, scaling, or raw damage amplification.

---

#### Legendary Skill Options

Once you have access to legendaries, Poseidon scales even harder.

- **Last Stand**  - Excellent survivability and damage timing
- Keeps Poseidon active long enough to cycle more ultimates
- **Attack & Defense**  - One of the most efficient stat-scaling skills for him
- Improves both output and durability without complicating the build
- **Drastic Action**  - Adds **damage + healing reduction**
- Particularly valuable if you’re *not* pairing him with Qin or Khan
- Helps maintain pressure against sustain teams

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-103.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-101.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-102.png)

From experience, Last Stand and Attack & Defense are the most universally reliable. Drastic Action becomes situationally excellent depending on your overall team composition.

---

### Conclusion Summary

Poseidon remains one of the **highest-impact damage immortals in the game**, defined by:

- Exceptional ultimate scaling
- Strong synergy with energy acceleration
- Unique and powerful anti-shield utility

However:

- He is **hard-countered by Nine Tails**
- This limits his viability as a main march centerpiece

### Best use case:

- Open Arena dominance
- Second-team carry in multi-march formats

If built correctly and positioned strategically, Poseidon is not just viable—he’s one of the most efficient ways to dismantle non–Nine Tails compositions in the current meta.
