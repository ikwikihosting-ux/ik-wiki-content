## Time to Shine – Skill Guide & Analysis (Infinity Kingdom)

The Tower of Knowledge continues to add skills that push the boundaries of what's possible for certain marches, and **Time to Shine** is one of the strongest offensive additions we've seen in quite a while.

Unlike many skills that require specific triggers or complicated interactions, Time to Shine is incredibly straightforward: it dramatically increases both your **Critical Rate** and **Physical Damage**. Those two stats happen to scale extremely well together, which makes the skill far more impactful than its simple description might suggest.

Let's take a closer look at where it shines, which Immortals benefit the most, and whether it's worth investing in.

---

## Skill Overview

**Type:** Passive (Tower of Knowledge)

### Can be equipped on:

- Physical Attack Immortals
- Ranged Physical DPS Immortals

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-88.png)

At maximum level the skill grants:

- **+35% Critical Rate**
- **+30% Physical Damage**

There are no activation conditions, no cooldowns and no RNG involved. The bonuses are active for the entire battle.

That reliability is a huge part of what makes the skill so valuable.

---

## Why This Skill Is So Strong

On paper, +35% Critical Rate already sounds impressive.

The important part is that it comes together with **30% Physical Damage**, meaning the skill boosts both:

- How often you critically hit.
- How hard every physical attack hits.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-89-1024x602.png)

Those two multipliers stack exceptionally well together and given how ranged physical damage dealers rely on landing critical hits by stacking crit damage through artifact bonus Annihilation and crit rate through talents. Time to Shine fits perfectly with that.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-90-1024x335.png)

Instead of increasing only consistency or only damage, Time to Shine increases both at the same time. For physical damage dealers that already rely on critical hits, this creates a very noticeable jump in overall DPS.

---

## Best Users

## Seon (Best-in-Slot)

If there is one Immortal that seems almost designed for Time to Shine, it's **Seon**.

Fire has always revolved around critical hits. Seon's passive and **Might of the Phoenix** reward frequent critical strikes, so increasing her Critical Rate directly translates into more damage output and more reliable burst windows. Because Fire already stacks Critical Rate and Critical Damage through multiple sources, adding another **35% Critical Rate** pushes her consistency to an entirely different level. For many Fire players, this is likely to become one of her core skills.

---

## Wukong

Wukong is another interesting candidate.

Although he isn't currently considered a meta-defining Immortal, Time to Shine gives him exactly the stats he wants:

- More critical hits
- More physical damage
- Better Blade Vortex damage

Players experimenting with alternative physical marches may find that this skill helps close the gap between Wukong and the more established damage dealers.

---

## Alexander

Alexander can also make reasonable use of the skill, particularly in physical-focused compositions.

He's probably not the first Immortal you'd prioritise, but if he's serving as your primary physical damage dealer, Time to Shine remains a solid upgrade.

---

## Skill Synergies

Where Time to Shine becomes especially interesting is when paired with other offensive Tower of Knowledge skills.

## Corrosive Power / Energy Mastery

Corrosive Power and/or Energy Mastery generates additional energy upon casting your ultimate ability, as a backline relying on ultimate damage you want crit and energy - Time to Shine and regen!

The result is both higher sustained damage and better Ultimate uptime.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-92.png)

---

## Unyielding

This may actually end up being the preferred combination for many e.g. Seon builds. Unyielding already provides tremendous offensive value, and combining it with Time to Shine creates a damage dealer that hits harder while critting far more consistently.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-91.png)

Rather than relying on occasional burst windows, your damage output stays threatening throughout the fight.

---

## Open Arena Testing

Testing was primarily performed in Open Arena using Fire marches centred around Seon. While the old hyper-regen Seon builds are no longer possible (since they nerfed her ultimate to no longer count as ultimate damage towards corrosive power), the principle still stands.

Results consistently showed:

- Noticeably higher damage output
- More reliable critical hits
- Strong overall performance against multiple meta marches

However, it's important not to overreact to Arena testing alone.

Open Arena contains a large amount of randomness:

- Critical hits
- Dodge chances
- Crowd control timing
- Skill targeting

Two identical fights can produce very different outcomes simply because one Zeon landed several critical hits while another didn't.

For that reason, Open Arena should be viewed as an indication rather than definitive proof of a skill's strength.

The underlying mechanics, however, are easy to evaluate—and Time to Shine's raw stat bonuses are undeniably powerful.

---

## Is It Worth Getting?

For physical-focused players, the answer is **yes**.

Few passive skills provide such a large amount of universally useful offensive stats.

The fact that the bonuses are permanent throughout combat makes it even more attractive than many conditional damage skills.

Priority should roughly look like this:

1. **Fire (Seon/Gilga)** – Outstanding.
2. **Physical Backline DPS marches** **(e.g. Khan)**– Excellent.
3. **Alternative physical builds (Wukong, Alexander, etc.)** – Decently strong but many rival skills on-par or above depending on builds.
4. **Frontline bruisers** – Lower priority unless they're your primary damage source.

---

## Final Verdict

Time to Shine is one of the better offensive Tower of Knowledge skills released so far.

It doesn't introduce complicated mechanics or require precise timing. Instead, it simply improves two of the most valuable offensive stats for physical damage dealers and lets them do what they already do—only much better.

Fire benefits the most, particularly Zeon, who can take full advantage of both increased Critical Rate and Physical Damage. Pairing it with skills like Corrosive Power or Unyielding creates one of the strongest offensive setups currently available for physical marches.

If your account relies heavily on physical damage, this is a skill you'll almost certainly want to pick up once it becomes available. It may not completely redefine the meta on its own, but it gives physical carries exactly what they were looking for: more consistent critical hits, higher sustained damage, and a very noticeable increase in overall combat performance.
