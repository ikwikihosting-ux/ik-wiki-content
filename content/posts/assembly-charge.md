## Introduction

Assembly Charge is one of those KvK skills that looks inconsistent at first glance, but performs reliably once you understand how it interacts with normal attacks. It isn’t about single big bursts—it’s about stacking value over time through repeated triggers.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-104-1024x376.png)

If you’re running physical DPS, especially attack-speed-based setups, this skill can quietly become one of your top damage contributors.

---

## Skill Overview and Acquisition

### Where to Get Assembly Charge

- **KvK Shop** (priority source — grab it when available from Conquest and onwards)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-105.png)

If you’re planning to build physical DPS marches long-term, it’s worth securing this skill early.

---

### Skill Effect Breakdown

- **Trigger Condition**: 60% chance on *each normal attack*
- **Effect**:  - Deals physical damage to the target + 2 nearby enemies
- Grants **+10% physical damage** for 6 seconds

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-106.png)

**Key Insight:**
The 60% chance is deceptive. It applies per normal attack, so heroes with high attack frequency effectively “roll the dice” many times, resulting in consistent uptime and damage.

---

## Best Users and Core Synergy

### Primary User: Alexander

Assembly Charge fits naturally on Alexander because:

- His kit revolves around frequent normal attacks
- He doesn’t rely on long cooldown skills
- He benefits directly from sustained physical damage scaling

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-107-1024x590.png)

In practice, when I tested this in multiple KvK fights, Assembly Charge consistently landed **top 1–2 damage contribution** on Alexander, especially in longer engagements.

---

### Alternative Users

- **Hannibal** (if sufficiently upgraded)  - Similar normal attack reliance
- Works well in secondary or experimental marches

More broadly:

- Any **physical DPS hero with high attack frequency** can use it effectively
- It’s less effective on burst or skill-dependent heroes

---

## Recommended Combinations

### Normal Attack Trigger-Based DPS Build (High Consistency)

- Assembly Charge
- Berserk
- Wind Rage

### Why it works:

- **Berserk** increases attack speed (more trigger chances)
- **Wind Rage** adds additional normal attack triggers
- Assembly Charge benefits from both → more procs, more AoE damage

### Getting into legendary skills:

- Swap out Berserk for Knife Through Butter

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-108-1024x609.png)

This combination turns Alexander into a **multi-trigger engine**, stacking damage across the entire enemy formation.

---

## Performance Observations

From actual combat logs:

- Assembly Charge can trigger easily ever single second
- Frequently ranks:  - **Top damage source**
- Or **second highest contributor**
- Performs better in:  - Longer fights
- Multi-target engagements
- Second marches where fights aren’t instantly decided

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-110-1024x458.png)

It’s not flashy, but over time it consistently adds pressure.

---

## Conclusion Summary

- Assembly Charge is a **high-value sustained DPS skill**, not a burst tool
- Best used on **fast-attacking physical heroes**, especially Alexander
- Its effectiveness scales with:  - Attack speed
- Number of normal attacks
- Strongest synergy comes from:  - **Berserk**
- **Wind Rage**
- Expect it to become a **top damage contributor in longer fights**

If you treat it as a trigger engine rather than a proc gamble, it becomes one of the more reliable KvK damage skills available.
