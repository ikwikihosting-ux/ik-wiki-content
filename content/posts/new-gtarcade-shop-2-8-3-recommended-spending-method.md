### How to Access the GTarcade Store

To access these deals, you need to use the GTarcade App, available on mobile or PC. You can also click through from the in-game shop by choosing the “GTarcade Store” link.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-123-1024x568.png)

Once inside, make sure to:

- Click **“View More”** to expand the available bundles
- Then click **“Paid Gift Pack”** to access the full list of value-packed items

This bundle is a reworked version of the in-game daily deals—only cheaper and more rewarding. For example, you can buy a $5 bundle and receive a $5.99 voucher, extra Action and Speed Potions, and VIP points. You can even redeem the voucher in-game to double up on rewards.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-117-1024x685.png)

This is hands-down one of the best daily values for spenders. If you buy the daily bundle in-game, STOP. Buy this instead. These will get you 6x daily bundle vouchers per week saving you money and getting 20x AP and SP flasks on top as well as gold and VIP points. Amazing value.

### Summary:

- Get more potions (AP/SP) than the standard daily deal
- Costs less ($5 instead of $5.99)
- Comes with VIP and a $5.99 voucher
- Can be stacked weekly for multiple redemptions

The Pearl event is one of the best events in the game for dust and moonlight resources. In-game, $5 gets you 18 pearls. But in the GTarcade store, $5 gets you **24 pearls**. Do the math—across multiple purchases, that’s a huge difference.

If you’re looking to spin the wheel for moonlight and chaos fragments, this is the place to do it. Just note that you **don’t get gems or VIP points** with this version—so it’s best for players who already have large reserves of those.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-118-1024x654.png)

### Summary:

- Better per-dollar ratio (24 pearls vs. 18 in-game)
- Ideal for dust/moonlight farming
- No gems/VIP included, but huge resource boost
