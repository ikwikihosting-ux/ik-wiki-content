## Immortal Outfits Arrive – Plus Quality-of-Life Improvements

Version **2.9.7** isn't one of the game's largest updates, but it introduces something that could become one of the most important long-term progression systems we've seen in quite a while: **Immortal Outfits**.

Alongside that, Lilith has added a new Decoration Shop and several welcome quality-of-life improvements across Asteria, Legion of Frostborne, Arena, and the Ruins interface.

Let's go through everything that changed and what it actually means for players.

---

## New Immortal Outfit System

The headline feature of this patch is the brand-new **Immortal Outfit** system.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-1-1024x547.png)

The first two Immortals receiving outfits are:

- Alexander
- Athena

These are called the **Summer Passion Outfits**, and they're much more than simple cosmetic skins.

## How to Unlock Outfits

Outfits are obtained by collecting **Outfit Fragments** during **Festival Events.**

Once you've collected enough fragments, the outfit becomes permanently unlocked for that specific Immortal.

A few important things to know:

- Outfits are permanently bound to their Immortal.
- Rebirthing an Immortal does **not** remove the outfit.
- Unlocking an outfit also activates a unique **Outfit Insignia**.
- Different outfits can modify that Immortal's Ultimate Skill.

The permanent binding works similarly to Mythic Equipment. Once you've unlocked an outfit, you don't need to worry about losing it during rebirths or rebuilding your Immortal.

---

## The Big Feature: Ultimate Skill Changes

This is easily the most interesting part of the entire update.

Changing an outfit doesn't simply change your Immortal's appearance—it also changes their **Ultimate Skill**.

That means these outfits are effectively a brand-new progression system rather than a cosmetic feature.

Exactly how much each Ultimate changes will vary by outfit, but the important takeaway is that these aren't visual upgrades—they can directly affect combat performance.

For now, only Alexander and Athena have alternate Ultimate Skills.

As more outfits are released, this system has the potential to completely reshape how certain Immortals are built and played.

---

## What Does This Mean?

It's still very early, but a few things already seem likely.

### 1. This is another long-term progression system

Like Artifacts, Mythic Equipment, Affinity, and Candy Defenders before it, outfits appear to be another layer of account progression.

If future outfits continue to provide new Ultimate Skills, collecting them could become a major part of strengthening your account.

---

### 2. Alexander Gets Even More Attention

Many players were surprised that Alexander was chosen as one of the first Outfit recipients.

Alexander has already been one of the strongest Immortals in the game for a long time.

If his alternate Ultimate ends up being stronger—or even just better in certain situations—it could make him an even more valuable investment.

Of course, we'll need testing before drawing conclusions, but it's definitely something worth watching.

---

### 3. We Don't Yet Know How Accessible They'll Be

Outfit Fragments come from Festival Events.

The big question is how generous those events will be.

If fragments are obtainable through normal gameplay, this could become another enjoyable progression system.

If they're heavily tied to spending, however, outfits could become another gap between free-to-play players and heavy spenders.

For now, it's simply too early to know.

---

## New Decoration Shop

The patch also introduces a brand-new **Decoration Shop**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-2-1024x570.png)

### Unlock Requirement

- Lord Level 6

Players can purchase **exclusive Collection Outfits** here.

At the moment, the shop appears to focus primarily on cosmetic collections rather than gameplay progression, although we'll likely see more items added over time.

For newer players, unlocking it is straightforward, and it provides another place to browse and collect account cosmetics.

---

## Optimizations

While the new Outfit system steals the spotlight, the patch also includes several welcome quality-of-life improvements.

## Battle of Asteria Adjustments

Alliance difficulty and Boss HP have both been adjusted.

The exact numbers weren't provided, but the goal appears to be improving overall balance and making progression smoother for alliances.

---

## Ruins Exploration Button Moved

The Ruins button has been relocated next to the Mail button.

It's a small change, but one that makes Ruins much easier to access during daily gameplay.

---

## Legion of Frostborne Alliance Log

The Alliance Log now records:

- Legion registration
- Registration cancellations

This gives alliance leaders much better visibility into who's signing up—and who isn't.

It's a small improvement that should make organizing Legions a little easier.

---

## Improved Battle Reports

Arena, Open Arena, and Throne of the Supreme now display battle reports more consistently.

Previously, HP values shown during wheel battles could be confusing.

Now, the HP displayed throughout the report matches the HP values present when combat actually began, making it much easier to follow what happened during a fight.

---

## Final Thoughts

Version 2.9.7 is relatively small in terms of content, but the **Immortal Outfit system** has the potential to become one of the game's biggest progression mechanics over the coming months.

Right now, we only have two outfits, so it's far too early to judge how impactful—or how accessible—the system will be. The real test will come as more Immortals receive outfits and we begin to see whether these alternate Ultimate Skills create meaningful new strategies or simply become another source of raw power.

The rest of the update is mostly quality-of-life focused, with cleaner interfaces, better battle reports, and a few welcome improvements for Asteria and Legion of Frostborne.

Overall, this feels less like a major content patch and more like the foundation for a feature that Lilith plans to expand over time. Whether it becomes a fun layer of customization or another mandatory progression system will depend entirely on how future outfits are released and balanced. For now, it's definitely the feature to keep an eye on.
