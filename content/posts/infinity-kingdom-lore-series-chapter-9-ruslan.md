## 💠 The Alchemical Council's Experiments

The episode opens with a rather disturbing look inside an alchemical laboratory.

We learn that the empire's desire for military strength and resources led to experiments involving human DNA. Through alchemy, researchers began modifying living bodies, reinforcing bones, strengthening muscles, and even incorporating steel into their creations.

The result is an army of monstrous, artificially enhanced soldiers.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-222507-nqoj3o.png)

This is particularly interesting when we consider what we already know about the Council. Earlier chapters revealed that it was harvesting the souls of fallen warriors to power its experiments, supposedly as part of an effort to forge a false god.

Now we're seeing another side of that experimentation: the creation of living weapons.

Whether these experiments are directly connected to the false god remains unclear, but the Council is certainly becoming one of the most dangerous forces in Norheim.

## 💠 Welcome to Ruslan

Meanwhile, Brynhild and our Hangover Knight arrive in Ruslan, hoping to pass through the region without attracting too much attention.

That plan doesn't last particularly long.

The landscape is devastated by war, with ruined fortifications and the remains of battles scattered across the desert. Our travelers are soon confronted by armored soldiers who mistake them for merchants from Vitas.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-222619-nkctg0.png)

The encounter gives us another interesting glimpse into Norheim's political situation. Ruslan appears to be caught in a prolonged conflict, and the region's soldiers are clearly suspicious of outsiders.

Our Hangover Knight manages to talk his way through the confrontation, although not without a little improvisation.

I've grown rather fond of this character. He continues to approach dangerous situations with an almost ridiculous level of confidence, which provides some welcome humor in an increasingly dark story.

## 💠 A Mysterious Survivor and the Council's Plans

After the encounter, our travelers discover an injured man among the remains of the fighting.

Before dying, the stranger makes a cryptic statement about the alchemists, describing them as new gods sent to save the world.

It's another indication that the Council's work may have developed into something resembling a religious or ideological movement. At least some people appear to believe that the alchemists are humanity's salvation rather than a threat.

The man is also carrying a mysterious document covered in ancient symbols.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261015-222642-dfzbrg.png)

When our Hangover Knight examines it, he recognizes its significance but cannot decipher the writing.

According to him, **only a Valkyrie's Sight can unravel the runes**.

Given that Brynhild is a Valkyrie, this is a particularly interesting development. Her importance to the story may extend far beyond her fighting abilities. Perhaps the Valkyries possess knowledge or supernatural abilities that are essential to understanding the Council's plans.

The episode doesn't explain exactly what Valkyrie's Sight involves, but it certainly gives us another reason to pay attention to Brynhild's mythological background.

## 💠 The Sorcerer's Stone Returns

The chapter ends with another intriguing development.

A mysterious man wearing a monocle approaches our travelers. He appears to know something about the ancient document and the Council's activities.

More importantly, he reveals that they are looking for information about the Sorcerer's Stone.

This brings us straight back to one of the biggest mysteries of the series.

In earlier chapters, the Philosopher's Stone appeared to be connected to the Council's experiments and its efforts to manipulate the souls of fallen warriors. We also learned about an ancient covenant between humanity and the Goddess, suggesting that the stone might have a much deeper significance within Norheim's history.

The English subtitles again use the term Sorcerer's Stone, which appears to refer to the same mysterious object.

If this new character understands the stone, he might finally be able to explain what the Council wants with it and why Brynhild and the Hangover Knight have become involved.

Of course, whether he can be trusted is another matter entirely.

## 💠 My Take – The Council's Influence Is Growing

Chapter 9 continues building the larger mystery surrounding the Alchemical Council, but what I find most interesting is how its influence appears to extend across Norheim.

We began this story with a mysterious emblem discovered in the black market of Vitas. Since then, we've encountered underground laboratories, stolen warrior souls, experiments involving artificial divinity, and now alchemically enhanced soldiers in the middle of a war.

The Council is clearly involved in much more than a single secret experiment.

I'm also particularly interested in the reference to Valkyrie's Sight. Brynhild's connection to Valhalla was already important when we discovered the imprisoned warrior souls, and now her Valkyrie heritage may hold the key to deciphering ancient texts. I really like seeing the writers incorporate these mythological details into the story rather than treating Immortals as ordinary fantasy soldiers.

And then there's our Hangover Knight, whose identity remains one of the series' longest-running mysteries.

For now, the biggest questions are whether the Council is deliberately fueling the conflict in Ruslan, what the ancient document reveals, and how these new alchemical soldiers connect to the attempt to forge a false god.

Most importantly, what does the mysterious stranger know about the Philosopher's Stone?

Hopefully, Chapter 10 will bring us a little closer to finding out.
