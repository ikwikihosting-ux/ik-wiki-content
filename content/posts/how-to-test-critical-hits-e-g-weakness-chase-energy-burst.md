### Unlocking the Secrets of Critical Hit-Based Tower of Knowledge Skills in Infinity Kingdom

In the ever-evolving world of Infinity Kingdom, mastering the intricacies of critical hit-based Tower of Knowledge skills is essential for any player seeking to maximize their combat effectiveness. With skills like Energy Burst and Chase, the potential for high energy regeneration and damage output on critical hits becomes a game-changer.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/image-55-1024x574.png)

Understanding the nuances of these skills, along with factors like critical hit chance boosts and resilience reduction, can significantly impact your battle outcomes.

## 1. Critical Hit Basics

Before delving into specific skills, it's crucial to grasp the fundamentals of critical hits. When an attack lands as a critical hit, all damage instances are multiplied by 2-3 times, resulting in a substantial increase in overall damage. Consistent critical hits are, therefore, a key goal for any player seeking to optimize their strategy.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/image-56-1024x612.png)

## 2. Boosting Critical Hit Chance

To enhance your chances of landing critical hits, consider utilizing Immortal William and the dragon, both of which boost critical hit chance. Additionally, Tower of Knowledge skills like "Weakness" can be instrumental in securing more critical hits by reducing the enemy's resilience rate. Strategically incorporating these boosts into your lineup can tip the scales in your favor.

## 3. Customizing Strategies

The challenge arises in determining when to deploy critical hit-based skills like Chase or Weakness. There's no one-size-fits-all answer; it depends on various factors such as your technology, skill levels, boost levels, and the specific enemy you are facing.

## 4. Reading Battle Reports

To fine-tune your strategy, regularly review battle reports. In one scenario, Himiko achieved a high critical hit rating, indicating that her critical hit chance surpassed the enemy's resilience.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/image-60-1024x612.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/image-61-1024x612.png)

In such cases, running Weakness becomes a viable option, ensuring more critical hits. Additionally, considering skills like Chase can further capitalize on the high chance of critical hits.

Conversely, if your opponent boasts high resilience, perhaps from a full dragon talent tree or skills like Malice, obtaining critical hits might be challenging. In such situations, countering with skills like Malice can neutralize the impact of critical hits.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/image-62.png)

## 5. Testing and Optimization

Experimentation is key. As you test your build in combat, observe the outcomes and adjust accordingly. If critical hits are consistently high, integrating skills like Chase may yield substantial damage. However, in late conquest, where space in your mage's skill set is limited, considerations like spell disintegration may take precedence over skills like Chase.

## 6. Considerations for Chase

While Chase can deliver impressive damage, late-game scenarios might necessitate prioritizing other magical damage skills over it. Even with a high critical hit rate, the damage contribution of Chase may be overshadowed by alternatives like Concentration, which guarantees a flat damage boost.

Regularly assess and refine your approach to stay one step ahead in the dynamic world of battles and conquests.
