## Infinity Kingdom Guide: Daily Pearls Shop Update (Patch 2.8.7)

With patch **2.8.7**, the **Daily Pearls Shop** received a big update: you can now directly buy **Unique Artifacts** using **Mysterious Pearls**. For many players, this looks tempting — but is it really worth it? Let’s break it down in detail.

---

## What’s New in the Pearls Shop?

There are now **two new artifact purchase options**:

1. **Shadow/Holy Immortal Unique Artifact Chest**  - Cost: **360 Mysterious Pearls**
- Gives a unique artifact for a Shadow or Holy immortal.
2. **Golden Path Season Selection Chest**  - Cost: **240 Mysterious Pearls**
- Lets you pick a unique artifact for one of the Golden Path immortals.
- Immortals included: **Matilda, Yi Sun-Sin (YSS), Seon, Artemisia, Louis IX, Tokugawa, Trajan, Cao Cao, Ragnar, Darius**, and a handful of others.

---

## Why This Is Important

Unique artifacts are **power spikes**. They unlock the full potential of an immortal and are often the difference between a mid-tier build and a meta build. Until now:

- **Holy/Shadow artifacts** have been relatively accessible via events, rewards, and steady progression.
- **Golden Path artifacts**, on the other hand, have been *extremely limited*. Unless you participated heavily in Golden Path seasons, getting them was nearly impossible.

So this change gives players another way to get their hands on **rare season-specific uniques** — but at a steep cost.

---

## Cost Analysis

Let’s break down what these purchases actually mean in gem and dollar terms.

### Golden Path Selection Chest (240 Pearls)

- **Gem value:** ~7,920 gems
- **Dollar equivalent:** $72 if you buy Pearls through Daily Pearl packs
- **Time:** 12 days of buying Daily Pearls packs to accumulate enough currency
- **Comparison:** You obtain the same item for 600 Golden Path coins = 9,000 gems ($81.8)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-40-1024x582.png)

💡 Key takeaway: **Similar cost to GP uniques, but without grinding Golden Path.**

---

### Shadow/Holy Artifact Chest (360 Pearls)

- **Gem value:** ~11,880 gems
- **Dollar equivalent:** $108 purchased via Daily Pearl packs
- **Time:** 18 days of Pearl purchases to gather enough currency

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-41-1024x724.png)

💡 Key takeaway: **Overpriced compared to how many Holy/Shadow artifacts you can naturally obtain through events and game rewards.**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-42.png)

**value estimates are made using the standard 10$ bundles offering 1100 gems.*

---

## Veteran Recommendation

### 1. Do NOT buy the Shadow/Holy Unique Chest

- You’ll naturally collect plenty of Shadow and Holy uniques just by playing.
- Events flood you with Shadow/Holy artifact fragments over time. If you main shadow or holy you likely do not need one more expensive artifact, but if you do - hey, it's here!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-43-1024x534.png)

- Spending 360 Pearls here is a **trap for newer players** who don’t yet see how common these will become.

### 2. Golden Path Selection Chest: Only If You Desperately Need It

- This is the **only good reason** to spend Pearls here.
- If you are actively running **Golden Path immortals** (Fu Fei/YSS/Seon/Artemisia builds are still quite relevant for mid-spenders), this can save your build.
- But beware: 240 Pearls is still **very expensive**. Think carefully before pulling the trigger.

### 3. Grind Golden Path for Free Whenever Possible

- Don’t forget you can still earn **Golden Path currency for free** through consistent participation.
- The Daily Pearls option should be treated as a **last resort** if you absolutely cannot wait.

---

## Final Thoughts

The **Daily Pearls Shop update** is a mixed bag:

- The **Shadow/Holy option** is essentially a **Pearl sink** — avoid it.
- The **Elemental Selection chest**, while expensive, is an option for players who wants to rush artifacts for the seasonal immortals, players who missed or skipped earlier seasons or who just need a critical artifact to unlock fast to unluck a new build’s full power.
