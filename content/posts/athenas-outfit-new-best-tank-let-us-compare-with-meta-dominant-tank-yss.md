## Introduction

***Athena Summer Skin vs. Yi Sun-sin (YSS): Is the New Skin Really an Upgrade?***

Athena has long been one of the strongest defensive support Immortals in Infinity Kingdom, particularly against physical damage compositions. Her Holy Grace provides powerful sustain, her Exclusive Artifact increases her durability against physical attackers, and her built-in Wound resistance makes her a natural counter to heroes like Khan.

The release of Athena's Summer Skin introduced several balance changes that initially looked like a significant upgrade. On paper, increased Energy Regeneration, stronger healing, and an earlier activation threshold all appear to make her considerably stronger.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-61.png)

After testing the changes in a variety of matchups, however, the picture becomes more nuanced. The skin certainly improves Athena in some situations, but it also weakens one of the mechanics that previously made her so difficult to kill. More importantly, it does very little to address her biggest weakness: magic damage.

As a result, Yi Sun-sin (YSS) remains the stronger choice in many battles despite Athena's apparent buffs.

---

## Understanding Athena's Summer Skin Changes

The Summer Skin introduces four notable adjustments to Athena's kit.

### New Passive: +40% Energy Regeneration Speed

The first addition is a passive bonus granting 40% Energy Regeneration Speed.

At first glance, this appears to be a major improvement. In practice, its impact is more modest because Energy Regeneration Speed scales from an Immortal's base regeneration value rather than acting as a flat increase.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-62-1024x560.png)

Athena's base Energy Regeneration is only **65.7**, one of the slowest values among support Immortals. By comparison, many offensive Immortals, for example quick fire immortals like Wu, sit at **120+** regeneration.

Because of that low starting point, the additional 40% increases her energy gain, but not nearly as dramatically as many players initially expected. It is still a buff, just not one that fundamentally changes how quickly she reaches her Ultimate.

---

### Holy Grace Healing Increased

Holy Grace's recovery rate increases from **80 to 100** at maximum level.

This is a straightforward improvement.

Whenever Holy Grace activates, Athena now restores significantly more health, making prolonged fights against physical teams even more favourable.

---

### Damage Reduction Activates Earlier

The activation threshold for Holy Grace's damage reduction increases from **60% HP to 70% HP**.

This means Athena begins protecting her team sooner during sustained physical engagements.

Earlier activation generally translates into better survivability because damage reduction comes online before large amounts of health have already been lost.

---

### Damage Reduction Reduced

The trade-off is easy to overlook.

Holy Grace's damage reduction falls from **40% to 30%**.

Although the effect activates earlier, the protection itself is weaker once it is active.

Whether this is an improvement depends entirely on the matchup.

---

## Where Athena Excels

Athena has always been designed to counter physical damage, and the Summer Skin reinforces that identity.

Against physical teams, several parts of her kit work together exceptionally well:

- Holy Grace activates consistently.
- Increased healing restores more health every trigger.
- Wound resistance reduces the effectiveness of heroes like Khan.
- Earlier damage reduction helps stabilize the team sooner.

When fighting physical-heavy compositions, Holy Grace often activates repeatedly throughout the battle. Every activation now provides more healing than before, allowing Athena to sustain her frontline through extended engagements.

This is where the Summer Skin performs exactly as intended.

---

## Why Magic Teams Are Still a Problem

The changes become much less impressive once magic damage enters the equation.

Athena's Exclusive Artifact primarily increases **Physical Defense**, providing little protection against magic-based attackers. More importantly, **Holy Grace only activates when Athena's team receives physical damage**.

Against teams built around Merlin, Baldwin, Empress Wu, Loki, or other magic carries, Holy Grace may activate very few times—or not at all.

That creates a significant problem. The improved healing cannot trigger. The earlier threshold becomes irrelevant. Even the increased Energy Regeneration has less impact because the core mechanic driving Athena's sustain simply isn't being used.

The Summer Skin strengthens Athena's existing specialty without solving the matchup she has traditionally struggled with.

---

## Why Yi Sun-sin Often Performs Better

This is where Yi Sun-sin deserves serious consideration.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-63-1024x666.png)

Unlike Athena, YSS provides immediate value regardless of the damage type your opponent uses.

His Ultimate grants:

- 30% damage reduction immediately
- Increased healing received
- No HP threshold requirement
- No dependency on physical damage

Those differences are more important than they initially appear.

Mage compositions are built around early burst damage. Infinite Fire powers, and similar abilities begin dealing heavy damage almost immediately after combat starts. YSS's protection is already active before much of that burst lands.

Athena, by comparison, may still be waiting for Holy Grace to activate. In many battles, that timing alone determines whether your frontline survives the opening seconds.

---

## Healing Comparison

One common argument in favour of Athena's Summer Skin is that Holy Grace now heals considerably more. That is true - however, YSS improves every healing source on your team rather than a single ability.

This includes effects such as:

- Fountain of Life
- Vitality Touch
- Bjorn's healing
- Fou Fei's Ultimate
- Any additional healing provided by your support setup

Instead of increasing one recovery skill, YSS amplifies your entire sustain package.

Against opponents without reliable Wound effects, that global healing bonus often produces more overall value than Athena's stronger Holy Grace alone.

---

## Timing Matters More Than Raw Numbers

One of the easiest mistakes when comparing support Immortals is focusing only on percentages.

A larger heal does not necessarily make an Immortal stronger. The timing of that heal is often far more important. During testing, YSS's Ultimate consistently activated early enough to reduce incoming burst damage before the team's health dropped into dangerous territory.

Athena's protection, even with the improved 70% threshold, frequently became relevant only after significant damage had already been taken. Against physical teams, that delay usually isn't an issue because Holy Grace activates repeatedly throughout the fight.

Against magic burst teams, those first few seconds are often the most dangerous part of the battle. If your frontline survives the opening burst, healing has time to work. If it doesn't, stronger healing values become irrelevant.

---

## Should You Replace YSS?

Not necessarily. The Summer Skin does not make Athena universally stronger than Yi Sun-sin. Instead, it pushes Athena further into the role she already performed well.

A simple way to think about the two Immortals is this:

### Choose Athena when:

- Facing physical-heavy teams
- Countering Khan or other Wound-based compositions
- Expecting long, sustained fights

### Choose Yi Sun-sin when:

- Facing mage-heavy teams
- Expecting early burst damage
- Running healing-heavy support compositions
- You need immediate damage reduction regardless of damage type

Many experienced players keep both Immortals built and simply switch between them depending on the opponent.

That flexibility often produces better results than trying to force one support into every matchup.

---

## Final Thoughts

Athena's Summer Skin is best described as a **conditional buff** rather than a universal upgrade.

The increased healing, earlier activation threshold, and Energy Regeneration all improve her performance against physical damage, reinforcing her role as one of the game's strongest anti-physical supports.

At the same time, reducing Holy Grace's damage reduction from 40% to 30% limits her effectiveness in situations where raw mitigation matters most, and the changes do little to improve her performance against magic-heavy teams.

Yi Sun-sin remains one of the most reliable defensive supports in the game because his protection works immediately, applies regardless of damage type, and enhances every source of healing your team receives.

Rather than asking which Immortal is stronger overall, the better question is which one is stronger for the matchup in front of you.

In most cases, the answer depends less on your own team than on the type of damage your opponent intends to deal.
