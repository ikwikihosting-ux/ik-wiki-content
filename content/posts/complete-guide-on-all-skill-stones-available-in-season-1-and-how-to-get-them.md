Season 1 is where players first meet the **Wisdom Well**, the **Cinder Shop**, and **KvK** stones. The trick isn’t just “what’s strong,” it’s **timing**. You *can* buy early to spike power in S1, but a lot of these stones are easier or cheaper to get later. If you’re planning long-term, read the **[Season 2](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/tower-of-knowledge-passives/ability-stones-availability-season-1-2-and-conquest/complete-guide-on-all-skill-stones-available-in-season-2-and-how-to-get-them/)** and **[Conquest](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/tower-of-knowledge-passives/ability-stones-availability-season-1-2-and-conquest/complete-guide-on-all-skill-stones-available-in-conquest-and-how-to-get-them/)**guides too; for now, this page is strictly **what’s available in Season 1** and how to use it without wasting currency. Skill value depends on your march, so cross-check with build guides before you commit.

## What’s in Season 1 (at a glance)

- **Wisdom Well (Jackpot Pool)** in S1 includes: **Weakening Curse, Siren’s Gaze, Weakness Protection, Berserker, Wind Rage, Overpower, Smite, Spring of Life, Battle Stance, Fear of Domination, Desire of Life, Unyielding, Storm Strike, Armageddon, Implosion, Energy Shield.**
- **Cinder Shop (S1)** sells: **Toxin Barrier, Blade Vortex, Full Coverage, Victorious Pursuit, Snipe, Stormeye, Spell Disintegrator, Guard, Vitality Touch, Purification, Jade Barrier, Spear Impale, Void Realm, Demonic Contract.**
- **KvK (S1)**: **Snipe** is in the **Cold Coin shop**; **Full Coverage** and **Snipe** can drop from **KvK chests**.

## Wisdom Well (Season 1)

**What’s inside:** the first jackpot pool (list above). When you hit jackpot, you pull **one of those stones** in a meaningful chunk, which can absolutely turbo a Season 1 march.

In season one you get the first iterations of the Wisdom Well. As the jackpot reward in season 1 you are able to get: Weakening curse, Siren’s gaze, Weakness Protection, Berserker, Wind Rage, Overpower, Smite, Spring of Life, Battle Stance, Fear of Domination, Desire of Life, Unyielding, Storm Strike, Armageddon, Implosion, Energy Shield.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-105.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-104.png)

**How to use it:**

- If you’re pushing **now**, rolling for things like **Weakening Curse** for most setups or **Wind Rage** for hybrids can flip fights fast (check build guides for details).
- If you’re more budget-minded, don’t over-commit. A lot of these stones show up in easier lanes later, so S1 Well pulls are basically you paying a premium for getting key skills early.

**My take:** I keep Well spending **targeted**. If a single stone changes my matchups this season, I go for it. Otherwise I hold and build via Cinder Shop picks.

## Cinder Wish Shop (Season 1)

**What you can buy:** **Toxin Barrier, Blade Vortex, Full Coverage, Victorious Pursuit, Snipe, Stormeye, Spell Disintegrator, Guard, Vitality Touch, Purification, Jade Barrier, Spear Impale, Void Realm, Demonic Contract.**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-106-1024x419.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-107-1024x561.png)

### Why it matters in S1:

- This is your **reliable** way to finish a march without gambling and to even get skills for free by selling KvK stone rewards and buying using cinders in the wish shop.
- If you pull something from KvK (or Well I guess) you don’t need, you can **sell it for Cinders** and convert into exactly what your lineup is missing from this list.

## KvK Shop (Season 1)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-108-1024x586.png)

**What’s in the shop:** **Snipe**.
**Should you buy?** Sure, but you wont be needing it since the skill is not particularly strong. But you can still purchase these just fine! Why? Because you turn them into Cinders (more below).

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-109.png)

All skill stones can be **sold for Cinders**. So you can:

1. Pick up a stone from **KvK** using **Cold Coins**,
2. **Sell** that stone for **Cinders**,
3. **Buy** something else from the **Cinder Shop** instead.

The **ratio** you get here is **1:1** between “KvK skill stone → Cinder Shop skill stone.” That’s huge for flexibility. If KvK is offering something you don’t actually want to run, you can still turn it into a Cinder Shop pick that you *do* need. This is how budget and F2P players quietly fix holes in a march without swiping.

## KvK Chests (Season 1)

**What can drop:** **Full Coverage** and **Snipe**. **I don’t recommend heavy spending gems here in S1.** **Full Coverage** can be cute if you’re running a **super niche high-regen build**, but Conquest gives you better tools in both the **KvK Shop** and **Cinder Shop**. For almost everyone, the efficient move is to **bank Cold Coins** in S1/S2 for Conquest.

## Player Perspective (how I play S1)

If I’m trying to win S1 server fights, I’ll take a **surgical** shot at the Well for a stone that fixes my worst matchup. If you are running an earth himiko spender build going for things like **Weakening Curse**, **Spring of Life**in the well and spending cinders on **Vitality or Purification or Jade Barrier**will provide you with top tier skills for season 1 and season 2. However these will NOT hold top tier ranking going into conquest and later Legendary seasons. Personally, the tricky part is not getting baited into finishing every shiny set—you’ll keep them going in season 1 and season 2 but you will replace them in conquest and legendary where the jackpot rewards are legendary skills and where many new more powerful skills are available.

In essence, if you want to be top dog contender for season 1 and 2, buy the best key skills for your chosen main build. If you are going for max long term efficiency, grind as many KvK Cold Coins as you can, skip Wisdom well skills until conquest and use KvK rewards to turn into free cinders to buy a key single skill if possible.
