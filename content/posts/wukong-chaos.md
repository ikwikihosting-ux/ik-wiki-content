**Wukong – Chaos Immortal**: Wukong is one of the most iconic and powerful Chaos Immortals in *Infinity Kingdom*, offering a rare mix of **survivability, self-sustain, and incredible damage potential**. With a skill set that enables him to block damage and deal massive bursts in return, Wukong has become a staple in many Conquest and Chaos-season PvP lineups.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-8-1024x541.png)

This guide will walk you through his **mythological origins**, how to **obtain him**, his **ultimate ability**, the **best skills**, and the **team compositions** where he shines the most.

---

## 📚 Mythological Origins: The Monkey King

Wukong originates from **Journey to the West**, a 16th-century Chinese novel where he is born from stone and acquires supernatural powers through Taoist practices. Though fictional, his story is inspired by a real Buddhist monk's pilgrimage for sacred scriptures. In mythology, Wukong is clever, rebellious, and nearly unstoppable — traits reflected perfectly in his in-game design.

---

## How to Obtain Wukong

Wukong is a **Chaos Immortal**, meaning his availability is limited and highly exclusive.

### Main Methods:

- **Chaos Roulette Event** – The primary method to obtain Wukong and his exclusive artifact.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-10-1024x635.png)

- **Seasonal Events** – Occasionally offers Chaos Immortal Selection Chests (containing his shards).

Because of this exclusivity, unlocking Wukong typically requires significant investment and careful planning — but for many players, he’s well worth it.

---

## 💥Ultimate Skill: Stone Armor

Wukong’s ultimate is one of the most **unique and powerful defensive-offensive hybrid abilities** in the game:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-9.png)

### Effect:

- Deals high **physical damage** (600%) to all enemies.
- Grants **Absolute Defense**, which:  - Reduces incoming damage significantly.
- **Applies to Wukong**, and also to **the allied Immortal with the highest troop loss**.

This not only makes Wukong incredibly tanky but also allows him to **shield allies** in dire moments. When timed correctly, it can **swing battles in your favor** or outright **prevent your team from being wiped**.

### Why it’s special:

- Self-sufficient: Needs no support to survive.
- Game-changer: Can turn around losing fights through clutch protection.
- Scales well in both solo play and coordinated team marches.

## Recommended Skill Builds

### 1. Ramping Damage Build

**Battle Stance + Demonic Contract** **+ Drastic Action**

- *Strategy*: Wukong is supported by full sustain/heal immortals, allowing him to survive the first 20–30 seconds while these skills build up to peak damage.
- *Benefit*: Once charged, Wukong's damage becomes explosive, able to delete enemy frontlines with ease.
- *Ideal for*: Long fights where Wukong is the sole damage dealer.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-13-1024x577.png)

### 2. Below 70% HP Build

**Last Stand + Unyielding** **+ Drastic Action**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-11-1024x405.png)

- *Strategy*: Capitalizes on passive, which increases damage below 70% HP.
- *Last Stand*: Increases damage dealt and reduces damage taken when under 70% HP.
- *Unyielding*: Restores HP and boosts crit rate by 50% in the same health window.
- *Synergy*: His ultimate adds survivability while he shreds the opponent.

### 2. Hyper regen Build

### Corrosive Power + Battle Frenzy + Last Stand / Drastic Action / Unyielding

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-12-1024x561.png)

- *Strategy*: Capitalizes on regen skill synergy providing incredibly high cast rate.
- Can be used both in a defensive setup providing high uptime on shields
- Or be used offensively with e.g. Drastic for high cast per min, and thus high damage per second.

## Team Compatibility & Sample Marches

### 🔹 Sustain Damage Dealer Setup

- **Holy Dragon**: Fu Fei, Ninetails, Anubis, Wukong
- **Earth Dragon**: Fu Fei, Theodora, Wukong, Anubis

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-15.png)

In these setups:

- Wukong is the **only damage dealer**.
- All other Immortals provide **healing, shielding, and sustain**.
- Battle Stance + Demonic Contract ramp up Wukong’s damage over time.
- His ultimate shields himself and allies, **making him nearly unkillable** once rolling.

### 🔹 Burst Chaos Setup

- **Shadow Dragon**: Tomyris/Loki, Ninetails, Anubis, Wukong

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-16.png)

This is a high-damage, low-duration PvP burst comp:

- Loki and Anubis **contribute massive AoE or debuff-heavy damage**.
- Wukong provides **protection and finish potential**.
- Aimed at **bursting down teams** before they can scale or retaliate.

## Final Thoughts: Why Use Wukong?

Wukong is:

- **Durable** – With built-in damage immunity and no reliance on support.
- **Versatile** – Works well in solo-damage or burst-team comps.
- **Scalable** – Performs even better in late-game due to his synergy with chaos artifacts and deep specializations.

Whether you’re building a hybrid comp, aiming to dominate in Conquest PvP, or just looking for a true damage-dealing tank, **Wukong is one of the most complete Immortals in Infinity Kingdom**.

If you manage to unlock him, **build around him**. He won’t disappoint.
