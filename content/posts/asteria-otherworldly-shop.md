## Introduction

Otherworldly Shop Guide – What to Buy and How the Affinity Gifts Work

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-11-1024x626.png)

The Otherworldly Shop is the new currency shop tied to Asteria and the Immortal Affinity system. Players earn Otherworld Crystals by completing Asteria's personal and alliance challenges, and those crystals can then be exchanged for Affinity gifts used to increase relationships with immortals.

At first glance, the shop looks like it contains a lot of different items with different values. After testing the Affinity gains, however, the shop is actually surprisingly well balanced. The important thing is not finding the highest-value item, but understanding which gifts should be used on which immortals.

---

## Universal Affinity Gifts

These gifts can be used on any immortal and always provide their full value regardless of faction or element.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-12-1024x203.png)

### Spiritfont Nectar

Spiritfont Nectar is the most expensive universal gift in the shop.

- Cost: 80 Otherworld Crystals
- Daily Limit: 1
- Affinity: 800

This works out to exactly 10 Affinity per crystal.

Because of its high point value, this will likely become one of the primary purchases for players building Chaos, Holy, and Shadow immortals.

### Starweave Silk

Starweave Silk provides a mid-tier option.

- Cost: 40 Otherworld Crystals
- Daily Limit: 3
- Affinity: 400

Like Nectar, it provides exactly 10 Affinity per crystal.

### Letter of Blessing

Letter of Blessing is the cheapest universal gift.

- Cost: 20 Otherworld Crystals
- Daily Limit: 5 (plus an additional letter earned from daily activities)
- Affinity: 200

Again, the efficiency remains identical at 10 Affinity per crystal.

Although the point value is lower, it provides excellent flexibility when you only need a small amount of Affinity to reach the next level.

---

## Elemental Affinity Gifts

Elemental gifts work a little differently from the universal gifts. Every elemental gift provides a base amount of Affinity that can be given to any immortal, but also includes a bonus when used on an immortal of the matching element.

For example, a Water gift can be given to any immortal, but only Water immortals receive the additional elemental bonus. This means elemental players can gain significantly more Affinity from these items than Chaos, Holy, or Shadow immortals.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-13-1024x319.png)

### Purple Elemental Gifts

Purple gifts cost **50 Otherworld Crystals** and provide **200 base Affinity** to any immortal. When given to the correct element, they grant an additional **300 bonus Affinity**, for a total of **500 Affinity**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-10.png)

When used on the matching immortal, every purple gift provides **10 Affinity per crystal spent**.

---

### Blue Elemental Gifts

Blue gifts cost **30 Otherworld Crystals** and provide **100 base Affinity** to any immortal. Matching the gift to the correct element grants an additional **200 bonus Affinity**, bringing the total to **300 Affinity**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-9.png)

Just like the purple gifts, the blue gifts also provide **10 Affinity per crystal** when used on the correct element.

### What This Means

After testing all of the Affinity gifts currently available in the Otherworldly Shop, **there does not appear to be a "best value" elemental gift.**Both the blue and purple versions are balanced around the same Affinity-per-crystal ratio. The only real difference is the amount of Affinity gained per purchase and the daily purchase limits.

As long as you are matching gifts to the correct element, every elemental gift converts crystals into Affinity at the same efficiency. The real optimization comes from making sure you never waste the elemental bonus by giving the wrong gift to the wrong immortal.

---

## The Most Important Thing to Understand

After running the numbers, one thing becomes immediately clear: the shop is balanced around a simple 10-to-1 conversion ratio.

Whether you buy:

- Nectar
- Silk
- Letters
- Purple elemental gifts
- Blue elemental gifts

the efficiency remains exactly the same when the item is used correctly. There is no secret "best value" item hiding in the shop.

---

## Chaos, Holy, and Shadow Immortals

One important thing to understand is that Chaos, Holy, and Shadow immortals do not receive elemental Affinity bonuses. Unlike Water, Earth, Fire, Lightning, and Wind immortals, they only gain the base Affinity value from elemental gifts.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-14-1024x335.png)

For example, an Abyssal Pearl provides a total of 500 Affinity when given to a Water immortal. That same gift only provides 200 Affinity when given to a Chaos, Holy, or Shadow immortal because the 300-point elemental bonus does not apply.

This makes elemental gifts a very inefficient way to increase Affinity on those factions. The crystal cost remains exactly the same, but a large portion of the item's value is effectively wasted.

As a result, players focusing on Chaos, Holy, or Shadow immortals should prioritize the universal gifts instead. Spiritfont Nectar, Starweave Silk, and Letter of Blessing always provide their full Affinity value regardless of the immortal receiving them, making them the most efficient use of Otherworld Crystals for these factions.

This also means that Chaos, Holy, and Shadow players are largely dependent on the same small pool of universal gifts, while elemental players can spread their purchases across both universal and elemental gift categories. Over time, that may make Affinity progression considerably more expensive for players investing heavily into premium factions.

---

## What Should You Buy?

For elemental immortals, the answer is straightforward. Purchase gifts matching the element of the immortals you are actively building. Whether you choose the blue or purple versions makes little difference from an efficiency perspective since both provide the same Affinity-per-crystal ratio.

For Chaos, Holy, and Shadow immortals, focus almost entirely on the universal gifts. They provide substantially better value than elemental gifts because they do not rely on bonus Affinity triggers.

If you are building multiple marches, especially a mix of elemental and premium factions, it is worth planning purchases ahead of time. Elemental teams can draw from several different gift pools, while Chaos, Holy, and Shadow teams are largely restricted to the same universal resources.

---

## Final Thoughts

The Otherworldly Shop initially looks like a complicated collection of Affinity items, but the underlying design is actually very simple. Every gift is balanced around the same efficiency ratio, and the real optimization comes from matching gifts to the correct immortals.

For elemental players, that means taking advantage of the elemental bonus system whenever possible. For Chaos, Holy, and Shadow players, it means treating Nectar, Silk, and Letters as your primary progression resources.

As more players begin pushing higher Affinity levels, understanding this distinction will likely become far more important than simply buying whatever happens to be available in the shop each day.
