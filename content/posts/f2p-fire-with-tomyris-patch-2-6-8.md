### Fire-Tomyris Build for Free-to-Play Players in Infinity Kingdom

For players on servers in Season 3 or above in Infinity Kingdom, the Galaxy event offers cheap and even free access to key immortals like Tomyris and Himiko. This guide focuses on leveraging these opportunities to build a potent free-to-play fire setup centered around Tomyris.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-71-1024x374.png)

## The Advantages of Fire

Fire is a compelling element for several reasons:

1. **High Damage Burst**: Ensures rapid troop count reduction, crucial in making an impact against stronger foes or in rally situations.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-72-1024x515.png)

1. **Energy Regeneration Reduction**: Immortals like Hippolyta excel in diminishing enemy energy regen, providing a strategic edge.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-73-1024x550.png)

1. **Critical Chance and Damage**: Building crit chance and damage is effective in the current meta, especially as high-power opponents often neglect Malice in their setups.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-74-1024x289.png)

## Key Immortals for the Build

- **Trajan**: Increases crit chance and damage and serves as a durable frontline presence.
- **Hippolyta**: Known for reducing enemy energy regen and attack speed, her multiple damage instances are vital.
- **Seondeok**: Acts as the primary damage dealer with a powerful AOE nuke ability.
- **Tomyris**: Provides battlefield control with her confuse ability, significantly aiding in damage ramp-up.

## Crafting Your Fire Setup

### Trajan's Configuration

- **Basic Setup**: Weakness Aura, Blessing of Rage, and Blessing of War are essential for foundational support.
- **Advanced Options**: Include Weakening Curse for added debuffing and consider No Escape, Purify, or Energy Shield for flexibility, maintaining Weakness Aura.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-75-1024x610.png)

### Hippolyta’s Loadout

- **Basic Setup**: Equip Death Breath, Energy Burst, and Full Coverage for significant damage output and instance frequency.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-76-1024x589.png)

### Seondeok’s Arsenal

- **Basic Setup**: Utilize Anger, Adrenaline Rush, and Fighting Master or Rage for control setups or additional crits.
- **Advanced Skills**: Incorporate Blade Vortex and Executioner for heightened damage, with Corrosive enhancing her destructive capabilities.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-77-1024x621.png)

### Tomyris’s Toolkit

- **Basic Setup**: Malice, Assist, and Energy Suppression offer control and support.
- **Advanced Skills**: Opt for No Escape and potentially Energy Shield, with Adrenaline Rush as an option for increased control effectiveness.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-78-1024x628.png)

## Strategy and Adaptation

The fire-Tomyris build's success hinges on skill synergy and strategic immortal deployment. Adapting to the enemy lineup and server meta is crucial. Flexibility allows for effective counter-strategies, making skill selection and immortal pairing key to maximizing the build’s potential.

## Conclusion

A fire setup with Tomyris offers a strategic advantage for free-to-play players in Infinity Kingdom, combining the elemental strengths of fire with Tomyris’s control abilities. By tailoring your strategy based on available skills and server-specific dynamics, you can create a formidable team capable of challenging a wide array of opponents. Adjustments and testing are essential to refine your approach and achieve optimal performance.
