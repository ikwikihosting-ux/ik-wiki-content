## Introduction

After being absent from Infinity Kingdom for years, Asteria has finally returned in patch 2.9.5 alongside the new Affinity system. From early testing, the relaunch looks extremely close to the original version mechanically, especially regarding progression structure and alliance boss behavior. The biggest difference is clearly the reward design, which is now heavily tied into Affinity progression through the new Otherworld Crystal currency.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-4-1024x553.png)

Players who experienced the original Asteria event will probably recognize the flow immediately:

- Push progressively harder personal camps
- Unlock alliance-wide bosses
- Coordinate alliance participation carefully
- Optimize contribution without accidentally ending encounters too early

The old strategies still appear highly relevant.

---

## How the New Asteria Works - Individual Challenges

The personal side of the event revolves around clearing increasingly difficult Shadow Rogue camps.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-5-1024x574.png)

### Key mechanics

- Camps scale upward after each clear
- Maximum difficulty is Level 30
- Enemy strength increases quite aggressively near the top
- Clearing higher levels contributes toward unlocking alliance bosses
- Even the highest level (6) challenge at camp level 30 seems doable by most everyone, which is quite good!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-6-1024x604.png)

From testing on the final personal stage:

- Level 30 Challenge Level 6
- Conquest season account
- Battle duration: ~33 seconds
- Casualties: only ~100 troops

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-8-1024x570.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-7-1024x613.png)

That battle length is important because it confirms the enemies are not simply paper targets. They survive long enough to interact with sustain and debuff setups properly. However, despite the longer fight duration, the incoming damage remains manageable with optimized PvE control builds.

## Recommended PvE Utility Skills

Two skills stand out immediately:

### No Escape

Excellent for extending control windows and stabilizing harder camps.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-9.png)

### Weakening Curse

Still one of the strongest PvE mitigation tools because reducing enemy output scales extremely well in prolonged boss-style encounters.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-10.png)

In practice, those two together noticeably smooth out the later stages. When I tested the final challenge, sustain remained stable throughout most of the encounter despite the longer combat duration.

## Alliance Challenges – Why 1-Troop Marches Matter

## The old strategy is probably still mandatory

The alliance bosses appear structurally identical to the original implementation:

- Bosses unlock after enough members complete personal stages
- R5/R6 summon the boss
- Entire alliance attacks collectively
- Multiple boss levels exist

The critical issue is boss durability.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-12.png)

Based on current scaling, alliance bosses are almost certainly too fragile for modern server power levels. Even moderately developed conquest alliances are likely capable of deleting bosses extremely quickly.

That creates the same problem older servers had years ago: Players miss participation rewards because the boss died too fast

## Recommended alliance procedure

The safest method is still the classic approach:

### Step-by-step

1. Gather entire alliance at one location
2. Summon the boss
3. Every player sends a 1-unit march first
4. Wait until everyone tags the boss
5. Kill the boss normally afterward

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-11-1024x562.png)

This minimizes accidental burst damage while ensuring everyone qualifies for rewards.

In older Asteria cycles, alliances that ignored this coordination routinely lost rewards for slower or offline members. Given current troop scaling and immortal power creep, the issue is probably even worse now than it was originally.

Large conquest alliances especially need strict coordination here.

## The Biggest Change – Rewards

## Old Asteria vs New Asteria

The original event was valuable mainly because of:

- Artifact rewards
- Resources
- Some progression materials

At the time, artifacts were enormous progression accelerators.

The new reward structure is significantly stronger overall and it ties directly into the Affinity system while also supplying universally useful account progression resources.

---

## Personal Rewards

Current rewards include:

- Otherworld Crystals
- Speedups
- Dragon Chest Keys

The Dragon Chest Key income is actually surprisingly generous considering how frequently those become bottlenecks during progression stretches.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-13-1024x604.png)

The speedups are also meaningful enough that active players will notice the gain immediately over a full event cycle.

---

## Alliance Rewards

Alliance-side rewards currently include:

- Additional Otherworld Crystals
- Philosopher Stones
- Random immortal fragments

That combination gives the event value for both:

- Affinity progression
- General immortal development

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-14-1024x659.png)

---

## Total Crystal Income

The rewards from Asteria are actually quite generous this time around. If you fully complete both the personal and alliance challenges, you end up with 6,000 Otherworld Crystals in total — 3,000 from personal rewards and another 3,000 from alliance rewards. Considering the current shop prices, that is a pretty significant amount of currency.

---

## Asteria Shop Pricing

Right now, buying out the entire Asteria shop costs 1,950 crystals per day. Since the event runs for three days, a maxed run technically gives enough crystals to clear the whole shop every day.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-15-1024x597.png)

Still, **that probably is not**the smartest long-term approach...

---

## Why Buying Everything May Be Inefficient

The important thing to remember is that the crystals are rewarded at the end of the event, while the shop itself stays open afterward. There is no real reason to rush into spending everything immediately.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-16.png)

The smarter play will most likely be figuring out which items give the best Affinity value for the crystal cost.

That is the part we still need proper testing on. Until we know exactly how much Affinity each item provides, it is hard to say what the optimal purchases are.

---

## Affinity Material Observations

One thing already seems fairly clear though. Chaos, Holy, and Shadow immortals appear to content for the same Affinity items — mainly Nectar, Silk, and Letters. These can be used on any immortal type, whereas the rest of the items available are tied to elemental specific immortals! So you want to use the universals on your chaos, holy or shadow immortals if you run any of these. Using them on an elemental immortal will most likely be inefficient (depending on actual affinity rates yet to be released). Stay tuned for more!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-17-1024x425.png)

The remaining materials seem tied to the elemental factions instead:

- Pearls for Water
- Amber for Earth
- Zircon for Fire
- Gem for Lightning
- Bouquet for Wind

If that turns out to be correct, it could become a fairly important bottleneck later on, **but for now I think the main takeaway is that you should not be spending universally applicable items on standard element immortals**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-18-1024x272.png)

## Player Perspective

Mechanically, this feels very close to classic Asteria. The progression pacing, boss structure, and alliance coordination requirements all resemble the original implementation closely enough that veteran alliances already know how to optimize it.

The difference is reward relevance.

Old Asteria helped account growth through artifact progression, new Asteria looks positioned as a core Affinity progression system.

That makes participation much more important than before!

The alliance coordination aspect is also likely underestimated right now. On high-power conquest servers, bosses may disappear almost instantly unless **leadership actively organizes low-damage participation tagging first**.

I would strongly recommend alliances establish procedures immediately before players start wasting summon opportunities!

---

## Conclusion Summary

Asteria’s return in patch 2.9.5 is less of a redesign and more of a modernization of the original event structure.

### Early conclusions:

- Personal challenges are manageable with proper PvE utility skills
- No Escape and Weakening Curse remain extremely effective
- Alliance bosses will likely require strict 1-troop coordination
- Rewards are dramatically better than the original version
- Otherworld Crystals appear generous relative to current shop costs
- Affinity item efficiency will become the real optimization layer

The next important step is testing the Affinity gain values from each shop item to determine the true crystal efficiency hierarchy. Once players can measure exact Affinity values per item, the optimal spending paths should become much clearer.
