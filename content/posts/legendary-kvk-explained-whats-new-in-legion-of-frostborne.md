While Conquest KvK largely revolves around alliance coordination, PvP strength, thermal towers, and capturing objectives, **Legendary KvK adds new mechanics that make production, specialized professions, and logistical planning essential to winning wars**.

The most important changes are the introduction of three new professions, a completely new Altar Stone system, and Energy Cores that must be destroyed before cities and gates can be captured.

Just like in Conquest, kvk lasts **32 days!** With the addition of altars and roles, you have a **massive time crunch/quota** as a result. Less freedom but more certainty. 

Let's take a look at what changes and what players should expect when entering their first Legendary KvK. 

## From Conquest to Legendary – What Changes?

The familiar foundation of Legion of Frostborne remains. Alliances still compete for territory, fight opposing armies, and attempt to capture strategically important cities and checkpoints.

However, Legendary KvK introduces several new systems that fundamentally change how these objectives are captured.

The result is a more complicated battlefield where even the strongest alliance need to ajust gameplay and strategy accordingly.

## Three New Professions – Everyone Has a Job

The biggest new mechanic is the **Lord Contract System**, which requires players to select one of three professions before the war begins.

* The reason you need people to manually choose contracts/jobs to do is because 

  **any unassigned lords in the alliance will default to Fighting  Master**

  !
* If there is a lack of alchemists and craftsman, you will have a terrible time. These two are essential to play kvk.
* Caps of 90 Fighting Master, 60 Craftsman, 30 alchemists. These are the caps so far. When you have unassigned players not choosing a contract, the game prioritizing FM to max is what will get you, this can be an issue since you need to cap both alchemists and craftsmen in order to keep up with production of materials and alter stones (see later).



![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261030-144525-w4bw6p.png)

Each profession has its own skill tree, objectives, and responsibilities.

### Master Craftsman – The Alliance's Supply Chain

Master Craftsmen form the backbone of the new production system.

Their main responsibility is producing materials that Alchemists need to manufacture Altar Stones. They can also produce temporary Unit Boost items that provide additional combat bonuses to alliance members.

This makes Craftsman an excellent profession for players who may not have the strongest marches but still want to make an important contribution to their alliance.

Without sufficient Craftsmen producing materials, the alliance's Alchemists cannot maintain an effective supply of Altar Stones.

### Alchemist – The Key to Capturing Cities

Alchemists are arguably the most strategically important new profession.

They take the materials produced by Craftsmen and use them to manufacture Altar Stones, which alliance officers can then deploy across the battlefield.

Altar Stones are essential because **they are the only structures capable of destroying the new Energy Cores defending cities and checkpoints**.

Alchemists also have specialized skills that improve Altar Stone production, attack range, and damage.

This profession is particularly suited to active players who can coordinate closely with alliance leadership and keep production running when major battles are approaching.

### Fighting Master – The Frontline Specialists

Fighting Masters are the combat-focused profession.

Rather than managing the production chain, they concentrate on improving their fighting capabilities through specialized combat bonuses.

This profession is naturally suited to powerful and active PvP players who expect to spend much of their KvK fighting other alliances.

However, selecting another profession doesn't prevent you from fighting. Craftsmen and Alchemists can still participate in PvP, defend objectives, and join alliance operations.

The profession simply determines your additional responsibilities and available bonuses.

## Altar Stones and Energy Cores – A New Way to Capture Cities

Perhaps the most significant battlefield change is the introduction of **Energy Cores**.

Previously, capturing cities and gates largely involved defeating defenders and destroying their remaining fortifications.

In Legendary KvK, that is no longer enough.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261030-145153-p5qqq0.png)

When the defenders have been defeated and the walls reduced to zero, a new Energy Core becomes exposed.

This Energy Core cannot be damaged by ordinary armies.

Instead, the alliance must deploy Altar Stones to destroy it.

### How a Legendary KvK Capture Works (Alter Stones)

Capturing a city or checkpoint in Legendary KvK involves four main steps:

1. **Defeat the defending armies and destroy the walls.** Clear the objective's conventional defenses.
2. **Expose the Energy Core.** Once the defenses are eliminated, the Energy Core becomes vulnerable.
3. **Deploy Altar Stones within range.** Alliance officers place Altar Stones produced by Alchemists to attack the core.
4. **Destroy the Energy Core.** The alliance whose Altar Stone delivers the final blow captures the city or checkpoint.

Altar Stones function as automated defensive and offensive structures. They can attack Energy Cores, enemy Altar Stones, castles, and marching troops.

They also introduce a new tactical challenge because ordinary troops cannot destroy enemy Altar Stones. Alliances must use their own Altar Stones to eliminate opposing structures.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261030-144336-lcsfe3.png)

Even more importantly, when several allied alliances attack the same objective, **the alliance whose Altar Stone delivers the final hit to the Energy Core receives control of the city or checkpoint**.

This makes positioning, timing, and Altar Stone management essential parts of Legendary KvK strategy.

## Contract Skills – Progression Matters

Each profession has its own talent tree that players develop throughout the KvK season.

Progression is tied to profession-specific achievements and Contract Crystals, with talents unlocking additional abilities and improving existing ones.

For example, Craftsmen can improve their production efficiency, Alchemists can strengthen their Altar Stones, and Fighting Masters can unlock additional combat advantages.

Developing these skills requires resources, including substantial amounts of Gold.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261030-144440-d94i3a.png)

Based on player experience, fully developing a profession can require tens of millions of Gold, so preparing a healthy reserve before KvK is strongly recommended.

Unlike conventional combat progression, these new talent trees reward players for actively performing the responsibilities associated with their profession.

This makes activity and participation important even for players who aren't among the alliance's strongest fighters.

## Choosing Your Profession Before KvK

Profession selection takes place during the preparation period after alliance registration.

Players can change their selection during preparation, but **contracts become locked once the war period begins**.

There are also limits to how many players can select each profession within an alliance. Players who fail to choose a contract are automatically assigned Fighting Master.

This means alliance leaders should coordinate profession assignments before the war begins.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261029-100348-4lpkwm.png)

A good alliance needs a balanced mixture of Craftsmen, Alchemists, and Fighting Masters. Too many fighters and the alliance may struggle to produce enough Altar Stones. Too few fighters and it may lack the military strength necessary to protect those structures.

The ideal distribution will depend on the alliance's roster, activity levels, and overall strategy.

## Why Legendary KvK Requires More Organization

The biggest difference between Conquest and Legendary KvK is the increased importance of teamwork outside of combat.

In Conquest, an alliance with excellent communication, strong players, and good battlefield leadership could accomplish most of its objectives through coordinated military operations.

Those things remain essential in Legendary KvK, but alliances must now also organize a production chain.

Craftsmen need to supply materials, Alchemists need to manufacture Altar Stones, and alliance officers must coordinate their placement and use.

This creates new opportunities for less powerful players to contribute meaningfully to their alliance's success.

A highly active Craftsman or Alchemist may become just as important to a successful city siege as the strongest rally leader.

Conversely, poor organization can prevent even a very powerful alliance from capturing objectives efficiently.

## Final Thoughts – A New Kind of KvK

Legendary KvK represents a major change in how Infinity Kingdom approaches large-scale alliance warfare.

The familiar battles, territory competition, and alliance politics remain, but the introduction of professions, Altar Stones, and Energy Cores means that military strength alone is no longer sufficient.

Alliances must now plan their production, develop specialized players, and coordinate logistics alongside their battlefield operations.

For players entering Legendary KvK for the first time, the most important thing is understanding that **everyone has a role, and those roles depend on one another**.

You don't need to master every new mechanic immediately. Start by understanding the three professions, how Altar Stones are produced, and why Energy Cores change the way cities are captured.
