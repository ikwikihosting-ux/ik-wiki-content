There are two talents you want to be aware of that affects your gold production.

1. Plentiful Gold
2. Economics Expert

## Plentiful Gold

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/image-17.png)

This talent is steightforward and insanely strong. Your dwellings produce a ton of gold for you as your citizens are taxed minute by minute. **[Read more about dwelling in my guide.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/development/buildings/dwellings/)** This talent is a plain 10% boost to the production of your dwellings. **You should always have this talent on.**

## Economics Expert

As you can read in my **[guide on Forge Minting](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/development/buildings/forge-mag-gold-minting-efficiency/)**, ratio is everything. So you need to be using the talent “Economics Expert” which increases your Forge minting conversion rate by 10%.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/image-14.png)

**When we use this talent to mint, our gold per second increases by 10%.**
