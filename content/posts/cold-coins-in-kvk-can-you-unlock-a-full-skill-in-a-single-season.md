One of the most common questions I see every KvK season — in alliance chat, faction planning discussions, and Discord — is simple:

### “Can I get a full skill from this KvK?”

The confusion usually comes from how the rewards are structured. Many players assume that winning the season or ranking first automatically guarantees enough Cold Coins for a full skill. In practice, the system is more specific than that.

This guide breaks down the Cold Coin structure clearly and shows, with concrete numbers, when a full skill is realistic — and when it is not.

---

## Understanding the Cold Coin Structure

A full skill requires:

- **60 Skill Shards**
- **800 Cold Coins per shard**
- **48,000 Cold Coins total**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/image-34-1024x568.png)

So the real question becomes:

**Can your KvK performance reach 48,000 Cold Coins?**

To answer that, we need to look at the reward sources individually.

---

## Fixed Income: The Base You Control

These rewards are largely within your control if you are active.

## Personal Contribution

- **25,000 Cold Coins**

This is stable and achievable with consistent participation.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/image-39-1024x564.png)

## Alliance Reputation Rewards

Alliance Reputation thresholds determine additional Cold Coins:

- 12,000,000+ reputation = 10,000
- 9,600,000 = 9,000
- 8,400,000 = 8,000
- 7,200,000 = 7,000
- 6,000,000 = 6,000

In organized alliances that actively farm gates and push faction reputation, reaching 9.6M–12M is realistic.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/image-37.png)

If you hit the maximum threshold:

25,000 (Personal)

- 10,000 (Alliance Reputation)
= **35,000 Cold Coins**

This is your stable baseline.

### Summary

- 35,000 coins is achievable without winning the season.
- Reputation farming is critical.
- This is not enough for a full skill on its own.

---

## Alliance Ranking Rewards

Ranking rewards are performance-based:

- 1st place (winners only) – 6,000
- 2nd – 5,000
- 3rd – 4,000
- 4th–8th – 4,500
- 9th–50th – 3,000

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/image-38.png)

The important detail is the spread:

The difference between first place and minimal ranking is only **3,000 Cold Coins**.

Let’s assume best case ranking without objectives:

25,000 (Personal)
10,000 (Reputation)
6,000 (Rank 1)

= **41,000 Cold Coins**

41,000 ÷ 800 = 51 shards

Even as Rank 1, you are still short of 60 shards.

This is why some alliances win KvK by reputation but still cannot unlock a full skill.

### Summary

- Ranking helps, but the difference is relatively small.
- Rank 1 without objectives is not enough.
- Ranking alone does not decide skill completion.

---

## Objective Control: The Real Deciding Factor

## Ymir

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/image-40.png)

Holding Ymir at the end of KvK grants:

- **9,000 Cold Coins**

This is the single largest variable reward.

If we add Ymir to the previous Rank 1 scenario:

25,000 (Personal)
10,000 (Reputation)
6,000 (Rank 1)
9,000 (Ymir)

= **50,000 Cold Coins**

50,000 ÷ 800 = 62 shards

Now you unlock a full skill and have shards remaining.

The difference between “almost” and “guaranteed” is entirely Ymir.

## Citadel

Holding a Citadel grants:

- **3,000 Cold Coins**

If we add just a Citadel but not Ymir to the previous Rank 1 scenario:

25,000 (Personal)
10,000 (Reputation)
6,000 (Rank 1)
3,000 (Citadel)

= **44,000 Cold Coins**

44,000 ÷ 800 = 55 shards. You are short 5 shards!

And just in case you are thinking, *'well rank one must mean Ymir is yours?*' Nope.. I literally just finished our first Conquest P1 season in first place but without Ymir.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/image-33-1024x505.png)

## Practical Player Perspective

After tracking Cold Coin totals across multiple seasons, the pattern is consistent:

- Personal activity guarantees steady progress.
- Reputation farming builds a strong foundation.
- Ranking matters, but not decisively.
- Ymir determines whether you unlock a full skill in a single season.

Many players focus on leaderboard placement. In practice, the more important question is strategic:

**Can your faction secure and hold Ymir at the end of the season?**

That single objective swings your Cold Coin income more than ranking ever will.

---

## Final Conclusion

If you are asking:

### “Can I get a full skill from my KvK season?”

The answer depends on this checklist:

- Did you max personal contribution?
- Did your alliance reach high reputation thresholds?
- Did your faction hold Ymir at the end?

If the answer to all three is yes, then one full skill per KvK is realistic and repeatable.

If Ymir is missing, even a Rank 1 finish is usually not enough.

Over multiple seasons, this difference compounds significantly. Choosing a faction capable of controlling Ymir consistently is one of the most important long-term progression decisions you can make in KvK.
