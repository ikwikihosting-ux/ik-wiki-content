## 1. Why Immortal Price Tags Matter

When you start in *Infinity Kingdom*, it’s easy to assume that the most expensive immortals are automatically the strongest. While there’s some truth to that—many pay-walled immortals are powerful—the reality is more nuanced:

- **Some of the best immortals are completely free** (🆓) and obtainable with only time and effort.
- **Some expensive immortals are underwhelming** in practice, especially outside their niche.
- Price tags are heavily influenced by *availability* rather than pure *combat strength*.

Understanding the cost classification of immortals helps you:

- **Plan your builds** around what you can realistically obtain.
- **Avoid wasting resources** on overpriced or short-lived options.
- **Maximize your investment** in Golden Path, seasonal events, and lucky draws.
- **Stay competitive** without overspending.

Example: In **Fire**, Empress Wu (🆓) is one of the strongest DPS immortals in the game and costs nothing. But for an optimal build, she shines brightest alongside **Trajan** (❄️), who can be much harder to get.

## 4. Price ≠ Power: Understanding the Difference

Not all costly immortals are top-tier, and not all free immortals are weak.

### Examples:

- **Empress Wu (🆓)** – One of the best AoE DPS immortals in the entire game, no spending required.
- **Trajan (❄️)** – Mandatory for many high-end Fire builds but locked behind KVK.
- **Himiko (💰)** – Pricey and powerful, but niche outside of Shadow setups.
- **Cao Cao (💰👑)** – Can be costly early, later cheap but paywalled. Only support for Lightning, and all substitutes are equally or more expensive.

## 5. How Price Tags Change Through the Seasons

Immortal pricing isn’t static. Seasonal progression changes accessibility:

- **Season 1:** Shadow and Holy are the most expensive elements; their key immortals are mostly locked in *Roulette* events. Chaos is unavailable.
- **Season 2:** Costs for older expensive immortals drop as new acquisition paths open (e.g., **Galaxy** and **Harbor**). Shadow/Holy become easier to obtain, freeing resources for other priorities.
- **Conquest Season:** Many once-expensive immortals become nearly free through events and market offers. But *newly released immortals* take their place as the premium chase units—like Chaos immortals in Conquest (🐳 tier).

**Key takeaway:** If you can wait, some of the most expensive immortals become accessible for free or much cheaper in later seasons. But in competitive servers, early access still has value.

---

## 6. Spending Strategy: Golden Path & Events

If you plan to spend:

- **Golden Path:** Great for guaranteed shards of targeted immortals; use it for those in your endgame build.
- **Roulette Events:** High risk, high reward. Only spin heavily for immortals you *must* have immediately.
- **Market Purchases (📜):** Gem-only immortals are reliable long-term goals; buy consistently when they appear.
- **KVK Exclusives (❄️):** Save KVK coins and plan early; missing them can block entire builds.

If you’re **F2P or low spender**:

- Focus on the strongest 🆓 immortals in your element first.
- Use events for shard boosts but avoid burning all resources on early hype.
- Time your efforts with seasonal price drops.

---

## 7. Building Around Price and Availability

- **Mix Free & Paid Wisely:** Example: Fire build → Wu (🆓) + Hippolyta (🆓) + Frederick I (🆓) + Trajan (❄️).
- **Plan for KVK Gates:** Don’t commit to a build where *multiple* key immortals are ❄️-locked unless you’re sure you can get them.
- **Prioritize Core Units:** Get the immortals that define your team’s playstyle first, even if that means delaying secondary supports.

---

## 8. Final Advice

Knowing immortal price classifications is not just about “who’s expensive.” It’s about:

- **Planning your growth path.**
- **Avoiding wasted investment.**
- **Getting the most powerful teams for your budget.**
- **Adjusting to seasonal price shifts.**

The strongest players—F2P and whales alike—are the ones who combine *timing, planning, and smart spending* with a deep understanding of the immortal economy.
