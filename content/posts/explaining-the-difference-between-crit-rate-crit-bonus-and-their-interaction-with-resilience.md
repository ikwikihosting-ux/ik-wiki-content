## Introduction

Critical hits are one of the defining damage mechanics in *Infinity Kingdom*. Nearly every high-damage lineup in the current meta relies on them in some form, especially ranged carries and magic-focused backlines that can stack offensive stats through artifacts, talents, research, and conquest equipment.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-49.png)

The problem is that many players confuse **Crit Rate** and **Crit Bonus**, or misunderstand how **Resilience** interacts with them. The game’s wording is not always perfectly clear, and some older explanations in community discussions mixed the mechanics together.

In practice, these are three separate systems:

- **Crit Rate** determines *whether* a critical hit happens.
- **Crit Bonus** determines *how much extra damage* the critical hit deals.
- **Resilience** reduces the *chance* of receiving a critical hit.

Understanding the difference matters because optimizing crit builds is not just about stacking every offensive stat available. A lineup with high Crit Bonus but low Crit Rate will produce inconsistent damage, while a lineup facing heavy Resilience may fail to crit reliably at all.

---

## Crit Rate: The Chance to Land a Critical Hit

Crit Rate determines the probability that an attack or skill becomes a critical hit. It controls how often your immortal is able to trigger bonus damage during combat.

A critical hit is essentially an amplified version of a normal attack. When a crit occurs, the game applies additional damage based on the immortal’s Crit Bonus stat. Crit Rate itself does not increase damage directly — it only increases the likelihood of triggering those stronger hits.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-50-1024x372.png)

For example:

- 10% Crit Rate means critical hits occur infrequently and highly unreliable
- 30–40% Crit Rate creates much more reliable burst output relying on RNG to connect with the crits you need
- 60-80% Crit Rate allows carries to crit consistently enough throughout a fight and is what you are aiming for if doing a crit-centric build

This becomes especially important for ranged and magic backline immortals because they attack repeatedly over long engagements. The more often they attack, the more value they gain from reliable critical hit frequency.

---

## Sources of Crit Rate

Crit Rate can come from many systems, including e.g:

- Artifacts
- Talent specialization trees
- Mythic equipment modifiers
- Research/technology
- Skills and buffs
- Certain immortal passives

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-51.png)

Backline damage dealers benefit the most because they attack consistently and usually scale harder from burst damage.

For instance, when I tested crit-focused Wind and Lightning setups in late-season Conquest, increasing Crit Rate often produced a larger real damage increase than adding raw attack once the lineup already had decent scaling. Consistent crit frequency tends to outperform occasional oversized hits.

## What Crit Bonus Does - Crit Damage Scaling

Crit Bonus determines how much additional damage a critical hit deals after it successfully triggers. While Crit Rate controls the chance to crit, Crit Bonus controls the strength of the crit itself. Crit Bonus appears in legendary season on equipment and prior to that through more explicit descriptions such as on Trajan's ultimate, Williams Unique Artifact, Artifact Annihilation Bonus, etc. The wording of Crit Bonus however, appears on mythic equipment modifiers and caused a great deal of confusion.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-53-1024x529.png)

In simple terms:

- Higher Crit Rate = critical hits happen more often
- Higher Crit Bonus = critical hits hit harder

Crit Bonus only activates when a critical hit occurs. If an attack does not crit, Crit Bonus provides no benefit to that hit.

For example, an immortal with high Crit Bonus but low Crit Rate may occasionally land extremely large burst hits, but overall damage can still feel inconsistent because critical strikes are not occurring often enough. On the other hand, combining strong Crit Rate with strong Crit Bonus creates the high burst damage profiles commonly seen in late-game PvP lineups.

The game itself describes Crit Bonus as affecting:

> “The amount of damage dealt after a critical hit.”

This is important because many players confuse Crit Bonus with Crit Rate. Crit Bonus does not increase the probability of critting. It is purely a damage amplification stat tied specifically to successful critical hits.

Crit Bonus scales especially well in the late game once players gain reliable Crit Rate through artifacts, conquest equipment, talent specialization, and technology upgrades. At that stage, increasing Crit Bonus can dramatically improve burst potential, particularly for backline carries that already crit consistently.

In actual combat, this is why optimized crit builds tend to scale both stats together. Crit Rate provides consistency, while Crit Bonus converts those successful crits into meaningful kill pressure.

---

## Why Crit Bonus Needs Crit Rate

Crit Bonus has no value if the immortal rarely crits.

That is why strong crit builds usually balance both stats instead of stacking only one.

A common mistake is over-investing into Crit Bonus early while lacking enough Crit Rate to trigger reliable critical hits. In actual combat testing, this often leads to unstable damage patterns where one battle looks excellent and the next feels weak simply because crits did not occur consistently.

Generally:

- **Crit Rate = consistency**
- **Crit Bonus = burst potential**

The best late-game crit carries usually scale both together and prioritizes crit rate as to not leave yourself reliant on RNG.

## Resilience: The Counter to Critical Hits

Resilience is the defensive stat that counters Crit Rate in *Infinity Kingdom*. Its purpose is to reduce the enemy’s chance of landing critical hits against your immortals.

This is one of the most commonly misunderstood mechanics in the game because many players assume Resilience reduces the damage of critical hits directly. It does not.

> Resilience reduces critical hit chance, not critical hit damage.

The game compares the attacker’s Crit Rate against the defender’s Resilience to determine the final probability of a critical hit occurring. If the defender has high Resilience, the attacker will crit less frequently overall.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-54.png)

However, if a critical hit still succeeds:

- The hit deals full critical damage
- Crit Bonus still applies normally
- The crit is not weakened by Resilience itself

For example, a backline carry with very high Crit Rate may normally crit consistently throughout a fight. Against a target stacking Resilience, many of those potential crits may instead become normal hits, significantly lowering the carry’s burst consistency.

This is why Resilience becomes increasingly valuable in PvP. As players gain access to stronger artifacts, conquest equipment, technology upgrades, and optimized talent setups, crit-focused lineups become much more common. Resilience acts as a direct counter to those builds by suppressing how often dangerous burst windows occur.

Resilience is arguably one of the most underestimated attributes in *Infinity Kingdom*, largely because its impact is less visible than raw offensive stats. Many players focus entirely on increasing damage output, while overlooking how much effective damage can be prevented simply by suppressing enemy crit frequency.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-55-1024x607.png)

Even many top-tier meta lineups benefit enormously from defensive anti-crit setups, especially through sources like Malice and optimized conquest gearing. In actual high-level PvP, reducing the consistency of enemy burst windows often has a bigger impact on fight outcomes than adding more raw stats to your own carries.

## Conclusion Summary

Critical mechanics in *Infinity Kingdom* are built around three connected but distinct stats:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-48.png)

The key takeaway is simple:

- **Crit Rate controls frequency**
- **Crit Bonus controls damage**
- **Resilience counters crit chance, not crit damage**

Players who understand this interaction can build far more effective carries, optimize burst setups properly, and defend against crit-heavy metas more intelligently.
