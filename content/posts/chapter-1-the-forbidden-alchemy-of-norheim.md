## A New Way to Experience the World of Norheim

For most players, *Infinity Kingdom* is all about building stronger marches, collecting Immortals and competing in Arena or Kingdom vs Kingdom. It's easy to overlook that behind all of those mechanics lies an original fantasy world with its own history, mysteries and memorable characters.

To bring that world to life, the developers have started releasing a brand-new **Infinity Kingdom Lore Series**. Rather than focusing on gameplay, these animated short stories explore the history of Norheim, the events that shaped the kingdom, and the ancient powers hidden beneath its surface.

The first chapter is now available, and it's an excellent introduction to the world behind the game.

Follow the full series on the official Facebook page of Infinity Kingdom, and checkout the first chapter [here](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1358370323167660).

## Why I'm Looking Forward to This Series

One of the things I've always enjoyed about *Infinity Kingdom* is that there's a genuine world behind the gameplay. The campaign, Ruins, Chronicle entries and Immortal stories all reveal small pieces of Norheim's history, but they're often spread across different parts of the game. Unless you're actively looking for them, it's easy to miss how those pieces fit together.

This series has the opportunity to bring all of that together.

Personally, I think it's a fantastic idea. The animation is beautifully produced, the storytelling is engaging, and it's refreshing to see the developers expand on the game's lore instead of focusing exclusively on new gameplay features. If I had one wish, it would simply be for each episode to be a little longer. Five or ten minutes would allow even more time to explore the characters and the world they've created.

Even so, Chapter 1 does an excellent job of establishing the setting and leaves me genuinely interested to see where the story goes next. Understanding more about Norheim, the Gnome invasion and the ancient powers that exist within the world makes the game feel richer and gives greater meaning to the battles we fight every day.
