Prepare yourselves for an exhilarating new game mode coming to Battle Chess! On June 7th, 2023, we are thrilled to announce the launch of Ranked Battle Chess on all servers. It's time to elevate your gaming experience and delve into the world of competitive chess like never before!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/image-4-1024x550.png)

## Ranked Mode

1. Ranked Matches: **Open daily from 14:00 to 16:00 and again from 22:00 to 24:00** during each season. Engage in intense battles against opponents who are matched based on similar rankings and skill levels. Please note that ranked matches can only be played during specific restricted time slots.
2. Rewarding Progress: After each ranked match, players will earn tier points based on their performance. These points contribute to your ranking and a unique matchmaking rating (MMR), which plays a significant role in determining your seasonal standings.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/image-6-1024x117.png)

### Ranking and Tiers

Climb the ranks and reap the rewards! Battle Chess features 10 distinctive ranks for players to achieve. At the end of each season, players can claim rewards corresponding to the rank they achieved. Think of it as similar to the rewards system in the arena. Tier and ranking rewards can be claimed once per season, adding an extra layer of excitement to your competitive journey.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/image-5-1024x523.png)

### Seasons

Each season lasts for 30 days, providing ample time for players to showcase their strategic prowess. As a season concludes, participants will be rewarded based on their final ranking, and a fresh start awaits with the beginning of a new season. Get ready for a continuous cycle of thrilling competitions!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/image-7-1024x105.png)

### Eligibility

To participate in Ranked Battle Chess, servers that have completed the Chronicle - Royal Ambition are eligible. In eligible servers, Lords who have reached level 11 and above can engage in the captivating world of Battle Chess.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/image-8-1024x339.png)

Mark your calendars and set your alarms for Wednesday at 14:00, as we kick off the very first time slot for Ranked Battle Chess. Join us as we embark on this exciting journey and take chess to unprecedented heights!
