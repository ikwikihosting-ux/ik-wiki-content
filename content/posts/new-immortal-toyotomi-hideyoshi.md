Toyotomi Hideyoshi

**Ultimate skill: Myojin Deterrence**
Creates a General's Blessing to all our units for 9 seconds, increase their dodge value by x% and each time they dodge an enemy attack, increase their damage by x% and reduce damage they recieve by x% for 3 seconds.

**Dodge Rate Scaling**:
4.5% / 6% / 7.5% / 9% / 10.5% / 12% / 13.5% / 15%

**Damage Increase + Recieved Damage Reduction Scaling**
4.5% / 6% / 7.5% / 9% / 10.5% / 12% / 13.5% / 15%

Infinity Kingdom have a new Immortal taking the field. Toyotomi was one of the "Great Unifiers" of Japan. Now he has risen to take up the fight in Norheim against those pesky physical meta-march-whales! Wind finally has a real support frontliner, and an extreemly strong one of that!

This guy is a game changer for dodge builds to counter the physical meta builds for earth and holy in Infinity Kingdom 2.4.

### General Info

- Role: Frontline support tank (**buffs & utility**)
- Wind element
- Spearmen Troops

## Why is Toyotomi interesting?

His ultimate ability is very interesting for a number of reasons:

First of all, we are finally able to replace King-Arthur with an Immortal that provides solid synergizing buffs and utility to our wind marches. What can wind really do well in Infinity Kingdom? It can counter holy. That is the role of a wind march in my opinion. Why you ask? Because Wind has a combination of strong sustained healing/utility, insane dodge stats to counter the many physical damage instances of holy, has access to wounds, and is excellent at back-row peeling the enemy. All of this makes is perfect counter to holy, if you build it right. Building it right, means running a dodge-exploiting build, taking advantage of Wind's superior dodge chance to counter the physical damage dealers. Since physical damage can be dodges, we are able to massively reduce the amount of damage taken.

This is where Toyotomi comes into the picture. Not only does he provide an extreemly strong dodge buff to our build, we are also getting a very very strong defensive and offensive buff every time we dodge. Since we are running a dodge build, in training grounds testing I have 90% uptime on Myojin Defense and Damage buff! That is MASSIVE for any setup.

On the drawback side, you do HAVE to run this as a dodge build. That means you are weak against shadow and fire as well as other magic heavy setups. Moreover, you MUST run Toyotomi with Missleading, After Image in order to get any effect. If you are able to swap in Manco for Barca running Toyotomi + Manco + Atalanta + Qin, works very very well to counter holy setups.

## How to unlock him?

He is one of the new Immortals being released as the reward from Legion of Frostborne's **conquest**season P2.

He will be in the SvS boxes, and available in the shop afterwards. The shop has 3 types of available bundles resetting daily.

1. **The first bundle costs 1.99$ and contain 1x chest with 5-10 Fragments inside.**
2. **The second bundle costs 2.99$ and contain 1x chests also with 5-10 Fragments inside each.**
3. **The third bundle costs 4.99$ and contain 2x chests with 5-10 fragments inside each.**

You will get around 30 Fragments for 9$ **= around 18$ to unlock** him. See below for info on **maxing**him.
