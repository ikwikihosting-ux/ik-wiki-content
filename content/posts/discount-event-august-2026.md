## GTA Store Time-Limited Sale: Best Packs to Buy

The **GTA Store Time-Limited Sale runs from August 20–23, 2026**, offering increased amounts of several currencies used for Divine Skills, Chaos Immortals, Thea’s Roulette, and Rune Factory progression.

Not every pack is equally useful. The best value in this sale comes from **Rune Factory Energy Cores and Divine Coins**, while the other offers depend much more on what you are currently building.

## Divine Coins

The limited-time Divine Coin packs give more coins than their normal GTA Store versions.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-96-1024x103.png)

The **$5 pack is an easy choice for anyone still working on Red Skills**. The $10 pack is also reasonable if you have a specific skill you are trying to finish.

I would be more selective with the higher-priced packs. Like most currency offers, the value becomes less attractive as you move into the larger tiers.

For a developed account, Divine Coins are often more useful than putting the same money toward another unfinished Immortal. Taking an important Red Skill from level 7 to 8 can have a much more immediate effect on your main march.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-92-1024x316.png)

## Chaos Eyes

The sale also increases the amount of Chaos Eyes:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-97-1024x135.png)

The value stays consistent through the $20 pack, but I would only buy these with a specific Immortal in mind. Chaos Eyes make the most sense when you are pushing something that is not conveniently available through Rune Factory or another regular seasonal events. **Ares artifacts or speeding to complete Apollo, or Medusa** are examples where buying Eyes may be worthwhile if finishing that Immortal is your current goal.

Otherwise, there is no real need to stockpile them simply because the sale is active.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-93-1024x320.png)

## Thea’s Roulette Tickets

The $5 sale pack provides **12 Roulette Tickets instead of the usual 10**.

Thea’s Roulette is more useful after its recent improvements, particularly when you are working on Dragon progression. If you need **Dragon Crystals for the Holy or Shadow Dragon**, the extra tickets make this a reasonable purchase.

I would still avoid using Roulette as my main way of developing Holy or Shadow Immortals. There are better opportunities for those through the monthly event. For me, the main reason to buy these tickets is Dragon Crystals.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-94-1024x319.png)

## Rune Factory Energy Cores

The **Rune Factory Energy Core packs are the standout offer in this sale**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-98-1024x106.png)

The normal $5 GTA Store pack gives 15 Energy Cores, so getting 18 for the same price is already a good improvement. More importantly, these packs have a **daily purchase limit**.

The $10 pack keeps exactly the same value as the $5 pack: twice the price for twice the Cores. If you are currently building **Ninetales, Anubis, or Rune Factory artifacts**, I would prioritize both of these packs before looking at most of the other offers.

The higher-priced Energy Core packs are less attractive. They can still save time if you are trying to finish something quickly, but they are no longer an automatic purchase.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-95-1024x321.png)

## Red Skills or Chaos Immortals?

If you are deciding between developing another Chaos Immortal and finishing the Red Skills on your main march, I would usually finish the march first.

Red Skills affect a team you are already using. Another Chaos Immortal does very little until it is developed enough to actually replace something in that team.

For example, if your main march is already strong but **Arcane Hunter or Divine Blessing is sitting at level 7**, putting resources into that remaining skill progression can be more useful than starting another expensive Immortal project.

There are exceptions when a new Immortal completes a specific build, but as a general rule, **finish what you are using before spreading resources into another project**.

## Final Recommendation

The **August 20–23 GTA Store sale is mainly worth looking at for Rune Factory Energy Cores and Divine Coins**.

The $5 and $10 Energy Core packs are the best offers in the current sale because of their improved Core count and daily purchase limit. Divine Coins come next for anyone still developing important Red Skills.

***[Link to event here.](https://shop.gtarcade.com/en/game?game_id=408&tab=shop&sort=&classifier=0)***

Chaos Eyes and Thea’s Roulette Tickets are more situational. Buy them when they directly support something you are already working on rather than simply because they are part of the sale.

For an established account, that is the simplest way to judge these offers: **buy the packs that finish useful progression, not every pack that happens to be discounted.**
