**Elemental Protection Skills – Are They Worth Using?**

*A breakdown of one of the most underused skill families in Infinity Kingdom*

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-24.png)

---

## 🔍 What Are Elemental Protection Skills?

Elemental Protection Skills are **situational Tower of Knowledge skills** that provide passive effects tied to specific elements (Wind, Lightning, Earth, Water, and Fire). They:

- Have a **55% chance to trigger every 12 seconds**, lasting for 12 seconds.
- Offer a **base effect** usable by any Immortal.
- Provide a **bonus effect** when used on an Immortal that matches the skill’s element.

These effects sound appealing — but they rarely make the cut for serious PvP or high-tier play.

---

## 🧪 Skill-by-Skill Breakdown

### 🌪️ Wind Protection

- **Effect**: 25% dodge rate boost (55% chance every 12s).
- **Bonus**: If a Wind Immortal dodges, it gives a shield to a random ally (120% damage absorb).

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-25.png)

**Verdict**: Too RNG-heavy. Dodge isn't a reliable mechanic, and the shield goes to a random ally. Better to use **Agile** or skip altogether.

---

### ⚡ Lightning Protection

- **Effect**: Grants a 40% chance for normal attacks to stun for 3s (triggered every 12s).
- **Bonus**: If used by a Lightning Immortal, their next ultimate gets +30% damage after a stun.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-26.png)

**Verdict**: Decent base stun, but the chain of effects (trigger → stun → ultimate) is too situational. Can be fun for experimental builds or alt marches.

---

### 🌍 Earth Protection

- **Effect**: Removes Silence/Root and grants 200 energy (55% chance every 12s).
- **Bonus**: For Earth Immortals, every 100 energy regen = +5% ultimate damage.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-27.png)

**Verdict**: Cleanse effect is nice, but bonus is weak. Earth teams rely more on shields and sustain, not burst damage.

---

### 🌊 Water Protection

- **Effect**: Reduces critical damage taken by 21%.
- **Bonus**: If a Water Immortal receives crit damage, they heal for 45% of the damage taken.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-28.png)

**Verdict**: Could work versus crit-heavy teams (e.g. Seondeok), but inconsistent. Could support Merlin builds, but better survivability options exist.

---

### 🔥 Flame Protection

- **Effect**: Increases critical hit rate by 6%.
- **Bonus**: Fire Immortals get +21% crit damage on top.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-29.png)

**Verdict**: The most viable of the bunch. Can make Fire marches hit very hard — perfect for secondary marches focused on burst damage.

---

## ❌ Why They’re Rarely Used

- **Unreliable Activation**: 55% chance means you can go entire battles without effects triggering at the right time.
- **Too Niche**: Bonus effects only work on element-matching Immortals, limiting flexibility.
- **Better Options**: Skills like **Adrenaline Rush**, **Shelter**, **Weakness**, or **Rage Blessing** offer consistent, team-wide value.

---

## ⚔️ When to Use Them (If At All)

Here are some niche cases where Elemental Protection Skills *might* be useful:

| Skill | Niche Use Case |
| --- | --- |
| Wind Protection | Dodge-based PvE setups, alternative shield builds |
| Lightning Protection | Crowd control builds in secondary marches |
| Earth Protection | Silence-heavy matchups (e.g. Mysterium bosses) |
| Water Protection | Anti-crit defense vs burst teams |
| Flame Protection | Secondary Fire burst marches with Wu or Hippolyta |

---

## 🧙‍♂️ What About Holy, Shadow, or Chaos Protection?

These don’t exist yet — but here's what they *could* look like:

- **Holy Protection**: Reduces healing or energy regen for enemies.
- **Shadow Protection**: Reduces magic damage taken and resists Silence.
- **Chaos Protection**: Counters Confusion or random debuffs.

Would they change the meta? Probably not — but they’d be fun tools for creative builds.

---

## 🧾 Final Verdict

Elemental Protection Skills are **themed and interesting**, but rarely provide enough value to be worth a skill slot. Use them:

- For fun or experimentation.
- In lower-tier marches.
- When you’re out of better options.

Otherwise, your skill slots are better spent on **tried-and-true options** that work in all situations, not just when RNG cooperates.
