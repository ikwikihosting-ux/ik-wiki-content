## What Is the Jadwiga Creation Design Event?

The event challenges players to create original content inspired by Jadwiga, the recently introduced Immortal.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261029-104351-ph7xbc.png)

There are very few restrictions on what you can create. The organizers explicitly welcome different creative formats, including:

* **Artwork and illustrations:** Draw or digitally design your own interpretation of Jadwiga.
* **Wallpapers and posters:** Create beautiful or dramatic artwork featuring the new Immortal.
* **Immortal skins and outfits:** Design your own outfit concept for Jadwiga.
* **Stories and lore:** Write an original story inspired by Jadwiga and the world of Infinity Kingdom.
* **Creative character concepts:** Explore Jadwiga's personality, emotions, or alternative appearances.

Hand-drawn artwork, Photoshop creations, and **AI-assisted designs are all allowed**, provided submissions are original, comply with the rules, and remain recognizable as Jadwiga.

The organizers also encourage participants to include design notes explaining their creative decisions, such as color choices, inspiration, and character details.

## Free Rewards for Everyone for consistent participation!

Simply participating with an eligible submission earns a reward package.

That's a nice collection of resources for creating something fun and sharing it with the community.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261029-104324-7y59g9.png)

### Additional Rewards for the Top 10

The Infinity Kingdom team will select **10 outstanding submissions**, whose creators will receive additional rewards:

These are additional to the standard participation rewards, giving selected creators a total of 2,000 Gems, 12 Philosopher's Stones, and other useful items.

## Exclusive Creator Avatar Frame

There's also a special reward for players who regularly participate in these community events.

Players who have participated in **three Creator Theme Events in total** will receive an exclusive Creator Avatar Frame.

So even if you're not particularly interested in competitive art contests, participating now can help you work toward this collectible cosmetic reward.

## How to Participate

To enter, simply create your Jadwiga-inspired submission and post it in the designated[ **\#creator-event・jadwiga-creation-design**](https://discord.gg/QhMVhAzduC) channel on the official Infinity Kingdom Discord server.

Make sure your work is original, clearly connected to Jadwiga, and follows the event's design requirements.

**Submission deadline: November 8, 2026.**
