Personal Legion’s Marks measure your individual activity during Legion of Frostborne, commonly called KvK. They determine your personal settlement reward, making them one of the most important resources to farm before the season ends.

The main target is **10,000,000 Personal Legion’s Marks**. Reaching it awards:

* **3,000 Gems**
* **25,000 Cold Silver**
* **20× 3-hour Speedup Chests**

That Cold Silver represents more than half the 48,000 required to unlock a complete KvK Ability Stone skill, so reaching 10 million Marks should be a priority whenever possible.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261008-202529-ntj0fo.png)

## 💠Where to Check Your Personal Marks

Open the **Legion Handbook** and select the Personal Legion’s Mark reward page.

Do not use the internal alliance ranking to check your total. That leaderboard resets weekly and can display a much higher accumulated figure. The Legion Handbook shows the seasonal total that determines your final reward.

## 💠The Best Method: Repeated Wall Attacks

For most players, repeatedly attacking the walls of an available city or checkpoint is the fastest and most accessible way to farm Personal Legion’s Marks.

When an objective is available:

1. Declare war and begin the 15-minute attack window.
2. Position your castle as close to the objective as possible.
3. Continuously attack with all four marches.
4. Stop before the objective is completely captured.
5. Wait through the 30-minute Protection Barrier.
6. Declare war again and repeat the process.

The objective must remain uncaptured. Capturing it removes the opportunity to continue farming repeated attack cycles.

A recorded example increased the player’s total from **4,951,812 to 5,176,283 Marks**, a gain of **224,471 Marks** from roughly 20 attacks. This should not be treated as a fixed rate: the amount earned depends partly on the damage dealt by each march.

Because four marches can complete far more than 20 attacks during a full siege window, a well-positioned player can generate a very large number of Marks from each cycle. You then wait just 30 minutes before being able to run at the very same gate again!

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261008-202543-obv9zw.png)

## 💠Cities Versus Gates

Cities are generally faster to farm because nearby castle positions can provide extremely short marching distances. However, gates are more commonly left available for repeated farming and remain an excellent option.

The ideal position allows a march to reach the objective and return within a few seconds. Shorter marching time means more attacks during the 15-minute window and therefore more Personal Legion’s Marks.

Do not occupy the best position after you finish. If you are going offline, relocate away from the objective so another federation member can use it. One practical option is to begin your relocation shortly before farming; your city will move automatically once the relocation timer finishes.

## 💠Preparing Your Marches

Use four marches equipped with Immortals and Dragons. They do not need to be complete competitive PvP formations, as even secondary marches can deal meaningful wall damage.

For efficient farming:

* Use the **Military Lord Talent** tree.
* Select **Riding Fast** to reduce marching time.
* Select **Survival Methods** to reduce troop losses.
* Position your castle immediately beside the objective.
* Avoid consuming valuable attack and defence boosts unless necessary.
* Monitor your seriously wounded and dead troops between cycles.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261008-202738-7akr4j.png)

Wall farming normally produces relatively limited casualties compared with sustained PvP or Honor trading. This makes it particularly suitable for free-to-play players and developing accounts that cannot afford to empty their troop bank.

## 💠Other Ways to Earn Personal Legion’s Marks

### Fighting for Honor

Honor earned against players outside your federation also contributes to Personal Legion’s Marks. Active PvP can therefore generate Marks naturally while helping your federation contest territory.

Some players arrange Honor trades with members of opposing federations. When properly organised, a player using **Rank Advancement** can earn large amounts of Honor from attacking a relocation containing four defending marches.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261008-202754-3tkwpg.png)

This can be very fast, but it is expensive in troops and hospital capacity. It is best used when your troop bank is healthy and no major battle is approaching.

### Donating Resources

Resources can be donated to restore the defenders of a city or checkpoint owned by your federation. These donations provide Personal Legion’s Marks as well as Alliance Points.

This is a safe method with no troop losses, but developing accounts should be careful. Buildings, technology, troop tiers and Dragon upgrades should normally take priority over donating hundreds of millions of resources.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261008-202841-gg365g.png)

Donation farming makes the most sense when:

* Your important building upgrades are complete.
* You have a large surplus of resources.
* You also need Alliance Points for Portals.
* Your federation genuinely needs the objective restored.

### Gnomes and Gnome Bosses

Defeating Gnomes and Gnome Bosses also contributes to Personal Legion’s Marks. This provides a steady source while farming experience, equipment and upgrade materials.

However, do not destroy your troop bank merely to gain more Marks. Farm levels that your marches can defeat efficiently, use your strongest sustainable formations and leave enough troops available for sieges and PvP. 

While gnomes farming has to be included here, as it is a source of Marks, this is not your main strategy and AP should be prioritized for PvP and other activities if your supply is at all strained.



### Cities, Checkpoints and Thermal Towers

Normal participation in Legion of Frostborne also generates Marks through activities such as:

* Defeating city and checkpoint defenders.
* Damaging city and checkpoint walls.
* Constructing Thermal Towers.
* Completely demolishing enemy Thermal Towers.
* Damaging enemy territories and forcing relocations.
* Participating in PvP battles.

These activities are especially valuable because they produce personal progress while directly helping your alliance and federation control the battlefield.

## 💠Personal Marks and Federation Reputation

Repeated wall attacks are valuable on several levels.

The player earns **Personal Legion’s Marks**, the alliance earns **Alliance Reputation**, and that reputation contributes to the federation’s overall score. A coordinated gate-farming programme can therefore work toward:

* The player’s 10 million Personal Marks reward.
* The alliance’s 12 million Reputation reward.
* The federation’s overall ranking.
* Individual and alliance seasonal rankings.

This is why gate farming remains useful even when your federation is not likely to win the entire KvK. Players can still secure personal Cold Silver while helping their alliance reach its own settlement threshold.

## 💠Marks and Legion Achievements Are Different

Personal Legion’s Marks should not be confused with **Legion Achievements**.

Personal Marks accumulate throughout the season and determine your personal settlement reward. Legion Achievements are individual objectives that award Expedition Battle Pass experience and, during Legendary Season, may also provide Contract Crystals.

Many activities contribute to both systems, but the rewards and progress counters are separate. Gathering resources may advance an achievement, for example, without being an efficient direct method of farming Personal Marks.

## 💠Recommended Farming Routine

For most players, the best routine is:

1. Earn Marks naturally through battles, objectives, towers and Gnome farming.
2. Preserve your troop bank during heavily contested phases.
3. Locate an available city or checkpoint during quieter periods.
4. Farm its walls with four marches for the full 15-minute window.
5. Leave the objective uncaptured.
6. Move away when finished so another player can take the best position.
7. Return after the 30-minute Protection Barrier expires.
8. Repeat until you reach 10 million Marks.

Alliance and federation leadership can make this considerably more efficient by assigning different gates, announcing attack windows and rotating nearby positions between players.
