## 💠How Does the 20% Sale Work?

The sale does not necessarily reduce the price shown for each bundle. Instead, you receive a larger quantity of the included items for the original price.

For example, the price that would ordinarily buy **8 coins now buys 10**. This effectively reduces the price per item by 20%, even though the total bundle price remains unchanged.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20260930-135845-27noj9.png)

The promotion covers several different resources, so compare the increased contents with what each bundle ordinarily provides before purchasing.

## 💠Is the Sale Worth Using?

The GTArcade Store generally offers better bundle value than purchasing directly through the game, and receiving another 20% discount on top makes this a good time to bring forward purchases you were already planning to make.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20260930-135710-zan379.png)

Players investing in Chaos Immortals, Holy and Shadow progression, Dragon development or other long-term upgrades may find some excellent value here. However, there are no exclusive rewards tied directly to the sale itself, so there is little reason to purchase resources you do not actually need.

The sensible approach is simple: if you were likely to buy one of these bundles later, buying it during the promotion gives you more for the same money.

## 💠Monthly Cumulative Top-Up Rewards

Purchases from the GTA Store also count towards the **monthly cumulative recharge rewards**. Your eligible spending accumulates throughout the month, with additional rewards unlocking whenever you reach one of the listed thresholds.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20260930-135735-php3gg.png)

For example, the reward levels shown in the store include:

* **$4.99 cumulative recharge:** 14 Random Immortal Fragments, 14 Artifact Schematic Fragment Selection Chests, 2 Precious Dragon Crystal Selection Chests and 520 Gems.
* **$19.99 cumulative recharge:** 20 Random Immortal Fragments, 20 Artifact Schematic Fragment Selection Chests, 4 Precious Dragon Crystal Selection Chests and 2,300 Gems.
* **$999.99 cumulative recharge:** 120 Random Shadow Immortal Fragments, 120 Shadow and Light Artifact Schematic Fragment Selection Chests, 200 Eternal Dragon Soul Chests, 100,000 Gems and the **Official Supporter – Leader Insignia**.

There are additional milestones between these examples, and each reward level can be claimed once per month. This means that purchases made during the 20% sale can provide value in two places: extra items from the discounted bundle itself and progress towards the monthly cumulative rewards.

## 💠New Player First-Purchase Gift

If you have never purchased anything through the GTArcade Store before, your first purchase of **any priced package** also unlocks the one-time Platform First Purchase Gift.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20260930-135745-y7jzgc.png)

The gift contains:

* King Arthur ×1
* Philosopher’s Stone ×9
* Medium SP Recovery ×3
* Medium AP Recovery ×3
* Gems ×100
* **Official Supporter – Common Insignia ×1**

Since any priced package qualifies, even one of the cheapest available purchases can unlock the full gift. The insignia is particularly valuable because insignias provide permanent account bonuses as your collection develops.

This first-purchase gift is separate from both the limited-time sale and the monthly cumulative recharge rewards, allowing an eligible new store customer to benefit from all three simultaneously.

## 💠Promotion Period

**Start:** September 28, 2026
**End:** September 30, 2026

You can find the participating bundles through the official [Infinity Kingdom GTA Store](https://shop.gtarcade.com/en/game?game_id=408).

The sale lasts for only three days, so anyone already planning a GTA Store purchase should inspect the available bundles and monthly reward thresholds before the promotion ends.
