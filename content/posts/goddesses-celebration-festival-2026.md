## Introduction

The 2026 **Goddesses Festival** continues the now-standard seasonal event framework that Infinity Kingdom has settled into over the past year. If you have played recent events, the structure will feel familiar: a battle pass, token-based gacha, alliance contribution system, PvE currency grind, and a grail-based progression layer.

What matters is not just understanding each tab, but how they connect. Tokens convert into grails, grails convert into fragments, and everything feeds into either chaos progression or resource efficiency depending on your account stage.

This guide breaks down every tab, explains the mechanics behind them, and provides practical recommendations based on actual in-game behavior.

---

## Judgment Realm (Battle Pass)

### Structure

- Free + Paid track
- 7 daily missions
- 100 EXP per mission
- ~70 total missions available
- No perfect attendance required for max level

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-48-1024x544.png)

- Troop training (multiple queues strongly recommended)
- Arena battles
- Well of Time
- Market purchases
- Pinnacle Trial
- Glorious Challenge
- Gnome bosses

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-49-1024x660.png)

### Rewards Overview

### Free Track

- 30 Tokens of Light

### Paid Track ($50)

- +170 Tokens of Light
- 7800 Gems
- 60 Castle Skin Fragments (Judgment Realm skin)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-50-1024x487.png)

**[See seperate guide here for more on the skin.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/development/skins/judgment-realm/)**

---

### Player Perspective

The value of the pass is heavily concentrated in the **tokens**, not the cosmetics.

- **200 total tokens (free + paid)**
→ ~865 Eternal Grails on average

That alone can fund:

- One top-tier chaos purchase (845 grails), or
- Mid-tier + leftover

In practice, this is one of the more efficient seasonal passes if your goal is chaos progression rather than visuals.

## Goddesses Celebration (Alliance Gifting)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-51-1024x582.png)

### Mechanics

- Send gifts to alliance members
- Earn:  - Celebration points (ranking)
- Eternal Grails
- Alliance members can claim rewards from your gifts

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-68-1024x527.png)

### Ranking Rewards

- Large gem payouts
- Eternal Grails for alliance
- **Top 3:**  - Temporary *Dark Night Sovereign* (top combat skin)
- *Supreme Ruler* teleport skin

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-52-1024x560.png)

### Player Perspective

This tab is **whale-dominated**. Rankings scale aggressively with spending.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-67.png)

However:

- Even light participation contributes to alliance grail income
- Gift generation from tokens (10% proc) feeds into this system passively

Treat this as:

- A **bonus system for low spenders**
- A **primary competitive track for high spenders**

## Dazzling Wish (Token Spins)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-55-1024x554.png)

### Drop Rates

- 0.45% → 300 grails
- 1.55% → 50 grails
- 3.5% → 25 grails
- 12% → 5 grails
- 72.5% → 1 grail
- 10% → 1 Celebration Gift

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-56.png)

### Expected Value

### 1 Token of Light = 4.325 Eternal Grails (average)

- 0.10 Celebration Gifts

### Practical Benchmarks

- 10 tokens → ~43.25 grails
- 100 tokens → ~432.5 grails
- 200 tokens → ~865 grails

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-57-1024x402.png)

### Player Perspective

Always evaluate tokens using **4.325 grails per token**. You need a lot to progress into e.g. chaos immortals with Grails, meaning the average rates are important here. While you can get lucky always assume average rewards.

Do not overvalue:

- High-roll jackpots (too rare)
- Gift value (too variable)

Treat gifts as bonus alliance value, not core progression.

## Glorious Challenge

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-62-1024x577.png)

### Mechanics

- Use tickets to fight gnomes
- Earn Festival Coins
- Progress unlocks higher difficulty (Hard mode on Day 4)

---

### Strategy Shift (Important)

Older advice: "*Save tickets until Hard Mode*"

That however, does not hold entirely true anymore. Since they changed the shop to hold daily stock instead of event-wide stock of items, you need to think more carefully and obtain currency early too.

Current reality (daily shop stock):

- Early spending is viable **if you spend coins immediately**
- Do NOT overcap coins
- Hard mode is still best for bulk farming

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-63.png)

### Recommended Approach

- Day 1–3:  - Spend tickets only if you need items immediately
- Day 4+:  - Push hard mode
- Bank coins for daily resets

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-64.png)

## Conclusion Summary

The Goddesses Festival follows a familiar structure, but the important layer is the math behind Tokens of Light:

- **1 Token = 4.325 grails (baseline)**
- Token volume directly determines your event outcome

Key takeaways:

- Battle pass value comes from tokens, not cosmetics
- Grail efficiency varies massively by immortal tier
- Daily-stock shops change how you approach Glorious Challenge
- Gift value is secondary—focus on guaranteed grail returns

If you approach the event with clear token-to-grail targets, it becomes much easier to decide where to spend, what to skip, and how to get the most out of the 10-day window.
