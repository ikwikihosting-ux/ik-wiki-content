## Introduction: The Hidden Daily Gem

For many Infinity Kingdom players, the GTarcade App remains an underutilized resource. Tucked into both the desktop client and the mobile app is a small but powerful feature: the **GTarcade Wheel**. While it might look like a gimmicky bonus system at first glance, those who use it daily know better. Over time, this free wheel can build up real value and provide exclusive rewards that bolster your game—especially for free-to-play and low-spend players.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-13.png)

In this guide, we’ll break down how the wheel works, what you can get from it, how much value it brings, and why you should absolutely make it a habit.

---

## How the GTarcade Wheel Works

The GTarcade Wheel is available once per day through the GTarcade desktop or mobile app. Each player gets **three free spins per day**. These spins reward you with **GTarcade Points**, which accumulate in your account and can be spent in the GTarcade Rewards Store.

- Each spin gives between 1 and 1,000 points.
- **Jackpot** spin is 1,000 points – rare but possible (more on this below).
- The system is entirely free and resets daily.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-14-461x1024.png)

## What You Can Get With Points

The GTarcade Points you earn can be spent in the **GTarcade Rewards Store**, which offers:

- **Mysterium Hearts** (for event entries)
- **Gems** (always useful, especially for daily bundle refreshes or speeding up builds)
- **Shields** (important for defense in PvP or when offline)
- **Artifact Fragments** (an extremely rare and valuable reward)
- **Shadow/Holy Dragon Crystals** (for upgrading dragons in late-game)
- **Market Refreshes** (can help significantly with optimizing purchases)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-15-1024x767.png)

If you manage to save up 30,000 points, you unlock access to **premium tier rewards** that include even more impactful items.

## What Kind of Return to Expect

Let’s get real: **you won't hit the jackpot every day**. But that doesn’t mean the GTarcade Wheel isn’t worth your time. Here’s what my own data shows after spinning every day for the past 60 days:

- **Total jackpot wins:** 2
- **Average points per day:** 132
- **Total points earned in 60 days:** ~7,920

This daily trickle might seem small, but it adds up. Two jackpots over two months is very good odds for a free system. Most days you’ll get under 100 points, but the occasional 1,000-point jackpot keeps things exciting and balances the average.

Spending money via Gtarcade you earn 80 points per dollar (roughtly). This means that daily you are getting value of spending one to two dollars per day for free. Nothing huge, but it all adds up - especially if you are free to play player!

If you're consistent, you're looking at around **4,000–8,000 points every 2 months**, which is enough to make meaningful purchases in the Rewards Store.

## Why It Matters for You

This wheel is especially important for players who:

- Don’t spend much money or are fully free-to-play

It takes **less than a minute** to spin all three wheels, and the long-term reward is more than worth the time. You could ignore it and save yourself 60 seconds, or you could be collecting thousands of GTarcade points toward real in-game value every few weeks.
