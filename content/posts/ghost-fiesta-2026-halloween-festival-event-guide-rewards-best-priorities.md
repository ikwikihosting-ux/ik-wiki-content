If you participated in August's Harvest Festival or September's Pirate Festival, most of the activities will feel familiar. Lucky Koi, the festival battle pass, Alchemical Secrets, Dazzling Wish, Pinnacle Trial, Glorious Challenge, and the Grail Shop all return.

However, there are some important changes worth knowing about. The biggest is **Witch Forest**, an entirely new board-based event replacing Fishing Contest, while Fu Fei and Louis IX receive new *Night of Terror* outfits.

Let's go through the event, where to collect your rewards, and what you should prioritize.

## TL;DR – What Should You Focus On?

* **Lucky Koi:** Claim the daily free rewards. For spenders, the five-purchase consolation is one of the best ways to obtain Eternal Grails.
* **Night of Terror Pass:** Complete daily objectives for Tokens of Light, Magic Wands, and other rewards. The $50 pass includes 60 Louis IX outfit fragments and 60 Temple of Edfu fragments.
* **Witch Forest:** The new replacement for Fishing Contest. Collect candy, complete daily milestones, and pursue Louis IX outfit fragments.
* **Alchemical Secrets:** The returning crystal-sorting puzzle. Complete stages for free Magic Wands.
* **Dazzling Wish:** The main source of Fu Fei outfit fragments, but extremely expensive to pursue through random drops.
* **Grail Shop:** Horus is the main Immortal reward, while the 250-Grail Chaos selection chest and 330-Grail artifact chest are particularly attractive.
* **Pinnacle Trial and Glorious Challenge:** Complete the available stages for free event currencies, Magic Wands, Tokens of Light, and additional rewards.

\---------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Lucky Koi – Don't Miss the Best Grail Deal

Lucky Koi returns with its familiar combination of daily free rewards and an optional paid lottery.

Everyone should log in daily and collect the free gift, which includes a Token of Light that can be used in the seasonal events.

For spenders, however, Lucky Koi is one of the most important parts of Ghost Fiesta.

The **$4.99 Koi Gift Pack** provides Tokens of Light and 1,500 Magic Wands, while also entering you into a lottery held every two days.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-140833-oh3jwm.png)

One lucky participant wins the grand prize of 4,250 Eternal Grails and a special reward. But winning the lottery isn't actually the main reason to participate.

Players who purchase all five rounds without winning receive a consolation reward containing **850 Eternal Grails**.

That guaranteed consolation alone makes Lucky Koi one of the most efficient Eternal Grail purchases available in Infinity Kingdom.

**Our recommendation:** For players already spending money on seasonal events, completing the five Lucky Koi purchases should be a priority. The consolation reward is excellent, and the included event resources improve the overall value further.

## Night of Terror – The Festival Battle Pass

The Halloween battle pass follows the familiar 30-level seasonal progression system.

Players complete daily objectives to earn EXP and unlock rewards across the free and premium tracks.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-140854-f9p1jh.png)

The premium $50 option is particularly interesting because it includes:

* 120 additional Tokens of Light
* 60 Louis IX *Night of Terror* outfit fragments
* 60 Temple of Edfu castle decoration fragments
* Additional Magic Wands and event resources

Louis IX requires 60 fragments to unlock his outfit, meaning the pass can provide an immediate outfit unlock once its rewards are collected.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-140925-jwriv1.png)

The Temple of Edfu decoration provides +5% Physical Attack and +5% Magical Attack for Immortals, alongside +5% Building Upgrade Speed.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-140902-nje61w.png)

It's a decent combat decoration, although we generally prefer Troop Attack bonuses over Immortal Attack bonuses because of how troop-based damage scales.

For players considering the pass, the most important rewards are arguably the Tokens of Light and Louis IX fragments. Both have substantial value elsewhere in the event.

## Witch Forest – The New Fishing Contest

The biggest gameplay change in Ghost Fiesta is **Witch Forest**, which replaces the traditional Fishing Contest with a Halloween-themed board game.

Main reward of the Witch Forest - much like the previous fishing event - is Outfit fragments. This time for Louis. 

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141144-fm1fn4.png)

Instead of casting fishing nets, players spend Magic Wands to move around a board containing different rewards.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141002-xhce19.png)

The board features three main types of spaces:

* **Candy Magic:** Awards random candy, contributing toward event progression.
* **Surprise Magic:** Triggers special effects, including reward multipliers or jackpot events, and grants an extra move.
* **Reward spaces:** Provide resource chests and other items directly.

Players can choose different spending multipliers, including ×100. Surprise Magic can increase this further; we've already observed a ×200 multiplier appearing during gameplay.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141014-3zkqus.png)

### Louis IX Outfit Fragments

Witch Forest also introduces the **My Candy Jar** collection, which rewards players for collecting special Evil Pumpkins.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141158-j6ymn3.png)

These are separate from the ordinary candy points used for daily progression.

This gives players another way to obtain Louis IX *Night of Terror* fragments beyond the premium battle pass.

The event also offers daily team rankings and overall personal rankings, providing additional incentives for more competitive players.

**Our recommendation:** Collect the free Magic Wands available throughout the festival before purchasing additional ones. As with Fishing Contest, the most efficient strategy is generally to identify your next outfit milestone and spend toward that target rather than purchasing blindly.

We'll cover Witch Forest's probabilities, expected candy returns, and Magic Wand spending efficiency in a dedicated guide.

## Alchemical Secrets – The Puzzle Returns

Alchemical Secrets returns almost unchanged from the Pirate Festival.

This mini-game challenges players to sort differently colored Primal Stones into matching treasure chests.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141225-mxd4jb.png)

The early levels are straightforward, but later stages introduce frozen chests, hidden stones, and other mechanics that require more careful planning.

Completing the stages provides useful rewards, including Magic Wands for Witch Forest.

Previously completed stages can also be raided to obtain additional rewards without solving the puzzle again.

One important tip from our earlier testing is that **even difficult puzzles can be solved without purchasing additional assistance items**. The optional Unlock item can make levels easier, but careful planning is generally sufficient.

For a complete explanation of the puzzle mechanics and solutions, see our [Alchemical Secrets guide](https://ik-wiki.com/posts/pirate-festival-2026---alchemical-secrets-new-mini-game/).

## Dazzling Wish – Fu Fei's Halloween Outfit

Dazzling Wish returns as the primary event for Fu Fei's new *Night of Terror* outfit.

The wheel offers 20 Fu Fei outfit fragments as its rarest reward, alongside various amounts of Eternal Grails.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141238-ogwn6b.png)

However, the outfit fragment drop probability is just **0.2% per draw**.

That means players need an average of 500 draws for a single 20-fragment reward, before accounting for any separate guarantees or additional sources.

Fu Fei requires 80 fragments to unlock the outfit, so obtaining it through random wheel drops alone is extremely expensive.

In fact, 200 spins produce an expected return of just eight Fu Fei fragments.

For most players, we recommend treating Dazzling Wish as an optional activity rather than a primary spending target.

Players mainly interested in Eternal Grails should strongly consider Wish Grail instead, which generally provides considerably better returns per Token of Light.

## Grail Shop – Horus and the Best Rewards

The Grail Shop returns with several attractive purchases.

The main featured Immortal is **Horus**, available for 845 Eternal Grails per chest containing 80 fragments, alongside his exclusive *Eye of Horus* artifact schematic.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141249-nm3dqj.png)

However, some of the most appealing purchases are the relatively inexpensive selection chests.

The **Fate of Chaos Selection Chest VIII** is particularly attractive, offering a choice of Chaos Immortal fragments, including Medusa, Ares, Apollo, Wukong, Loki, Poseidon, and others.

At 250 Eternal Grails for 80 fragments, this is considerably more accessible than purchasing many of these Immortals through their original premium events.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141300-vfyktq.png)

Likewise, the 330-Grail artifact selection chest provides a relatively affordable source of specialized artifact progression.

For many players, these selection chests may offer better practical value than committing large amounts of currency toward Horus.

**The Challenge Shop** also offers Tokens of Light, Stardust, and additional resources in exchange for the candy currency earned from event activities.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141311-t84fzi.png)

**Our recommendation:** Prioritize the rewards your account actually needs, but give the Chaos fragment and artifact selection chests serious consideration before committing to the most expensive items.

## Pinnacle Trial and Glorious Challenge

Both combat activities return in their familiar formats.

**Pinnacle Trial** rewards players for clearing increasingly difficult stages, providing Magic Wands, random Immortal fragments, Tokens of Light, and event collection rewards.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141324-wkkdo5.png)

Additional stages unlock as the festival progresses, so check back regularly rather than expecting to finish everything immediately.

**Glorious Challenge** also returns with its familiar combat progression.

Completing stages provides candy currency for the Challenge Shop and Magic Wands for Witch Forest.

The higher difficulties unlock later during the event.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-141331-y293sm.png)

These activities are particularly important for free-to-play players because they provide event resources without requiring direct purchases.

Even if you don't intend to compete for ranking rewards, clearing as many accessible stages as possible should be part of your festival routine.

## Final Thoughts – Where Is the Best Value?

Ghost Fiesta is largely a traditional Infinity Kingdom seasonal festival, but Witch Forest and the new outfit rewards provide some welcome variety.

For free-to-play players, the priority should be collecting daily Lucky Koi gifts, completing the free battle pass, clearing Alchemical Secrets and combat challenges, and using the accumulated Magic Wands in Witch Forest.

For low and moderate spenders, **Lucky Koi remains one of the best purchases in the game**, while the premium festival pass offers an attractive combination of Tokens of Light and Louis IX outfit progression.

The Grail Shop also deserves attention, particularly the efficient Chaos Immortal and artifact selection chests.

Meanwhile, Dazzling Wish continues to be a particularly expensive way to pursue the newest seasonal outfit. Unless you're specifically targeting Fu Fei or its ranking rewards, there are generally more efficient places to spend your Tokens of Light.

Ultimately, the strategy remains familiar: **collect your free event resources first, identify which rewards matter to your account, and only spend additional money when it helps you reach a worthwhile milestone.**

Ghost Fiesta may be Halloween-themed, but getting the best value from it is still about careful planning rather than chasing every shiny reward.
