### Ultimate Whale Composition Guide: Season 1 Triple March Setup

For the true Infinity Kingdom whales looking to dominate Season 1, the path to total PvP control lies in constructing and executing not just one, but three fully optimized marches: Holy, Shadow, and Hybrid. Each of these builds is tailored for full synergy, skill efficiency, and immortal specialization. With the recent buffs to Shadow and increasing diversity in builds, we now present the definitive guide to setting up the strongest triple-march lineup possible.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-112-1024x411.png)

This composition assumes all top-tier immortals, unique artifacts, Tower of Knowledge skills, and Immortal Talent trees are maxed. If you're spending at the whale level, this is how you get the absolute most out of your investment.

---

## March 1: Holy – The Ramp-Up Control Breaker

*Refer to our full Holy Whale Build guide for details.*

### Immortal Setup:

**Theodora**: Purify, Shelter, Vitality Touch
**Nebuchadnezzar**: Snipe, Malice, Rage Blessing
**Khubilai Khan**: Full Coverage, Spear Impale, Life-Link
**Manco Cápac**: Void Realm, Victorious Pursuit, Demonic Contract

This march excels in sustained battles, shrugging off control and debuffs through Theodora’s cleanse and ramping Manco’s devastating output with skill stacking and 100% uptime on Victorious Pursuit. Healing and defense keep the team alive through the initial 20 seconds, after which it snowballs hard.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-115-1024x619.png)

---

## March 2: Shadow – Explosive Frontload Nuker

The Shadow team has surged in strength due to Tutankhamun and Tomyris. This version is optimized to shred enemies quickly with unmatched burst damage and oppressive control denial.

### Immortal Setup:

**Himiko**: Annihilation, Weakness, Spell Disintegration
**Tomyris**: Assist, Oaken Guard, Energy Suppression
**Tutankhamun**: Fighting Master, Duel Master, Blade Vortex
**Bjorn**: Guard, Toxin Barrier, Jade Barrier (alternatively, leftover aura skills)

This team has proven to decimate other top-tier marches, especially by leveraging control immunity on Tut and causing confuse with Tomyris. Himiko delivers AoE nukes and backline-controls while Tutankhamun vaporizes the enemy team.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-113-1024x589.png)

---

## March 3: Hybrid Physical – Fast, Flexible, Lethal

This setup leans on synergy between Qin and Zenobia for early buffs and survivability, with Hannibal and Alex delivering fast physical bursts. It's particularly potent against glass cannon or control-based teams.

### Immortal Setup:

**Alexander**: Combo, Dominate, Physical Shield
**Hannibal**: Anger, Cleave, Berserk
**Qin**: Misleading, Resist, Blessing of Speed
**Zenobia**: Sacrifice, Blessing of Defense, Blessing of War

With full Immortal Talents unlocked, this team offers excellent protection and speed for Alex to rapidly bring down priority targets. Hannibal works well here to burst through weakened lines. This march excels in blitz scenarios and squad wars.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-114-1024x606.png)

---

## March 4: Leftover Fire (Optional)

Without access to top-tier skills, the fourth march is best reserved for PvE or clean-up duty. A Fire setup can still be useful for territory fights, low-risk PvP skirmishes, or Spider Boss raids. Use any remaining skills here that do not overlap with the three primary marches.

## Final Thoughts

This triple-march setup gives whales supreme flexibility and control in every PvP format, from throne fights to KvK. By reserving and dividing high-impact Tower of Knowledge skills across these three elite builds, players can pressure, counter, and overwhelm any opponent.
