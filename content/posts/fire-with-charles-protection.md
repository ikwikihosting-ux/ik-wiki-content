This guide is part of my article series on Prime League Illusion Builds. If this build is not to your likeing, have a look at the rest of my builds on the main Prime League page.

**This build is made for:**Round 2 of Pre-league

This article is on fire with Charles. We know fire to be a hefty damage dealer, with awesome nuke potential. Moreover, in Prime league it is the only march that has crit-value enough to make a dps impact and the march with the biggest huge nuke from dragon. What is lacks is sustain power - well well well, not any more!

## Setup

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/image-57.png)

**Empress Wu**: Annihilation + Fire nova + Weakness

**Ashoka**: Death Breath + Cleave + Anger

**Charles**: Fighting Master + Assist + Coercion

**William I**: Adrenaline Rush + Energy Suppression + Malice

## Strengths and weaknesses

This setup is a solid wind-lightning killer. With the high damage nuke it eats through slow-sustain and no-sustain setups quickly. Moreover, since we are running Charles, we are not weak to the wounds debuff from Qin / Khan.

We are weak to hefty sustain from other Charles setups. Like people running pure earth with both Zeno and Charles and perhaps even Berserk/shelter healing. We do not have wounds to help us out.

**In conclusion, a strong march that will kill most things. Great for rallying!**
