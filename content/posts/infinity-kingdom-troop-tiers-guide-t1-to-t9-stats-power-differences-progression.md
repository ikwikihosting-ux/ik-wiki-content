With Legendary Season introducing **Tier 9 troops at Barracks level 60**, it's time to look at how all nine tiers compare, how much stronger each upgrade makes your troops, and why troop attributes become increasingly important as your account develops.

All the statistics in this guide have been collected directly from the in-game Barracks menus in November 2026.

## TL;DR – How Much Stronger Are Higher-Tier Troops?

Moving to a new troop tier increases the base attributes of every soldier of that type.

The early tiers offer particularly large percentage improvements, often increasing Attack and Defense by approximately 50% per upgrade. From T5 onward, progression becomes more gradual, but the bonuses remain substantial.

The latest T8-to-T9 upgrade provides approximately:

* **+19% base Troop Attack**
* **+19% base Troop Defense**
* **+11% base Troop HP**
* **+28% Troop Load**

These are increases to the troops' base attributes, not guaranteed increases to final combat damage or total march power.

By T9, individual soldiers have roughly ten times the base Attack and Defense of their T1 counterparts, while HP has increased by more than four times.

## All Nine Troop Tiers and Unlock Requirements

Infinity Kingdom currently has nine troop tiers, unlocked by upgrading the Barracks responsible for training each troop type.

Each of the four troop types has its own Barracks, allowing players to upgrade their Shieldmen, Spearmen, Cavalry, and Bowmen independently.

When a Barracks reaches the next tier threshold, the existing troops of that type are automatically upgraded. You don't need to retrain your entire army from scratch.

This is why players should generally fill their Training Grounds before completing a major Barracks upgrade. Converting an existing stockpile of soldiers can save substantial resources compared with training large numbers of higher-tier troops afterward.

## Complete Troop Attack Comparison – T1 to T9

Attack is the most obvious offensive attribute. Higher Attack increases the offensive potential of soldiers, although final battle damage depends on several additional mechanics.

The following table shows the base Attack attributes of every troop type.

An interesting observation is that Spearmen have the highest base Attack across every tier, followed by Cavalry. Shieldmen and Bowmen have identical base Attack values.

This doesn't automatically mean Spearmen deal the most total damage in every situation. Attack frequency, targeting, troop counters, Immortal skills, and the combat damage formula all influence the actual result.

Nevertheless, the numerical progression demonstrates how dramatically troop Attack improves as new tiers become available.

For example, T9 Shieldmen have 225 base Attack compared with just 22 at T1. That's more than ten times the starting value.

Even compared with T8, the difference is substantial: 189 Attack increases to 225, a gain of approximately 19%.

## Complete Troop Defense Comparison – T1 to T9

Defense improves the ability of troops to withstand incoming attacks.

As expected, Shieldmen have the highest base Defense, while Bowmen have the lowest.

The Defense improvements follow almost exactly the same percentage pattern as Attack.

At T9, Shieldmen reach 213 base Defense, compared with 179 at T8. That represents another approximately 19% increase.

This is particularly important for frontline Immortals, whose troops must withstand enemy attacks throughout the battle.

However, Bowmen also benefit considerably from higher Defense. Although they normally fight from the back row, additional survivability becomes valuable against area attacks, targeting effects, and abilities that threaten the backline.

## Complete Troop HP Comparison – T1 to T9

HP determines the health available to individual soldiers.

Unlike Attack and Defense, HP scales somewhat differently between tiers, especially after T5.

Cavalry have the highest base HP, reaching 525 at T9. Shieldmen follow with 500, while Spearmen and Bowmen have 465 and 430 respectively.

The difference between T8 and T9 is approximately 11% for most troop types, slightly lower for Bowmen.

HP has grown much less rapidly than Attack and Defense over the complete progression from T1 to T9.

This is an important distinction. Higher-tier soldiers aren't simply scaled-up versions of earlier troops with every attribute increasing by the same percentage.

Attack and Defense have increased roughly tenfold, while HP has increased approximately fourfold.

The relative balance between these attributes therefore changes considerably as players advance through the game.

### Early Tiers – Enormous Relative Gains

The most dramatic percentage increases occur between T1 and T5.

For example, moving from T3 to T4 increases Attack and Defense by approximately 50%, while HP increases by 25%.

These upgrades can make an enormous difference for developing accounts, especially when fighting other players who have not yet reached the same troop tier.

This also explains why reaching an important Barracks milestone can be more impactful than several smaller upgrades to other parts of your account.

### T5 to T8 – More Gradual Progression

Once players reach T5, the relative increases become smaller.

Moving from T5 to T6 provides approximately 24% additional Attack and Defense, while T6 to T7 provides around 20%.

The increase from T7 to T8 is slightly smaller again, at approximately 17%.

However, it's important not to underestimate these improvements.

By this stage, players are commanding much larger armies, and the base attributes of individual troops are already considerably higher.

Even a relatively modest percentage increase can therefore represent a substantial amount of additional raw troop strength.

### T8 to T9 – A Significant Legendary Upgrade

Interestingly, T9 breaks the gradual decline in percentage improvements.

Instead of continuing toward a smaller bonus, the upgrade from T8 to T9 provides approximately 19% additional Attack and Defense, compared with the 17% received when moving from T7 to T8.

The HP improvement is also greater than the previous two tier upgrades.

This makes T9 particularly interesting for players entering Legendary Season.

Reaching Barracks level 60 isn't simply a small additional increase in combat power. It provides a meaningful upgrade to the underlying attributes of every soldier of that troop type.

## T8 vs. T9 – The Exact Differences

Since T9 is the newest troop tier, it's worth looking more closely at its attributes compared with T8.

The percentages are remarkably consistent between the four troop types, with almost identical Attack and Defense improvements.

For practical purposes, players can regard T9 as offering approximately **19% stronger base offensive and defensive attributes** than T8, alongside roughly 11% more base HP.

The upgrade also increases Troop Load from 180 to 230, an improvement of 27.8%. Load helps with resource transport and gathering but isn't itself a combat attribute.

## Which Barracks Should You Upgrade First?

Although every troop tier provides substantial bonuses, upgrading all four Barracks simultaneously isn't always the best use of resources.

This becomes particularly important during Legendary Season, where building upgrades beyond level 55 require Energy Core charging in addition to ordinary resources and speedups.

For most players, the best approach is to prioritize the troop types used in their main march.

Bowmen are often an especially important consideration because both back-row Immortals command Bowmen. This means every standard full march uses two Bowman units, making Bowmen relevant across a wide variety of teams.

However, if your main march relies heavily on powerful frontline damage dealers, upgrading the Barracks associated with those Immortals may be equally important.

The goal should be improving the march you actually use for competitive combat rather than spreading resources across troop types you rarely deploy.

Players preparing for T9 should also remember that reaching Castle level 60 doesn't automatically upgrade every troop type. Each relevant Barracks must reach level 60 to unlock its T9 soldiers.

## Why Troop Percentage Bonuses Are So Powerful

One of the most important lessons from troop progression is how valuable percentage-based Troop attributes become in the late game.

Troop Attack, Defense, and HP bonuses affect the soldiers commanded by your Immortals. As the base attributes of these soldiers increase through higher tiers, the raw attribute increase provided by a percentage bonus grows as well.

Consider a simple example.

A T9 Bowman has 225 base Attack. A 5% Troop Attack bonus adds 11.25 Attack per soldier, before considering the wider combat calculation.

For a hypothetical combined backline of 40,000 Bowmen, that represents an additional 450,000 points in the aggregate base Attack pool.

This illustrates how percentage bonuses scale across a large troop stack. But do remember, they are not literal predictions of actual damage inflicted during a battle, which also depends on defensive attributes, existing bonuses, skill effects, and many other combat mechanics and variables.

Nevertheless, they demonstrate why Troop Attack bonuses are so highly valued.

This is also the reason that the **Abyssal Temple castle skin**, sometimes referred to as Abyssal Throne in community discussions, is considered one of the strongest offensive castle skins in Infinity Kingdom.

Its upgradeable Troop Attack bonus ranges from +5% to +15%, applying a powerful percentage modifier to an increasingly large base of troop attributes. The skin also provides a reduction in soldier death rate.

By comparison, modifiers that improve only an Immortal's personal attributes affect a different part of the combat calculation and may provide less overall benefit when troop-derived attributes dominate.

This doesn't mean that every Troop percentage modifier is automatically better than every Immortal modifier in every situation. Their relative effectiveness depends on the attribute, current bonuses, and the formation involved.

However, for late-game combat, percentage-based Troop Attack bonuses are among the most attractive general-purpose offensive improvements available.

## Final Thoughts – How Important Are Troop Tiers?

Troop tiers remain one of the most important progression milestones in Infinity Kingdom.

From T1 to T9, the base Attack and Defense attributes of soldiers increase by approximately ten times, while HP grows by more than four times.

More importantly, these improvements affect the soldiers you actually bring into battle, providing a larger base for your other Troop attributes and percentage bonuses.

For players progressing through the early seasons, reaching each new troop tier should remain a major development objective.

For experienced Conquest players entering Legendary Season, upgrading from T8 to T9 offers an especially attractive improvement: approximately 19% additional base Attack and Defense, alongside around 11% more HP.

And as troop counts increase, the value of powerful Troop percentage bonuses becomes even more apparent.

**My recommendation:** Prioritize the Barracks used by your main march, prepare sufficient troops before completing major tier upgrades, and don't underestimate the combined effect of higher troop tiers and percentage-based Troop buffs.
