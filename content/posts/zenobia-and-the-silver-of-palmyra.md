### Zenobia and Her Unique Artifact: Silver of Palmyra

In the world of Infinity Kingdom, unique artifacts offer a significant enhancement to an immortal's combat capabilities, tailoring specific powerful effects to their skills. One such artifact, the Silver of Palmyra, is designed exclusively for the immortal Zenobia. This guide explores Zenobia's unique artifact, its effects, and how to acquire it for those looking to optimize their strategies in the game.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-31-1024x625.png)

#### Overview of Silver of Palmyra

The Silver of Palmyra is a unique artifact that enhances Zenobia's ultimate ability, "Empress' Favor," by adding a substantial boost to attack speed. When equipped, the artifact grants the following effect:

- **Empress' Favor Enhancement**: Increases the attack speed of the healing target by 25%.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-32.png)

This effect is particularly potent considering Zenobia's role as a healer in the game. The increase in attack speed can be crucial in battles, especially when paired with immortals like Alexander or Hannibal, who benefit immensely from faster attack speeds due to their high base stats in this area.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-33-1024x387.png)

The synergy created with these immortals can significantly amplify a team's damage output, making Zenobia an even more valuable asset in your lineup.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-34-1024x607.png)

## How to Obtain Silver of Palmyra

The Silver of Palmyra can be acquired through two main avenues in Infinity Kingdom:

- **Stellar Compass Event**:  - In this event, players earn or purchase Crystal Balls, which are then redeemed for artifact fragments. This method is straightforward and accessible during the event period, making it a primary source for players looking to equip Zenobia with her unique artifact.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-35-1024x580.png)

- **5-Day Bundles**:  - Zenobia's artifact can also be obtained through the game's 5-day bundles, where players purchase skill stones. Each day of purchasing yields 5 selection chests containing various rewards, including artifact fragments. On the fifth consecutive day, a bonus is awarded, which can include the Silver of Palmyra. This method is more costly, as it involves daily purchases, but it also provides valuable skill stones and other benefits that can enhance your immortals' abilities.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-36.png)

#### Strategic Implications

Equipping Zenobia with the Silver of Palmyra can drastically alter your battle strategies, particularly in prolonged engagements where sustained healing and increased attack speed can turn the tide. This setup is especially effective in formations that are designed to outlast and overpower the enemy through superior healing and rapid, consistent damage output.

### Conclusion

Zenobia’s unique artifact, Silver of Palmyra, is a game-changer for players who utilize her in their formations. By boosting the attack speed of key immortals, this artifact not only enhances Zenobia's healing efficiency but also significantly increases the overall damage potential of the team. Whether you choose to acquire it through the Stellar Compass event or invest in 5-day bundles, the strategic advantages of equipping this artifact are undeniable. Make sure to integrate it thoughtfully with other team enhancements for maximum effectiveness in your quest for dominance in Infinity Kingdom.
