## A Castle level 20 guide to Water

At Castle level 20, you have limited access to Tower of Knowledge passives. Moreover, you will quite quickly reach level 20 and will not have the most amazing stable of Immortals nor many stars ranks on the Immortals in play. As such, this guide attempts to zero in on the average player and where he or she is at in terms of Immortals and Passives around Castle level 20. It is based on an average player accounts and experiances with where we expect to be at around this progress level. Special thank you to Jessica who has been the primary driver, inspiration and brains behinds all of this!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/image-38-1024x710.png)

## Immortals

At this point in your progress, the best options for Immortal composition is to run**Merlin, Helen, Eulji and Bryn**.

**Note that if you have been able to buy Attila at this early stage or be super lucky and obtain, Hippolytta, Richard or William you want to swap one of these Immortals in to replace Eulji. If you have Attila + one of the other subs swap both Eulji and Bryn to Attila + either of the previously mentioned.**

## Passive (ToK) setup

At this point in your progress, you most likely have **0 stars on Merlin, 7 stars on Helen, 5 stars on Eulji and 5 stars on Bryn**.

**You should not spend your Purple Crystals on upgrading passive levels just yet. Wait untill you reach higher levels to spend this valuable ressource!**

### You should run the following passive setup at this stage:

**Merlin**: no stars

**Helen**: Wisdom (1) + Meteor (1)

**Eulji**: Onslaught (1) *or Combo (1) if you want to do bosses more efficient.*

**Bryn**: Silence (1)
