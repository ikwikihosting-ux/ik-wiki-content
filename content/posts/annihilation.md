### Unleashing Power with Annihilation: A Player's Guide to Dominating in Infinity Kingdom

Welcome, fellow lords and ladies, to a deep dive into one of Infinity Kingdom's most formidable powers: the Tower of Knowledge skill, "Annihilation." As a beacon for free-to-play (F2P) players seeking to amplify their magical onslaught, Annihilation stands out as a quintessential skill for any mage looking to leave a mark on the battlefield. Let's explore how to harness this skill to its full potential, turning your magical immortals into unstoppable forces of destruction.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-136-1024x536.png)

#### Overview of Annihilation

Annihilation is a skill that, by its very nature, promises devastation. With a 45% chance every 6 seconds to unleash 300% magical damage to all four enemy immortals, it epitomizes the essence of "area of effect" (AOE) prowess. This translates to an astronomical 1200% magical damage output each time the skill activates, positioning Annihilation as a top-tier choice for any player keen on maximizing damage.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-143-1024x383.png)

#### Synergy with Critical Builds

The true beauty of Annihilation lies in its compatibility with critical hit-based builds, particularly those emphasizing fire elements. The skill not only delivers massive damage but also serves as a catalyst for the "Chase" skill. Since Chase is triggered by critical hits and Annihilation targets all enemies, it significantly heightens the probability of activating Chase on every Annihilation proc. This synergy is not just effective; it's a game-changer, making Annihilation a cornerstone for any crit-based magical damage setup.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-138-1024x455.png)

#### Optimal Usage of Annihilation

**Choosing the Right Immortal:** Annihilation should be the crown jewel in the arsenal of your most potent mage. Immortals like Himiko, Empress Wu, Baldwin, Hammurabi, and Merlin are prime candidates for this skill, as their inherent strengths and capabilities are magnified through its use.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-139.png)

**Pairing with Chase:** For those committed to a crit-based approach, integrating Chase alongside Annihilation can transform your mages into harbingers of doom. This combination not only ensures high damage output but also leverages the crit mechanism to disrupt and decimate enemy ranks efficiently.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-140.png)

**Strategic Considerations:** Deploying Annihilation demands a strategic mindset. Given its significant impact and the timing of its activation, positioning your mage and timing their attacks in sync with other skills and immortals on your roster can maximize the devastation inflicted upon your adversaries.

#### Building Around Annihilation

To truly capitalize on Annihilation, your strategy should extend beyond skill selection. Consider the following:

- **Gear and Artifacts:** Equip items that enhance magical damage, critical hit rate, and speed. These will ensure that Annihilation's effects are felt more frequently and with greater intensity.
- **Supporting Skills:** Beyond Chase, look for skills that complement Annihilation's damage potential or that provide buffs to your mage's critical hit capabilities.
- **Team Composition:** Surround your Annihilation-wielding mage with immortals who can benefit from the chaos it creates—be it through additional AOE skills, debuffs on enemies, or buffs for your team.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-141.png)

#### Conclusion

Annihilation is not just a skill; it's a statement—a declaration of your intent to dominate the magical battlefield in Infinity Kingdom. By strategically incorporating Annihilation into your lineup, pairing it with synergistic skills like Chase, and optimizing your immortal's build, you can unlock unparalleled levels of magical destruction. Remember, in the hands of a savvy player, Annihilation is more than just a skill; it's the key to turning the tide of battle in your favor. Embrace its power, and let the annihilation begin!
