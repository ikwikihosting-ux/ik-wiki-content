## Respendent Holiday

One of the highlights of the Original Festival is the Resplendent Holiday. Players can log in each day to earn rewards. This is a great way to start the day and earn some valuable prizes. So make sure to log in daily and collect your rewards.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-28-1024x571.png)

## Fertile Harvest

Another exciting activity is the Fertile Harvest. You can collect resources, defeat Gnome armies or Bosses, or collect resources for a certain amount of time to obtain Marvel Chests. These chests contain Frigg Souvenir Coins, which can be used to exchange for rewards during the event. This is a great way to earn rewards while enjoying the game.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-29-1024x572.png)

## Wondrous Celebration

The Wondrous Celebration is also an exciting activity during the Original Festival. By completing daily tasks, players can earn Frigg Souvenir Coins, Multicolor Brushes, and points. Once you reach a certain amount of points, you can unlock a progress chest that contains even more Multicolor Brushes. These brushes can be used to participate in the Colorful Party.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-30-1024x572.png)

## Colorful Party

The Colorful Party is an event that allows you to show off your style by dressing up. By using Multicolor Brushes, players can unlock valuable rewards such as the Original Festival Territory Decoration, Gems, Original Crystals, and more. There are three reward tiers waiting to be unlocked, so make sure to participate in the Colorful Party and earn some amazing prizes.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-31-1024x571.png)

## Surprise Original Festival (redeem shop)

The Surprise Original Festival is another activity that you won’t want to miss. You can exchange your Frigg Souvenir Coins and Original Crystals for your favorite gifts such as the Original Festival Territory Decoration, Avatar Frame, Chat Msg Box, and Nameplate. There are plenty of gifts to choose from, so make sure to check out this section of the event and see what treasures you can uncover. This is also where the prime rewards from this event are from. You can get a unique artifact for your faviorite immortal as well as a full unlock 60 fragments for one of the paywall immortals of choice! **[See article rewards for more info.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/events/spring-fiesta/easter-festival-2023/event-rewards/)**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-15-1024x556.png)

## Vibrant Original Festival (5-step-bundles)

Lastly, the Original Festival Limited-Time Gift is a great way to get even more out of the event. This giftpack contains tons of Original Crystals that you can use to exchange for rewards during the Original Festival. With three tiers to unlock, this is also an opportunity to obtain the cosmetic effects available in this event - including Castle Skin. **[More info here.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/events/spring-fiesta/easter-festival-2023/event-rewards/)**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-32-1024x576.png)

In conclusion, the Original Festival is an exciting event that offers a wide range of activities and rewards. Whether you’re looking to collect resources, defeat Bosses and Gnome armies, or simply dress up and have fun, there’s something for everyone during this event. So make sure to get ready to celebrate the spring season with Frigg’s Rabbit and other players from all over the world!
