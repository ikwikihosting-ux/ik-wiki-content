The *Infinity Kingdom* team frequently hosts community events that offer both free and paid opportunities for players to grow their accounts. These events often take place on the official **[Discord server](https://discord.gg/WydHjQM6w4)** or through **GTarcade** platforms.

### Important Notes

- **Instant Vouchers:** Vouchers purchased during the event are credited to your account immediately.
- **Delayed Rewards:** The first-purchase and five-purchase milestone rewards will be sent to your in-game mail **within 3 workdays after the event ends** (January 20).
- **GTarcade Points:** All purchases made during the event also earn **GTarcade Points**, which can be spent in the **Point Shop**.
