## Introduction

The Insignia system is a permanent progression feature that rewards players for increasing the Affinity of their immortals and collecting cosmetic items such as dragon skins, wonders, and immortal portraits. Unlike equipment or artifacts, Insignias provide **account-wide permanent attributes** rather than bonuses for individual marches.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-6-1024x564.png)

Because several parts of the interface are cosmetic while others grant real power, the system can be confusing at first. Understanding the difference is essential before deciding where to invest your Affinity resources.

---

## What Are Insignias?

Insignias represent milestones earned by collecting and upgrading various items throughout the game. Every Insignia contributes toward your overall collection and may provide permanent attribute bonuses.

Your collection is divided into several categories, including:

- Immortals
- Dragon skins
- Wonders
- Event collectibles
- Other collectible items introduced through updates

Each unlocked Insignia contributes stars toward your overall collection progress.

---

## Activated Bonuses vs Exhibits

One of the easiest mistakes to make is confusing **Activated** bonuses with **Exhibits**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-7-1024x196.png)

### Activated Bonuses

Activated bonuses are the permanent account-wide attributes you have unlocked through your collection.

These bonuses are always active and apply automatically. Examples include:

- Physical Attack
- Magical Attack
- Physical Defense
- Magical Defense
- Accuracy
- Dodge
- Resilience
- Attack %
- Defense %
- Defense Bonus

You do not need to equip or display anything for these bonuses to work.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-8.png)

### Exhibits

The Exhibit section is purely cosmetic.

You can display your favorite:

- Immortals
- Dragon skins
- Wonders
- Event collectibles

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-9.png)

These appear on your player profile for other players to see, but they provide **no additional attributes**. Changing your displayed items has no impact on your combat performance.

---

## Total Collection Stars

Every Insignia contributes stars to your overall collection.

As your Total Stars increase, you unlock milestone rewards that apply to your entire account.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-10.png)

For example, reaching **50 Total Stars** unlocks:

- +1% Regen
- +1% Receive Regen
- +1% Critical Bonus
- +1% Damage Bonus

Future milestones continue providing additional permanent bonuses, making Total Stars one of the most valuable long-term progression systems in the game.

---

## How Affinity Connects to Insignias

Affinity is the primary way players unlock immortal Insignias.

Every immortal has an Affinity level that can be increased by giving gifts. As Affinity reaches specific milestones, the immortal unlocks new Insignias. These milestones increase your collection stars while also granting permanent account-wide attributes. The first major milestone is **Affinity Level 15**, where the first Insignia is unlocked.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-12-1024x281.png)

Additional Insignias become available at Affinity level 30.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-11-1024x399.png)

---

## Affinity Rewards Differ Between Immortals

Not every immortal grants the same permanent attributes.

Each immortal has predetermined Affinity rewards.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-13.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-14.png)

For example:

- Physical damage immortals often grant Physical Attack.
- Mage immortals commonly grant Magical Attack.
- Others provide Physical Defense, Magical Defense, Accuracy, Dodge, or Resilience.

The permanent attribute gained depends entirely on the immortal, not on the gifts used.

This allows players to gradually shape their account's permanent stats by choosing which immortals to prioritize.

---

## Dragon Skins and Other Collections

Immortals are only one source of Insignias.

Collecting legendary Realm castle skins, dragon skins and Wonder skins also unlocks permanent account-wide attributes.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-15-1024x389.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-17-1024x382.png)

Similarly, these collectibles contribute additional Insignias and collection stars.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-16-1024x293.png)

Many players overlook these collections because they appear cosmetic, but simply owning them permanently increases your account's overall strength.

---

## Player Perspective

Unlike temporary power systems, Insignias improve every march on your account. Permanent attributes gained through the collection system remain active regardless of which immortals, dragons, or formations you use. Over time, these bonuses become a significant source of combat power, especially once multiple collection milestones have been reached.

When the system was first introduced, many players assumed the displayed Exhibits were responsible for activating bonuses. After spending time testing the feature, it became clear that the real progression comes from collecting Insignias and increasing Total Stars. Once you understand that distinction, the interface becomes much easier to follow, and Affinity starts to make sense as a long-term account progression system rather than a way to strengthen individual heroes.

---

## Conclusion

The Insignia system is an account-wide collection feature built around permanent progression.

The key concepts are:

- Activated bonuses are always active once unlocked.
- Exhibits are purely cosmetic.
- Every collected Insignia contributes stars toward collection milestones.
- Affinity unlocks immortal Insignias and their permanent attributes.
- Dragon skins, Wonders, and other collectibles also contribute to your collection.
- Higher Total Star milestones unlock powerful permanent bonuses for your entire account.

Understanding these fundamentals provides the foundation for optimizing Affinity and growing your Insignia collection efficiently, which will be covered in the next guide in the Insignia section.
