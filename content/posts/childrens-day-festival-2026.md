## Introduction

This seasonal event initially looks fairly standard, but two systems significantly alter how most players should approach it:

- Candy Defenders
- Sky Paradise Puzzle Challenges

Neither mechanic is particularly difficult on paper. The challenge comes from resource timing.

For F2P and light spenders, this event is less about activity and more about preserving flexibility. Players who spend resources as they acquire them will often find themselves locked out of later objectives, while players who delay spending generally maintain enough resources to complete the most valuable milestones.

## Lucky Koi

### Structure

- Daily free draw
- $5 bundle:  - 5 Lucky Koi Tokens
- Gems & VIP
- Grail lottery rewards

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-26-1024x535.png)

Grand Prize remains unchanged at roughly 4250 Grails.

### Player Perspective

Lucky Koi continues to be one of the strongest low-spend purchases available during seasonal events.

Even without hitting the jackpot rewards, purchasing all five bundles effectively guarantees 850 Eternal Grails while also providing the usual gem and VIP value. For players building Chaos immortals, the efficiency remains difficult to match elsewhere.

- Tokens of Light

Paid Track:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-28-1024x575.png)

### Skin Evaluation

Sky River Palace is not a meta-defining skin, but it remains perfectly serviceable.

For accounts that do not already own the skin, unlocking it is generally worthwhile. For accounts that already possess it, the value shifts toward evolution progress rather than the skin itself. However, as this is not a troop % castle skin it remains inferior to other combat skins.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-29-1024x523.png)

### Player Perspective

The real attraction here is still the Token of Light package. The skin is a bonus rather than the primary reason to purchase the pass.

## Candy Defenders

### System Changes

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-31-1024x454.png)

The biggest adjustment this season is the increase from:

- 10 Levels to 15 Levels

This dramatically increases the amount of Candy required to fully clear the system but also the possible rewards for big spenders!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-32.png)

Based on current testing, reaching Level 15 generally requires somewhere around 55,000–60,000 total Candy, although individual runs can vary depending on damage rolls.

---

### Why Timing Matters

Unlike previous iterations, early participation is often inefficient.

As Glorious Challenge progresses, players gain access to:

- Additional Candy income
- Hard Mode rewards
- More challenge completions
- Additional raid opportunities

Because of this, Candy accumulated during the first few days is typically far lower than what becomes available later in the event.

---

### Player Perspective

The most successful approach I have seen is treating Candy as a stockpile resource rather than a daily-spend resource.

Instead of gradually pushing bosses every day, players accumulate Candy until enough exists to complete a full progression push. The reward quality improves significantly at higher chest levels, which makes concentrated progression noticeably more efficient than fragmented progression.

Farm accounts still contribute value, although not as effectively as during the previous version of the event.

## Glorious Challenge

### Structure

No significant mechanical changes.

Day 4 unlocks Hard Mode and remains the major progression breakpoint.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-36-1024x545.png)

### Strategy

The same principle applies as previous seasonal events:

- Avoid excessive spending before Hard Mode
- Secure valuable daily purchases
- Increase investment once Hard Mode becomes available

Because Glorious Challenge also feeds Candy Defenders, efficient participation here has greater overall impact than usual.

## Sky Paradise Puzzle Challenges

This is where resource management becomes critical.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-38-1024x578.png)

Most objectives complete naturally through ordinary event participation. The problems arise when puzzle progression intersects with Dazzling Wish requirements.

---

### Puzzle 7 & Puzzle 9

For most F2P and light-spending accounts, these are the limiting objectives.

Both place pressure on:

- Token of Light reserves
- Dazzling Wish pulls
- Grail generation

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-39-1024x593.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-40-1024x597.png)

This creates a conflict between immediate progression and future puzzle resets.

---

### Understanding Puzzle Resets

One of the most common misconceptions is assuming a single completed puzzle secures the important rewards. The puzzle resets every second day, meaning you have 5 total puzzles to solve.

To obtain all milestone rewards, players must complete the entire puzzle repeatedly throughout the event cycle. That makes it quite tricky, as you need spend 20 Tokens of Light and collect 100+ grails for five days to clear the event and obtain the Insignia.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-41.png)

That requirement dramatically changes how Tokens of Light should be managed.

---

## Conclusion Summary

This seasonal event revolves around resource timing far more than resource quantity.

### Key Takeaways

- Candy Defenders is significantly more demanding after expanding to 15 levels.
- Sky Paradise puzzle requirements place heavy pressure on Token of Light reserves.
- Dazzling Wish should be approached with future puzzle resets in mind.
- Eggs of Mischief remain a poor Grail conversion for most players.
- Hard Mode Glorious Challenge remains the primary progression breakpoint.

The strongest accounts during this event will not necessarily be the ones spending the most resources. They will be the ones preserving enough flexibility to capitalize on the later stages of the event cycle.
