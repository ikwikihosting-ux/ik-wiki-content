The v2.9.6 patch is now live, bringing a new event, several quality-of-life improvements, changes to Battle of Asteria, and a surprisingly interesting buff to Great Tengu. While this isn't a major content update on the level of a new season or immortal release, there are a few changes that could have a noticeable impact on progression and endgame team building.

Let's go through the patch notes one section at a time and look at what they actually mean for players.

## Deepsea Venture Event

The headline feature of this patch is the new **Deepsea Venture** event. Unfortunately, the patch notes don't tell us much beyond the fact that it has been added.

From the event interface, we can already see a new currency called **Colorful Scales** and a **Mermaid Shop** containing rewards related to Oley and Shadow progression. Based on what is currently visible, my first impression is that this looks more like a spending event than a standard participation event.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-1-1024x583.png)

The important question isn't whether the rewards are good. It's whether they are better than the alternatives. Will this be a free-to-play friendly way to progress holy and shadow? Will is be more cost-efficient that e.g. Rune Factory? Time will tell and we will make sure to keep you updated here on site when the event is live.

Until we see the reward structure and bundles, it's too early to judge. This is definitely the section of the patch that deserves a follow-up once the event actually goes live.

---

## New Daily Mission: Affinity Gifting

This is a small change, but it's one that most players will benefit from immediately.

Daily missions now include gifting one affinity item. For active players, this is a very minor thing. Since the daily stock for the Otherworldly shop is well.. daily, you are most likely buying these every day anyway.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-2-1024x321.png)

---

## Norheim Survival Guide Improvements

The Survival Guide now includes more detailed explanations for immortal attributes and how they work.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-3-1024x566.png)

Veteran players probably won't spend much time here, but newer players should. One of the biggest learning curves in Infinity Kingdom is understanding stats, scaling, and progression systems. Having better explanations inside the game is always a positive change.

A lot of information currently comes from community guides, so seeing the game itself provide more detail is long overdue.

---

## Squad Survival Tournament

The Squad Survival Tournament is now being divided between Season 1 and Season 2 servers.

The rules themselves aren't changing, but the separation should create more balanced competition.

This feels like a matchmaking improvement rather than a gameplay change. Players competing against others at a similar stage of progression should make the tournament more enjoyable and more competitive overall.

---

## New Legendary Skill: Thorn Armor

The new legendary skill introduced in this patch is **Thorn Armor**.

At a basic level, the skill reduces incoming physical damage for the entire team. That's already useful, but the more interesting part is the interaction with Counterattack effects.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-4-1024x504.png)

When Counterattack is active, Thorn Armor also reflects damage back to attackers. 20% physical damage reduction does not look like anything huge, but given the thorn reflect damage (requires counterattack from e.g. Tengu) this looks a lot like another attempt at making a whale counter for Alexander builds. And this one might work quite well I suspect!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-5.png)

On its own, Thorn Armor looks like a situational defensive skill. Combined with the Great Tengu changes (and him applying Counterattack), however, it starts to make a lot more sense.

---

## Great Tengu Gets a Significant Buff

The biggest balance change in the patch is the adjustment to Great Tengu's skill, **Unbreakable Pact**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-6-1024x484.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-7.png)

The new version still provides cleansing and debuff immunity, but now also grants Counterattack to front-row immortals. Looking at this alongside Thorn Armor, it feels clear that the developers are trying to expand Counterattack into a real team archetype rather than a niche mechanic. Does this suddenly make Tengu a must-have immortal? I don't think so.

Tengu is still an expensive Chaos immortal and most players will likely prioritize immortals such as Fu Fei or Medusa first. However, players who already own Tengu have gained a lot of additional utility.

He now brings:

- Cleansing
- Debuff immunity
- Healing
- Counterattack support

That's a much more complete package than before.

---

## Battle of Asteria Rework

For the average player, this may actually be the most important part of the patch.

After the update, everyone starts back at Difficulty 1 and must progress through the stages again. At first glance this looks like a reset, but I think it's mostly a positive change. The new system is much easier to understand, progression is more structured, and players will earn rewards while climbing back through the difficulties. More importantly, many players will collect additional Otherworld Crystals during the process.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-8-1024x524.png)

That means:

- More purchases in the Otherworld Shop
- More affinity items
- More progression resources

Yes, you'll spend some AP and troops while climbing again, but the extra rewards should offset that for most active players.

---

## Quality-of-Life Changes

The remaining changes are fairly minor but still welcome.

Affinity levels are now visible directly on immortal information screens, saving players from jumping through multiple menus just to check progress.

The Alliance Championship result changes appear to be mostly an interface adjustment. We'll probably need to see a few championship cycles before knowing whether there is any meaningful impact beyond presentation.

---

## Final Thoughts

The biggest unknown remains Deepsea Venture. Until players can actually participate, it's impossible to know whether it will become a valuable progression event or simply another premium activity competing for resources.

Outside of that, the standout changes are the Battle of Asteria rework and the new Counterattack synergy between Thorn Armor and Great Tengu. Neither change is likely to completely reshape the meta overnight, but both have the potential to become more important as future content arrives.
