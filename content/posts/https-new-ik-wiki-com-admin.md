## Recommended Formation

## Saladin: The Main Damage Dealer

Saladin is the primary carry. **Blade Vortex** and **Lightning Strike** give him strong immediate damage, while **Unyielding** improves his Critical Rate and provides valuable sustain.

This is also why Saladin is generally preferred over Hannibal Barca in this formation. IBL battles are often decided too quickly for Hannibal’s normal-attack scaling to reach its full potential. Saladin begins applying meaningful pressure much earlier.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-145851-3wb0f7.png)

Lightning Strike works particularly well here because Qin applies **Wound**, giving Saladin the condition he needs to produce additional damage.

## Arthur: Protecting the Carry

Arthur’s main responsibility is keeping Saladin alive long enough to do his job.

**Guard** allows Arthur to protect his more offensive frontline partner, while **Toxin Barrier** provides additional protection against the physical damage that dominates many IBL formations. **Fighting Will** adds another source of control and disruption.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-145858-py0yhp.png)

This defensive package is what allows Saladin to run such an aggressive build without immediately collapsing under frontline pressure.

## Qin Emperor: Anti-Healing and Control

Qin is one of the defining Immortals in the formation. His Ultimate applies **Wound**, preventing enemy healing while also supporting the conditions required by Lightning Strike.

His additional skills reinforce that support role:

* **Energy Suppression** slows the enemy’s access to Ultimate Skills.
* **No Escape** adds another form of control.
* **Weakness** provides a persistent aura effect throughout the battle.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-145907-00gvxc.png)

Qin is especially valuable against healing and sustain formations. Removing him changes how the entire formation functions, not merely one part of its damage.

## Baldwin IV: Defensive Support

Baldwin is not intended to be the primary carry. He contributes Magical Damage, but his more important job is supporting the formation defensively.

**Energy Shield** and **Oaken Shield** help the team survive incoming pressure, while **Misleading** interferes with enemy Accuracy and makes Dodge considerably more valuable. Accuracy and Dodge effects are particularly noticeable in IBL, where attributes are more restricted than on fully developed live accounts.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-145917-acntfr.png)

Together with Arthur’s defensive skills, Baldwin makes this formation exceptionally comfortable against physical teams.



## Situational Changes

### Against Healing Formations

Dont change a thing. You got **Qin and Lightning Strike**. Wound is essential for stopping enemy recovery and activating the intended Saladin combination.

### Against Teams With Little or No Healing

Qin can be replaced by **Matilda** for additional offensive pressure and Energy support. However, you should also replace Lightning Strike because Matilda does not provide Qin’s reliable Wound application.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-145957-ioxqje.png)

Use a direct damage skill that does not depend on the enemy carrying a specific debuff.

### Against Physical-Damage Teams

The default formation is already heavily prepared for this matchup. Keep Arthur, Baldwin, Toxin Barrier, Oaken Shield and the rest of the defensive package intact.

### Against Magic-Heavy Teams

Consider using the **Wind Dragon** and replacing Baldwin with **Cleopatra**. Baldwin’s support skills can be transferred to Cleopatra, creating a variation designed to reduce Magical Damage rather than physical pressure.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-150043-ni192g.png)

The purpose of this change is survival, not increasing the formation’s damage.

### Against High-Dodge Teams

Accuracy becomes much more important. If repeated misses are costing you battles, introduce **Coercion** or another anti-Dodge option rather than blindly adding more damage.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-150056-mhihko.png)

## Fire Dragon or Wind Dragon?

Both dragons work extremely well, and the difference is closer than it may initially appear.

The **Fire Dragon** is the more aggressive choice. Its early burst complements Saladin and can decide shorter battles before the opponent establishes healing, control or defensive momentum. Despite breaking the full Wind theme, the additional opening damage can easily justify its inclusion.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-150112-zu9n4g.png)

The **Wind Dragon** offers a more balanced approach. Its Accuracy reduction, True Damage and additional sustain improve consistency, while all four Immortals still benefit from being Wind. The healing portion can lose value against Qin and other anti-healing effects, but the remaining benefits remain powerful.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261003-150136-x006fz.png)

As a general rule:

* Choose **Fire** when you want maximum early pressure.
* Choose **Wind** when you value consistency, True Damage and Accuracy disruption.

## What Counters This Formation?

This setup is especially strong against physical opponents, but magic-heavy formations and teams capable of surviving its initial pressure can create problems. No formation is universally best, and relying on only one march will eventually leave you exposed.

The strongest IBL preparation therefore resembles **rock-paper-scissors**: maintain three functional setups, understand which matchup each one is intended to fight and switch according to your opponent. This Wind formation is an excellent default—but it should be the foundation of your options, not your only option.
