## ⚔️ Infinity Kingdom Guide: The Power of Death Breath + Chase Combo

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-93-1024x591.png)

---

## 1. What is Death Breath ?

**Death Breath (DB)** is a **Triggered** Tower of Knowledge skill that automatically activates every **6 seconds**, dealing significant magical and physical damage based on your Immortal’s stats. At max level, it inflicts **75% of both Physical and Magical Damage** as a one-time burst.

### Why it's strong:

- **Consistent damage instances**every 6 seconds throughout a battle. **Causing 8 damage instances per 6 seconds** (on avg. 5-6 instances since enemies die and cycles dont line perfectly with battle durations).
- **Hybrid scaling**—effective for both physical and magical Immortals.
- Ideal for **backline burst**, especially when paired with other multipliers.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-94.png)

---

## 2. What is Chase ?

**Chase** is an **Elite Triggered skill** that activates on **critical hits** with a **~30% chance**. When triggered, it often deals a burst of damage or applies a damaging effect—multiplying your critical bursts.

### Why it's powerful:

- Stacks with crit-heavy builds.
- Every extra damage instance from Death Breath is another chance to **proc Chase**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-95.png)

---

## 3. How the Combo Works

Here’s why this setup is *explosively effective*:

### 🔁 Multiple Damage Instances

- In a 12-second window, **Death Breath will fire twice** giving you 16 damage instances if all enemies are alive and able to be hit.
- That means **16 independent damage instances**, each with 30% chance of triggering **Chase** on a crit.

### 🎯 Crit + Crit = Damage Explosion

- If you have high Crit Rate and Crit Damage, each DB hit is likely to crit.
- With Chase triggering on crits, each DB can lead to **a double burst**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-96-1024x602.png)

---

## 4. Optimal Build Setup

To maximize burst with DB + Chase:

- **Crit Rate Focus**
Prioritize Crit% and Crit Damage% in Talents, Artifacts, Gems, and Dragon nodes.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-97.png)

- **Timing Synchronization**
Build your build around the **6-second DB cooldown**—aim for maximum output during that window using e.g. Blessing of War.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-98.png)

---

## 5. Real-World Use Cases

Players on Reddit and forums have confirmed that pairing **Death Breath with Chase** transforms Immortals into near-unstoppable burst DPS machines.

This setup excels in:

- **Arena / PvP**: Out-burst rival immortals quickly.
- **Shard / Boss Battles**: Stack rapid cooldown bursts to shave off health spikes.
- **Hybrid March**: Fits in both physical and magical teams due to DB's dual scaling.

---

## 6. How to Build It

1. **Unlock both skills** in the Tower of Knowledge (DB at level 39, Chase at level 25).
2. **Gear and Sculpt for Crit**:  - Artifacts with Crit Chance/Damage.
- Talents favoring Crit buildouts.
3. Use **Weakness Aura** togeather with this combo to boost your crit rate.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-100-1024x396.png)

Both Chase and Death Breath are free to play skills, making this combo a very good one for anyone able to stack enough crit!

TIP: As you progress, you can use skills like Nova's on lower levels while you progress to level 40 for Death Breath.

---

## 7. Limitations & Counters

- **Skill RNG**: If DB doesn’t crit, fewer Chase procs.
- **Resilience Counters**: High enemy resilience can block crits. This is for instance **hardcountered**by high resiliance enemies using **Malice**.
- **Battle Length Sensitive**: Best for quick, high-damage windows (12–24s).

---

## 🧠 Final Verdict

Death Breath + Chase is a **devastating burst combo**, leveraging:

- **Time-based burst frequency** (every 6s)
- **Multiple damage instance triggers**
- **High probability of compounding burst damage**

It’s a **top-tier build choice** for players aiming to maximize their Immortals’ burst potential, and well worth including in any competitive PvP or PvE setup. Stack Crit, sync bursts, and watch your enemies crumble.
