## Introduction

Infinity Kingdom’s creator events have quietly become some of the most enjoyable community-driven content the game offers, and this latest **[Creator Frame Design](https://discord.gg/JDfx7Tpvr8)** theme is a strong example of why.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-118-1024x572.png)

What started as a niche creative challenge has now grown into something much larger. The first frame event already produced a surprising number of high-quality entries, but this second iteration has clearly reached a wider audience. The difference is immediately noticeable: more participants, more experimentation, and a much higher overall level of polish.

Even if you have no intention of submitting a design yourself, this is one of those events worth paying attention to purely for the creativity on display.

---

## What Is the Creator Frame Design Event?

The **Creator Frame Design** event is a community art competition where players design a custom in-game frame that represents Infinity Kingdom creators.

### Core Concept

Participants are asked to design a frame that combines two key elements:

- **Infinity Kingdom identity** (immortals, dragons, elements, or overall game aesthetic)
- **Creator identity** (community contribution, creativity, influence, or expression)

The goal is not just to decorate a frame, but to create something that feels like a natural extension of the game’s visual style while also representing creators within the community.

### Design Rules

- The design must be **original**
- Any tools are allowed, including **Photoshop, AI-assisted tools, or other media software**
- The final result should function as a **frame usable in-game**, meaning readability and structure matter as much as artistic detail

## How to Join and Submit

### Participation Process

1. Create your frame design following the event requirements
2. Submit your work in the designated **Discord channel**
3. Follow the submission format outlined in the event post

***[Link to the channel here](https://discord.gg/JDfx7Tpvr8)***

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-119-703x1024.png)

### Important Notes

- Submissions are handled **entirely through Discord**
- Visibility within the channel can influence feedback and iteration
- The event runs **until April 6**, so timing your submission can matter

## Rewards and Incentives

The event offers both **competitive rewards** and **participation-based incentives**, which is an important distinction.

### Main Reward

- The **winning design** will be selected by the official team
- That design becomes the **official Creator Frame**
- The creator receives the frame directly as recognition

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-120.png)

### Participation Rewards

- **All participants** receive in-game item rewards
- Players who take part in **3 creator theme contests** earn the Creator Frame as a **long-term honor reward**

This structure makes the event accessible even if you are not aiming to win outright.

## Why This Event Is Worth Your Time

There are two very different reasons to engage with this event, and both are valid.

### 1. If You Want to Compete

This is one of the few opportunities to have your design permanently represented in-game.

From past events, the strongest entries tend to:

- Focus on **clear, readable shapes** rather than excessive detail
- Use IK elements in a way that feels **intentional, not decorative**
- Maintain visual clarity at **small UI scale**

### 2. If You Just Want to Explore the Community

Even without submitting anything, simply browsing the Discord channel is worth it.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-121.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-123.png)

You will find:

- **Surprisingly creative interpretations** of the theme
- Some entries that are genuinely **high-end, professional-quality work**
- Others that lean more into humor, which adds a lot of personality to the event

It’s a rare mix—serious design work alongside lighthearted experimentation—and that balance is part of what makes the event stand out.

---

## Player Perspective

From experience with previous creator events, the real value here goes beyond rewards. These competitions tend to reveal how players actually interpret Infinity Kingdom’s identity.

For instance, in the last frame event, some of the most memorable designs weren’t the most complex—they were the ones that immediately communicated a clear idea when scaled down. That same principle will likely apply again here.

If you’re participating, it’s worth thinking less about making something impressive at full resolution and more about how it reads in-game. If you’re just observing, this is one of the best windows into the creative side of the IK community.

---

## Conclusion Summary

The **「Creator Frame Design」** event is both a competition and a showcase. It offers meaningful rewards, but more importantly, it highlights the creativity of the player base at a level you don’t often see in regular gameplay.

Whether you plan to submit a design or simply browse the entries, it’s well worth engaging with. At minimum, you’ll come away with a better sense of how players interpret the game—and at best, you might find inspiration to join in yourself.
