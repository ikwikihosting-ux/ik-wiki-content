Immortal Outfits do more than change how your Immortal looks. Once an outfit is unlocked, you can **develop** it up to level 8, and every level adds a percentage bonus to all five of the Immortal's attributes. The develop screen only ever shows you the cost of the *next* level, though, so it is hard to plan how many fragments you actually need.

This guide lists the full cost of every outfit level, fragments and gold, and shows which levels are worth paying for. If you are new to outfits, start with [Basics: Everything You Need to Know About the New Outfit System](/posts/basics-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-new-outfit-system).

## How Outfit Levels Work

* **Unlocking** an outfit costs **60 outfit fragments**. The outfit becomes permanently available for that Immortal.
* An unlocked outfit starts at **level 1 with +0% to all attributes**. Unlocking alone gives you the look and the skill effect, but no stats.
* You then **develop** the outfit with more fragments plus gold. There are 7 stars, so 7 develop steps take the outfit from level 1 to the **maximum, level 8**.
* Every level raises **Strength, Agility, Intelligence, Physique and Aptitude** by the same percentage, up to **+15% each at level 8**.

## Cost of Every Level

| Level            | Fragments | Gold        | Bonus to all 5 attributes |
| ---------------- | --------- | ----------- | ------------------------- |
| Unlock (level 1) | 60        | –           | +0.0%                     |
| 1 → 2            | 30        | 15,000,000  | +6.0%                     |
| 2 → 3            | 60        | 30,000,000  | +7.5%                     |
| 3 → 4            | 90        | 45,000,000  | +9.0%                     |
| 4 → 5            | 120       | 60,000,000  | +10.5%                    |
| 5 → 6            | 150       | 75,000,000  | +12.0%                    |
| 6 → 7            | 180       | 90,000,000  | +13.5%                    |
| 7 → 8            | 210       | 105,000,000 | +15.0%                    |

The pattern is simple: every level costs **30 more fragments and 15M more gold** than the one before, and adds **+1.5%** to every attribute. The only exception is the jump from level 1 to level 2, which gives a big +6% on its own.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-101412-dbzrab.png)

We checked the unlock, level 1 → 2, level 2 → 3 (Louis IX – Night of Terror) and level 7 → 8 (Alexander the Great) in-game. All four match the pattern exactly, so the levels in between follow the same steps.

A fully maxed outfit costs **900 fragments and 420 million gold**.

## Which Levels Are Worth It?

Fragments are the real bottleneck, so the question is how much bonus each fragment buys:

| Step  | Fragments | Bonus gained | Bonus per 100 fragments |
| ----- | --------- | ------------ | ----------------------- |
| 1 → 2 | 30        | +6.0%        | 20.0%                   |
| 2 → 3 | 60        | +1.5%        | 2.5%                    |
| 3 → 4 | 90        | +1.5%        | 1.7%                    |
| 4 → 5 | 120       | +1.5%        | 1.3%                    |
| 5 → 6 | 150       | +1.5%        | 1.0%                    |
| 6 → 7 | 180       | +1.5%        | 0.8%                    |
| 7 → 8 | 210       | +1.5%        | 0.7%                    |

* **Level 2 is a must.** Just 30 fragments after unlocking gives +6% to everything, which is 40% of the outfit's total bonus for 3% of its total cost.
* **Unlock + level 2 = 90 fragments.** Treat that as the real "price" of an outfit.
* **Levels 3–8 get steadily worse.** The last 810 fragments only add the remaining +9%. Push them on your main Immortals, where every percent counts, and stop at level 2 on everyone else.

## Gold

Gold is rarely the limiting factor late in the game, but it adds up: 420M for a single maxed outfit. If you plan to max several outfits at once, keep an eye on your gold before an outfit event.

## Quick Summary

* Unlock = 60 fragments, no stats.
* Level 2 = +30 fragments for +6% to all attributes. Always do it.
* Each level after that costs 30 more fragments and 15M more gold than the last, for +1.5%.
* Max (level 8) = 900 fragments and 420M gold in total for +15% to all five attributes.
