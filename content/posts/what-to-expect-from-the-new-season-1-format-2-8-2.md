As *Infinity Kingdom* transitions into a reworked **Season 1**, many players—especially veterans from early release servers—are wondering what has changed. The upcoming version of Season 1 appears to follow the framework of what has traditionally been known as **Season 2**, incorporating multiple new systems and content updates. Here's a breakdown of the key features and what players should prepare for.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-75-1024x576.png)

---

## 🆕 Major Changes in the New Season 1

### 🧙‍♂️ Level 50 Castle Cap

- The maximum castle level increases from 40 to **50**, allowing further progression.
- Players will need substantial **resources** and **speedups** to reach this new cap.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-77-1024x514.png)

Link to all **[building costs](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/development/buildings/castle-upgrade-costs/)** and **[requirements](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/development/buildings/castle-upgrade-requirements/)**.

### 🌀 Chaos Immortals

- All **Chaos-element Immortals** are available through the **Chaos Roulette**.
- Expect them to be **expensive**, priced similarly to how Holy Immortals were on release around 6-9K each depending on speed.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-78.png)

[**Chaos Roulette (2.6.9) - Wiki & Tools | Infinity Kingdom**](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/events/wheels-roulette/chaos-roulette-2-6-9/)

### ⚔️ Immortal Specializations

- Specialization system is added for **Immortals**, granting powerful stat boosts and effects.
- Players will need to collect large amounts of immortal fragments again - begin banking now!
- **Dragons do not get specializations** yet—that feature is introduced in Season 3.

### 💎 Artifact System

- **Elite Artifacts (EAs)** are available, much like in original Season 1.
- Slight improvement in accessibility via **more coins in Mysterium**, but not drastically easier.

### 📘 Skill System Updates

- No Chaos-element exclusive skills yet.
- **Well of Time** skill pool is refreshed or updated.
- Expect tweaks that bring some meta-shifting possibilities.

---

## 🎯 Event & Currency Changes

### 🌌 Galaxy & Theia Changes

- **Holy and Shadow Immortals** are available in the **Galaxy** shop—**A LOT cheaper** than Theia.
- This significantly lowers the cost for completing Holy/Shadow teams.
- Chaos Immortals remain tied to **Chaos Roulette**, with premium pricing.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-76.png)

### 👑 King of the Hill (KoH) Updates

- KoH now features **all GP heroes** (including strong options like YSS).
- These previously season-locked Immortals can now be obtained more flexibly.

### 🧭 Improved Event Rewards

- Rewards across events are **slightly better** than old Season 1, though not radically different.

## 🧰 Preparation Guide for Players

To prepare for the new Season 1 (Season 2-style), here’s what to focus on:

### Resource Banking

- Save **resources** and **speedups** for pushing to Castle 50.
- **AP (Action Points)** will be valuable for farming new equipment and events.
- Stockpile **EXP scrolls** for new Immortals and specialization leveling.
- Stockpile Enchanted Stones for new levels on Equipment.

### Currency Management

- Save up and bank **Galaxy Crystal Balls**: Save for Holy and Shadow Immortals.
- Spending funds: Prepare for **Chaos Roulette** if aiming for Chaos units.

### Progression

- Aim to reach **Castle 40** before the season update.
- Maximize **tech upgrades**, and build strong **dragon levels** and **equipment** setups.
- **T5 troops** are expected, so complete that progression path where possible.

## 💬 Current Meta Snapshot

- **Pure Earth teams** remain strong and popular (e.g., Fu Fei, Alex, Charles).
- **Mixed marches** are still viable, especially for mid-tier or flexible strategies.
- **Whales** dominate with full Chaos setups, but aren’t the only meta-defining option.
- **Apollo specialization** is notable among top players.
