Quarries unlock at Castle level 3. You can get Stone from the Quarries, an important resource used to train troops and to manufacture fortifications. Stone is also the key research material for Defense related technologies. So do not forget to check your Quarries regularly if you need them!

![](https://oss.gtarcade.com/forum/png/2020-08-28/25_1290aa23-7588-4272-a5bf-a7770c979ba1_193214.png)
