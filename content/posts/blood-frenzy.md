## Introduction

Blood Frenzy is one of the newer Physical skills introduced to *Infinity Kingdom*, and it has quickly become a popular choice for several high-end physical damage dealers. Unlike many offensive skills that provide an immediate damage increase, Blood Frenzy builds its power over the course of a battle, rewarding immortals capable of repeatedly dealing direct damage with their ultimate skills.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-135-1024x481.png)

Because of its unique stacking mechanic, Blood Frenzy performs very differently depending on the immortal using it and the length of the battle. Understanding how the skill builds its stacks—and which immortals can take advantage of them most effectively—is the key to unlocking its full potential.

---

## Understanding Blood Frenzy

At maximum level, Blood Frenzy grants **20% Physical Damage** each time its effect is triggered, stacking up to three times for a total bonus of **60% Physical Damage**.

Unlike skills that provide their full benefit from the opening seconds of combat, Blood Frenzy has to earn its value. The first few ultimates establish the stacks, while every attack that follows benefits from the increased Physical Damage. As a result, the skill becomes progressively stronger as the battle continues.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-136.png)

This naturally favours encounters where immortals have enough time to cast several ultimates. In shorter fights, the battle may be decided before Blood Frenzy has reached its full potential, whereas longer engagements allow the skill to spend much more time at maximum effectiveness.

---

## Building Around Blood Frenzy

Blood Frenzy performs best alongside skills that strengthen ultimate damage or increase the number of ultimates an immortal can cast during a battle. Rather than competing with existing offensive options, it complements them by adding another layer of scaling that continues to grow as the fight progresses.

**Corrosive Power** is its most natural partner. While Blood Frenzy gradually increases Physical Damage, Corrosive Power directly amplifies ultimate skill damage, allowing every fully stacked ultimate to hit considerably harder. Since most physical carries derive a large portion of their damage from ultimates, the two skills naturally reinforce one another.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-137.png)

**Battle Stance** approaches the same goal from a different angle. Once it activates, the additional Energy Recovery allows immortals to cycle ultimates more quickly, helping Blood Frenzy reach maximum stacks sooner while also increasing the number of empowered ultimates cast during the remainder of the fight. The timing works particularly well, as Battle Stance comes online just as Blood Frenzy begins reaching its highest value.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-138.png)

**Demonic Contract** fills a similar role as another scaling offensive skill, although it follows a much slower curve. In battles that continue well beyond a minute it can eventually surpass Blood Frenzy, but many Arena matches end long before that happens. In most situations, Blood Frenzy reaches meaningful value much earlier, making it the more consistent option.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-139.png)

The immortals that benefit most from Blood Frenzy share a similar characteristic: they rely on repeated direct-damage ultimates rather than a single burst window. The longer they continue casting, the more opportunity they have to establish and benefit from the skill's full damage bonus.

**Tutankhamun** is arguably the skill's best user. His damage profile revolves around repeated ultimate casts, so Blood Frenzy follows almost exactly the same rhythm. Both spend the opening portion of combat building momentum before reaching full effectiveness, which is why many experienced players pair him with Corrosive Power and Battle Stance.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-140.png)

**Genghis Khan** is another excellent candidate thanks to his multi-target ultimate. He already excels in prolonged engagements where repeated ultimates steadily wear down the enemy team, making Blood Frenzy a natural addition to his kit. If Blood Frenzy proves to count individual damage instances rather than ultimate activations, Genghis Khan would become even more effective thanks to his ability to hit multiple targets with every cast.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-141.png)

**Manco Cápac** benefits for similar reasons. His damage naturally ramps up over the course of a fight, making Blood Frenzy feel like an extension of his own playstyle rather than a separate mechanic. By the time Manco reaches his strongest phase, Blood Frenzy is often fully stacked as well.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-143.png)

**Darius** also makes good use of the skill. His ultimate strikes every enemy, allowing him to take advantage of the increasing Physical Damage bonus throughout extended battles. Although his slower Energy Recovery limits his overall efficiency compared with the strongest physical carries, Blood Frenzy remains a solid choice for players building around him.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-144.png)

## PvP and PvE

Blood Frenzy's value changes depending on how long a battle lasts.

In Arena, where many fights are decided quickly, the skill may not always have enough time to establish all three stacks before combat ends. That doesn't make it a poor choice, but it does place greater importance on using it with immortals capable of casting ultimates consistently throughout the fight.

Longer PvE encounters tell a different story. World bosses and other sustained battles provide ample opportunity for Blood Frenzy to reach maximum stacks, allowing physical carries to spend a significant portion of the fight benefiting from the full 60% Physical Damage bonus. As encounters become longer, Blood Frenzy becomes increasingly attractive compared with skills that provide only a short burst of damage.

## Obtaining Blood Frenzy

Unlike many Tower of Knowledge skills, **Blood Frenzy is not currently available through the Cinder Wish Shop or the Kingdom vs Kingdom Shop**. At the time of writing, the only way to obtain the skill is through **Ability Stone Selection Chest VI**.

These chests are awarded as part of the weekly **5-Day Combo** recharge event. Each daily bundle includes **5 Ability Stone Selection Chest VI**, while purchasing five consecutive days unlocks an additional bonus chest containing **25 more Ability Stone Selection Chest VI**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-145-1024x540.png)

Unlocking Blood Frenzy requires **60 Ability Stone Fragments**, which means obtaining the skill from scratch currently requires:

- Purchasing all seven daily bundles during the event.
- Receiving **35 fragments** from the daily rewards (7 × 5).
- Claiming the **25-fragment bonus chest** for completing five consecutive purchases.
- Collecting **60 fragments** in total to unlock the skill.

At the current bundle price of **$19.99 USD per day**, unlocking Blood Frenzy costs approximately **$140 USD** if starting with no fragments.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-146-1024x487.png)

Because the skill is exclusive to Ability Stone Selection Chest VI, players should consider carefully whether Blood Frenzy fits their long-term physical march before committing to the event. Unlike skills available through permanent shops, there is currently no gradual free-to-play path to unlocking it.

---

## Conclusion

Blood Frenzy is best viewed as a long-term investment rather than an immediate damage boost. It rewards immortals capable of casting repeated ultimates, steadily increasing their damage ceiling as the battle progresses and making it particularly effective in sustained PvP fights and longer PvE encounters.

Its greatest strength lies in how naturally it fits alongside established offensive skills rather than replacing them. Combined with Corrosive Power and Battle Stance, it creates a build that scales throughout the battle instead of relying on a single burst window. Although its acquisition cost makes it one of the more exclusive Tower of Knowledge skills, players investing in physical marches will find that it offers excellent long-term value on the right immortal.
