Hammurabi stands out as a rare bow-wielding spellcaster in *Infinity Kingdom*. His ultimate skill's potency increases with each debuff present on enemy targets, positioning him as a top choice for strategies centered around debuff-heavy lineups. This mechanic allows him to deliver devastating blows when enemies are sufficiently weakened by various negative effects.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-39-1024x552.png)

---

## Historical Reference: The Real Hammurabi and His Legacy

Before his electrifying presence in *Infinity Kingdom*, Hammurabi was a significant historical figure, reigning as one of Babylon’s most powerful rulers from 1792–1750 BCE. He is best known for the Code of Hammurabi, one of the earliest recorded legal systems, introducing the principle of "an eye for an eye." This connection to law and order is mirrored in the game through his emphasis on punishing enemies laden with debuffs.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-40.png)

## Hammurabi’s Ultimate Skill: Mechanics & Scaling

Hammurabi's ultimate skill delivers magical damage to all enemies, with its effectiveness scaling based on:

- **Magical Damage Modifiers**: Enhancements from gear, research, and skills that boost magic attack.
- **Critical Hit Bonuses**: Increased chances to land more potent hits.
- **Debuff Count**: The core of his damage potential; more debuffs equate to higher damage output.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-41.png)

### What Counts as a Negative Effect?

Not all status effects contribute to Hammurabi's damage scaling. Valid debuffs include:

- **Auras**: Permanent debuffs from Tower of Knowledge skills like Misleading, Malice, Weakness, Coercion, and Paralysis.
- **Control Effects**: Stuns, roots, and silences from Immortals such as Richard (Stun) and Attila (Silence).
- **Immortal-based Debuffs**: Effects like Wounds from Genghis Khan or Qin, Bleeds from Elizabeth Bathory, and various negative effects from Tokugawa.

## Skill Synergies: Maximizing Hammurabi’s Damage Output

To unlock Hammurabi's full potential, equipping him with complementary skills is essential:

- **Heavy Lifter**: Increases damage and adds an additional debuff, enhancing his ultimate's effectiveness.
- **Spell Disintegrator**: Inflicts damage and applies a debuff, further boosting his damage output.
- **Energy Suppression (Grace)**: Adds extra debuffs, synergizing with his ability for increased damage (equipped on e.g. Tokugawa)
- **Weakness (Aura)**: Reduces enemy resilience rate, improving Hammurabi's critical hit chances and overall damage (equipped on e.g. Tokugawa).

As a free to play, skills such as Toxin Nova and Fire Nova that leave the enemy with a damage over time effect are superior on Hammurabi compared to other mages, but you are still going to see more value from Concentration and Annihilation. Hence, something like the following is a good setup if you do not have access to Spell Disintegrator and Heavy Lifter.

## Best Immortal Pairings for Hammurabi

Hammurabi excels in teams that can stack debuffs efficiently. Ideal pairings include:

- **Tokugawa**: His poison debuffs complement Hammurabi's need for multiple negative effects on enemies.
- **Elizabeth Bathory**: Her bleeding effects add to the debuff count, enhancing Hammurabi's ultimate skill.
- **Genghis Khan**: Provides wounds, contributing to the variety of debuffs required for maximum damage.
- **YSG**: His transferance serve as additional debuffs, further amplifying Hammurabi's power.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-43.png)

Hammurabi's exclusive artifact enhances his debuff-based damage mechanics, making it a valuable asset for players focusing on debuff strategies. While not mandatory, it significantly boosts his performance in well-structured teams.

## Strengths & Weaknesses: When to Use Hammurabi?

**Strengths**:

- High damage potential in debuff-centric teams.
- Synergizes well with various control and debuff-inflicting Immortals.
- Effective in both PvE and PvP scenarios when properly supported.

**Weaknesses**:

- Relies heavily on team composition; less effective without sufficient debuff support.
- Requires strategic planning and coordination to maximize damage output.

## Is Hammurabi Worth Investing In?

For players who enjoy strategic gameplay and building synergistic teams, Hammurabi offers a rewarding experience. His unique mechanics provide a fresh approach to combat, and when paired with the right Immortals and skills, he can be a game-changer. Investing in Hammurabi is worthwhile for those willing to craft and manage debuff-focused strategies.

## Conclusion: Should You Build Hammurabi?

Hammurabi stands as a powerful addition to any team that leverages debuffs to their advantage. His scaling damage based on negative effects introduces a dynamic layer to team composition and battle strategy. While he requires thoughtful planning and the right allies to shine, the payoff is a formidable Immortal capable of turning the tide of battle. For players seeking depth and synergy in their gameplay, Hammurabi is a compelling choice. He is a very good damage dealer for any lightning setup and can scale well into both magic based as well as a combination of magic and physical damage builds, since he scales off of any debuff applied. That being said, magic debuffs are naturally stronger since you unlock dual-synergy.
