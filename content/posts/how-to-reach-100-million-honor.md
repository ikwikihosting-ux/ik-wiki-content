I am Myrahk and in this article I will share some insights about how my main castle got world first 100 million Honor. I have gotten many questions about how to get honor fast and why I have the number of alts I do, how I manage them and what I get out of it – so here we go!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Skaermbillede-2022-07-03-215742-1024x94.png)

## Relying on your alts to farm is the fastest way to grow!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/image-53-1024x574.png)

## Honor farming

Alts produce a ton of troops. Don't let that go to waste! If you maintain a steady routine where you stack up your strongest alt marches in proper form and attack with Rank Advancement honor talent = you are able to consistently keep a very high daily income of honor.

### 1. Make sure you prepare your alt's marches for honorfarming

You want to remove dragons, all passives, artifacts and equipment. Make them as weak as possible while maxing out the level and boost level of the immortal so the **troop count is as high as possible**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/image-57-1024x419.png)

### 2. Make sure you have the right talent on in your main before you attack. You need Rank Advancement talent

Rank advancement works by boosting the honor gain of 1 battle by 100%. To get the full potential from this, you want to make sure you ALWAYS farm a big stack of marches when you use this talent.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/image-58.png)

### 3. You now need to stack garrisons in one alt to max out the potential from your talent-attack.

That means the biggest of your alts make full garrisons (in SvS you can push the cap with technology) and make for huge single attacks daily. On the Cooldown you need to login and do them atleast once a day to maintain a high daily honor gain.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/image-56.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Skaermbillede-2022-02-23-193810.png)

### 4. Enjoy endless supply of honor
