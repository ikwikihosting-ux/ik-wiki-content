## Apollo or Medusa? What Conquest Players Should Build for Legendary Season

The seasonal discount chests have changed an important decision for players progressing through Conquest. With **Apollo and Medusa both available for 250 Grails**, these Immortals are no longer choices reserved almost entirely for the biggest spenders. A patient mid-spender can realistically build one of them, which naturally leads to the question I have been hearing more often lately: **Should I build Apollo or Medusa?**

If we were only talking about Conquest, my answer would be Apollo. He is easier to get working with the skills available during those seasons and can be remarkably difficult to deal with when built properly.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-28-1024x608.png)

The problem is that Conquest does not last forever.

After Conquest P5 you move into Legendary Season, where the Tower of Knowledge gains another set of skills. One of those skills, **Eternal Battlelust**, changes the comparison considerably. It gives Medusa exactly the kind of scaling she needs to turn from a good Conquest Immortal into one of the more frightening damage dealers available in Legendary.

For someone deciding where to spend 250 Grails today, I therefore wouldn't make the decision based only on which Immortal is strongest in the season you are currently playing. You also need to think about where that investment leaves you several seasons from now.

## Why Apollo Is So Good in Conquest

There is a reason Apollo is tempting.

His ultimate, **Corona Godstrike**, deals physical damage to all enemies at a **750% Damage Rate** when fully upgraded and has a 70% chance to reduce their healing effect by 30% for six seconds. Each time he casts his ultimate, he also gains a stack of Light Blessing, reducing the damage he takes by 10%, stacking up to three times.

With Apollo's exclusive artifact effect active, Corona Godstrike receives another **30% increased damage**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-29-1024x543.png)

That gives him an unusual combination for a damage dealer. He can pressure the entire enemy march while gradually becoming harder to remove as the fight develops. He doesn't need an elaborate Legendary-only setup before he starts doing his job either, which is an important distinction when we're talking about players still moving through Conquest.

I would run **Energy Mastery** with him. Getting Apollo through his ultimate cycle quickly allows Corona Godstrike to begin applying pressure while building his Light Blessing stacks at the same time. In practice, I've seen him remain extremely effective even when facing **Knife Through Butter** setups that should, on paper, make life uncomfortable for him.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-30-1024x547.png)

This is also where Apollo has a real advantage for a mid-spender. You can build him during Conquest and actually make use of the investment immediately. You aren't buying an Immortal today while waiting several seasons for the pieces that make him relevant.

If your only concern were winning during Conquest, I would be quite comfortable recommending Apollo.

## So Why Do I Recommend Medusa?

Because the question changes once Legendary Season enters the picture.

Medusa is already a capable physical damage dealer, but her real ceiling becomes much easier to reach when Legendary Tower of Knowledge skills enter the game. The important one is **Eternal Battlelust**, and understanding what it does explains most of my reasoning here.

At level 8, Eternal Battlelust reads:

> Upon entering battle, each instance of passive damage or triggered skill damage increases physical damage by 5% and reduces damage taken by 1%, stacking up to 20 times.

At full stacks, we are therefore looking at up to **100% increased physical damage** alongside **20% damage reduction**.

Now look at Medusa's own ultimate.

## Gaze of Silence and Eternal Battlelust

Medusa's **Gaze of Silence** contains both a passive and an active component. Its passive deals **600% physical damage to all enemies every six seconds**, with damage against targets carrying Mark of Revenge increased by another 30%.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-26-1024x839.png)

Its active component removes all debuffs from Medusa and attempts to petrify all enemies for three seconds with a 70% success rate. Targets that resist the petrification receive **Mark of Revenge** instead.

With Medusa's exclusive artifact effect, the duration of Mark of Revenge from Gaze of Silence is extended by another three seconds.

What matters here is how naturally her damage profile works with Eternal Battlelust. Medusa repeatedly produces the kind of passive damage that allows the Legendary skill to build its stacks, and as those stacks accumulate, her physical damage continues climbing while she simultaneously becomes more resistant to incoming damage.

The result is very different from simply giving a damage dealer another offensive skill.

You are building a feedback loop around the way Medusa already fights.

Her passive damage helps develop Eternal Battlelust. Eternal Battlelust makes subsequent physical damage increasingly dangerous. Gaze of Silence continues hitting the entire enemy march, while its petrification and Mark of Revenge mechanics create additional pressure of their own.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-31.png)

Once the march has had enough time to develop those stacks, Medusa can become an absolute damage machine.

## The Important Catch: You Need Eternal Battlelust

There is one warning I would attach to any recommendation of Medusa, particularly for players working with a limited budget.

**Do not plan around Legendary Medusa without also planning for Eternal Battlelust.**

Buying Medusa from the discounted seasonal chest is only one part of the investment. Eternal Battlelust is a Legendary Tower of Knowledge skill, so players approaching the end of Conquest should start thinking ahead about the resources required to unlock and develop it.

That means saving **Wishing Well coins or Skill Stones** rather than arriving in Legendary with Medusa ready but no way to build the skill that makes the setup so powerful.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-32-1024x471.png)

This matters because 250 Grails can make Medusa herself look surprisingly affordable. In reality, the complete Legendary build requires more preparation than the chest price suggests.

Apollo is considerably more forgiving in that respect. He can make good use of Energy Mastery and the skill pool already available during Conquest, whereas Medusa's long-term recommendation assumes you are prepared to make the additional Legendary investment.

The reason I'm comfortable recommending this path isn't based solely on reading the skill descriptions.

The march is still a work in progress rather than a completely finished T9 build, yet it has already reached the elimination rounds in Legendary and has been able to take consecutive Tower fights without refreshing.

His advice to players still in Conquest was essentially the same conclusion I've reached: build Medusa gradually during Conquest, then have enough resources saved to obtain Eternal Battlelust as soon as Legendary opens.

## Apollo Is Still the Better Immediate Investment

None of this suddenly makes Apollo a bad choice. In fact, there is a slightly awkward truth behind the whole comparison. Apollo is probably the better Immortal for many players at the exact moment they are being asked to choose. A player in early Conquest may have several seasons ahead before Legendary. Apollo can provide value throughout that period without asking you to reserve resources for a Legendary skill that you cannot even access yet. For someone primarily interested in immediate results, that matters.

**Medusa is the recommendation because we're looking further ahead.** If you're in Conquest P1, for example, Apollo could potentially serve you extremely well for several seasons. There is nothing wrong with choosing him if those seasons are your priority. What I would avoid is spending 250 Grails on Apollo simply because he looks stronger today without considering what happens once your account crosses into Legendary.

For mid-spenders especially, these decisions tend to stay with the account for a long time. You cannot approach every discounted Chaos Immortal as though another 250 Grails will be waiting whenever the meta changes.

## Which One Should You Build?

For players who want the simplest answer, I would separate the decision by timeframe. **If your priority is Conquest performance right now, Apollo is the stronger and easier choice.** Build around Energy Mastery and he can become a very effective battlefield Immortal without requiring access to the Legendary skill pool. **If you are choosing one Immortal as a longer-term investment through Conquest and into Legendary, I would build Medusa.** You may have to be patient with her. Apollo can look like the better purchase while both accounts are still sitting in Conquest, and depending on where you are in the progression cycle, he may remain the better performer for several seasons.

Once Eternal Battlelust becomes available, however, the reason for investing in Medusa starts to become clear. That is why my recommendation for players currently working their way through Conquest is to **slow-build Medusa, prepare her for Legendary, and start banking the resources needed for Eternal Battlelust before you get there**.

The 250-Grail seasonal chest makes the Immortal much more accessible. Planning ahead is what turns that opportunity into a march you can continue using after Conquest is over.

## Final Thoughts

Apollo and Medusa being available for only 250 Grails is a significant opportunity for mid-spenders, but I wouldn't treat the two purchases as interchangeable. Apollo gives you more immediate value and is arguably the better pure Conquest choice. Medusa asks for more patience and another major investment once Legendary begins, but the arrival of Eternal Battlelust changes her potential enough that I believe she is currently the better long-term project.

For a player with unlimited resources, there is no real dilemma: build both and use whichever fits the season and march you are playing. For everyone else, the decision deserves a longer view.

Personally **every single day I make sure to spend 250 Grails progressing with Legendary season in mind (despite being in conquest) hence, I spend my chests on Medusa fragments — and I am saving during Wisdom Well events for Eternal Battlelust once I land on Legendary.**
