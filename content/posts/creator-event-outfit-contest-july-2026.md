## Show Off Your Creativity

### Design the Next Immortal Outfit and Earn Rewards

Infinity Kingdom has launched its sixth Community Creator event, giving players the opportunity to design an original outfit for their favourite immortal. Whether you're an experienced artist or simply enjoy creating concepts, this is a chance to put your own spin on one of the game's iconic characters.

Even more interesting, outstanding designs may go beyond the competition itself. High-quality entries can be featured by the official team and may even be considered for future in-game collaborations.

---

## Event Theme: Immortal Outfit Design

The goal is simple: create a brand-new outfit for any immortal in Infinity Kingdom while staying true to the game's world and the character's identity.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-70-1024x370.png)

There are plenty of directions you can take your design, including:

- Holiday or seasonal outfits
- Kingdom or faction-inspired designs
- Battle armour and alternate combat appearances
- Casual or everyday clothing
- Completely original concepts that still fit the Infinity Kingdom universe

Your submission can be presented as:

- A full-body illustration
- Three-view character sheets
- A design or concept showcase with additional details

Hand-drawn artwork, digital illustrations, Photoshop creations, and AI-assisted artwork are all accepted, provided the design is your own original work. Copying or heavily using someone else's artwork is not permitted.

Adding design notes explaining your inspiration, colour choices, or outfit details is optional, but it can help showcase the thought behind your concept.

---

## Rewards

Every eligible participant will receive:

- 500 Gems
- Superior Resource Package ×2
- Superior Speedup Chest ×2
- Market Orders ×10
- Philosopher's Stones ×3

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-71-1024x179.png)

The official Infinity Kingdom team will also select ten outstanding designs. Those creators will receive additional rewards:

- 1,500 Gems
- Stardust ×30
- Superior Resource Package ×5
- Superior Speedup Chest ×5
- Philosopher's Stones ×9

After that, the community will vote on the ten finalists to decide the most popular outfit design, with the winning entries showcased across Infinity Kingdom's official social media channels.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-74-1024x451.png)

---

## A Chance to Be Featured In-Game

One of the most exciting parts of this event is that exceptional designs may be officially collected for future collaboration opportunities. While this doesn't guarantee an outfit will appear in the game, it's a rare opportunity for community creations to catch the developers' attention.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-72-1024x385.png)

If you've ever imagined giving your favourite immortal a completely new look, this is the perfect opportunity to share your ideas.

Players who participate in three Community Creator events also unlock an exclusive Creator Avatar Frame, making it worthwhile even if you're just getting started.

---

## How to Participate

Submit your design in the **Creator Event – Immortal Outfit Design** channel on the official Infinity Kingdom Discord server. **[Go to the channel directly HERE.](https://discord.com/channels/646176940503466004/1526159305970028655)**

**Event Deadline:** **July 27**

If you've been looking for an excuse to dust off your drawing tablet, open Photoshop, or experiment with AI-generated concept art, this event is well worth entering. Whether your design is inspired by mythology, historical armour, seasonal festivals, or something entirely original, the community is always eager to see fresh ideas for its favourite immortals.
