Gems are one of the most valuable resources in Infinity Kingdom, and as a new player, it’s critical to spend them wisely to maximize your growth and long-term success. While many tempting options for gem usage exist, this guide will show you the most efficient way to use your gems in the early game.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-8-1024x564.png)

---

## Common Gem Spending Pitfalls

As a new player, you’ll encounter many alluring ways to spend your gems, such as:

- Speeding up building or castle upgrades.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-9-1024x193.png)

- Purchasing **Philosopher’s Stones** to summon immortals.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-10-1024x505.png)

- Accelerating technology research.

While these options might seem appealing, they are short-term gains that won’t provide the long-term value you need to excel in Infinity Kingdom.

---

## The Best Way to Spend Gems Early Game

### Invest in VIP Points

The single most effective way to spend your gems in the early game is by purchasing **VIP Points**. VIP levels offer powerful permanent buffs and rewards that significantly boost your growth and combat effectiveness. Reaching higher VIP levels early will give you a strong foundation to build upon.

### Why VIP Levels Matter

1. **Powerful Buffs and Boosts:**  - Increased resource production.
- Market refreshes
- AP and SP items
- Troop limit buffs
- Combat buffs
- and many more..

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-12-1024x542.png)

1. **Daily Philosopher’s Stone (VIP 10):**  - At VIP level 10, you unlock **1 free Philosopher’s Stone daily**. This reward is crucial for:  - Summoning immortals to strengthen your army.
- Converting immortal fragments into **Purple Crystals**, used to upgrade skills in the **Tower of Knowledge**.
2. **Long-Term Benefits:**  - Infinity Kingdom is a marathon, not a sprint. The free Philosopher’s Stone you receive every day from VIP 10 will compound over time, ensuring you have a steady supply for immortal summons and skill upgrades.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-13-1024x534.png)

---

## How to Reach VIP 10 Quickly

### Step 1: Focus All Gems on VIP Points

- Ignore other gem spending options and allocate every gem you earn towards VIP Points.
- Purchase VIP Points directly from the VIP menu in the game.

### Step 2: Utilize Free Gems

- Complete quests, chronicle and activity daily bonus to earn free gems.
- Participate in events that reward gems.

### Step 3: Track Progress

- Regularly monitor your VIP Points to track how close you are to the next level.
- Plan your gem spending strategically to hit VIP milestones.

---

## Why Not Spend on Philosopher’s Stones Directly?

While it might seem quicker to buy Philosopher’s Stones directly with gems, this approach is inefficient because:

- The gem cost for stones is high, and you’ll deplete your gems quickly.
- A daily free Philosopher’s Stone from VIP 10 provides better long-term value.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-14.png)

---

## Additional Tips for Maximizing Gem Efficiency

1. **Avoid Impulse Spending:**  - Resist the urge to use gems on speeding up builds or research unless absolutely necessary.
2. **Focus on Growth:**  - Use resources from daily activities and events to complement your gem strategy.
3. **Be Patient:**  - Reaching VIP 10 takes time, but the rewards are well worth the investment.

---

## Conclusion

Spending gems on VIP Points is the smartest strategy for new players in Infinity Kingdom. Reaching VIP 10 unlocks powerful buffs and the invaluable daily Philosopher’s Stone, setting you up for sustained growth and success. Stay focused, resist temptation, and invest your gems wisely to dominate in Norheim!
