### Infinity Kingdom's Siren's Gaze Bundle: A Comprehensive Player Guide

Welcome to the latest and arguably one of the most intriguing 5-day bundle offers in Infinity Kingdom: the Siren's Gaze bundle. Priced at $20 per day for five days, this bundle is your ticket to acquiring a total of 50 stone fragments for Siren's Gaze, a skill that, while not considered a must-have for every player, holds significant value in specific situations. Let's dive into the details and strategies around this offering.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-12-1024x520.png)

### Bundle Overview

**Price:** $100 total ($20/day over 5 days)
**Reward:** 50 stone fragments for Siren's Gaze

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-13-1024x391.png)

Siren's Gaze is more than just a skill; it's a game-changer in the right hands. Its ability to disrupt enemy lines by taunting and silencing backrow units makes it a formidable asset in your Tower of Knowledge arsenal.

### Siren's Gaze: Unleashing Silence

**Official Description:** "Every 12 seconds after the battle starts, you have a 75% chance to taunt a random backrow enemy unit for 6 seconds. Every time the skill is active, you will gain Siren's Shell: Enemy who landed a normal attack on you will be silenced for 1 second."

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-14.png)

**Strategic Implications:** Siren's Gaze excels in interrupting the enemy's flow during battles. By targeting backline units, it not only disrupts their ultimate abilities but also temporarily halts their energy regeneration. This dual impact of taunting and silencing can pivot the direction of a battle, especially against opponents reliant on their backline's special abilities.

### Synergistic Strategies

Siren's Gaze doesn't operate in a vacuum. Its strength is amplified when paired with other skills, such as Lost Dimension. The latter activates upon the equipped immortal taking physical damage, offering a 15% chance to reduce the enemy's energy regeneration by 50% and their hit rate by 15% for 6 seconds.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/image-15.png)

**Combination Play:** Imagine a scenario where Siren's Gaze draws the attention of an enemy, forcing them to attack and inadvertently activating Lost Dimension. This creates a compounded debuff, significantly reducing the enemy's effectiveness and opening them up for counter-attacks.

### Investment Consideration

Investing in the Siren's Gaze bundle is a strategic decision. The total cost of $100 for the full 50 stone fragments is a considerable amount, but for players looking to enhance their control and disruption capabilities, it offers valuable utility. Evaluate your current roster, strategic needs, and the overall impact Siren's Gaze could have on your gameplay before making the investment.

### Final Thoughts

Siren's Gaze is a testament to the depth and strategy inherent in Infinity Kingdom. While not essential for every player, its unique blend of control and disruption makes it a worthy consideration for those looking to refine their tactical approach. As with any investment in the game, consider how it fits into your broader strategy and whether its benefits align with your goals on the battlefield.
