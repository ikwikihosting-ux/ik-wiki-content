## The build setup

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/image-107-1024x472.png)

For Merlin we have a very standard setup. **Concentration + Annihilation + Death Breath** is a very run of the mill build for Merlin that will always secure impressive performance from our master damage dealer Merlin.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/image-108-1024x472.png)

For Emperor Qin we have **Berserk + Assist + Fighting Master**. Basically, Qin becomes a wounds and attack speed vending machine. He functions very well in this role and has huge synergies with Hannibal Barca (especially if you obtain his Unique Artifact). Moreover, because we are only interested in attack speed buffs and wounds from Qin we can comfortably run assist on him and allow his troop count to be nuked instead of Merlin's.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/image-109-1024x472.png)

For Hannibal Barca we have a pretty standard cleave - attack speed setup: **Anger + Combo + Cleave**. It is clear that we are relying on the attack speed buffs from Qin and from a powerful Hannibal Unique Artifact to make this build work. I would argue that he can be replaced 1:1 with Alex if you are in a situation where you have high level Alex unique but not Hannibal.

However, with this build setup it is clear that this is an extreemly powerful build that costs you almost nothing to run if you are in a younger server and you have had the chance to obtain free or cheap Hannibal Barca unique artifacts.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/image-110-1024x472.png)

Last up we have Richard. A surprising choice of Immortal for me personally, but also a very underrated Immortal. Richard's stun is very powerful and he performs the role of tank quite well too. In this build we are running: **Misleading + Life Link + Oaken Guard**. Playing on our enemies being non hit-capped will work very well until you reach the 400k+ threshold giving huge defensive value to misleading. Moreover, Life Link is an amazing tower of knowledge skill mitigating damage and even turning a tanky Richard into a thorns-damage-dealer. Lastly, Oaken guard is just solid solid solid and a must have in the current physical damage meta.

For the Dragon this team wants to get the control from the water Dragon in combination with Richard stuns to take control of the battlefield. To further the control this team wields we use the energy regen dragon specialization, effectively removing the energy regen of up to 3 enemy Immortals while Frozen. A very powerful control mechanic.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/image-111.png)

Moreover, you want the healing from the water dragon to keep your troop count up. This does leave you vulnerable to wounds, but if your enemy does not have access to wounds you get a nice and healthy boost from the added healing over time from the dragon specialization talents.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/image-112.png)
