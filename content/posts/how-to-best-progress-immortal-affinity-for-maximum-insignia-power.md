The Insignia system is one of the best sources of permanent account progression in **Infinity Kingdom**, but it's also one of the easiest systems to waste resources on.

When Affinity was first introduced, I assumed the goal was simple: take my main immortals as high as possible and collect their Insignias along the way. After spending more time with the system, it became clear that this isn't the most efficient approach. The Affinity cost rises sharply at higher levels, while the stat increase from later Insignias is often surprisingly small.

The real objective isn't maximizing one immortal—it's maximizing your **entire collection**.

If you approach Affinity as a collection system instead of an individual hero progression system, you'll unlock permanent account-wide stats much faster and hit the important Collection milestones far sooner.

This guide explains why.

---

## Understanding What You're Actually Progressing

Every immortal has a series of Insignias that unlock at specific Affinity levels.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-19-1024x510.png)

Each Insignia gives you two things:

- A permanent account-wide attribute bonus.
- One Collection Star.

That second reward is incredibly important because Collection Stars unlock additional global bonuses that affect every immortal you own.

The game summarizes the system quite well:

- Activated Insignias permanently increase your account's attributes.
- Collecting more Insignias unlocks stronger global attribute bonuses through Total Collection milestones.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-20.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-21.png)

In other words, every new Insignia makes your account stronger twice:

- once through its own attribute,
- and again by moving you closer to the next Collection milestone.

---

## Why Collection Stars Matter So Much

Many players focus only on the attribute printed on an individual Insignia.

That's only half the picture.

Every Insignia also contributes one star toward your Total Collection, which unlocks powerful global bonuses.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-22-1024x198.png)

These bonuses apply to **every immortal on your account**, regardless of team composition. That means every Collection Star has value beyond the Insignia itself. Reaching these milestones sooner is one of the biggest long-term power increases available.

## The Hidden Trap of Higher Affinity Levels

This is where many players unintentionally slow their progression. Let's look at a real example.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-26.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-25.png)

### Alexander the Great

At **Affinity Level 15**, Alexander unlocks his Bronze Insignia.

Reward:

- **+8 Physical Attack**
- **1 Collection Star**

At **Affinity Level 30**, he unlocks his Silver Insignia.

Reward:

- **+10 Physical Attack**
- **1 Collection Star**

At first glance, progressing from Bronze to Silver seems like the obvious choice. But look closer. You're investing a huge amount of additional Affinity for just **2 more Physical Attack**.

The resource cost increases dramatically, while the stat increase barely changes. This is where efficiency starts to matter.

**A Better Investment**: Instead of taking Alexander from Level 15 to Level 30 immediately, imagine spending those resources on another physical immortal.

For example, **Mehmed II**. Taking Mehmed from Level 0 to Level 15 gives you:

- **+8 Physical Attack**
- **1 Collection Star**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-28.png)

The difference is only **2 Physical Attack**, but the second option costs dramatically less Affinity. You also broaden your collection instead of concentrating resources into one immortal. Repeat this process across your roster, and the gains become significant. Rather than unlocking one expensive Insignia, you're collecting multiple efficient ones while racing toward the next Collection milestone.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-29.png)

## Prioritize the Right Immortals

Not every immortal grants the same attribute.

Some provide:

- Physical Attack
- Magical Attack
- Physical Defense
- Magical Defense
- Accuracy
- Dodge
- Resilience
- Percentage bonuses

This means you shouldn't simply level your favourite immortals. You should level the immortals that provide the permanent stats your account actually needs. For a physical account, prioritize immortals that reward Physical Attack or offensive physical bonuses. For mage accounts, focus on immortals that grant Magical Attack or offensive magic bonuses. Once your offensive stats are well established, you can begin collecting defensive bonuses.

Planning your Affinity this way gives every investment a clear purpose.

---

## Use the Correct Affinity Gifts

Another small optimization that adds up over time is using the correct gifts.

Elemental gifts provide bonus Affinity when used on immortals of the matching element.

For example:

- Fire gifts → Fire immortals
- Water gifts → Water immortals
- Earth gifts → Earth immortals
- Wind gifts → Wind immortals

For Chaos immortals, use **Spirit Font Nectar**, which is designed specifically for them.

Matching gifts properly gives you the most progress for every resource you spend.

---

## When Should You Push Beyond Level 15?

Stopping at Level 15 isn't a permanent rule. It's simply the most efficient way to grow early.

Once you've built a healthy collection and reached important milestones, the value of deeper Affinity investments increases. At that point, you've already secured many of the powerful account-wide bonuses, so improving individual immortals becomes a much better investment.

Until then, breadth consistently beats depth. I think the optimal is going for the 100 bonus threshold and then upgrading into finished 30 affinity levels for the attributes you need the most.

## Player Perspective

After comparing the rewards, I completely changed how I spend Affinity.

Originally, I was feeding resources into the immortals I used most often. On paper, that seemed logical, but once I compared the actual rewards, the numbers told a different story. Spending a large amount of Affinity to increase Alexander's reward from **+8 Physical Attack** to **+10 Physical Attack** simply wasn't as valuable as unlocking another immortal that also granted **+8 Physical Attack** while adding another Collection Star.

That change in mindset made the system much easier to optimize. Rather than trying to finish one immortal before moving on, I started treating every first Insignia as another step toward strengthening my entire account. The permanent stats accumulated much faster, and the Collection milestones came noticeably sooner.

---

## Final Thoughts

The biggest mistake players make with Affinity is thinking vertically instead of horizontally. Affinity isn't about creating one perfect immortal. It's about building a powerful collection.

Unlocking first Insignias across multiple immortals gives you:

- More permanent attributes.
- More Collection Stars.
- Faster access to powerful global milestone bonuses.
- Better value for every Affinity resource you spend.

Once you've established that foundation, you can always return later and push your favourite immortals to higher Affinity levels.

In the long run, you'll end up with both a stronger collection **and** stronger individual immortals—but you'll get there much more efficiently.
