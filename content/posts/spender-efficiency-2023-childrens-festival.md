It is the joyest time of the year where the Children play and in Norheim we celebrate all of childish souls - let's be honest here, we are all childrend playing an awesome game here! Anyway, in this guide we are going to skip the general event overview and the 101's and instead move streight to the point and discuss the main spender rewards and how to efficiently obtain these.

**What are the prices or the unique artifact rewards and of the Immortal unlocks?**

If you are looking for the general overview of this seasonal event, please see my guide **[here](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/events/spring-fiesta/childrens-day-event/childrens-day-event-2023/)**.

## How do we most efficiently obtain a unique artifact?

This event's unique artifact reward is very cheap compared to the average pricing of a Triss or Stellar Compass unique. **If we are efficient we can claim our artifact for just 70 USD**. Comparatively, in my experiance a Triss and a Stellar Compass easily costs you 120-150 USD for the same artifact. As such, a pretty decent discount and one you aught to make use of if you are indeed looking for e.g. that juicy Alex upgrade.

### How you ask?

First of all, you need a total of 120 marbles for the UA and 150 marbles for the full Immortal unlock on Khan/Charles/Baldwin.

There are a couple different ways of obtaining a few free Marbles.

| Joyful Park |  |
| --- | --- |
| - Star-chests | 10 |
| - Login rewards | 6 |
| Wonderful Home |  |
| - Wonderful Home to level 20 | 4 |
| - 3.5K gems Wonderful Home | 8 |
|  |  |
| Total Joyful Park = 16 |  |
| Total Joyful Park + Wonderful Home = 28 |  |

## Price of Genghis Khan / Charles / Baldwin

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/image-122.png)

You also have a shot at getting a full 60x Immortal fragments for either Khan, Charles or Baldwin. This will cost you 150 marbles and should plan your spending as follows:

| Joyful park 5 days | 16 |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wonderful Home | 12 |  |
| 5x 5 USD bundles daily | 40 | $25.00 |
| 4x 10USD bundle | 48 | $40.00 |
| 2x 20 USD bundles | 40 | $40.00 |
| SUM | 156 | $105.00 |

The changes for not completing Wonderful Home side event, is more easily dealt with here, as you simply add another 10$ bundle if you are unable to complete this event.

At a pricetag of 105$ for a full Immortal it is not particularly cheap to get your Immortal in this way, after all a simple 100$ bundle gets you the same fragments from the normal shop bundle. However, you do get some nice side rewards from the event bundles, namely soul crystal chests. Unfortunately, there are no Dragon Essences in this seasonal event's bundles.

## I want to both Immortal and Unique Artifact!

In that case, you are going to be left with a smaller discount since you cannot use the free to play rewards twice. Unfortunately the marbel rewards from the bundles are poorly optimized making it hard to stay efficient for this event. For instance, purchasing all 5$ bundles, all 10$ bundles, completing Wonderful Home tier 1 and 2, completing Joyful Park and also purchasing 7 of the expensive 20$ bundles nets you a disappointing 268 marbles... just 2 shy of our target.

| Getting both Artifact and Charles/Khan/Baldwin: 120 + 150 = 270 |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Joyful park 5 days | 16 |  |
| Wonderful Home | 12 |  |
| 5x 5 USD bundles daily | 40 | $25.00 |
| 5x 10USD bundle | 60 | $50.00 |
| 7x 20 USD bundles | 140 | $140.00 |
| Wonderful Home 20$ tier | 12 | $20.00 |
| SUM | 268 | $215.00 |
|  | --> this sucks! We missing 2 Marbels |  |

Instead, you have two options in getting to the full 270 marbels and both of these rewards.

1. Getting the third 20$ tier on Wonderful Home (I recommend this for players who can use the other rewards in the bar)
2. Saving 5$ and instead buying more bundles in the shop (recommended for those with little to no use for the Wonderful Home rewards)
