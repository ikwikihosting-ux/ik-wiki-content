Prosperity is one of those systems most players ignore until the day their castle suddenly flies across the map. It sits quietly in your Territory Info, but it controls how much your territory produces, how close you are to being zeroed, and how many gems you will burn if you panic-restore it in the middle of a fight.

This guide puts everything about Prosperity in one place: where to find it, what it actually does, how you lose it, how it comes back, and when gemming it is worth it.

## Where to Find Your Prosperity

Tap your Castle and open **Territory Info**. At the top you will see your kingdom's unique ability (on my kingdom Ruslan that is *Cavalry HP +5%*), and below it the Prosperity panel. The panel shows three things:

* **Current Territorial Resource Production Speed** – how much of your normal production you are actually getting
* **Current / Max Prosperity** – your Prosperity bar
* **Recovering Speed** – how much Prosperity you regain per hour

The blue **+** next to the bar is the gem restore button. More on that further down.

## The Rules

The game's own details page lists five rules. In plain words:

1. **Your Castle level sets your maximum Prosperity.** The higher the Castle, the higher the cap.
2. **You lose Prosperity when your territory is attacked and your defense fails.**
3. **At 0 Prosperity you are relocated to a random location**, and a Protection Barrier opens automatically for a while.
4. **Low Prosperity cuts your territory's resource production**, but Gold production from Dwellings is not affected.
5. **Gemming Prosperity back to full gets more expensive each time you do it.** The cost resets daily at UTC 0:00.

## Prosperity Bonus Tiers

High Prosperity does not just protect you, it also boosts your resource production:

| Prosperity | Resource Production Increase |
| ---------- | ---------------------------- |
| 5,000      | +2%                          |
| 15,000     | +4%                          |
| 30,000     | +6%                          |
| 40,000     | +8%                          |
| 50,000     | +10%                         |
| 60,000     | +12%                         |

My Castle 57 caps at **60,690 Prosperity**, just above the 60,000 threshold for the final +12% tier. Lower castles have lower caps, so check which tier your own cap actually reaches before you assume you get the full bonus.

## The Production Penalty

This is the part most people miss. Here is my castle at the time of writing, sitting at **1 / 60,690 Prosperity**:

* Current Territorial Resource Production Speed: **43% (100% − 57%)**

In other words, near zero Prosperity my territory produces less than half of what it normally would. Food, Wood, Stone and Iron all take the hit. Gold from Dwellings does not.

Swing that around and you get the real value of Prosperity for a farmer: the gap between a castle at the bottom and a castle at full Prosperity is the difference between 43% production and 100% production **plus** a 12% bonus on top.

If you bank hundreds of millions of resources and mostly fight, this barely matters. If you rely on your territory and gathering to fund upgrades, it matters a lot.

## How You Lose Prosperity

**Failed defenses.** Every lost fight at your walls costs Prosperity. The amount depends on how badly you lose: a loss to a much stronger army with many troops dead costs more than a narrow loss. There is a cap on how much you can lose in a single attack, and that cap depends on your Walls level.

**Legion of Frostborne.** Castles sitting in the snow outside a Thermal Zone lose Prosperity over time. During **Sleet** weather, territories outside a Thermal Zone lose Prosperity 50% faster. A Protection Barrier stops the Sleet drain. My castle is parked in the snow on the Legion map right now, which is exactly why it reads 1 Prosperity and **0/hour** recovery.

**Last Stand.** The Last Stand talent adds a strong damage boost to your army, but it comes at a Prosperity cost. Great in a close fight, risky if you are already low.

**Sparring and Squad Survival.** Alliance Sparring uses normal combat rules, so Prosperity loss still applies (territory resources are never lost, though). In Squad Survival, standing in lava drains Prosperity fast, and you cannot gem it back there.

## What Happens at Zero

When Prosperity hits 0:

* A short timer starts, and when it ends your castle flies to a random location.
* A Protection Barrier opens automatically.
* **Second Wind** triggers, and you receive a resource package based mostly on the troops you lost since your last zeroing.

Being zeroed is not always a disaster. Some players deliberately let Prosperity fall to 1 and then get zeroed on purpose to cash in Second Wind. See [Understanding Second Wind](/posts/understanding-second-wind) for how that works.

## How Prosperity Comes Back

Outside of combat zones, Prosperity recovers on its own at the rate shown under **Recovering Speed**. You can speed that up with:

* **"King of the Supreme" Territory Decoration** – increases Prosperity recovery speed
* **Paradeisos Castle Skin** – +5% Prosperity Recovery Speed
* **Capital cabinet position** – a Governor can appoint a position worth +30% Prosperity regeneration speed
* **Sturdy** (Legion of Frostborne skill) – extra Prosperity recovery during Legion

## Gemming Prosperity: When It's Worth It

Tap the **+** on the Prosperity bar and the game offers a full restore for gems. My quote at 1 / 60,690 was **1,097 gems** to go all the way back to max. Since patch 2.8.6 the price climbs with every restore you do on the same day and only resets at UTC 0:00, so the old trick of gemming back to full again and again during a fight gets very expensive very fast.

![Gem restore prompt: 1,097 gems to recover to max Prosperity](/uploads/screenshot-20261126-214101-lzg0o7.webp)

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-091956-2pwlqb.png)

**Gem it when:**

* You are holding an important position (a frontline castle in Legion, a garrison next to an objective) and getting zeroed would remove you from the fight.
* You need your territory production back for a long farming stretch and you are not planning to fight in the next day or two.

**Don't gem it when:**

* You are about to be hit again by a much stronger player. You will just pay to lose it twice.
* You want to be zeroed for Second Wind.
* You are playing a low-Prosperity raiding style where being ported to new hunting grounds is the point.
* You are in Illusion Battlefield, where gemming Prosperity is disabled anyway.

A better habit than gemming in a panic: if you are frontline and dropping low, swap position with a healthier teammate. A weakened player behind the lines can still help with rallies and garrisons.

## Quick Summary

* Check Prosperity in Castle → Territory Info.
* Bonus tiers go from +2% at 5,000 up to +12% at 60,000.
* Near zero, my production dropped to 43%. Gold from Dwellings is never affected.
* You lose it from failed defenses (capped per attack by Walls level), Legion snow and Sleet, and Last Stand.
* At zero you relocate, get a barrier and trigger Second Wind.
* Gem restores get more expensive with each use and reset at UTC 0:00, so save gems for moments where staying in place actually matters.
