The Infinity Kingdom 2.6.6 patch, is a major update with numerous features and optimizations. Highlights include the Equipment Gem Slotting feature, which allows players to enhance their gear with attack, defense, or support gems. This system provides a significant power boost and is accessible without a paywall, a major positive shift for the game.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-113-1024x483.png)

Let's delve deeper into the key aspects of the Infinity Kingdom's 2.6.6 patch, focusing on the Equipment Gem Slotting feature and Legendary Skills. In this guide I shall go over what I find to be interesting in the new patch, so we are going to skip a fair bit of it too! **[Read the full patch notes here.](https://infinitykingdom.gtarcade.com/en/news/7019371.html)**

## Equipment Gem Slotting Feature

This feature revolutionizes how players can augment their equipment. Here's a breakdown:

- **Gem Types:**  - **Attack Gems** boost Physical and Magical Attack, crucial for offensive strategies.
- **Defense Gems** enhance Physical and Magic Defense, pivotal for durability in battles.
- **Support Gems** improve Resilience and Accuracy, offering tactical advantages.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-115-1024x569.png)

- **Obtaining Gems:**  - Primarily acquired by defeating monsters, ensuring that all players have access to these enhancements without a paywall.
- Eight levels of gems exist, allowing for progression and strategic depth. Combining three lower-level gems of the same type creates a higher-level gem, adding an element of resource management.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-114-1024x513.png)

As you farm gems from gnomes you bank quite a few gems quickly. That's great, because we are going to need them! You need to combine gems to upgrade their levels. Max level and requirements are unknown as of now.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-116-1024x601.png)

## Legendary Skills in the Tower of Knowledge

- **Introduction of Legendary Skills:**  - These are separate from common skills and offer potentially game-changing abilities.
- Upgrading requires red passive skill stones, indicating a higher resource investment.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-117-1024x94.png)

- **Strategic Implications:**  - Likely to be powerful, these skills could redefine battle strategies.
- While potentially expensive, they offer a new echelon of gameplay for invested players.
- The introduction of these skills might make existing high-tier skills more accessible, balancing the playing field.

This patch represents a significant evolution in Infinity Kingdom's gameplay, offering both enhanced strategic depth and accessibility. Players should explore these features to fully leverage their potential in their quest for dominance. Whie these new Legendary Skills are most likely going to be heavily paywalled. But there may be some upside to that for us none whales - let's face it, chaos spenders are playing their own game at this point anyway requiring no skill or brains to beat anyone without chaos - but for the rest of us we may just get access to the expensive Triss skills more cheaply as new legendary skills become the spender primary target!
