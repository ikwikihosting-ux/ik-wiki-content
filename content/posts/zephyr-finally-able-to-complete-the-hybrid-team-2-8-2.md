### Using Zephyr (Wind Dragon) in Hybrid Setups

With the release of **Patch 2.8.2**, a groundbreaking shift has finally arrived in Infinity Kingdom: the **Wind Dragon, Zephyr**, is no longer locked to exclusively buffing Wind-element Immortals. That’s right—**Zephyr’s powerful skills can now be applied to all elements**, which means one of the game’s most stat-rich and synergistic dragons is finally unlocked for **Hybrid teams**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-53-1024x499.png)

For players running the **popular Earth-Wind Hybrid team** (think Alexander + Hannibal), this is **huge news**. Let’s break down why the Wind Dragon is about to **replace Earth Dragon (Dimitr)** as the go-to option for hybrid setups.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-52.png)

---

## 🌪️ Why Zephyr Over Dimitr in Hybrid?

### 🔋 1. No More Wind Restriction

Previously, all of Zephyr’s active and passive abilities were **tied to Wind-element Immortals only**, which meant hybrid players couldn’t use him effectively. That’s now changed. **All allied Immortals** can benefit from the dragon’s effects—**Alex, Hannibal, Peter, etc.** This finally opens the door for full Zephyr integration in your hybrid build.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-54-1024x179.png)

### 💥 2. Massive Normal Attack Synergy

Hybrid setups thrive on **high attack speed and normal attack damage**, which is where Zephyr shines:

- **Passive Bonus**: +25% **Normal Attack Damage** — a flat and consistent boost that scales perfectly with the attack-speed-heavy builds of hybrid teams.
- **High Crit and Dodge**: Zephyr grants both, making your frontlines tankier and your damage more consistent.

The Earth Dragon is great for survivability and decent stat boosts, but it lacks the **raw offensive synergy** that Zephyr brings to a hybrid comp.

---

## 💣 Zephyr's Key Skills for Hybrid Builds

### 🌀 Turmoil Breath

- Deals damage to all enemies.
- **Reduces Accuracy**.
- Applies a debuff that **increases the physical damage taken by enemies by 24%**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-55-1024x385.png)

> This is HUGE for Alex, Hannibal, and any physical damage dealer. That 24% increased physical damage taken is one of the most powerful debuffs for hybrid burst strategies.

### 🌿 Stormy Breath

- On **normal attack triggers**, has a **60% chance** to recover HP over **15.6 seconds**.
- Applies to any allied troops (not just wind troops anymore).

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-56-1024x380.png)

> This gives your march **sustain** without sacrificing offensive power. It also helps protect your key damage dealers like Alex or Hannibal during longer engagements.

### 🔧 Squall Howling Passive Effects

- +25% **normal attack damage**
- +Crit Rate and +Dodge

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-57-1024x325.png)

> This set of passives is uniquely tailored to the **high-tempo combat style** of hybrid teams, enabling huge damage output while keeping your frontliners hard to kill.

| Feature | Zephyr (Wind) | Dimitr (Earth) |

> Verdict: Zephyr now outshines Dimitr in almost every way for offensive hybrid builds.

## 💡 How to Use Zephyr in Hybrid Marches

If you're running a classic hybrid like:

- **Alexander (Main DPS)**
- **Hannibal Barca (Secondary DPS)**
- **Support Zenobia and Qin in the backline**

Then Zephyr will enhance all of them by:

- Amplifying normal attack damage
- Increasing team sustain
- Boosting crit output
- Applying a critical physical damage debuff to enemies

Hybrid marches already rely on **stacking attack speed**, **Combo**, **Demonic Rage**, and **Blade Vortex**. With Zephyr’s bonuses, these skills scale even harder.

> 🔁 **Pro Tip**: Use Zephyr alongside Adrenaline Rush or Time to Shine on Alex to trigger more normal attacks and capitalize on his +25% normal damage buff.
