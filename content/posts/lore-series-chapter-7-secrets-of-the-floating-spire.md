## 💠Secrets of the Floating Spire

Chapter 7 opens with a dying man delivering a warning:

> “Go to the West. Tell them the Council is forging a false god.”

This immediately establishes that the Floating Spire is part of something much larger than an isolated alchemical experiment. The Council is not simply studying forbidden magic. It appears to be attempting to create some form of artificial divinity.

The episode then returns to Brynhild and the Hangover Knight inside the Spire’s laboratory. The glowing figures held inside its enormous cylinders are finally identified as the souls of fallen warriors.

Brynhild is horrified. These warriors should have travelled to Valhalla after death, but instead their souls have been captured and imprisoned inside the machinery.

The Hangover Knight explains the truth very simply:

> “They’re used as fuel.”

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261004-201356-pns4rn.png)

## 💠The Council Is Harvesting Warrior Souls

This is the most important revelation of the chapter. The Floating Spire appears to be powered by the souls of the dead, including warriors who were supposed to pass into Valhalla.

It also explains why so many people have disappeared around the Spire and why the Council has worked so hard to conceal what happens inside it. The missing scouts and stolen alchemical components uncovered in earlier chapters were only pieces of a much larger operation.

The Hangover Knight’s first reaction is to destroy the soul containers, but Brynhild stops him. Smashing the machinery directly could kill them both and potentially endanger the souls trapped inside.

When guards arrive, Brynhild instead strikes the central mechanism. The machinery begins to overload, giving them an opportunity to escape and perhaps disrupting the process that was holding the warrior souls.

## 💠The Philosopher’s Stone Appears

Before they leave, the Hangover Knight retrieves a glowing red crystal from the machinery. This appears to be the **Philosopher’s Stone** mentioned in the official description.

Interestingly, the English subtitles refer to it as the **Sorcerer’s Stone**, but this seems to be a translation difference rather than a separate object.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261004-201448-8ahuo2.png)

The pair now possess the stone, but they still do not understand exactly what it is, how it was created or what role it plays in the Council’s attempt to forge a false god.

Obtaining the stone therefore does not end the mystery. It gives Brynhild and the Hangover Knight the first physical piece of evidence connecting the Council, the Floating Spire and the imprisoned souls.

## 💠The Journey Continues West

After escaping through the Spire’s ventilation system, Brynhild asks what their next move should be.

Their destination is a place in the West where humanity once made a covenant with the Goddess. They believe it may contain answers about the Philosopher’s Stone and perhaps the power the Council is trying to recreate.

When they emerge, however, they find a distant city already burning.

Whatever is happening is no longer contained inside the Floating Spire. The destruction ahead suggests that the Council’s plans are advancing quickly, and Brynhild and the Hangover Knight may already be running out of time.

## 💠Why Chapter 7 Matters

Chapter 7 gives the Floating Spire a much darker purpose. It is not merely a laboratory or storage facility. It is a machine powered by stolen souls.

For Brynhild, this is also deeply personal. The warriors inside were denied their rightful passage to Valhalla and reduced to fuel for the Council’s experiments. Her anger makes the discovery feel like more than another clue in the investigation.

The warning about a “false god” is even more important. It suggests that the Council may be using the Philosopher’s Stone and the power of countless warrior souls to create, summon or imitate something divine.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261004-201421-p0jdim.png)

## 💠My Take

This is one of the strongest lore chapters so far because it finally begins connecting the different mysteries introduced throughout the series. The missing people, the alchemical components, the Floating Spire and the Philosopher’s Stone now appear to be parts of the same project.

We still do not know what the Council’s false god will be, why the covenant with the Goddess matters or whether the souls inside the Spire were truly freed. However, the story has clearly moved beyond a simple hunt for a magical object.

Brynhild and the Hangover Knight now have the Philosopher’s Stone, but their next destination is already burning. Chapter 8 should take us west and, hopefully, closer to understanding what the Council is actually trying to create.

## 💠Follow the Journey

**Resonance of the Sacred Emblem** continues to expand the world of Norheim by giving familiar characters, locations and items a proper place within the game’s story.

Stay tuned for the next chapter as Brynhild and the Hangover Knight travel towards the site of humanity’s covenant with the Goddess.
