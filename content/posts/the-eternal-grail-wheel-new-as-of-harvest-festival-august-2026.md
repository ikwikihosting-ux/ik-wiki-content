## Wish Grail vs. Dazzling Wish – Which Is Better?

Both Wish Grail and Dazzling Wish use Tokens of Light, but they now offer distinctly different approaches to obtaining outfit fragments.

During Ghost Fiesta, **Dazzling Wish offers 20 Fu Fei *Night of Terror* fragments at a 0.2% drop chance**, while Wish Grail offers older Immortal outfits at a 0.45% jackpot chance.

The difference in Eternal Grail rewards is also substantial.

### Why Does Dazzling Wish Give Only 8 Expected Fragments?

The Dazzling Wish outfit reward contains 20 Fu Fei fragments, but its probability is only 0.2% per draw.

Over 200 draws, that's an expected 0.4 successful outfit drops:

**200 × 0.002 × 20 = 8 expected fragments.**

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261022-134326-1y96ut.png)

That doesn't mean you'll actually receive eight fragments. In practice, you'll usually receive none, while lucky players receive 20 or more.

In fact, the probability of obtaining at least one outfit drop in 200 draws is approximately 33%.

To unlock Fu Fei's outfit from zero, players need 80 fragments, equivalent to four successful 20-fragment drops. The probability of achieving that through 200 ordinary draws is only about 0.076%, excluding other reward sources.

By comparison, Wish Grail offers a 59.4% chance of at least one selected outfit jackpot within 200 spins.

### Which Event Should You Choose?

For farming Eternal Grails, Wish Grail is clearly superior.

Even when selecting an outfit jackpot, its 787 expected Grails from 200 spins are almost twice the 408 expected from Dazzling Wish.

Wish Grail also offers better direct outfit drop probabilities, although the featured rewards are different.

**Choose Dazzling Wish if you specifically want the newest seasonal outfit**, such as Fu Fei's *Night of Terror*, or are competing for its ranking rewards.

**Choose Wish Grail if you want older outfits or want to maximize Eternal Grail income.**

### How Many Spins Does a Jackpot Take?

With a 0.45% drop chance, a jackpot would take an average of approximately **222 spins without pity**.

However, because Wish Grail guarantees a jackpot after 300 consecutive spins without one, the actual average waiting time becomes considerably shorter.

Including the pity mechanic, players need approximately **165 spins per jackpot on average**.

That means the expected waiting time is the same whether you select:

* 300 Eternal Grails
* 60 Alexander outfit fragments
* 20 Athena outfit fragments

The reward changes, but the probability does not.

This is a long-run average across repeated jackpot cycles. Individual players may hit their jackpot on the first spin, while others may need to reach the pity threshold.

## How Many Eternal Grails Can You Expect?

The regular wheel probabilities produce an average of **2.935 Eternal Grails per spin**, excluding the selected grand prize.

If you choose 300 Eternal Grails as your jackpot, the natural 0.45% jackpot probability contributes another 1.35 Grails per spin before pity effects.

This produces the familiar baseline of **4.285 Eternal Grails per Token of Light**, excluding milestone bonuses and pity improvements.

Choosing an outfit instead removes the 300-Grail jackpot reward, but leaves all ordinary Eternal Grail prizes and milestone bonuses intact.

### Comparing 200 Spins

Choosing the outfit jackpot costs an expected **270 Eternal Grails over 200 spins**.

In return, players have a 59.4% chance of receiving at least one outfit jackpot.

The expected rewards are:

* **Alexander:** 54 fragments from 200 spins.
* **Athena:** 18 fragments from 200 spins.

These are statistical averages, rather than the number of fragments a player will necessarily obtain.

Alexander fragments are awarded in batches of 60, meaning one successful jackpot is enough to unlock his outfit. Athena fragments come in batches of 20, so players may require multiple jackpots depending on their existing fragment balance.

**MY takeaway:** Outfit jackpots offer an attractive tradeoff. You're giving up approximately 26% of your expected Grail income at 200 spins, but gaining a meaningful chance of obtaining an older Immortal outfit.

## Final Recommendations – Which Jackpot Should You Choose?

**Choose 300 Eternal Grails if:** Your priority is maximizing Eternal Grail income for Immortals, artifacts, and other seasonal shop purchases. This remains the strongest option for players focused on progression resources.

**Choose Alexander's outfit if:** You want *Summer Passion*. A single jackpot awards the full 60 fragments needed to unlock the outfit, making it a particularly attractive option.

**Choose Athena's outfit if:** You specifically want to unlock or upgrade *Summer Passion*. Her jackpot awards 20 fragments, so consider your existing fragment balance before committing Tokens.

**Choose Dazzling Wish if:** You want the newest seasonal outfit, such as Fu Fei's *Night of Terror*, or are interested in its ranking rewards.

For most casual spenders, 200 spins remains my recommended milestone. It offers excellent bonus rewards and allows players to participate without committing to the much more expensive 400–1,500-spin milestones.

## Final Thoughts

The October 2026 Wish Grail update is a meaningful improvement for players interested in collecting Immortal outfits.

Rather than obtaining new outfits only during their original seasonal release, players may now have opportunities to collect older designs through the Wish Grail.

Most importantly, selecting an outfit doesn't mean abandoning Eternal Grail farming entirely.

At 200 spins, players can still expect approximately 787 Eternal Grails while having a 59.4% chance of obtaining at least one outfit jackpot.

For players who don't care about outfits, selecting 300 Eternal Grails remains the better choice, providing approximately 1,057 Grails at the same milestone.

The long-run jackpot frequency is also relatively generous once the pity mechanic is considered, averaging approximately one jackpot every 165 spins.

**My recommendation:** Decide whether you value the selected outfit more than the Eternal Grails you're sacrificing. If the outfit matters to you, Wish Grail is now a compelling option. Otherwise, stick with the traditional 300-Grail jackpot and maximize your seasonal currency.

If Infinity Kingdom continues rotating previous seasonal outfits into the Wish Grail, this may become one of the game's most important recurring activities for outfit collectors.
