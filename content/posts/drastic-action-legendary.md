### Introduction

*Drastic Action* is one of the more impactful **legendary Tower of Knowledge skills** for physical-focused builds. At a glance, it reads like a standard high-damage triggered skill, but in practice it behaves more like an **execute tool**—designed to quickly remove weakened targets and shift the momentum of a fight.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-10-1024x658.png)

Its strength doesn’t come from raw damage alone, but from how that damage is applied: targeted, conditional, and repeatable within a short cycle. Because of this, it often overperforms compared to other single-target skills, especially in coordinated damage setups.

---

### Skill Mechanics and Breakdown

### Core Properties:

- **Type**: Triggered skill
- **Restriction**: Archers + Attack Immortals
- **Trigger Cycle**: Every 6 seconds
- **Trigger Chance**: 60%

### Effects:

- Deals **600% physical damage** to the enemy with the **highest HP lost**
- If the target is **below 70% HP**, the skill triggers an **additional damage instance**
- Reduces healing received by **all enemies**by **70% for 6 seconds**

---

### Why Drastic Action Functions as an Execute Skill

The key to understanding Drastic Action is target selection and conditional scaling.

- It prioritizes **the most damaged enemy**
- It gains **extra damage once that target drops below 70% HP**
- It cycles every 6 seconds with a **high trigger rate**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-8.png)

This creates a natural execution pattern:

1. Your team spreads initial damage
2. One target becomes the “most damaged”
3. Drastic Action repeatedly hits that same unit
4. Once below 70%, damage spikes due to the second hit

The result is **accelerated target elimination**, often before healing or defensive rotations can stabilize the unit.

---

### Synergy with Single-Target Damage Dealers

Drastic Action performs best in builds that already apply **focused pressure**.

It pairs naturally with immortals like:

- Alexander
- Ares

These units specialize in **single-target damage**, which helps quickly establish a priority target. Once that target is identified (highest HP lost), Drastic Action reinforces that pressure.

Important distinction:

- The skill **does not need to be equipped on these immortals**
- It simply benefits from being in a lineup that creates **focused damage patterns**

For example:

- Running Drastic Action on a **backline archer**
- While Alexander/Ares apply frontline pressure

This separation often results in more consistent uptime and safer positioning, while still benefiting from the execute effect.

---

### Anti-Heal Component (Often Overlooked)

While most players focus on the damage, the **70% healing reduction** is just as impactful.

In practical terms:

- It prevents recovery on the target you are trying to eliminate
- It amplifies effective team damage during its duration
- It weakens sustain-heavy compositions

In longer fights, this effect can be the difference between:

- A target stabilizing and recovering
- Or being permanently pushed into execution range

This is one reason Drastic Action performs well even outside pure burst setups.

---

### Interaction with Multi-Hit and Proc-Based Builds

Because Drastic Action can:

- Hit twice under conditions
- Apply repeated damage cycles

It contributes additional **damage instances**, which has synergy with:

- Executioner-type effects
- Other proc-based scaling mechanics

In testing, builds that rely on chaining effects tend to gain more value than expected from this skill—not because of its base numbers, but because of how often it contributes to the damage pipeline.

### Player Perspective

From a practical standpoint, Drastic Action is one of those skills that performs better in real fights than it appears on paper.

When I first tested it, the expectation was a standard single-target damage proc. What stood out instead was how reliably it **locked onto one unit and finished it**. Once a target dipped below 70%, the double-hit effect created a noticeable spike in pressure.

In coordinated setups—especially alongside units like Alexander—the skill consistently shortened fights by removing one enemy earlier than expected. That alone often decides engagements.

The anti-heal component becomes more noticeable in higher-level fights. Against sustain-heavy teams, you can see healing numbers drop off during its window, making it much harder for opponents to recover.

Where players sometimes misjudge the skill is placement. It doesn’t need to sit on your main damage dealer. In many cases, running it on a safer backline unit results in **more consistent triggers and better overall value**.

---

### Conclusion Summary

- Drastic Action is a **high-impact legendary triggered skill** focused on single-target pressure
- Deals **600% damage**, with a **second hit below 70% HP**
- Functions as an **execute tool**, rapidly eliminating weakened enemies
- Synergizes with **single-target damage dealers** like Alexander and Ares
- Provides strong **anti-heal (70%)**, increasing effective damage
- Does **not require frontline placement** to be effective
- High investment cost makes it a **long-term strategic choice**

In practical terms, Drastic Action is less about raw numbers and more about **control over fight pacing**. It rewards builds that can focus damage and turns that pressure into reliable eliminations.
