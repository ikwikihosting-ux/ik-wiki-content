## Introduction

Matchmaking is one of the least transparent systems in Legion of Frostborne. The developers have never revealed the exact formula, but they have explained the factors used to build each Conquest season. Understanding those basics makes it much easier to prepare your alliance before KvK begins.

Over the years, the system has also evolved. Older Legion of Frostborne seasons often produced uneven matchups or worlds with very little activity. The current matchmaking system aims to create more competitive wars by considering more than just alliance power.

This guide covers the core mechanics of matchmaking, faction assignment, and Federations as they work today.

---

## How Matchmaking Works

Legion of Frostborne matchmaking is used during the **Conquest Season**.

Instead of grouping servers using a single statistic, the game evaluates several factors before creating each KvK world. According to the developers, these include:

- Lord Power
- Historical battle performance
- Player activity
- Previous participation in Legion of Frostborne

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-20-1024x571.png)

The exact weighting of these factors has never been published, so nobody outside the development team can calculate matchmaking in advance. From experience, however, active servers with a history of strong KvK performance generally face opponents of similar strength.

The goal is to create worlds where every faction has a realistic chance to compete, rather than matching highly active servers against inactive ones.

---

## Faction Assignment

Once matchmaking is complete, each KvK world is divided into **four factions**.

The eight strongest alliances, based on Alliance Power, are automatically assigned to those factions. Every remaining alliance has a 48-hour window to choose which faction it wants to join.

If an alliance does not make a selection before the deadline, the game assigns one automatically.

One important restriction begins at this stage: once faction selection opens, server migration is locked until the Legion of Frostborne season ends.

---

## Understanding Federations

One of the biggest changes to Legion of Frostborne was the introduction of the **Federation** system.

A Federation replaces much of the old alliance-only cooperation by allowing up to four alliances from the same faction to work together almost as a single group.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-21-1024x563.png)

Any alliance leader can create a Federation by spending Gems, and leadership can later be transferred if needed.

In practice, Federations have become the foundation of alliance coordination throughout KvK.

---

## What Federations Can Do

Alliances inside the same Federation gain access to several forms of shared gameplay.

They can:

- Launch rallies together.
- Garrison cities, checkpoints, and Thermal Towers together.
- Donate troops to shared garrisons.
- Reinforce players from other Federation alliances.
- Defend each other during battles.
- Attack enemy Federations as a coordinated force.

Friendly fire is also disabled between Federation members. You cannot attack players or Thermal Towers belonging to another alliance within your Federation.

This level of cooperation makes Federations far more effective than individual alliances operating independently.

---

## City Ownership and Rewards

Although Federations fight together, city ownership still follows alliance rules.

If multiple Federation alliances participate in a siege, the city belongs to whichever alliance defeats the final defending garrison.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/image-22-1024x651.png)

Rewards are divided differently depending on the objective.

**First Occupation Points** are shared between participating alliances based on the percentage of damage each alliance contributed during the capture.

**Continuous Occupation Points** belong only to the alliance that officially owns the city.

Federation members also share city specialties and city benefits, allowing every participating alliance to benefit from successful expansion.

---

## Why Planning Matters

One detail catches many servers by surprise.

The top eight alliances are assigned to factions automatically. Because of this, two allied alliances from the same server can occasionally end up in different factions before anyone has a chance to create a Federation.

If that happens, they cannot join the same Federation during that Legion of Frostborne season.

From my experience, this makes communication before matchmaking incredibly important. Server leadership should already have a rough plan for alliance organisation before faction assignments appear. Once the assignments are locked in, there is very little room to fix mistakes.

---

## Veteran Tips

A few habits consistently make Legion of Frostborne preparation much smoother.

- Keep your alliance active throughout the season. Activity appears to be one of the factors considered during matchmaking.
- Plan Federation leadership before KvK starts instead of deciding after factions are assigned.
- Coordinate city ownership early so alliances know which objectives they are responsible for.
- Remember that a Federation is much more than a diplomatic agreement. Use its shared rallies, garrisons, and reinforcements to their full potential.

I've found that the strongest servers aren't always the ones with the biggest spenders. More often, they're the servers that organise well before the map even opens.

---

## Final Thoughts

While the exact matchmaking formula remains unknown, the overall system is much easier to understand than many players think. The game evaluates several indicators of server strength before creating each Conquest season, then divides those worlds into four factions supported by the Federation system.

For most players, success starts long before the first battle. Understanding how factions are formed, how Federations operate, and how city rewards are distributed allows your server to prepare properly instead of reacting once the war has already begun.

No matchmaking system is perfect, but knowing how the current one works gives your alliance the best chance to enter Legion of Frostborne organised and ready to compete.
