## What Are Stellar Core Crystals?

Stellar Core Crystals are a premium alternative to the ordinary Azure Crystals produced through the Coin Forge.

Both crystals contribute Energy toward upgrading level 55–59 buildings, but their values are very different.

In other words, **one Stellar Core Crystal is equivalent to 100 Azure Crystals**, before accounting for any critical hits that may occur when charging with Azure Crystals.

The biggest advantage is that Stellar Core Crystals don't require you to spend hours producing Azure Crystals in the Coin Forge.

Instead, you can purchase them using Eternal Grails and immediately inject the Energy into your buildings.

The price is **30 Eternal Grails for 10 Stellar Core Crystals**, with a permanent purchase limit of 30 bundles during the event.

## How Much Forge Time Can You Save?

The Coin Forge takes approximately **36 seconds to produce one Azure Crystal**, meaning players can produce around 100 Azure Crystals per hour when maintaining continuous production.

Since each Azure Crystal provides one Energy, the baseline production rate is approximately 100 Energy per hour.

Purchasing the maximum 300 Stellar Core Crystals therefore saves the equivalent of **300 hours, or 12.5 days, of continuous Azure Crystal production**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261029-102429-1g4x9t.png)

That's almost two weeks of forging skipped through a single seasonal purchase.

These figures use the normal 36-second forging time and don't account for critical hits during charging, which can increase the Energy gained from ordinary Azure Crystals.

## How Much Energy Do Legendary Buildings Need?

The reason Stellar Core Crystals are so valuable is the enormous amount of Energy required for Legendary building upgrades.

For example, upgrading the Castle to level 58 requires **13,500 Energy**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261029-102439-6mcnvw.png)

That's equivalent to 13,500 ordinary Azure Crystals, requiring 135 hours of continuous production at the normal forging rate.

Alternatively, players could purchase 135 Stellar Core Crystals, providing the same amount of Energy immediately.

The important thing to remember is that this represents the Castle's Energy requirement alone.

Before reaching Castle 58, players must also upgrade prerequisite buildings, many of which require thousands of additional Energy.

Consequently, progressing through Legendary building levels requires an enormous amount of production time.

Even the maximum 30,000 Energy available from seasonal Stellar Core Crystal purchases will cover only part of the total Energy required for continued progression.

## You Also Save Hundreds of Millions of Resources

The production time isn't the only thing players save by purchasing Stellar Core Crystals.

Forging ordinary Azure Crystals also consumes resources.

Based on player calculations shared within the Infinity Kingdom community, producing 300 Azure Crystals requires approximately:

* 50 million Wood
* 50 million Stone
* 50 million Iron

For players who are already struggling to maintain the resources required for Legendary building upgrades, skipping production can provide additional benefits beyond simply saving time.

That said, the extremely long forging times often mean that active players can gather additional resources faster than they can convert them into Azure Crystals.

For many accounts, production time remains the bigger bottleneck.

## Is Spending 900 Eternal Grails Actually Worth It?

This is where the decision becomes interesting.

At the maximum purchase limit, you're effectively spending **900 Eternal Grails to skip 300 hours of forging**, alongside the resources that would otherwise have been consumed.

That's equivalent to:

**3 Eternal Grails per hour of forging time saved.**

For players trying to reach higher building levels quickly, that may sound extremely attractive.

But Eternal Grails are also one of Infinity Kingdom's most valuable premium currencies.

They can be used to purchase Immortal fragments, exclusive artifact schematics, Chaos Immortal selection chests, and other highly valuable progression materials.

For example, the October festival offers an 80-fragment Fate of Chaos Selection Chest for just 250 Eternal Grails, while a 60-fragment Chaos Artifact Schematic Selection Chest costs 330.

Spending 900 Eternal Grails on Stellar Core Crystals means giving up the opportunity to purchase several of these rewards.

And unlike Immortal fragments and artifacts, **Energy is something players can eventually obtain entirely for free**.

That distinction is important.

### Long-Term Efficiency vs. Short-Term Progression

If your priority is maximizing long-term account value, spending Eternal Grails on Energy generally isn't the most efficient choice.

The Coin Forge will continue producing Azure Crystals, and eventually you'll accumulate enough Energy to complete your upgrades without spending premium currency.

Every Eternal Grail spent on Energy is one that cannot be invested into Immortals or other premium rewards.

However, if your priority is reaching higher building levels as quickly as possible, Stellar Core Crystals can offer tremendous value.

For example, reaching a new building milestone ahead of your opponents may unlock better research, higher troop tiers, or other progression advantages that improve your performance in PvP.

In that situation, saving almost two weeks of forging could be worth substantially more than the Eternal Grails you spend.

The key is recognizing that **Stellar Core Crystals are a purchase for progression speed, not necessarily long-term resource efficiency**.

## Should You Buy Stellar Core Crystals?

My recommendation depends on what kind of player you are.

**For F2P and low-spending players:** Generally skip Stellar Core Crystals. Eternal Grails are too valuable to spend on something that can eventually be produced for free. Prioritize Immortal development, artifact progression, and efficient selection chests instead.

**For moderate spenders:** Consider them if you're approaching an important building milestone and the saved production time will make a meaningful difference. You don't necessarily need to purchase the full 300-crystal allowance.

**For competitive players and heavy spenders:** Stellar Core Crystals can be an excellent way to accelerate Legendary progression. Saving up to 300 hours of forging every seasonal event is a substantial advantage, particularly when racing toward higher troop tiers and important research milestones.

## Final Thoughts – Buying Time Rather Than Power

Stellar Core Crystals are an interesting addition to Infinity Kingdom's seasonal shops because they offer something different from the usual Immortal and artifact rewards.

They don't directly strengthen your marches or unlock new Immortals. Instead, they allow you to accelerate one of the slowest parts of Legendary progression.

For **900 Eternal Grails**, players can obtain 30,000 Energy and skip the equivalent of 300 hours of continuous Azure Crystal production, alongside substantial resource consumption.

That's a very meaningful acceleration.

However, it's important to remember that the Energy Core system is ultimately a time gate. Given sufficient time, players can progress through it without purchasing Stellar Core Crystals.

For most players, Eternal Grails are better invested in permanent combat progression, especially Immortal fragments and artifact schematics.

But for players who prioritize speed over long-term spending efficiency, Stellar Core Crystals offer a straightforward way to turn premium currency into faster Legendary building upgrades.

**Our recommendation:** Don't purchase Stellar Core Crystals simply because they're available. Purchase them when the time saved allows you to reach a meaningful progression milestone sooner than you otherwise could.

Sometimes the best investment is permanent power. Other times, getting there two weeks earlier is exactly what you need.
