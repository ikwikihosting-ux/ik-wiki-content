## Infinity Kingdom Equipment Upgrade Cost Guide (Level 15–40)

Equipment plays a **crucial role** in the strength of your Immortals in *Infinity Kingdom*. While talent setups, artifact upgrades, and Tower of Knowledge skills all matter, it’s **your gear**—especially **epic +1 equipment**—that truly skyrockets your stats as you progress into the mid and late game.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/image-154-1024x557.png)

Recently, a player asked me:
**"How much does it actually cost to upgrade equipment?"**

I didn’t know off the top of my head—so I did the work and **melted down gear** to bring you this breakdown. Based on current testing (Season 1 capped at Level 40), here's **everything you need to know** about **Enchanted Stone costs**, where to get them, and **how steep the curve gets.**

---

## 📊 Equipment Upgrade Cost Table (Level 15 to 40)

| Level Range | Upgrade Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Level 15 | 700 |
| Level 15–20 | 2,500 |
| Level 20–25 | 15,900 |
| Level 25–30 | 41,900 |
| Level 30–35 | 119,100 |
| Level 35–40 | 294,200 |
| Total Cost | 475,390 Enchanted Stones |

> ⚠️ **Note:** This is the **total cost for upgrading a single piece of Epic +1 equipment** from Level 15 to 40.

---

## 🔥 Where to Get Enchanted Stones

As you’ve probably noticed, the **later levels get very expensive**—with **over 400K stones required past level 25** alone. Here’s how to stock up efficiently:

### 1. Defeating Gnomes

- Gnomes regularly drop **small amounts of Enchanted Stones**
- Great for **daily farming**, but won't keep up with late-game costs on its own

### 2. Spider Boss Battles

- Fought **twice a week**
- **Major source** of Enchanted Stones (especially at higher difficulties)
- Your **best method for stacking big amounts quickly**

💡 **Tip:** Make sure you're in an active alliance doing **high-difficulty Spider runs**—it’s by far the best bang for your time.

---

## 📈 Why Equipment Matters

Each upgrade boosts your Immortal’s **core attributes**:

- Physical Attack / Magic Attack
- Defense
- HP
- Speed
- Crit, Dodge, Block, etc.

By the time you're upgrading beyond Level 30+, the stat increases are **massive** and can be the difference between winning and losing PvP fights or boss challenges.

---

## 🛠️ What This Means for You

- Don’t ignore Enchanted Stone farming just because early upgrades are cheap—**you'll need hundreds of thousands** later
- **Prioritize your main Immortals’ gear upgrades first**—don’t spread resources thin
- If you're still in **Season 1 or 2**, it's best to **stockpile Enchanted Stones now** so you’re ready when Season caps increase
- **Level 35–40 alone costs 294.2K**—which is **over 60% of the total upgrade cost**
