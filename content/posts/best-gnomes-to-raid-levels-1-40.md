Equipment items are super important in Infinity Kingdom. They provide large attribute buffs for your immortals and are an invaluable part of your power. Upgrading them as much as possible means raiding gnome camps for Enchanted Stones. Mastering gnome raids is one of the **biggest advantages** you can give yourself in Infinity Kingdom. Whether you’re a casual player or pushing for top-tier dominance, knowing **when and where to strike** will dramatically boost your growth.

Every piece of equipment you craft and upgrade is a direct **power increase** for your immortals, making them stronger in battle. But without a steady supply of Enchanted Stones and high-tier gear, you’ll fall behind. This is where efficient gnome raiding comes in—**the smarter you farm, the stronger you become!**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-3-1024x471.png)

But it’s not just about gear. The **EXP** you earn from gnome raids is crucial for leveling up **three full marches** of immortals, ensuring you have the depth to compete in PvP, PvE, and major kingdom wars. If you’re not raiding the right camps at the right time, you’re simply slowing yourself down.

Want to **keep up with the strongest players?** Want to **make sure you’re not wasting AP on bad raids?** Then let’s break down exactly how to optimize your gnome farming strategy!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-1-1024x468.png)

Additionally, you’ll need three full marches of max-level immortals, which requires farming gnome camps for massive amounts of EXP. So, let’s break down which gnome camps to raid at different stages of your progression.

## Best Gnome Camps for Farming

When we talk about the best gnome camps, we mean those that offer the highest loot and experience relative to the losses sustained in battle. As you grow stronger, you can take on higher-level camps for better rewards.

### Gnome Loot Drops:

- **Trynt** drops: Helmet & Weapon
- **Bakan** drops: Chest & Weapon
- **Rogue** drops: Offhand & Weapon

## Optimal Gnome Camps to Raid

At endgame, the best gnome camps to farm are **Earth and Water** element gnomes, meaning most players should follow this path:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-1024x561.png)

### Inside Forbidden Zone:

- **Trynt** – Level 38
- **Bakan** – Level 39
- **Rogue** – Level 39

### Outside Forbidden Zone:

- **Trynt** – Level 36
- **Bakan** – Level 36
- **Rogue** – Level 37

**Special Case: Fire March Players** If your main march is **Fire**, Water gnomes are not optimal due to their elemental advantage. Instead, raid the following:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-2-1024x445.png)

### Inside Forbidden Zone:

- **Trynt** – Level 36 or 40
- **Bakan** – Level 39
- **Rogue** – Level 37

### Outside Forbidden Zone:

- **Trynt** – Level 36
- **Bakan** – Level 34
- **Rogue** – Level 37

There is a clear pattern in the best gnome camps for each level range. As you progress, the best camps increase by **10 levels** in each section:

- **Levels 1-9:** Trynt 6 or 8, Bakan 6 or 9, Rogue 7 or 9.

- **Levels 20-29:** Trynt 26 or 28, Bakan 26 or 29, Rogue 27 or 29.
- **Levels 30-39:** Trynt 36 or 38, Bakan 36 or 39, Rogue 37 or 39.
