## 💠How Does the Insignia Workshop Work?

There is no fixed monthly theme. Players are free to create an original insignia inspired by Infinity Kingdom, its Immortals, elements, dragons, events, factions or wider game world.

At the end of each month, the official team reviews the qualified submissions. Outstanding designs can receive additional rewards, while selected insignias may be adapted and released in-game.

If a design is officially adopted:

* The insignia may be added to Infinity Kingdom.
* Its creator receives priority access after release.
* The creator’s name will appear in the insignia’s in-game description.
* The developers determine its final attributes and acquisition method.

This makes the workshop more than an ordinary fan-art competition. Players can make a lasting contribution to the game and receive visible credit for their work.

## 💠How to Submit an Insignia

Entries must be submitted through the designated **Insignia Workshop event channel** in the official Infinity Kingdom Discord.

Every submission must use the following format:

**Insignia Name:**
**Design Description:**
**Attached Work Image:**

The image itself must meet these requirements:

* PNG format
* Transparent background
* Exactly 100 × 100 pixels
* Only the central insignia pattern
* No insignia frame or background
* Original player-created artwork
* Based on Infinity Kingdom elements and compatible with the game’s visual style

You may submit original designs throughout the month. However, do not submit the same design more than once, as duplicate submissions are considered invalid.

## 💠Monthly Participation Rewards

Every player who submits a qualified entry receives:

* 500 Gems
* 5 Superior Resource Packages
* 5 Superior Speedup Chests

These rewards make the workshop worth joining even if your design is not selected as one of the month’s outstanding entries.

## 💠Excellence Award

The official team will also select outstanding monthly submissions. Excellence Award winners receive:

* 1,500 Gems
* 9 Philosopher’s Stones
* 15 Superior Resource Packages
* 15 Superior Speedup Chests
* 30 Market Orders

The greatest reward, however, is the possibility of seeing your own insignia added to Infinity Kingdom.

## 💠Important Rules

All submitted work must be original. Plagiarism or reposting someone else’s artwork results in disqualification from the current workshop and the following three months.

Designs must not contain violent, pornographic or politically sensitive material. Entries that do not fit Infinity Kingdom’s style or comply with the event rules may be removed.

By submitting, players allow the official team to modify and use their work. Creators retain authorship rights and must be credited whenever the design is used officially.

Monthly rewards are distributed within 72 hours after the results are announced. The final appearance, attributes and availability of any adopted insignia remain at the discretion of the Infinity Kingdom team.

## 💠Why Players Should Participate

The Insignia Workshop is a genuinely interesting form of community-driven development. Instead of merely voting on content created by the developers, players can design something that may become a permanent part of Infinity Kingdom.

The workshop is now entering another monthly round, so there should be repeated opportunities to participate. Whether you are an experienced artist, enjoy casual graphic design or simply have a good concept for an insignia, this is a chance to contribute to the game, earn useful rewards and potentially have your name recorded inside Infinity Kingdom.

Head to the official Infinity Kingdom Discord, visit the Insignia Workshop event channel and take a look at what the community is creating. Even if you do not submit an entry this month, the designs themselves are well worth following.
