Hey there, fellow commanders! There's a brand-new event in *Infinity Kingdom*—the **Lucky Spin: Special Stardust Edition**! If you've been around for a while, you know we've seen many versions of the Lucky Spin over the years. But this one? It's **next level**. Unlike the standard Lucky Spin that offers Immortals, this special edition is **all about Stardust**—and I seriously hope it’s not a one-time thing because it is incredibly good!

## What’s Inside the Lucky Spin: Stardust Edition?

So, what makes this event so special? Simple: **It’s a Stardust bonanza.** The entire wheel is filled with Stardust rewards, meaning you’ll never walk away empty-handed. Here’s the breakdown of what you can win:

### Spin Prizes:

- **250 Stardust** – 0.4% chance
- **200 Stardust** – 0.5% chance

## How to Obtain Lucky Spin Tickets (Pearl of Finding)

To participate in this event, you'll need **Pearl of Finding** (Lucky Spin Tickets). These can be obtained through shop bundles, and here's the pricing breakdown:

- **$5** – 18 Pearls (**3 in stock per day**) → **$0.28 per Pearl**
- **$15** – 30 Pearls (**4 in stock per day**) → **$0.50 per Pearl**
- **$30** – 40 Pearls (**3 in stock per day**) → **$0.75 per Pearl**
- **$50** – 50 Pearls (**2 in stock per day**) → **$1.00 per Pearl**
- **$100** – 64 Pearls (**1 in stock per day**) → **$1.56 per Pearl**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-114-1024x557.png)

If you were to go all out, you could obtain up to **458 Pearls per day**—but honestly, **don’t do that!** The later bundles become increasingly inefficient as the cost per Pearl rises. The **$5 and $15 bundles** provide the best value, with the **$5 bundle offering an exceptional deal** at just **$0.28 per Pearl**.

### Best Buying Strategy

- **Buy the $5 and $15 bundles**—these provide **the best value per Pearl**.
- **Consider the $30 bundle only if you are desperate for Stardust**.
- **Avoid the $50 and $100 bundles**—the cost per Pearl skyrockets and is not worth it.

Even in the worst-case scenario, each Pearl gives at least **5 Stardust**, meaning you’re getting **90 Stardust for just $5**—a **great deal!** And who knows, you might hit the jackpot and get **400 Stardust in a single draw!**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-115-1024x569.png)

## Should You Go All-In on This Event?

This event isn’t about **min-maxing efficiency**—which is weird to say since we usually optimize everything! Instead, it boils down to **how much Stardust you need.**

- **If you're in dire need of Stardust**, go nuts! This event is an amazing way to stock up.
- **If you're only mildly interested**, it might be worth a few spins, but don’t go overboard.
- **Beyond 350 spins, the bonus rewards aren’t great**, so keep that in mind when planning your spending.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-116-1024x514.png)

## Why Is Stardust So Important?

Stardust is **crucial** for upgrading Immortal portraits. These upgrades provide **massive power boosts**, including:

- **5% Damage buffs** for Attack, Ranged, and Magic Immortals
- **5% Damage Resistance** for Tank and Support Immortals

If you want your Immortals to **reach their full potential**, you’ll need a ton of Stardust to level up their portraits!

## The Cost of Upgrading Immortal Portraits

Portrait levels increase your Immortal’s attributes and provide **game-changing bonuses** at levels 10 and 20. Here’s a quick rundown of how each Immortal type benefits:

- **Tank Immortals** – Gain **Physical Attack, Physical Defense, and Magical Defense**. At level 20, they get **5% Damage Resistance**.
- **Attack Immortals** – Focus on **Physical Attack and defenses**, with a **5% Accuracy Rate at level 10** and **5% Physical Damage at level 20**.
- **Ranged Immortals** – Similar to Attack Immortals but with **ranged precision bonuses**.
- **Magic Immortals** – Get **Magical Attack and defensive buffs**, with a **5% Crit Rate at level 10** and **5% Magical Damage at level 20**.
- **Support Immortals** – Gain **defensive and evasion stats**, with a **5% Dodge Rate at level 10** and **5% Damage Resistance at level 20**.

The **biggest power spike** comes at level 20, so focusing your Stardust investments there is key!

## Best Ways to Get Stardust (Outside of Lucky Spin)

If you’re looking for consistent ways to farm Stardust, here are your best options:

### Deep Sea Merchant Stardust Bundles (Best for Gem Spenders)

Each week, you can buy **10 bundles of Stardust**. However, **the gem cost increases with each purchase**:

1. **560 Gems**
2. **630 Gems**
3. **700 Gems**
4. **840 Gems**
5. **980 Gems**
6. **1120 Gems**
7. **1260 Gems**
8. **1400 Gems**
9. **1750 Gems**
10. **2100 Gems**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-121.png)

The first few bundles are **affordable and efficient**, but higher-tier purchases require careful consideration.

### Arena Shop (Daily Stardust Gains)

- **60 Stardust per day** for **15,000 Insignias**
- **Great if you’re active in PvP!**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-117.png)

### Throne of the Supreme Shop (Seasonal Stardust)

- **250 Stardust per season** for **25,000 Glory Coins**
- **A solid long-term investment if you’re competitive.**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-118.png)

### Marketplace (Gem or Soul Crystal Exchange)

- Buy **1 Stardust for 120 Gems or 200 Soul Crystals**
- **Flexible but expensive—use wisely!**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-119.png)

### Mysterious Pearl Shop (Daily Stardust Boost)

- **20 Stardust per day** for **20 Pearls**
- **A consistent source but requires Pearl farming.**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-120.png)

## Summary: Is the Lucky Spin Stardust Edition Worth It?

✅ **YES** if you desperately need Stardust for upgrades. ❌ **NO** if you have enough Stardust and would rather save resources.

**Final Thoughts:** This event is **one of the best ways** to farm Stardust, and if you're serious about maxing out your Immortals, you should **strongly consider investing**. However, if you’re only casually upgrading, you might be better off sticking to the regular farming methods.

**Happy spinning, and may the Stardust be ever in your favor!** ✨
