Patch 2.9.8 introduced the **Endless Tower**, a new PvE mode built around fighting your way through a series of increasingly difficult floors. The name makes it sound like an infinite survival mode, but the first version is more structured than that: each run lasts 20 days and contains 100 floors, with one Sentinel formation waiting on every floor.

The general idea is simple enough. You defeat the Sentinel, collect the reward and move to the next floor. The interesting part comes from the Floor Traits, which change the conditions from one battle to the next and are meant to stop players from clearing the entire tower with one unchanged march.

For newer players, the Endless Tower is mainly a free source of Gems, speedups, resources and rare Immortal fragments. For more established players, it is also a place to experiment with different Immortals and skill setups without losing a daily attempt when a battle goes wrong.

## How to Unlock the Endless Tower

Your Castle must be at least **Level 20**, and your server must have been open for at least **15 days**. Both requirements need to be met when the event begins. If your server reaches the required age or you reach Castle Level 20 while a run is already underway, you will have to wait for the next Endless Tower event rather than joining halfway through.

The difficulty is adjusted according to the server's season. This is important because it means a young server is not expected to fight the same Sentinel formations as an old Conquest server. Endless Tower is intended to be relevant throughout the game's seasonal progression, although we will need several runs before we can properly judge how well the scaling works.

## How Daily Progress Works

Players begin on Floor 1 and must defeat every Sentinel in order. There is no option to skip a difficult floor or jump ahead, and a floor cannot be challenged again after it has been cleared. Battles are automatic once they begin, so all of the actual decision-making takes place before pressing the challenge button.

You receive five attempts each day, but the wording is slightly misleading because an attempt is only used when you win. Losing against a Sentinel does **not** reduce your remaining attempts, which means you are free to change your march, move skills around and try again until you find a setup that works. Your real daily limit is therefore five successful floor clears rather than five battle entries.

Unused attempts disappear at **00:00 UTC** and cannot be carried into the following day. This makes daily participation more important than it may initially appear:

**20 event days × 5 cleared floors per day = 100 floors.**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-17-1024x372.png)

The numbers leave no spare days. Under the current rules, finishing all 100 floors requires five victories on every day of the event. Missing one day means losing five possible floors, while clearing only three or four floors before reset also leaves progress behind that cannot be recovered later. Even if you are not interested in the leaderboard, it is worth treating all five daily clears as part of your normal routine.

## Floor Traits and Building Your Team

Every floor contains four enemy Immortals and a Dragon. Before starting the battle, you can inspect the Sentinel formation in detail, including its Immortal levels, stars, equipped skills, skill levels and specializations, along with the Dragon's level and abilities. This gives you considerably more information than a simple Combat Power number, and it is worth looking through it whenever your usual march begins to struggle.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-19-1024x470.png)

Each floor also has a **Floor Trait** that strengthens the Sentinels or changes the rules of the fight. Examples include immunity to Physical Damage and periodic HP recovery. While some are almost pointless, many are not to be ignored! A physical march cannot simply overpower an enemy that is immune to Physical Damage (level 4), while a formation with regular healing may require better burst damage, healing reduction or Wounds to prevent the fight from dragging on.

This is where the Endless Tower has the potential to become more than another daily reward screen. A player with one very strong march may clear the early floors quickly, but later traits can reward a broader account with both physical and magical damage options. Even if your secondary Immortals are not as developed as your main team, using the correct damage type or countering the floor mechanic can be more effective than repeatedly sending the formation with the highest listed power.

For beginners, I would approach a difficult floor in the following order:

1. Read the Floor Trait carefully and identify what it is changing.
2. Inspect the enemy Immortals, skills and Dragon instead of relying only on the displayed power.
3. Decide whether the floor favors physical damage, magical damage, burst, sustain or healing reduction.
4. Move suitable skills between your existing Immortals before spending resources on permanent upgrades.
5. Run another attempt and check the battle report to see whether the change actually helped.

Because failed challenges are free, there is no reason to keep repeating the same losing setup. The tower is one of the safer places to test how different skills and formations perform against a known opponent.

## Clear Rewards and Stage Rewards

Endless Tower has two separate reward tracks. The first is the **Clear Reward**, which is granted automatically whenever you defeat a Sentinel. These floor rewards include useful everyday progression items such as Superior Resource Optional Chests and Building, Training and Healing Speedups. The exact reward changes as you climb, but every victory gives you something even when you have not reached the next major milestone.

The second track is made up of **Stage Rewards**, which unlock after every five floors. These rewards must normally be claimed from the event screen and include **200 Gems** together with either five random Holy/Shadow Immortal fragments or five random Chaos Immortal fragments. If you leave a Stage Reward unclaimed when the event closes, it should be delivered through in-game mail rather than disappearing.

The word **random** matters here. These are not selection chests that let you target one specific Immortal, so the fragments will not necessarily advance the exact march you are building. Even so, Chaos fragments remain difficult to obtain without spending, and a recurring free source of them has obvious long-term value. The 4,000 Gems are also substantial for an activity that does not require purchases or consume attempts when you fail.

## How the Leaderboard Works

Endless Tower includes a seasonal leaderboard showing the top 100 players. Ranking is determined first by the highest floor cleared. If several players have reached the same floor, the faster clear time is used to separate them, and the leaderboard updates approximately every five minutes.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-21-1024x497.png)

The daily floor limit means active players will often be tied at the same point during the early part of the event. Keeping up with all five clears ensures that you remain on the highest floor currently available, while optimizing battle time becomes relevant for players chasing the top positions. For everyone else, the valuable part of the event remains the floor and milestone rewards; there is no need to rebuild your account around shaving a few seconds from a Sentinel fight unless the ranking itself matters to you.

## Is Endless Tower Worth Doing?

Yes. Endless Tower is free, failed challenges do not consume attempts, and the reward pool includes materials that most players need alongside Gems and rare Immortal fragments. The main danger is not wasting resources or losing troops; it is simply falling behind because unused daily clears cannot be saved.

The longer-term question is whether the later Floor Traits create genuinely interesting mechanical puzzles or whether progress eventually comes down to account power. The foundation is promising because players can inspect the opposing formation and retry without punishment, giving them room to learn from failed battles and adapt their teams. If future versions continue adding varied traits and sensible seasonal scaling, Endless Tower could become one of Infinity Kingdom's more useful permanent PvE activities rather than another event cleared on autopilot.

For the first run, the best advice is straightforward: unlock the event, complete five floors every day and read the trait before assuming that your strongest normal march is automatically the right answer.
