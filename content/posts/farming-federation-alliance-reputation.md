Federation Reputation is one of the numbers that ultimately determines the outcome of Legion of Frostborne, commonly referred to as KvK. Most federations build their score by capturing and holding cities, particularly the valuable cities found deeper within the battlefield. However, there is another repeatable source of reputation that many alliances overlook: attacking the walls of uncaptured gates without finishing them.

Gate farming does not replace territorial control, but it can add millions of reputation over the course of a KvK. In a close bracket, that additional score may genuinely affect the final result. Even when your federation is not competing for victory, the method can help your alliance reach the **12,000,000 Alliance Reputation milestone** and secure **10,000 Cold Silver for each eligible participating member**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-47-1024x535.png)

## How Reputation From Gate Sieges Works

Technically, attacking a gate does not award Federation Reputation directly. The reputation passes through several layers of the KvK scoring system:

### Gate wall damage → Personal Legion’s Marks → Alliance Reputation → Federation Reputation

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-49-1024x617.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-50-1024x586.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-51-1024x499.png)

Dismantling city and checkpoint wall durability awards Personal Legion’s Marks to the players involved. Those marks are automatically converted into Alliance Reputation, which then contributes to the federation’s combined score.

This is important because gate farming advances several objectives at once. Participating players increase their Personal Legion’s Marks, their alliance moves closer to its progression rewards and the federation gains additional reputation in the overall ranking.

## How to Farm an Uncaptured Gate

The method relies on damaging a gate without completely destroying its walls. If the gate remains uncaptured, its durability can recover and the objective can be attacked again after its Protection Barrier expires.

The basic process is:

1. Select an eligible uncaptured gate.
2. Begin an attack and send siege marches against its walls.
3. Stop all attacks before the wall durability reaches close to zero.
4. Allow the walls to recover during the Protection Barrier.
5. Attack the gate again once the approximately 30-minute barrier expires.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-52-1024x617.png)

The most important part is stopping early enough. If the wall reaches zero durability and the gate is captured, you lose it as a repeatable farming target.

In our example, the alliance used a clear instruction to **stop at 100 million durability**. This left enough room to account for marches already travelling toward the gate, simultaneous attacks and delayed recalls.

Waiting until only a few million durability remains is unnecessarily risky. When dozens of marches are involved, several attacks can land at nearly the same time and accidentally finish the gate. Alliance leadership should choose a stopping point before the siege begins and communicate it clearly throughout the attack.

## How Much Reputation Does One Gate Provide?

During our test against a **level 17 Nammu Mine gate**, the federation ranking increased from **88,746,352 to 88,922,513**. That represents an increase of **176,161 Federation Reputation**, slightly below 200,000.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-53.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-54-1024x586.png)

This should be treated as an observed result rather than a fixed reward for every gate. The final amount may depend on the gate’s wall durability, how much damage is inflicted and whether other reputation-generating activity is taking place at the same time.

Nevertheless, the example shows how quickly repeated gate sieges can add up. Even if one cycle produces approximately 175,000 reputation, seven successful cycles provide more than 1.2 million. Spread that activity across several gates and the federation can generate a meaningful amount of additional reputation every day.

## Farming Several Gates at Once

The strongest version of this strategy is not placing the entire federation on one gate. Instead, alliances can spread their available players across several eligible gates and run multiple farming cycles simultaneously.

Each alliance or assigned group can manage its own gate, with someone responsible for announcing when the Protection Barrier expires and when players must stop attacking. A basic organization might include:

- One gate assigned to each participating alliance or player group.
- A shared timer for the 30-minute Protection Barrier.
- A predetermined durability limit for stopping the siege.
- Clear retreat instructions before the gate approaches that limit.
- Siege-focused marches to deal wall damage efficiently.

This allows the federation to continue generating reputation even during quieter periods of KvK. It also gives smaller alliances a useful task when they cannot meaningfully participate in battles for the most important cities.

## Why the 12 Million Alliance Milestone Matters

Gate farming is especially valuable for alliances that are not winning the overall KvK. Federation victory may already be out of reach, but Alliance Reputation rewards can still provide a substantial amount of Cold Silver.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image-55-1024x535.png)

These are progression rewards based on the highest Alliance Reputation reached during the season. Alliance members must have entered the Legion of Frostborne battlefield during the current season to qualify for distribution.

Reaching **12,000,000 Alliance Reputation** therefore gives each eligible member 10,000 Cold Silver. For an alliance otherwise struggling to earn meaningful end-of-season rewards, organized gate farming can be the difference between missing that milestone and receiving the full payout.

## Gate Farming Does Not Replace City Control

Repeated gate sieges are useful, but they should remain a secondary strategy. Capturing cities grants first-occupation points, while holding them produces continuous occupation points for the controlling alliance. High-level cities can build a major reputation advantage throughout the season and remain central to winning Legion of Frostborne.

Do not preserve a gate for farming if capturing it is necessary for expansion, blocking an opponent or securing access to important cities. The method should be used on gates that your federation can safely leave uncaptured without compromising its position.

The best approach is to combine both sources. Your main forces should fight for strategically valuable territory, while spare players and marches farm suitable gates whenever the battlefield allows it.

## Concluding remarks

Gate farming is a simple but powerful way to generate additional Federation Reputation. Attack the walls of an eligible uncaptured gate, stop before its durability reaches zero and repeat the siege once the Protection Barrier expires. Used across several gates, this can help decide a close KvK or push an alliance toward the important **12 million reputation milestone and its 10,000 Cold Silver reward**.

However, the value does not stop with the federation. Damaging gate walls first awards **Personal Legion’s Marks**, which are then converted into Alliance Reputation and added to the federation total. This means gate farming is also one of the strongest strategies for progressing your personal KvK rewards.
