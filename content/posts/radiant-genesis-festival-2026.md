## Introduction - Sneak Peek at the coming Seasonal Event

**Radiant Genesis** is a limited-time seasonal event in **Infinity Kingdom**, launching on **January 16** and running for **10 days**. The event combines daily participation rewards, optional paid packs, combat challenges, a seasonal Battle Pass, and multiple currencies that feed into a shared shop ecosystem.

Radiant Genesis follows the established seasonal framework but introduces notable adjustments to reward distribution, token availability, and grail acquisition. This guide provides a full breakdown of all event components, rewards, and value considerations. Take a look at the video here for a sneak peek at the coming event.

[Embedded media](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Radiant-Genesis.mkv)

While the overall event offers strong value through daily sign-ins, the Koi Gift Pack, and the Battle Pass, Dazzling Wish remains the most mathematically opaque system for players deciding how far to invest. This article breaks down the new grail rates, compares them directly with the previous Snow Festival event, and evaluates what the changes mean in real gameplay terms.

---

## Event Duration and Core Structure

- **Start Date:** January 16
- **Duration:** 10 days
- **Primary Event Currencies:**  - Token of Light
- Splendor Insignia
- Eternal Grails

These currencies are earned through participation across multiple event modes and are spent in the **Challenge Shop** and **Grail Shop**.

---

## Koi Gift Pack & Daily Sign-In

### Daily Sign-In & Lucky Coin Lottery

Throughout the event, players gain access to:

- Daily sign-in rewards
- Lucky Coin Lottery
- Free **Token of Light** each day

These rewards require minimal effort and form the baseline participation value of the event. Even players who skip combat content should collect these daily items.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/image-91-1024x551.png)

### Koi Gift Pack (Optional Paid Pack)

The **Koi Gift Pack** is one of the strongest low-cost packs currently available.

**Price:** $4.99
**Contents:**

- 5 Tokens of Light
- 520 Gems
- 500 VIP Points
- Chance to obtain **4,250 Eternal Grails** every two days

If purchased five times without winning the grail draw, the guaranteed total is **850 Grails**, which converts to **80 Chaos Immortal shards** via the Grail Shop (price tag 750 grails each).

From a value-per-dollar standpoint, no other $25 investment provides Chaos shards as efficiently.

## Radiant Genesis Battle Pass

Radiant Genesis introduces a seasonal Battle Pass with **free** and **premium** tracks.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/image-92-1024x546.png)

### Free Track

- Levels 1–30
- **1 Token of Light per level**
- Total: **30 Tokens**

### Premium Track

- Expected price: ~$50
- Levels 1–29: **4 Tokens of Light per level**
- Total: **116 Tokens**
- Level 30 reward: **Sun Chariot Castle Skin** (60 frags)

Notably, this version removes additional castle skin fragments typically included in premium passes and replaces them with extra Tokens of Light, increasing overall progression value.

### Battle Pass Progression

- 7 daily missions
- 100 EXP per mission
- Approximately 70 missions available across the event
- Perfect attendance is **not required** to reach level 30

## Glorious Challenge

Glorious Challenge returns as a PvE combat mode against Gnome forces, functioning similarly to Well of Time with separate difficulty tiers.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/image-94-1024x544.png)

### Normal Mode

- Available from Day 1
- 16 stages
- Low difficulty
- Easily cleared by most players

### Hard Mode

- Unlocks on Day 3
- Comparable to Elite campaign difficulty
- Increased emphasis on skill setup and formation planning
- More punishing enemy mechanics

### Attempts and Costs

- 5 free attempts per day
- Up to 100 additional attempts purchasable for **8,000 Gems**

### Rewards

- First clear per stage: **50 Gems** **+ Splendor Insignia Challenge Shop Currency**
- Raid rewards: ******Splendor Insignia****** (amount depends on level, make sure to raid highest possible level, preferably in Hard Mode only)

**Efficiency Note:**
Purchased attempts should be saved exclusively for **Hard Mode**, wherecurrency yield is significantly higher. Free attempts alone are sufficient to obtain all practical rewards; purchased attempts mainly accelerate cosmetic acquisition.

### Speed Rankings

Speed Rankings track completion time and reward leaderboard placement. This system is designed for competitive players and provides prestige rather than progression-critical rewards.

## Dazzling Wish – Radiant Genesis Reward Structure

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/image-96.png)

During Radiant Genesis, Dazzling Wish awards only **Gold** and **Eternal Grails**, with the following probabilities:

- **0.45%** chance → 200 Grails
- **1.5%** chance → 50 Grails
- **3.5%** chance → 25 Grails
- **94.55%** chance → Gold rewards

Large grail jackpots have been removed entirely, compressing rewards into smaller but more frequent grail hits.

## Expected Grail Value per Token (Radiant Genesis)

To determine the real value of one Dazzling Wish token, we calculate the expected grails per pull:

- 200 × 0.0045 = **0.90**
- 50 × 0.015 = **0.75**
- 25 × 0.035 = **0.875**

### Average grails per token: ~2.53

This establishes a predictable, low-variance reward curve. Players can reliably estimate long-term returns without depending on extreme luck.

## Comparison: Snow Festival Dazzling Wish

For context, the Snow Festival event used the following grail probabilities:

- **0.1%** → 1000 Grails
- **0.15%** → 500 Grails
- **0.8%** → 100 Grails
- **1.5%** → 50 Grails
- **10%** → 5 Grails

### Expected Value Calculation

- 1000 × 0.001 = **1.00**
- 500 × 0.0015 = **0.75**
- 100 × 0.008 = **0.80**
- 50 × 0.015 = **0.75**
- 5 × 0.10 = **0.50**

### Average grails per token: ~3.80

From a purely statistical standpoint, Snow Festival offered significantly higher grail value per token.

Although Snow Festival had superior average returns, those returns were heavily dependent on rare outcomes. Many players experienced extended streaks of low-value pulls before seeing any meaningful grail rewards - if ever. **The new consistency is a very good chance for most players** however for whales who are able to play the odds with high risk high reward content, there will be a reduction in the Grails earned this time.

## Conclusion

Radiant Genesis maintains the familiar seasonal structure while improving reliability and planning efficiency:

- Strong baseline value through daily participation
- One of the best low-cost paid packs currently available
- A Battle Pass that prioritizes Tokens of Light over cosmetics
- Reduced reward volatility in Dazzling Wish
- Excellent long-term Chaos shard conversion through the Grail Shop

Rather than encouraging jackpot chasing, this event rewards consistent participation and disciplined resource management, making it especially suitable for players focused on steady account progression.
