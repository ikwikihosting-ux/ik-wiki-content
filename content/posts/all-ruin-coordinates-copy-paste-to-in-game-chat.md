### Exploring the Mystical Ruins in Infinity Kingdom

In the captivating realm of *Infinity Kingdom*, the **Ruins** are an essential feature that every player should prioritize. These ancient remnants scattered across Norheim hold valuable **rewards**, including gems, philosopher’s stones, and progress milestones that help accelerate your kingdom’s development.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/image-41-1024x472.png)

## Why Ruins Matter

The Ruins offer not only **immediate loot** but also contribute to a **progress bar** that unlocks better Ruins and more rewards. The **faster you discover all Ruins, the sooner you gain access to Super-Ruins**, which spawn consistently and yield **superior loot**, including **large amounts of gems** and **philosopher’s stones**.

Finding Ruins in *Infinity Kingdom* is simple. You can enter the **exact coordinates** into the **X/Y fields** at the top of the screen while in the world view. This will lead you directly to the Ruin’s location, making it easy to explore systematically.
