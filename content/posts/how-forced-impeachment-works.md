## Introduction

*A practical guide to understanding activity status, voting requirements, and why many impeachment attempts fail.*

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-34-1024x596.png)

Forced impeachment is one of the least understood alliance systems in *Infinity Kingdom*. The game does provide instructions in the alliance interface, but the wording leaves several important mechanics unclear—particularly how the game defines **“active members”** and which players actually count toward the vote.

Most failed impeachment attempts happen because alliances calculate the numbers incorrectly. Players often assume the vote is based on **total alliance size**, when the game actually calculates participation using **activity status categories**.

This guide explains the system clearly, focusing on the most confusing points:

- What counts as an **active member**
- Which members **can vote**
- How the **80% participation rule** works
- How **winning votes are calculated**

The explanations are based on in-game text and observed behavior tested by alliances.

---

## Who Can Start a Forced Impeachment

Only alliance members with the following ranks can initiate a forced impeachment:

- **R4 (Officer)**
- **R5 (Alliance Leader)**

Without an R4 or R5 initiating the process, the impeachment vote cannot begin.

---

## Activity Status — The Most Important Mechanic

Every player in an alliance is automatically assigned an **activity status** based on their recent login behavior.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-35-1024x573.png)

The game uses four activity categories:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image-36-1024x245.png)

### Key Rule

**Only Silent members are excluded from the impeachment system.** All other statuses count as **active members**.

This means the impeachment system calculates participation using: **Hyper + Active + Present**

Silent accounts are completely ignored.

---

## How the 80% Voting Requirement Works

For impeachment to succeed, **at least 80% of active members must vote**.

Active members are defined as:

```
Hyper + Active + Present
```

Silent members do not count toward the total.

### Example

Alliance size: 100 members

Status distribution:

- Hyper: 20
- Active: 30
- Present: 25
- Silent: 25

Active member pool:

```
20 + 30 + 25 = 75 active members
```

Required voters:

```
75 × 0.80 = 60 votes required
```

If fewer than **60 players vote**, the impeachment automatically fails.

---

## Why “Present” Players Cause Confusion

Many community explanations incorrectly claim that **only Hyper and Active players count**. Testing and in-game behavior show this is incorrect.

**Present members do count as active members and can vote.**

This has two important consequences:

1. Present players **increase the total active member count**
2. They **raise the 80% voting requirement**

For example: If many players are “Present,” the alliance must gather **more votes** to reach the 80% threshold.

---

## Winning the Vote — The 50% Rule

After the participation requirement is satisfied, the game checks the **majority condition**.

The candidate must receive: **More than 50% of the votes cast.**

Example: Votes cast: 60

Winning threshold: More than 30 votes

A candidate must receive **31 votes or more** to become the new leader.

---

## What Happens If Conditions Are Not Met

The system checks three failure conditions when the voting period ends.

Impeachment fails if:

### 1. Less Than 80% Participation

If fewer than **80% of active members vote**, the impeachment is invalidated.

The current leader remains in position.

---

### 2. No Candidate Reaches 50%

If both candidates receive **less than 50% of the votes**, the leader stays unchanged.

---

### 3. Tie Vote

If both candidates receive the **same number of votes**, the current leader remains.

---

## When the Vote Ends

The voting process ends when **one of two things happens**:

1. **All eligible members vote**
2. **The voting timer expires**

If everyone votes early, the result is calculated immediately.

---

## Player Strategy and Practical Observations

From a practical alliance management perspective, the impeachment system is less about raw alliance size and more about **controlling the active member pool**.

Experienced alliances often pay attention to:

- The number of **Silent members**
- The number of **Present members**
- Coordinating voting to reach the **80% threshold**

Because Silent members are excluded from calculations, alliances sometimes remove or relocate inactive players before starting impeachment.

Similarly, alliances that control many **active accounts** can reliably meet the participation requirement and control the vote outcome.

---

## The Core Formula

When calculating whether impeachment will succeed, alliances only need two numbers.

### Active Member Pool

```
Hyper + Active + Present
```

### Required Votes

```
Active Members × 0.80
```

### Winning Votes

```
More than 50% of votes cast
```

---

## Conclusion

Forced impeachment in *Infinity Kingdom* follows a straightforward system once the activity rules are understood.

The most important detail is how the game defines **active members**.

Active members include:

- **Hyper**
- **Active**
- **Present**

Only **Silent members are excluded**.

From this active pool:

- **80% must participate in the vote**
- The winner must receive **more than 50% of the votes**

Understanding these mechanics allows alliances to correctly calculate their voting requirements and avoid failed impeachment attempts caused by incorrect assumptions about alliance size or activity status.
