## 🏰 Corridor of Ghostflame Castle Skin Guide

A brand new combat castle skin is arriving with the upcoming **Hunting Festival 2025** event, and it’s shaping up to be one of the best-looking and most useful skins to date. Let's take a closer look at the **Corridor of Ghostflame**, how to obtain it, and why it’s an important addition to your collection.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-62-1024x451.png)

---

## 🌟 Overview: Corridor of Ghostflame

The **Corridor of Ghostflame** is the newest limited-time **castle skin** in *Infinity Kingdom*, launching alongside the **Hunting Festival 2025**, which begins today **May 14th** **2025**and runs for 10 days. Visually stunning and bathed in eerie blue-green flame aesthetics, this skin is more than just cosmetics—it offers real combat value.

### Corridor of Ghostflame Stats:

- **Troop HP +2%**
- **Honor Gain +5%**

**IMPORTANT**: These are the **same**powerful buffs found on **City of Glory**, making this one of the few castle skins in the game that provides a **direct combat stat boost** while also supporting your **honor farming efforts**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-60-1024x441.png)

---

## 💰 How to Obtain It

This castle skin is **exclusive** to the **premium tier** of the **Hunting Festival Battle Pass**. To unlock it, you must:

1. Purchase the **$49.99 Premium Battle Pass**.
2. Complete the full 30 levels of the pass by earning **Battle Pass EXP** through daily tasks.
3. Reach the final reward tier to claim the Corridor of Ghostflame.

You’ll also receive additional rewards such as gems, Resonance Crystals, and a full random Light/Shadow immortal, adding further value to your purchase.

> ⚠️ Important: The pass requires **4,800 EXP** to complete, and you can earn **700 EXP per day** from tasks, meaning you have room to skip a couple of days or tasks and still finish it before the event ends.

---

## ⚔️ Why It Matters

Like **City of Glory** and **Goddess of Victory**, the **Corridor of Ghostflame** provides **2% bonus HP to all troops**, which is a significant combat stat, especially in **PvP** and **season-based warfare**. On top of that, it boosts **honor gain by 5%**, giving you an edge in **climbing the Nobility ladder** and unlocking powerful **title buffs** faster.

When compared to other top-tier castle skins:

| Skin | Combat Buff | Utility Buff | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| City of Glory | 2% Troop HP | +5% Honor Gain | Best for PvP/Honor Farming |
| Goddess of Victory | 2% Troop HP | -3% Death Rate | Best for Troop Preservation & IB |
| Mt. Paradise | 2% Troop HP | +5% Marching Speed | Best for Sieging/Map Control |
| Corridor of Ghostflame | 2% Troop HP | +5% Honor Gain | Best for PvP/Honor Farming |

So, while the stats are not unique, **having more than one skin with strong bonuses** lets you **alternate based on your playstyle** and the **needs of your current season**. If you missed City of Glory or want a second skin with identical buffs and a different visual flavor, this is your opportunity.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-61-1024x429.png)

Even if you did obtain the Glory Castle Skin, in my humble opinion Ghostflame just simply looks incredible!

## 📝 Final Thoughts

The **Corridor of Ghostflame** is not just another skin—it’s a **combat-relevant and honor-efficient** asset that fits perfectly into any PvP-focused player’s toolkit. Combined with the other strong rewards in the Hunting Festival Battle Pass, this skin makes the $50 investment well worth it for most spenders.

> **Recommendation:** If you’re serious about PvP and Honor grinding—or if you simply want one of the game’s best-looking skins—this is one castle you won’t want to miss.
