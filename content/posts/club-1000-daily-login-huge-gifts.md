So you’ve been logging into Infinity Kingdom for 1000 days straight — that’s almost three years. If you’ve been this loyal, the devs have a special thank-you waiting for you… but the catch is, it’s not something you’ll ever see inside the game itself.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-141.png)

This reward isn’t part of the standard in-game login chain. It’s part of the **IKVIP program**, a special perk for verified spenders who’ve been around since the early days. Once you hit your **1000-day login milestone**, you can claim a one-time veteran reward — and depending on what you choose, it could be one of the rarest power boosts in the game.

---

### How It Works

You can check your total logins by going to:

> **Profile → Statistics → Logins** // **or via the Daily Login screen (VIP number on name plate)**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-141.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-142-1024x591.png)

That’ll show your total number of logins and your current chain. When you reach 1000, head to the **IKVIP Discord** and message the official **IKVIP account** (make sure you’re verified as VIP first — check their verification channel or see the separate guide on that). They’ll confirm your account and give you your choice of reward.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-143-1024x228.png)

### Dont panic if you miss one day..

While it used to be believed that you needed *1000 consecutive* logins, reports from verified players show that **having 1000 total logins** is enough. So if you missed a few days here and there, don’t panic — players with solid chains and 1000+ total logins have still received the reward.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-144-634x1024.png)

Make sure you send the IKVIP a screenshot of your logins statistics as well as your Lord ID.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-145-1024x100.png)

## How do you become "verified VIP?"

verified as VIP means you have spent more than 2000 USD since your account was created. Contacting IKVIP can get you a full account of your spending on your account if you are curious to know. Once you hit 2000 you are eligible for the secret veteran reward at 1000 day login chain!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-148-1024x747.png)

### What You Get

You’ll be offered **one of the following rewards:**

- 60x **Ability Stones** for *any* **Epic-quality Tower of Knowledge skill** (except Legendary skills)
- 60x **Immortal Fragments** for *any* **non-Chaos Immortal**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/image-146-1024x472.png)

Basically, you can unlock or upgrade *one full Epic skill* of your choice — or grab 60 fragments for an Epic or lower Immortal.

But let’s be real here — if you’re VIP verified, you already have access to a ton of Immortal fragments through regular events, shop bundles, and summons. So don’t waste this reward on Immortals. The real gold here is the **rare skill stones**.

**[Read more about all the skills here.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/tower-of-knowledge-passives/ability-stones-availability-season-1-2-and-conquest/complete-guide-on-all-skill-stones-available-in-conquest-and-how-to-get-them/)**

### Best Use: Rare Epic Skills

The most valuable pick from this reward is absolutely one of the rare Epic skills found only in the **“Ability Stone Selection Chest I”**. These are the ones that don’t drop anywhere else except through the extremely RNG-heavy **Wisdom Well** during Conquest season.

Those hard-to-get skills are:

- **Unforeseen Attack**
- **Abundant Spirit**
- **Assembly Charge**
- **Psionic Tide**
- **Blood Frenzy**
- **Chaos Storm**
- **Time to Shine**
- **Corrosive Power**

Most players don’t realize how brutal the drop rate is for these. You’re looking at roughly **a 2% drop rate per wish** for *a single stone* of these rarities. And since you need 60 stones to max one, the math looks like this:

**[More details here.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/events/blessings-of-triss/wisdom-well-pricing-of-skills-and-value-guide-season-1-2-and-conquest/)**

> **60 stones / (2% drop rate for 1)** = **~3000 wishes on average**

That’s roughly double the average cost of maxing a Legendary skill (which takes about **1663 wishes**). So yeah — these are *insanely* rare. Most endgame players will have several maxed Legendary skills *before* even unlocking one of these.

That’s why the 1000-day veteran reward is so special: **you get one of these completely for free**, no RNG, no wasted Wisdom Crystals.

### What to Choose

If you’re not sure what to grab, here’s my honest take after testing and seeing tons of builds over the years:

- **Corrosive Power** is probably the single best choice for most players. It performs insanely well across many top-tier setups, especially in sustained fights and against high-resistance teams. It’s one of those “always useful” skills.
- **Assembly Charge**, **Chaos Storm**, **Time to Shine**, **Abundant Spirit**, and **Psionic Tide** are all top-tier too, depending on what you run. If you’re heavily invested in certain comps or specific Immortal synergies, one of these might edge out Corrosive Power for you.

That said — this is a *once-in-a-lifetime* pick. Don’t rush it. Hop on the **official IK Discord** or one of the major community servers and ask around. Show your current march setups and let experienced players weigh in. Every veteran has slightly different advice based on synergy, and you really don’t get a do-over on this one.
