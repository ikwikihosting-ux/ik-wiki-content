The introduces several long-term progression systems at once, while also reviving older content that many veteran players have not seen in years. Some of these additions look promising immediately, while others still raise important questions that the official notes do not fully answer yet.

The official patch notes confirm three major additions: the Immortal Affinity system, the new Insignia system, and the return of Battle of Asteria. On top of that, the preparation phase for Conquest and Legendary Seasons has finally been shortened, which is likely the most impactful quality-of-life change for active competitive alliances.

You can read the full official notes on the [Infinity Kingdom Official Forum](https://infinitykingdom.gtarcade.com/en/news/7024309.html).

---

## Immortal Affinity – A New Long-Term Progression System

The new Immortal Affinity feature allows players to send gifts to specific immortals in order to increase affinity levels. According to the official notes, reaching certain milestones unlocks rewards and additional story content, while affinity level 30 grants rare insignia rewards.

At the moment, the largest uncertainty is whether this system is purely narrative and cosmetic, or whether it has direct gameplay implications beyond the confirmed insignia rewards.

For now, the official answer from the development team on press officer requests for more information is: "Please wait for us to organize the content and I will get back to you all". Once they do, I will make sure to keep you all updated!

From experience with previous Infinity Kingdom systems, there are a few possibilities here:

- Pure lore and story progression
- Cosmetic profile rewards (which would be quite cool)
- Permanent account combat bonuses
- Immortal-specific stat increases
- Unlockable new combat features/effects tied to affinity levels

The wording in the patch notes strongly suggests there is more attached to this than simple flavor text. The mention of “rare insignia rewards” seems to indicate to me that there will either be some tie into the existing Insignia systems and shops, perhaps adding insignia rewards upon completion of Immortal Affinity levels which can be used in the shops for new immortal fragments or artifacts? I hope so! Or an entirely new Insignia type specifically for the new rewards tied to affinity levels (most probably in my humble opinion).

What remains unclear is whether:

- affinity progression is time-gated,
- gifts are farmable or premium-limited,
- rewards reset seasonally,
- or certain immortals provide better progression value than others.

## The Insignia System – Cosmetic Collection or Another Power Layer?

The Insignia System is probably the feature generating the most discussion right now.

Officially, insignias are obtained through:

- Immortal Affinity
- Appearance Collection
- Festival Events
- Gameplay events

The important line from the patch notes is this:

> “Activating insignias grants additional attribute bonuses. Collecting the specified number of each insignia type provides total attribute bonuses that affect all Immortals.”

That wording matters. It strongly suggests stat scaling rather than temporary event buffs, most likely additional attribute bonuses tied to specific immortals upon completing affinity levels with that immortal? I would think so, let's see..

If that interpretation is correct, insignias could function similarly to the boost levels where we completed lore and story content to earn currency/materials to activate new boost levels for buffs. Sounds like we are getting a new layer of something kind of similar but with a lot more to it than pure combat buffs as they also mention various cosmetic features.

## Battle of Asteria Returns

Older players will immediately recognize Battle of Asteria.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-42-1024x403.png)

This event existed during the earlier years of Infinity Kingdom before eventually disappearing from rotation. At the time, it had interesting concepts but eventually became outdated because the rewards no longer matched the pace of account progression.

The new version appears significantly expanded.

According to the patch notes:

- the event will now be server-wide,
- difficulty scales with season progression,
- both solo and alliance participation exist,
- and there are multiple challenge types including rally content.

The addition of:

- Single Squad Challenges,
- Multi-Squad Challenges,
- and Rally Challenges

suggests the developers are trying to modernize the event structure around current account diversity and alliance coordination.

The most important factor will ultimately be rewards.

The official notes already confirm:

- event currency,
- resource chests,
- and Immortal Affinity items.

If reward scaling is competitive with current seasonal progression, Battle of Asteria could become meaningful weekly or periodic content again. If rewards remain tuned like the older version, players may treat it as optional filler content instead.

From a veteran perspective, bringing back older content is generally positive when it is modernized properly. Infinity Kingdom has accumulated many systems over the years, and rotating older events back into relevance is healthier than endlessly stacking entirely new modes on top of existing ones.

## Season Preparation Time Reduction – Quietly the Most Important Change

For many active alliances, this may actually be the best part of the patch.

The preparation phase changes are straightforward:

- Off-season reduced from 14 days to 7 days
- Announcement phase reduced from 5 days to 2 days
- Cold Night duration remains unchanged

This significantly improves pacing for competitive kingdoms and active alliance leadership.

Long preparation downtime has been a recurring frustration for high-activity servers because momentum tends to collapse during extended waiting periods. Shortening dead time keeps:

- recruitment active,
- alliance organization tighter,
- and player engagement more stable between seasons.

For R5 and R6 players especially, this reduces a lot of unnecessary idle management.

It is also one of the few recent changes that benefits nearly every type of player equally, regardless of spending level.

## Smaller Optimizations That Still Matter

Two smaller quality-of-life updates are also included:

### Alliance Statistics Mail

The addition of 24-hour and 48-hour filters is a simple but genuinely useful improvement for alliance management, especially during:

- IB registration,
- migration prep,
- inactivity tracking,
- and event participation reviews.

### Forge Interface Improvements

The Forge coin conversion interface now displays material types more clearly. Not a game-changing update, but interface cleanup for older systems has been overdue for a while.

---

## Conclusion Summary

v2.9.5 introduces several systems that could significantly affect long-term account progression, especially through the new Insignia mechanics and Affinity rewards. However, the current patch notes still leave many important questions unanswered regarding scaling, accessibility, and permanent stat impact.

The return of Battle of Asteria is a welcome surprise for older players, particularly if rewards are modernized properly, while the reduced preparation time for Conquest and Legendary Seasons is likely the most universally appreciated change in the patch.

We will publish updated guides and deeper system breakdowns once the development team responds to the press officer requests for additional clarification, or once the patch is fully live and tested in-game.
