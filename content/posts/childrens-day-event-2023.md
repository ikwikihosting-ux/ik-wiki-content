### Event Overview & Guide to the unique Artifact reward

🧸🧸Children's Day event has started! Collect as many Ride Tokens and Crystal Balls as possible to redeem great rewards!!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/141444_1b6c60d0-c87b-4485-b548-276a0721c3d4_170006-1024x576.png)

## How does it work?

This event works much like many of the other Infinity Kingdom celebration events. Over the course of five days, we are able to complete a number of different tasks and obtain event-currency rewards. This time the blue-tier currency is called Ride Token og the purple-tier currency is called Crystal Marbels. Using these currencies we can redeem event-specific rewards from the Exchange of Joy page.

**The event has five domains**, these are as follow.

### 1. Joyful Park:

Each day we are unlocking a long list of tasks to complete over the course of the five days of festivities. E.g. daily logins, troop production, Immortal summons are day 1 challenges.

The Joyful Park rewards Marbels both via daily consecutive logins from Day 1 quests as well as from the Star-chests. All in all you can earn 16 Marbels from Joyful Park.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Screenshot_20220624-142551-1024x474.png)

At the time of writing we are in day 1 of the event, needless to say I cannot know for certain what we will find in the rewards tabs for day 2-5 quests. But based on the previous years we should not expect additional free Marbels from this event tab in the other days.

**Chaos Immortal on the loose! Wukong Fragments!**

What we can get is something entirely new! For the first time ever we have a chance to obtain free Wukong Fragments. Completing the daily quests and unlocking all five star-chests will reward to a total of 40 Wukong fragments. Quite nice and interesting! I had personally hoped for more Loki fragments, but I suspect we are going to be forced to spend to collect what remains on Loki from now on.

### 2. Heart of Innocence :

By playing the game and gathering resources, collecting resources from your farms, killing gnomes and killing gnome bosses you earn **Ride Tokens.**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Screenshot_20220624-142549-1024x474.png)

### 3. Wonderful Home:

This is a tiered 1-20 progress bar, where you spend Imagination Blocks to decorate the child home. In doing so, you unlock level rewards 1-20. Moreover, you can spend 3500 gems to unlock a higher tier rewards, and 20 USD to unlock the highest tier of Wonderful Home rewards.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Screenshot_20220624-142542-1024x474.png)

### 4. Exchange of Joy

Here you can redeem your event currency for juicy rewards! See the list below.

Most noticably, we are able to get a full schematic for a **unique artifact for** **Attila, Alexander, Cid or Saladin!** as well as a chance to get a full **60x**Immortal Fragments for either **Charles, Genghis Khan or Baldwin!**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Screenshot_20220624-142559-1024x474.png)
