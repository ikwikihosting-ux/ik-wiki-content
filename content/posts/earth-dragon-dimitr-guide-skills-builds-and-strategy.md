## Why Earth Dragon Dimitr is So Good

Before dragon skill trees were introduced, Earth Dragon wasn’t seen as one of the top-tier dragons. But with the arrival of **customizable dragon talent trees**, Dimitr has risen to become a **top choice** among elemental dragons, rivaled only by Holy and Shadow in terms of versatility.

He brings:

- High **damage mitigation**
- Supportive **buffs and control**
- Synergy with both **tank-heavy** and **hybrid high-speed** marches

## Basics

- Earth Dragon Dimitr has **skyrocketed in popularity** since the release of dragon skill trees
- His skills are **highly adaptable** to various team compositions
- **Collapse** is the go-to pick for defense
- **Aftershock** supports hybrid and speed comps
- **Fissure** is best reserved for aggressive burst lineups

Upgrade him early, tailor his tree to your team, and you’ll see results in both PvP and Conquest performance.

Happy fighting, and may your Earth Dragon bring victory to your march!

### Guide to the Earth Dragon "Demeter" in Infinity Kingdom

Demeter, the Earth Dragon, is a pivotal character in Infinity Kingdom, offering distinct advantages and strategies with its abilities and talent specializations. This free dragon, unlocked during the tutorial, provides a robust foundation for your march, particularly against physical threats.

### Demeter's Stats at Level 55:

- **Physical Attack**: +114
- **Physical Defense**: +152
- **Magical Attack**: +126.6
- **Magical Defense**: +114
- **Analysis**: These stats make your march resistant to physical damage but more vulnerable to magical attacks. While the lower Physical Attack can be a concern for Earth carry immortals like Alexander, it's less impactful compared to Defense due to diminishing returns.

### Passive Skill - Earthshake:

- **Effect**: Provides +30% crit and dodge value.
- **Unique Feature**: Earth immortals get +4% damage increase for every 10% troops power loss, stackable up to 5 times.
- **Strategy**: This encourages the use of a Healer with your earth team to maximize the passive while keeping your immortals healthy.

## Skill Tree 1: Collapse

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-70-1024x438.png)

Collapse is arguably the **best skill tree** for Earth Dragon in current meta, especially for **double tank** teams.

- **Earthquake (Collapse)**: Deals true damage (starts at 30,000 and increases with levels).
- **Sandstorm Breath (Collapse)**: Reduces damage taken by **3 random allies**, with:  - Up to **98% chance** to trigger
- **35% damage reduction**
- Lasts for **16.8 seconds** at level 8

### Why Use Collapse?

- Best choice for **tanky team compositions**
- Incredibly high **damage mitigation uptime**
- Ideal if you're running frontline Immortals like **Charles, Alex, or Bjorn**

> "Collapse is the tree I use on my main team. It's one of the best defensive tools in the game right now."

---

## Skill Tree 2: Aftershock

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-71-1024x435.png)

Aftershock is a **flexible skill tree** with utility for **hybrid** or **attack-speed based** teams.

- **Earthquake (Aftershock)**: Extends stun duration by 1 second; adds 3 seconds to Earthquake duration
- **Sandstorm Breath (Aftershock)**:  - **90% chance** to trigger
- Reduces damage taken by 35%
- Increases **attack speed by 3%** for 12 seconds

### Why Use Aftershock?

- Excellent for **high attack speed teams** (e.g. **Alex + Hannibal with Rapid Artifact**)
- Good synergy with Immortals focused on **DPS burst**
- Can fit **double tank + hybrid mix** if built properly

> "Aftershock is a solid pick for high-speed marches or for frontlines that need both mitigation and aggression."

---

## Skill Tree 3: Fissure

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/image-72-1024x440.png)

Fissure is the most **offensive-focused** of Dimitr’s skill trees.

- **Earth Roar (Fissure)**: Increases enemy damage taken by 5%
- **Sandstorm Breath (Fissure)**:  - 90% chance to reduce damage taken by 25%
- Grants lifesteal (heals on normal attacks)
- Deals bonus true damage

### Why Use Fissure?

- Good for **burst-heavy DPS teams** with **Alex + Himiko** or other raw damage setups
- Amplifies damage output
- Adds **healing through attacks**, giving sustain for DPS units

However, Fissure is **less optimal** for **tank-focused teams**.

> "Fissure has potential in heavy-hitting teams, but it falls short in defensive comps. Use it when you're going full damage."

## Final Thoughts and Recommendations

### Current Meta Pick: Collapse

- Collapse is currently the most **popular and reliable** skill tree, especially for competitive play
- Offers **high uptime on damage reduction**, essential for tough Conquest fights

### When to Use Each Tree:

| Skill Tree | Best For |
| --- | --- |
| Collapse | Double tank, defensive setups |
| Aftershock | Hybrid and high attack speed teams |
| Fissure | Burst damage, DPS-heavy teams |

### Upgrade Recommendation

If you're deciding which dragon to focus on next, **Earth Dragon Dimitr should be your top priority**:

- **Balanced**: Offers both **sustain and utility**
- **Versatile**: Works in many different team compositions
- **Popular**: Commonly used in top-ranking Conquest teams

> "If I were to recommend one dragon tree to upgrade first, it would be Earth Dragon’s Collapse. It offers the best balance of survivability and team-wide support."
