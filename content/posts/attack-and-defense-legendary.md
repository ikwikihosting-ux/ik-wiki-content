## Introduction

Among the earliest Chaos skills introduced through the first iteration of the Well of Time event and now available as one of only two skills as early as Preparation Season, **Attack & Defense** has holds its own remarkably well through each season.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-43.png)

Many early skills eventually fell behind newer Conquest- and Legendary-era mechanics, but this one remained relevant because it combines two things physical teams value more than almost anything else:

- damage burst pressure
- huge survivability against physical damage

In the current physical-heavy meta shaped by Medusa, Apollo, revived Alexander builds, and modern Khan setups, the defensive half of the skill often matters even more than the damage itself. That balance is what keeps Attack & Defense viable all the way into high-end Legendary Season play.

---

## Official Skill Description

> “After battle starts, has a 60% chance to deal Physical Damage every 6s (Damage Rate 400%) and increase own Physical Defense by 100% for 6s.”

At first glance, the skill reads fairly simply. In practice, there is more going on here than many players initially realize.

---

## Damage Performance - Proc Rate and Timing

The main weakness of Attack & Defense is in its damage consistency.. or well in-consistency.

A **60% trigger chance every 6 seconds** is noticeably less reliable than some of the more stable top-tier physical skills. In shorter fights especially, poor proc timing can heavily reduce value. There will absolutely be battles where the skill underperforms due to RNG.

That said, the **400% damage rate** is genuinely strong.

In repeated testing on physical carries like Medusa, the skill commonly lands within the same overall damage contribution range usually somewhere around:

- **20–30% of total damage**
- depending on fight length, proc timing, and enemy mitigation

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-44-1024x607.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-45-1024x620.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-46-1024x617.png)

That is already enough to make the skill viable offensively.

The important distinction is that Attack & Defense is not competing purely as a damage skill. If it only dealt damage, it would still be good — but probably not elite. What elevates it is the second effect.

---

## The Real Value: 100% Physical Defense - Why the Defense Buff Matters So Much

The physical defense increase is enormous. A flat **100% Physical Defense increase for 6 seconds** is one of the strongest defensive buffs attached to any offensive Chaos skill. In older mage-dominated metas, players often underestimated this effect because magical burst bypassed much of its value. That environment has changed significantly.

Modern high-level PvP revolves heavily around physical pressure, Medusa, Apollo, Alexander, Khan, all are amongst the top used and best performing immortals in the current meta. That is why this skill is so incredible. Against these teams, physical mitigation becomes one of the most valuable stats in the game.

## Player Perspective

Attack & Defense is one of those skills that looks merely “good” on paper but consistently overperforms in real PvP environments.

The damage contribution is respectable, though somewhat RNG-dependent. The real strength is how effectively it mitigates physical carries against the current meta. Once physical-focused teams became dominant again, the value of doubling up on physical defense during combat became extremely difficult to ignore.

In many high-end fights, surviving one additional rotation matters more than squeezing out slightly higher burst damage. That is exactly where this skill shines.

For players investing long-term into physical carry teams, especially Medusa-based compositions, Attack & Defense remains one of the safest Chaos skill investments available from the early Well of Time pool.

---

## Conclusion Summary

Attack & Defense remains one of the strongest early Chaos Tower of Knowledge skills because it successfully combines:

- meaningful physical damage
- exceptional defensive utility
- long-term meta relevance

While the proc consistency can occasionally feel unreliable, the combination of a 400% damage rate and a massive 100% physical defense boost gives the skill value far beyond raw DPS numbers.

In the modern physical-heavy meta, that defensive effect alone is often enough to justify the investment. For sustained physical carries like Medusa, Alexander, and Khan setups, Attack & Defense continues to perform at a level worthy of endgame play years after its original release.
