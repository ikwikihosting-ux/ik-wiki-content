## Ares – The God of War (Chaos, Cavalry)

Ares, the god of war, joined Infinity Kingdom with Patch 2.8.5 on **July 10th**, entering as the newest **Chaos Immortal**.

Unlike **Apollo**, who combines burst damage, healing suppression, and scaling durability, **Ares** represents pure, relentless aggression. His signature trait is a **permanent +50% Attack Speed bonus**, making him one of the fastest cavalry Immortals from the moment combat begins.

What truly sets Ares apart, however, is his ability to **remove Control Immunity** and **weaken Barriers**—two defensive mechanics that once defined the durability meta. Teams that rely on shield stacking or immunity rotations quickly crumble against him. In turn, he enables allies’ control skills—stuns, silences, confusions, roots—to land with full effect.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-10.png)

This dual function gives Ares a rare identity among frontliners: he is both a **high-pressure attacker** and a **team enabler** who opens the way for allies to capitalize on control and burst windows.

Ares is designed for tempo play. He sets the pace early, cracks defensive layers open, and lets the rest of the team flood through. Players who prefer fast, decisive battles will find him a powerful addition to Chaos lineups.

## Core Strengths & Battle Identity

### Skill: Battlefield Trample

- **Passive:** Increases Normal Attack Speed by 50% permanently.
- **Active:** Deals physical damage to all enemies (scaling 150–500%) and has a 50% chance to apply **Frighten** for 6 seconds, which:  - Removes the target’s **Control Immunity**
- Reduces the effectiveness of **Barriers** by 30%

Ares thrives on momentum. The permanent speed boost ensures he begins outputting damage immediately, while Frighten opens defensive gaps that allow allied control effects and damage to land unhindered. This makes him one of the most aggressive openers in the Chaos roster.

While his early tempo is unmatched, his impact declines slightly in prolonged battles without sustain or scaling. For players focused on early dominance and team coordination, however, Ares provides a distinct advantage.

## Battlefield Trample – Mechanics & Energy Flow

Battlefield Trample defines Ares’ combat rhythm:

- **+50% Attack Speed (passive)**
- **AoE Physical Damage (150–500% scaling)**
- **50% chance to apply "Frighten" on enemies which strip them of any Control Immunity (e.g. Fountain of Life or Fu Fei) and weaken Barriers by 30% for 6 seconds**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-8.png)

### Why It Matters

- **Anti-Barrier Utility:** Ares doesn’t just pierce shields—he destabilizes them. Barrier-heavy teams, especially those relying on sustain or timing-based protection, lose their key advantage.
- **CC Enablement:** Removing Control Immunity allows allied controllers—such as **Tomyris**, **Himiko**, and **Loki**—to land disables that previously failed. Once these effects connect, the fight shifts heavily in your favor.
- **Damage Conversion:** Each CC connection opens a punishment window for high-damage allies like Apollo or Anubis to exploit.

### Energy Regen & Ultimate Timing

Ares also benefits from above-average energy regeneration, starting at **93.3/s** base. With equipment and boosts, he can reach 105–130/s, letting him cast Battlefield Trample frequently—crucial for maintaining his debuff uptime.

| Regen Rate | Time to 1000 Energy | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 93.3/s | ~10.7s | Baseline |
| 105/s | ~9.5s | Smooth cycle |
| 130/s | ~7.7s | Near-continuous pressure |

Ares’ debuff lasts **6 seconds**. At **105/s**, there’s a ~3.5s gap between effects; at **130/s**, the downtime narrows to ~1.7s. In live battles, even minor boosts (on-hit energy gain, ally support, etc.) can create near-continuous debuff pressure—transforming him into a constant disruptor.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/image-7-1024x559.png)

The most impactful element of his kit is not the AoE damage itself, but the **immunity removal** that changes how entire lineups interact.

## Optimal Builds – Artifact, Specialization & Skills

### Best Artifact

### War Spear·Surge

Prioritize **Physical Damage %** as the main attribute. The Surge effect aligns with Ares’ short ultimate cycle, helping him refresh Battlefield Trample faster and maintain pressure.

### Best Specialization

**Knight’s Spirit** — Offers +21–70% Damage and +9–30% Attack Speed.
This specialization amplifies his aggressive playstyle and keeps him effective from the opening seconds.

### Recommended Skills

- **Last Stand:** Provides vital survivability during the opening exchange, ensuring Ares stays alive to apply his debuffs.
- **Wind Rage:** Doubles down on his attack speed, turning him into a relentless source of auto-attack and skill pressure.
- **Knife Through Butter:** Excellent synergy. Every 6 attacks trigger AoE cleaves that cut enemy energy regen by 300. With Ares’ speed, this procs often, crippling sustain teams.
- **Optional alternatives:**  - **Assembly Charge:** For early snowball.
- **Battle Frenzy:** For pure offense.
- **Corrosive Power:** For smoother ult rotations.

### Essential Team Skill

**Fountain of Life** on an ally is strongly recommended. It prevents Ares from being silenced during his initial charge, preserving his tempo advantage.
**Spring of Life** is also useful for sustain-based setups, reducing incoming damage when healed.

The optimal skill trio for Ares is **Knife Through Butter + Wind Rage + Last Stand**—a balance of speed, disruption, and durability that lets him sustain aggression while amplifying team control.

## Team Synergies

Ares excels when paired with allies who can exploit his immunity removal and barrier reduction. The best combinations pair him with high-burst or control-heavy Immortals.

- **Loki:** With Ares removing immunity, Loki’s **Confusion** connects reliably, causing enemies to attack themselves. This combo revives CC-heavy compositions and can dismantle coordinated teams.
- **Apollo:** Once barriers are weakened, Apollo’s **Corona Godstrike** and healing reduction dominate. Together, they crush sustain-based defenses in seconds.
- **Anubis:** A dependable frontline bruiser who absorbs damage and amplifies incoming damage. Pairs well for sustained pressure setups.
- **Fu Fei:** Provides critical sustain and control immunity, allowing Ares to survive long enough to cycle multiple ultimates. A must-have for balanced compositions.

Ares functions best as an **enabler**—he doesn’t carry fights alone, but his debuffs allow his team to deal far greater damage and control than they otherwise could.

## Conclusion

Ares redefines Chaos frontline play. His strength lies not in raw damage numbers but in **meta disruption**—breaking barriers, removing control immunity, and letting allies dictate the tempo.

He fits perfectly into teams focused on **early aggression**, **tempo control**, and **crowd control synergy**. Pair him with **Apollo** for explosive offense or **Loki** for sustained CC dominance.

However, players favoring **slow, sustain-based battles** may find his impact tapering off over time. Ares is not a wall—he’s a hammer built to break them.

**Bottom Line:**
Ares is a meta-shifting Immortal who changes how fights are played. He doesn’t just deal damage—he changes the rules of engagement. For players who want to resurrect CC comps, dismantle barrier-heavy teams, and seize control from the first second, Ares delivers exactly that.
