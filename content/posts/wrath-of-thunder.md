## What does "Wrath of Thunder" do?

Every castle skin has unique beneficial effects, from building speed to rss gathering and troop HP. The Wrath of Thunder castle skin increases **SP recovery rate by 5%** AND**increase building speed 5%**. It is following the line made by the development team on these unique element dragon skins as this much like the Frozen Dragon Hive. SP recovery is a pretty decent buff although not anything on par with healing.

Contrary, the 5% buff to production speed is a big one. We expect the progression of our castle buildings to continue slowly as time passes. We started with level 40 as the max which was then increased to level 50. It is more than likely that level 60 is coming up in which case you want this skin buff ready!

## How to get it?

You have to purchase bundles (any bundles) which yield a total of 50K gems. It dosen't matter what bundles you purchase, as long as there is a gem reward it counts towards your castle skin.

## Bundles with gems

Most all bundles have gem rewards, but how to they scale?

Usually, a 5$ bundle yield 520 gems, a 10$ bundle yields 1,100 gems, a 20$ bundle yields 2,300 gems, a 50$ bundle yields 6,000 gems and a 99$ bundle yields 12,500 gems. These are the standard rewards and can be slightly different on special bundles.

### Read more about shop bundles

| USD | 4.99 | 9.99 | 19.99 | 49.99 | 99.99 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gems | 520 | 1100 | 2300 | 6000 | 12500 |
| 1 USD = in Gems | 104.21 | 110.11 | 115.06 | 120.02 | 125.01 |

As you can see, the more expensive bundles are finally worth buying! They offer a higher gem/USD spent reward.

Do however take note, that the contents of expensive bundle tiers is MUCH worse than the cheaper ones. Overall, it would still be FAR MORE value to purchase many small bundles despite the lower gem reward.

At an average cost between 104-125 gems per dollar you are doing to be spending around 400$-480$ to unlock the new Castle Skin.
