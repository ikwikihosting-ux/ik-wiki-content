### Player Guide: Seondeok's Unique Artifact - Endless Artwork

In the realm of Infinity Kingdom, artifacts play a pivotal role in enhancing your immortals' prowess on the battlefield. Unique artifacts, in particular, are a special breed of items that not only boost stats but also provide exclusive effects tailored to specific immortals. These artifacts can significantly influence your strategy and the effectiveness of your immortals in combat.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-70-1024x532.png)

## Seondeok's Unique Artifact: Endless Artwork

Seondeok, known for her devastating offensive capabilities, receives a tremendous boost through her unique artifact, the Endless Artwork. This artifact amplifies the effects of her already powerful ultimate ability, "Might of the Phoenix."

### Unique Effect:

The Endless Artwork extends the duration of the explosive effect from Seondeok's ultimate ability by an additional 3 seconds, increasing it from 6 seconds to 9 seconds. This enhancement allows Seondeok to maintain a higher frequency of explosive triggers, drastically enhancing her damage output over time.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-71.png)

### Impact of the Unique Effect:

Seondeok’s ultimate, "Might of the Phoenix," channels the ferocity of a mythical bird to inflict significant physical damage, with an explosive effect that triggers additional damage whenever the target receives a critical hit within the effect's duration.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-72.png)

By extending this period, the Endless Artwork allows for more frequent explosions, thus maximizing the potential for widespread enemy devastation. This is particularly potent in builds focused on high critical rate and rapid attack speed, enabling continuous detonations that can obliterate opposing forces swiftly.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-73-1024x608.png)

### Strategic Advantages:

1. **Increased Damage Potential:** With a 50% longer duration for the explosive effect, Seondeok can leverage extended windows of enhanced damage, making her a formidable force in prolonged battles.
2. **Synergy with High-Crit Builds:** Combining Seondeok with immortals and skills that enhance critical hit rates can turn her into a relentless destroyer, capitalizing on the extended duration to maximize area damage.
3. **Better Energy Utilization:** In setups optimized for quick energy regeneration, the extended duration of her explosive effects ensures that once her ultimate is active, the battlefield remains volatile for the enemy for an extended period.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-74-1024x642.png)

### How to Obtain the Endless Artwork:

The Endless Artwork can be acquired through participation in the Stellar event, where players collect and redeem Crystal Balls for artifact fragments.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-68.png)

You can also obtain this artifact as a side reward in the Blessings of Triss event. However, it will be quite costly!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/image-75-1024x480.png)

### Conclusion:

Seondeok's unique artifact, the Endless Artwork, transforms her from a formidable archer into a relentless harbinger of destruction. Its ability to extend the explosive effects of her ultimate ability makes it a must-have for players looking to maximize their damage output and dominate in both PvP and PvE encounters. By integrating this artifact into your arsenal, you ensure that Seondeok remains not just a threat but a decisive factor in your conquests across the kingdom.
