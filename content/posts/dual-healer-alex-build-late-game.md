### Alexander with Battle Stance: a late game setup

In this guide, we explore an unconventional yet surprisingly effective setup featuring **Alexander the Great** using **Battle Stance** in an **Earth sustain march**. While Battle Stance is typically not recommended for Alex, this guide highlights how specific synergies can unlock insane potential, allowing Alex to deliver some of the highest damage figures seen in current Infinity Kingdom metas.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-97.png)

---

## 💡 March Overview

The march includes the following Immortals:

- **Alexander the Great** (Main DPS)
- **Charles/Athena** (Frontline sustain/support)
- **Theodora/Fou Fei** (Healer)
- **Zenobia** (Healer)

The setup focuses on **high sustain**, using **double healers** to keep Alex alive and allow him to ramp up damage over time. Athena provides Guard support, shielding Alex, while Zenobia not only heals but boosts **attack speed** through her exclusive artifact effect.

---

## ⚔️ Battle Stance on Alex: Why It Works Here

Normally, Battle Stance isn't optimal for Alex due to the 70% damage reduction for the first 20 seconds and energy regen that he doesn’t benefit from (as his ultimate is passive). However, in this **high-sustain march**, the drawbacks are compensated for:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-92.png)

- **Alex survives longer** due to double healers and Athena’s protection.
- Once Battle Stance activates (after 20s), Alex **deals 100% increased damage**.
- He benefits massively from **attack speed boosts** via Zenobia's artifact.

This synergy enables Alex to perform exceptionally well in **extended fights**, especially against setups that don’t have countermeasures against sustained damage.

---

## 📊 Performance Highlights

### Massive DPS Output

The damage amplification from **Battle Stance**, combined with **continuous damage effects** like Blade Vortex, is key. Executioner also shines here because Theodora (a typical counter via healing suppression) is **not present** in the lineup, allowing continuous damage to persist.

These reports consistently show **Alex outperforming expectations**, often topping 70-90 million damage with proper support in late-game conquest combat.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-96-1024x219.png)

### Key Skills Used on Alex

- **Battle Stance** – Delayed burst potential with 100% damage increase
- **Blade Vortex** – Reliable continuous damage
- **Executioner** – Amplifies total output due to persistent DoT effects

Alternative setups use **Demonic Contract** instead of Battle Stance, also yielding great results, though not as dramatic.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-93-1024x596.png)

---

## 🧪 Skill Comparison: Battle Stance vs. Demonic Contract

### Battle Stance Pros:

- 100% damage increase after 20s
- Extreme synergy with high sustain and long fights
- Turns Alex into a **late-game carry**

### Battle Stance Cons:

- Weak first 20 seconds
- No benefit from energy regen

### Demonic Contract Pros:

- Earlier power spike
- More consistent performance throughout the fight

### Demonic Contract Cons:

- Lower peak burst damage

---

## 🆚 Zenobia vs. Theodora

### Why Zenobia?

- Grants **25% attack speed** to Alex
- Supports the sustain strategy with **Embrace's Favor** healing

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-94.png)

### When Theodora Might Be Better:

- Against **continuous damage** builds (e.g., Blade Vortex)
- If you need **healing immunity cleanse**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/image-95.png)

Both options are viable, but Zenobia is superior when maximizing Alex's **damage output** through attack speed and heal burst.

## 🧪 Additional Variations

Other reported setups include:

- Swapping Zenobia for Theodora
- Pairing Demonic Contract with Executioner for earlier damage ramp

## ✅ Final Thoughts

This build showcases how creative setups and smart synergies can unlock surprising results in Infinity Kingdom. While Battle Stance isn’t traditionally used on Alex, in a **high-sustain, healer-heavy earth march**, it becomes a powerful enabler.

If you're looking to test new builds or optimize Alex for long, drawn-out Conquest battles, **this setup is worth exploring**. Consider your opponents, adapt your support immortals accordingly, and you might just unlock a new powerhouse in your lineup.

> High sustain + delayed burst = Battle Stance Alex supremacy!
