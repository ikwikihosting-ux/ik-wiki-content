Infinity Kingdom is a popular mobile strategy game that offers players the opportunity to build their kingdoms, recruit Immortals, and engage in epic battles. One of the main currencies in the game is **Cold Coin**, which is used to purchase a variety of rewards and items in the game's shop. Another important currency in Infinity Kingdom is Legion Marks, which is used to rank players on the Legion Marks leaderboard of an Legion of Frostborne battle. While both currencies are important, they are very often misunderstood.

What the heck is the difference??**

## Cold Coin

First, let's look at Cold Coin. This currency is the main currency used to buy items in the game's shop. Players can use Cold Coin to purchase a wide range of rewards, including Ability Stone Fragments to unlock new Tower of Knowledge skills, Dragon Essences for upgrading their dragon's talent tree, and various buffs and speeds.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Skaermbillede-2023-04-04-172934-1-1024x572.png)

However, it is essential to note that players can only obtain Cold Coin through two means: **ranking in the Legion Marks leaderboard**and earning alliance rewards.

To earn Cold Coin, players must rank high on the Legion Marks leaderboard of an Legion of Frostborne battle. The Legion of Frostborne is a server versus server battle mode in which players can earn Legion Marks by attacking enemy players, donating resources to their alliance structures, constructing thermal towers, defeating gnomes, dismantling enemy Thermal Towers, and sieging checkpoints and cities. However, players can only earn Cold Coin by ranking high on the Legion Marks leaderboard. This means that players who want to maximize their Cold Coin rewards must focus on earning Honor points, which directly translate to Legion Marks.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Skaermbillede-2023-04-04-172821-1-1024x553.png)

The second way to earn Cold Coin in Infinity Kingdom is through alliance rewards. Alliance rewards are a way for players to earn Cold Coin by participating in alliance activities. These activities can include donating resources to the alliance, attacking enemy players, and participating in alliance events. The amount of Cold Coin earned through alliance rewards can vary depending on the alliance's performance and the player's participation in alliance activities.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/image-4-1024x559.png)

## Legion Marks

Now, let's talk about Legion Marks. As previously mentioned, Legion Marks are used to rank players on the Legion Marks leaderboard of an Legion of Frostborne battle. Players earn Legion Marks by performing various activities in the game, including attacking enemy players to obtain Honor, donating resources to alliance structures, constructing thermal towers, defeating gnomes, dismantling enemy Thermal Towers, and sieging checkpoints and cities.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Skaermbillede-2023-04-04-172740-1-1024x447.png)

However, it is essential to note that Legion Marks cannot be used to buy anything in the game's shop. Instead, Legion Marks serve as a way for players to rank themselves against other players in the Legion of Frostborne battle.**This means that players who want to earn more Cold Coin must focus on ranking high on the Legion Marks leaderboard by earning more Honor points.**

The most crucial point to understand is that Cold Coin and Legion Marks are not the same thing. Many players make the mistake of thinking that they are earning Cold Coin when they perform various activities in the game, such as dismantling a Thermal or donating to a City. However, this is incorrect. These activities only earn players Legion Marks, which are used to rank them on the Legion Marks leaderboard. To earn Cold Coin, players must focus on earning Honor points and ranking high on the Legion Marks leaderboard.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Skaermbillede-2023-04-04-172847-1-1024x560.png)

**In conclusion**, Cold Coin and Legion Marks are two distinct currencies used in the Legion of Frostborne game mode in Infinity Kingdom. Cold Coin is used to purchase rewards, while Legion Marks are earned through various activities in the game. Players can earn Legion Marks by donating resources to their alliance structures, constructing thermal towers, defeating gnomes, dismantling enemy thermal towers, and sieging checkpoints and cities. However, the only worthwhile method to obtain Legion Marks is by earning Honor.

On the other hand, Cold Coin is the main currency used to purchase rewards such as ability stone fragments, dragon essences, and various buffs and speeds. Players can earn Cold Coin by ranking high in the Legion Marks leaderboard and through their alliance's performance.

It's important for players to understand the difference between these two currencies in the Legion of Frostborne game mode. Many players mistakenly believe that they are earning Cold Coin when they dismantle a thermal tower or donate to a city, but in reality, they are earning Legion Marks for rankings. So, always prioritize earning Honor to maximize your Legion Marks rewards and subsequently earn Cold Coin rewards.

With a good understanding of the mechanics behind Cold Coin and Legion Marks, players can better strategize their gameplay and earn valuable rewards in the Legion of Frostborne mode. So, get ready to dominate the battlefield and earn those coveted rewards!
