Every Elemental Domain dungeon ends with the same kind of fight: a single Elemental Overlord with **301.3 million HP** and a skill set built to punish one particular kind of team. Knowing what that boss does often decides whether you stall at 50% or reach the next reward milestone.

This guide covers all four Overlords (**Skarn, Nyxara, Thalgron and Vorthar**): what each skill does, what makes each boss hard, and what your formation has to counter to kill it.

For how the rewards work and how to plan your week, read the [Elemental Domain Strategy Guide](/posts/elemental-domain-strategy-guide-how-to-maximize-weekly-boss-rewards) first.

## Quick Overview

| Boss                      | Role   | Damage   | Immune to       | Resists (−30%)   | Below 50% HP          |
| ------------------------- | ------ | -------- | --------------- | ---------------- | --------------------- |
| Skarn the Frozen Doom     | Attack | Physical | Freeze          | Water            | +80% damage           |
| Nyxara Queen of Dread     | Mage   | Magical  | Stun            | Earth            | Heals every 6 s       |
| Thalgron the Storm Reaper | Attack | Physical | Seriously Wound | Wind & Lightning | 48% Life Steal        |
| Vorthar the Devourer      | Tank   | Physical | Critical hits   | Fire             | Takes 32% less damage |

All four are level 56, 7-star, T8 +30 boss Immortals with 87,000 troops, 301.3M HP and roughly 1.04–1.08M power.

## How Every Overlord Is Built

Every boss has the same four-skill structure:

1. **An ultimate** that hits your whole team and adds a control or damage-over-time effect.
2. **An immunity** that switches off one type of effect completely.
3. **An elemental resistance** that reduces damage from one or two elements by 30%.
4. **A phase skill** that activates when the boss drops below 50% HP.

The phase skill matters most. **Every boss gets harder in the second half of the fight.** Reaching 50% doesn't mean you're halfway to a kill, because the second half is a harder fight.

Before you build a team, check two things:

* Does your damage depend on the effect the boss is immune to?
* Are your Immortals hit by its 30% resistance?

## Skarn the Frozen Doom

**Attack · Physical damage · Resists Water**

**Skills**

* **Winter's Judgement (ultimate):** Ice spikes deal Physical damage (damage rate 126% → 420%) to all your units and apply **Chill**, which reduces attack speed by 3% → 10% for 12 seconds. Chilled units have a **50% chance to be Frozen** for 3 seconds.
* **Heart of Eternal Frost:** Immune to Freeze.
* **Frost Resistance:** Water Immortals deal 30% less damage.
* **Frostbitten Rage:** Below 50% HP, Skarn enters Rage and deals **80% more damage**.

**What makes him hard**

Skarn snowballs. Each ultimate slows your team and then has a coin-flip chance to freeze every chilled unit. A frozen damage dealer loses three seconds of output, and a slowed team builds Energy more slowly. Once Skarn drops below 50%, the same ultimate hits 80% harder, so many runs that look fine at first collapse after the halfway point.

**What to counter**

* **Survive the second half.** Physical defense, damage reduction, shields and healing matter more against Skarn than against any other Overlord. Build the team to still be standing at 40% boss HP, not just for a strong opening.
* **Stop the Freeze chain.** Control immunity or cleanse protects your carries. Freeze only lands on Chilled units, so removing Chill also removes the freeze risk.
* **Delay his ultimate.** Energy suppression means fewer Winter's Judgements, so less damage and fewer freeze rolls.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-112825-axozp5.png)

* **Leave Freeze at home.** He's immune, so freeze effects do nothing. Stuns and other controls still work.
* **Water teams lose 30%.** When Skarn is the boss in the Water dungeon, expect lower damage than your formation's numbers suggest.

## Nyxara Queen of Dread

**Mage · Magical damage · Resists Earth**

**Skills**

* **Toxic Eruption (ultimate):** Sprays venom that **Poisons all your units**, dealing Magical damage (damage rate 54% → 180%) every 3 seconds for 12 seconds. When a unit reaches **3 Poison stacks, the venom explodes**, dealing another 168% → 560% Magical damage to all your units.
* **Heart of Steel:** Immune to Stun.
* **Earth Resistance:** Earth Immortals deal 30% less damage.
* **Absorb Life:** Below 50% HP, Nyxara **recovers HP every 6 seconds** (recovery rate 600%).

**What makes her hard**

Nyxara is the only Overlord that deals **Magical damage**, so a team built with physical defense for the other three can fall apart against her. Her Poison stacks, and the third stack sets off an explosion that hits your whole team. In the second half she heals herself every six seconds, so a team without enough steady damage can stall and never finish her.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-112905-mk25q0.png)

**What to counter**

* **Magic defense.** Swap physical defense for magic defense and magic damage reduction where you can.
* **Cleanse before the third stack.** Removing Poison early should stop stacks from reaching three and exploding.
* **Healing reduction works.** She isn't immune to Seriously Wound, so healing-reduction effects cut into Absorb Life.
* **Steady damage beats burst.** Burst gets her to 50% quickly, but you need consistent damage to out-damage her healing.
* **Leave Stun at home.** She's immune. Other controls and Energy suppression still apply.
* **Earth teams lose 30%** if Nyxara is in their dungeon.

## Thalgron the Storm Reaper

**Attack · Physical damage · Resists Wind and Lightning**

**Skills**

* **Swarm Storm (ultimate):** A mechanical swarm deals Physical damage (damage rate 114% → 380%) to all your units, with a 10.5% → 35% chance to **Silence** each one for 6 seconds.
* **Resilient Shell:** Immune to Seriously Wound.
* **Storm Resistance:** **Wind and Lightning** Immortals deal 30% less damage.
* **Violent Plundering:** Below 50% HP, Thalgron gains **48% Life Steal**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-112947-y3zeqe.png)

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-112957-vmp555.png)

**What makes him hard**

Silence stops your Immortals from casting their Energy skills for six seconds. If it hits your main damage dealer just before an ultimate, you lose a big chunk of damage. Thalgron is the only Overlord that resists two elements. Below 50% he heals for almost half of the damage he deals, and because he's immune to Seriously Wound, healing reduction can't stop it.

**What to counter**

* **Protect your casters.** Cleanse or control immunity on your carries keeps their ultimates on schedule.
* **Cut his damage to cut his healing.** Life Steal is based on the damage he deals. Physical defense, damage reduction and effects that lower his damage output reduce both the pressure on your team and his healing.
* **Leave Seriously Wound at home.** He's immune.
* **Push through the second half quickly.** The longer he spends below 50%, the more Life Steal he gets.
* **Wind and Lightning teams lose 30%** if Thalgron is in their dungeon.

## Vorthar the Devourer

**Tank · Physical damage · Resists Fire**

**Skills**

* **Scorched Earth (ultimate):** Shockwaves deal Physical damage (damage rate 99% → 330%) to all your units, with a 9% → 30% chance to **Stun** each one for 3 seconds.
* **Impregnable:** Immune to critical hits.
* **Fire Resistance:** Fire Immortals deal 30% less damage.
* **Obsidian Armor:** Below 50% HP, Vorthar **takes 32% less damage**.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-112634-km0ky5.png)

**What makes him hard**

Vorthar hits the softest of the four, but he's the hardest to damage. Crit immunity means every point of Crit Rate and Crit Damage on your team does nothing against him. In the second half he takes 32% less damage on top of that. If a Fire team meets Vorthar below 50% HP, it deals only about half its normal damage (0.70 × 0.68 ≈ 0.48), assuming the two reductions multiply.

**What to counter**

* **Rebuild without crits.** Where you can, replace Crit Rate and Crit Damage on gear, artifacts and skills with attack, damage bonuses and defense reduction.
* **Damage amplification and defense reduction** pay off more here than anywhere else.
* **Protect against Stun.** A 30% stun chance on every ultimate adds up, so cleanse or control immunity helps.
* **Plan for a long second half.** His damage is low, so survival is rarely the problem. Sustained damage is what matters.
* **Fire teams lose 30%** if Vorthar is in their dungeon.

## Which Boss Will You Face? The Rotation Question

The in-game rules only fix which **element** each dungeon accepts on which day:

| Day               | Open dungeons                                                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Monday            | Water                                                               |
| Tuesday           | Fire                                                                |
| Wednesday         | Wind                                                                |
| Thursday          | Lightning (shown as "Thunder" in game)                              |
| Friday            | Earth                                                               |
| Saturday & Sunday | All eight: Water, Fire, Wind, Lightning, Earth, Holy, Shadow, Chaos |

The rules don't say **which Overlord** you'll fight.

**We don't know how the bosses are assigned.** We do know that they switch around. The same day of the week can bring a different Overlord from one week to the next, and the same Overlord can turn up in different elemental dungeons. Don't expect last week's boss to be waiting for you this week.

So before every run:

* **Check which Overlord you're facing before you set your team.** Its immunity and resistance decide which of your effects and Immortals are worth bringing.
* **Use the Simulation Challenge** to test your formation against that day's boss without using a reward attempt.

*Do you know the pattern? If you've worked out how the Overlords rotate, reach out to us on Discord and we'll update this guide.*
