The Tycoon's Club is back. The casino-themed event from October returns for Black Friday 2026 with a few important changes: a new **chip currency** you earn by spinning the Fortune Wheel, a reworked Private Treasure Gallery, a **daily** points leaderboard, and a set of Black Friday Amethyst packs on GTarcade.

At first glance it looks brutal. The headline rewards in the Gallery cost **34,000 Tycoon Coins**, which is roughly **$2,800** worth of Amethyst. But once you work through the numbers, there is one efficient route through the event that is cheap or even free. This guide explains how the event works, what everything really costs, and where your money (if any) is best spent.

If you missed the October run, start with the [Tycoon's Club – New Event Overview & Beginner's Guide](/posts/tycoon-s-club-new-event-overview-beginner-s-guide) and the [Tycoon's Club Spending Guide](/posts/tycoon-s-club-spending-guide-best-buys-fortune-wheel-odds-value-analysis). This article covers what is different this time.

## Event Dates

* **Event:** 26 November – 3 December 2026 (ends at 00:00 UTC)
* **Private Treasure Gallery:** stays open one extra day, until 4 December 00:00 UTC, so you can spend leftover coins and chips
* **Daily reset:** 00:00 UTC (Limited-Time Rewards, Fortune Wheel Energy and the leaderboard)
* **Requirement:** Castle level 15+

## The Basics: Coins, Chips and Amethyst

There are three currencies to keep track of:

* **Amethyst** – the premium currency you buy with real money.
* **Tycoon Coins** – the event currency. **100 Tycoon Coins cost 50 Amethyst** (1 Amethyst = 2 coins), the same rate as in October. You also earn coins for free from the VIP Pass and Max Bounty.
* **Chips** (the purple token) – **new this time**. You cannot buy chips. You only get them from the Limited-Time Rewards tab by *consuming* Tycoon Coins on the Fortune Wheel.

That last point is the key to the whole event, so keep it in mind.

Amethyst is sold on GTarcade. During Black Friday you get 25% more Amethyst per dollar than usual on the special packs. Each Black Friday pack can be bought **once per day**.

Buying all six Black Friday packs costs **$187.94 a day** and gives **1,188 Amethyst (2,376 Tycoon Coins)**. **Rule of thumb for this guide:** at the big-pack rate of 6 Amethyst per dollar, **1 Tycoon Coin ≈ $0.08** and **1,000 coins ≈ $83**.

## VIP Pass (Holiday Battle Pass) – $4.99

![VIP Pass purchase screen: $4.99 for 60 Amethyst, 250 Gems and 3 chests now, and 450 Amethyst, 500 Gems, 23 blue and 5 gold chests by level 30](/uploads/screenshot-20261126-222831-p9jpa3.webp)

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-091901-azizqn.png)

The pass has 30 levels. You earn EXP from daily event missions.

* **Free track (Silver Insignia):** 10 Tycoon Coins per level = **300 coins**.
* **Paid track (Gold Insignia, $4.99):** 60 Amethyst, 250 Gems and 3 chests immediately, then 15 Amethyst per level, for a total of **450 Amethyst, 500 Gems, 23 blue chests and 5 gold chests** at level 30.

That is **510 Amethyst (1,020 coins) for $4.99**, about 100 Amethyst per dollar, or roughly 13 times better than the best Black Friday pack. If you plan to spend anything at all, buy this first. Just make sure you will actually do the daily missions; the Amethyst arrives level by level.

## Fortune Wheel – the Real Odds

![Fortune Wheel with Energy bar, spin button and daily points leaderboard](/uploads/screenshot-20261127-083513-q5ubzx.webp)

One spin costs **10 Tycoon Coins**. You can raise the reward multiplier, which multiplies both the cost and the rewards. When the Energy bar reaches 100% you trigger **Gold Rush**: the next spin costs 10% less. Energy resets daily.

**What this means:**

* On average a 10-coin spin pays back **7.98 coins**. The wheel returns **79.8%** of what you put in, so each spin really costs you about **2 coins** (a little less than in October, where the return was close to 90%).
* Each spin also gives on average **1.48 leaderboard points**, about 0.24 Superior Resources Optional Chests, 0.48 Medium Speedup Chests and a 0.1% shot at 100 Midnight Star.
* The swings are large. Over 120 spins the coin result typically varies by around ±360 coins, mostly decided by whether you hit one of the 450 or 600-coin jackpots.

Because coins you win go straight back into the wheel, a small bankroll can generate a lot of "consumption". That matters for the next part.

## Limited-Time Rewards – Where Chips Come From

Every day you earn chips for the total number of coins you consume on the wheel. Coins you won back and spun again count again.

| Coins consumed (daily) | Chips | Total chips | Expected real cost | Coins per chip (this tier) |
| ---------------------- | ----- | ----------- | ------------------ | -------------------------- |
| 1,200                  | 100   | 100         | ~242 coins         | 2.4                        |
| 3,600                  | 150   | 250         | ~727 coins         | 3.2                        |
| 7,200                  | 200   | 450         | ~1,454 coins       | 3.6                        |
| 12,000                 | 250   | 700         | ~2,424 coins       | 3.9                        |
| 18,000                 | 300   | 1,000       | ~3,636 coins       | 4.0                        |
| 25,000                 | 350   | 1,350       | ~5,050 coins       | 4.0                        |

"Expected real cost" is what you lose on average, after the wheel pays back its 79.8%. The first tier is by far the best value: **100 chips for about 242 coins (~$20 of Amethyst)**, every single day. The higher tiers get steadily worse, and maxing every tier costs about **5,050 coins (~$420) per day**.

(The tab's banner says *"Wir bieten euch erstklassigen Mitgliederservice!"* in German, even on English clients. Someone at the localization desk was busy with Black Friday too.)

## Max Bounty

Max Bounty is unchanged from October. Six bounties worth 1–6 stars (21 points per round) refresh every hour, and the progress bar has chests at 80, 110, 140, 170, 200, 230 and 260 points. The chests go from 80 Tycoon Coins + 50 Gems at the start to 220 Tycoon Coins + 450 Gems + 5 Philosopher's Offers at 260. In total the track is worth **roughly 1,000 Tycoon Coins**, free.

## Gilded Club

The Gilded Club tiered shop is back and **refreshes daily**. Tier 1 is free (10 Tycoon Coins, a scroll and potions), tier 2 costs **$0.99 for 10 Amethyst plus chests and 1,000 VIP points** (about 10 Amethyst per dollar, better than any Amethyst pack), and tier 3 costs 200 Gems for 5 Amethyst and more consumables. Further tiers continue the ladder with more cash and gem purchases. Always claim tier 1; tier 2 is a fine impulse buy if you are spending anyway.

## Is It Worth It? Four Budgets

All numbers below are averages. The wheel is random, so your own results will swing up or down.

**Free-to-play (\~1,300 coins: 300 from the free pass + \~1,000 from Max Bounty)**

Spin exactly **1,200 coins a day** (120 spins) to collect the first 100 chips, and stop. That costs about 242 coins a day, so your free coins last around five days, for **~500 chips**. That is enough for the **Louis IX Night of Terror** *and* **Alexander Summer Passion** outfits (200 chips each) plus a Core Immortals Selection Chest or Fate of Chaos chests with the rest. Not bad for zero dollars.

**$4.99 – VIP Pass (~2,300 coins)**

The pass adds about 1,000 coins. Now you can take the first tier every day of the event (\~700 chips) and sometimes the second. That covers both 200-chip outfits with \~300 chips to spare, or puts you most of the way to an 800-chip Fu Fei or Athena outfit.

**~$80–$120 – Pass + small Black Friday packs**

Add the $2.99, $4.99 and $9.99 Black Friday packs on some days (138 Amethyst for $17.97 per day). Taking tiers 1 and 2 on most days gets you to roughly 1,200–1,300 chips: **Louis IX + Alexander + Fu Fei or Athena**. This is the sweet spot for a light spender.

**$400+ a day – Leaderboard and max tiers**

Maxing all six chip tiers costs around **$420 per day**, so roughly **$2,900** over the event, for 1,350 chips a day. Just appearing on the daily leaderboard needs 1,500 points (~1,000 spins, about $170–$210 a day). The top three on my server were at 145,000–163,000 points on day one. That is around 110,000 spins, or very roughly **$20,000+ in a single day**. The leaderboard is strictly whale territory.

## Verdict

The first impression is correct for most of the event. The coin-priced Gallery items are priced for whales, the leaderboard is out of reach for almost everyone, and buying Amethyst to convert straight into coins is poor value even at Black Friday rates.

The chip shop is the exception. Because the wheel pays back almost 80% of every spin and chips are awarded on everything you *consume*, the first daily tier turns about 240 coins into 100 chips. That makes two Immortal outfits reachable for free-to-play players and a third for anyone buying the $4.99 pass and a few small packs.

**Quick plan:**

1. Buy the **$4.99 VIP Pass** if you will spend anything at all, and do the missions every day.
2. Clear **Max Bounty** whenever it refreshes.
3. Each day, spin until the **1,200-coin** tier is done, and stop there unless you have coins to spare.
4. Spend chips on **Louis IX** and **Alexander** outfits first (200 chips each), then **Fu Fei** or **Athena** (800 chips).
5. Never buy chests with coins when the same chest is available for chips.
6. Claim the free Gilded Club tier every day.
