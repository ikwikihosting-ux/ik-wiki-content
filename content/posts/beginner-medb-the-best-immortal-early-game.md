Medb is one of the most powerful and versatile early-game immortals in Infinity Kingdom. As a chaos-element mage, she offers unparalleled damage output and accessibility, making her a must-have for new players starting out in Norheim.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-16-1024x536.png)

---

## Why Medb?

### 1. Exceptional Damage Output

Medb’s ultimate ability, **Toxic Thorns**, delivers both burst damage and sustained damage over time (DoT). This combination makes her one of the highest damage-dealing immortals in the game for new servers.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-17-1024x502.png)

- **Toxic Thorns Damage Breakdown (Base Level):**  - **Initial Damage:** 127.5% to three enemies.
- **DoT Damage:** 51% every 3 seconds for 12 seconds (4 ticks total).
- **Total Damage Per Cast:**  - Initial Damage: 127.5% x 3 = 382.5%
- DoT Damage: 51% x 4 x 3 = 612%
- **Grand Total:** 994.5% to three enemies.

By comparison:

- **Merlin** (Pay-to-Play): 1,060.8% per cast.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-18-1024x521.png)

- **Cleopatra** (Legendary): 612% per cast.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-19-1024x509.png)

Medb’s damage rivals that of Merlin and far surpasses Cleopatra, **even before we are considering her elemental advantage!**

---

### 2. Elemental Advantage

As a **chaos-element** immortal, Medb deals **25% bonus damage to all other elements** (except holy, which is unavailable in new servers). This universal advantage makes her a dominant force in both PvE and PvP battles.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-20-1024x404.png)

---

### 3. Free and Easy to Upgrade

Medb is an **Elite (Purple)** immortal, which means:

- She is **freely obtainable** through the **Market** and other in-game rewards.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-21-1024x507.png)

- Upgrading her is cost-effective, requiring only **Purple Crystals** and fragments from the Market.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-22-1024x560.png)

Compared to Legendary (Gold) immortals, Medb is much faster and cheaper to max out, making her an ideal choice for free-to-play (F2P) players.

---

## Medb’s Role and Placement

- **Type:** Mage (Magic Damage)
- **Role:** Backline damage dealer.
- **Skill:** Toxic Thorns (AoE + DoT damage to three enemies).

Medb’s ability to deal massive AoE damage over time makes her an essential part of any troop formation, regardless of your elemental team composition.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-23-1024x605.png)

---

## How to Obtain Medb

- **Market Refreshes:** Prioritize buying Medb fragments whenever they appear in the Market.
- **Summoning:** Occasionally, you can earn her at random via the Hall of Immortals summons.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-24-1024x517.png)

---

## Using Medb Effectively

### 1. Prioritize Her Skill Level

Invest in **Purple Crystals** to upgrade Medb’s skill, **Toxic Thorns**, as quickly as possible. Higher skill levels significantly increase her damage output.

### 2. Flexible Team Composition

Medb’s chaos element allows her to fit seamlessly into any elemental setup, such as:

- Earth
- Water
- Lightning
- Fire

While having a 5-element bonus provides a 10% HP buff, Medb’s raw damage output often outweighs the need for such synergy.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/image-25-1024x393.png)

### 3. Dominate Early PvE and PvP

Medb’s combination of AoE damage, DoT, and elemental advantage makes her exceptional in:

- **PvE:** Farming gnomes and bosses.
- **PvP:** Early server battles and raids.

---

## Conclusion

Medb is an incredible immortal for new players, combining high damage, accessibility, and affordability. Her chaos-element advantage and cost-effective upgrades make her the best choice for F2P players looking to dominate in both PvE and PvP. Prioritize obtaining and upgrading Medb fragments from the Market, and you’ll have one of the strongest early-game immortals in Infinity Kingdom. Don’t miss out on this powerhouse!
