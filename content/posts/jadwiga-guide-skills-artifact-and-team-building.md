## Jadwiga at a Glance

Jadwiga is an offensive support. Twin Crowns strengthens your attackers rather than providing healing or protection, so the rest of the formation needs to supply enough durability to make use of her buffs.

## Twin Crowns Explained

Twin Crowns makes two target selections:

* The allied Immortal with the **highest Physical Attack**.
* The allied Immortal with the **highest Magical Attack**.

**Both recipients receive the Attack Speed increase and the damage dealt bonus** for six seconds. The effect cannot stack.

![](/uploads/screenshot-20261017-232713-976emk.png)

At maximum level, each selected ally gains **25% increased Attack Speed and 60% increased damage dealt**. Attack Speed increases the frequency of normal attacks, while the damage bonus strengthens damage dealt during the buff window.

The bonuses are not divided between the two targets. The physical attacker does not receive only Attack Speed while the magical attacker receives only damage amplification. **Both receive both effects.**

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261018-193251-rmbcjh.png)

### Targeting Confirmed Through Testing

The skill’s wording has caused understandable confusion. The phrase referring to the allied unit with the highest Physical Attack and Magical Attack could be read as selecting one Immortal based on their combined attack stats.

Repeated testing resolves that question: Twin Crowns separately selects the highest Physical Attack ally and the highest Magical Attack ally. In our tests, every cast applied the Attack Speed and damage buffs to both recipients.

The recording below shows the buffs active on two allies at the same time. Watch Alexander and Loki’s status rows: both display the timed Attack Speed and damage buffs together.

### Why This Matters for Team Building

Jadwiga can support two damage dealers simultaneously, which makes physical-and-magical combinations especially interesting. Alexander can receive the Physical Attack selection while a mage receives the Magical Attack selection, allowing both to benefit from the same cast.

However, dealing a particular damage type does not automatically mean an Immortal has the highest corresponding attack stat. Check the actual values in your formation, especially when using unusual builds or Immortals whose damage mechanics involve stat conversion.

If your highest Magical Attack belongs to a support with limited damage output, the second selection still occurs, but the formation may gain less from it than a team with a dedicated magical damage dealer.

## Energy Regeneration and Exclusive Artifact

Jadwiga’s displayed **83.0 Energy Regen** is solid for a support whose contribution depends on repeatedly casting her ultimate.

![](/uploads/screenshot-20261017-232728-wpydvn.png)

Her Exclusive Artifact extends Twin Crowns by three seconds, taking its duration from **six to nine seconds**.

That is a **50% increase in duration per application**, giving both selected allies more time to deal damage with the buffs active. It makes the artifact an important part of assessing a developed Jadwiga.

Longer duration does not automatically establish permanent coverage. Actual uptime still depends on her casting intervals, survival, control effects and energy disruption. Her Energy Regen stat is encouraging, but replays are needed to judge uptime in competitive battles.

## Potential Team Combinations

With the targeting confirmed, the most interesting starting points are formations that give both selections a valuable damage dealer to support. These remain team concepts for testing rather than established best builds.

### Alexander + Himiko or Loki

Combining Alexander with a magical carry such as Himiko or Loki is a natural direction to explore. Alexander can receive the Physical Attack selection, while the magical carry receives the Magical Attack selection.

A formation combining **Alexander, Himiko or Loki, Jadwiga and Zenobia** would retain healing while adding offensive amplification for both attackers, provided they hold the relevant highest attack stats.

This gives us a reason to revisit Alexander hybrids. Whether Jadwiga adds enough pressure to improve their competitive position still needs testing against comparable opponents.

### Alexander + Louis IX

Alexander + Louis is also worth exploring because Twin Crowns can support both of them, provided Alexander has the highest Physical Attack and you somehow are able to get Louis to have the highest Magical Attack. I am not sure if this it possible, but IF it is this could be a new hybrid build in the making!

That last condition matters. **Louis dealing magical damage does not automatically establish that his Magical Attack exceeds the supports in the formation.** Check his actual stats, try to boost his magical attack through gems, etc. and confirm the recipients in replays.

If the selections line up, this could allow two frontline attackers to benefit from Jadwiga while leaving the remaining backline slot available for another support.

Neither version is proven. The first tests whether healing can sustain the attackers through their buff windows; the second tests whether greater offensive pressure justifies giving up Zenobia’s healing.

### Louis + YSS

Louis + YSS with Jadwiga and Qin remains another formation to investigate. Louis can be the intended magical recipient, with the physical recipient determined by the actual attack stats of the team.

The relevant question for this team is how much value each recipient gains, and whether the formation performs better with Jadwiga than with an alternative support.

## Does Jadwiga Replace Zenobia or Qin?

**TBD.** We still lack enough comparative testing with a fully developed Jadwiga to establish a reliable verdict.

Keeping Zenobia preserves healing, while keeping Qin explores a different balance of offensive support. Jadwiga needs to contribute enough additional damage through Twin Crowns to justify what the replaced Immortal brings to the formation.

The targeting tests answer how her skill works. They do not yet establish which support pairing wins more battles. That requires comparisons across several fights against comparable opponents, looking at damage, survival and buff uptime.

## Tower of Knowledge Skills

Choose Jadwiga’s support skills around the needs of your overall build. She does not need a specific Tower of Knowledge combination to make Twin Crowns function.

Healing, protection, energy support and other utility can be distributed according to the formation and the skills already assigned to its other Immortals. The priority is making the whole march work together.

## How to Obtain Jadwiga

Jadwiga is obtained through **Elemental Bond**. Select her as your target and collect fragments through the card-matching event using Soulglass Crests. She requires **60 fragments to summon**, with further fragments needed for development.

![](/uploads/screenshot-20261017-232749-ao1roc.png)

For the event mechanics and spending approach, see our dedicated [Elemental Bond guide](https://ik-wiki.com/posts/should-you-spend-on-the-elemental-bond-event/).

Unlocking her and developing a competitive replacement are separate investment decisions. Her skill progression and Exclusive Artifact both matter when assessing the version you intend to use.

## Final Thoughts

Repeated testing confirms the important mechanic: **Twin Crowns selects the ally with the highest Physical Attack and the ally with the highest Magical Attack, giving both recipients both the Attack Speed and damage buffs.**

That makes Alexander with Himiko or Loki, and potentially Alexander with Louis, interesting combinations to explore. Solid energy regeneration and an artifact that extends the buff to nine seconds add to her appeal.

Her place in the meta remains TBD. The next step is establishing whether that offensive contribution outperforms the healing or utility of the support she replaces in a fully developed formation.
