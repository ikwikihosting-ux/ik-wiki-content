Immortal Portraits in *Infinity Kingdom* are a pivotal feature introduced in patch 2.6.3, allowing players to enhance their Immortals' attributes through the use of a resource called Stardust. Upgrading these portraits can significantly boost your Immortals' combat effectiveness, making strategic investment in this system crucial for competitive play.​

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-1024x539.png)

---

## Understanding Immortal Portraits

Each Immortal has a unique portrait that can be leveled up to increase their stats. These enhancements include boosts to physical/magical attack and defense, accuracy, dodge, and critical rates, depending on the Immortal's role (e.g., Tank, Mage, Support). **[Portait guide video here!](https://youtu.be/xAXWN3w0iU4)**

Notably, at certain milestone levels, Immortals receive percentage-based bonuses, which are particularly impactful:​

- **Level 10:** Grants a 5% boost to a specific attribute, varying by Immortal type.
- **Level 20:** Provides an additional 5% bonus, such as increased physical/magical damage or damage resistance.​

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-4-1024x317.png)

These percentage bonuses are substantial and can turn the tide in battles, especially in PvP scenarios. ​[Wiki & Tools | Infinity Kingdom](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/combat/immortal-portraits/portrait-level-table/?utm_source)

## Stardust Costs for Portrait Upgrades

Upgrading an Immortal's portrait requires Stardust, with the cost increasing at higher levels. The total Stardust needed to reach level 50 is approximately 6,341. Here's a breakdown of the costs:​

- **Levels 1–10:** 117 Stardust
- **Levels 11–20:** 466 Stardust
- **Levels 21–30:** 1,104 Stardust
- **Levels 31–40:** 1,684 Stardust
- **Levels 41–50:** 2,715 Stardust​

It's important to note that while the initial levels are relatively inexpensive, the cost escalates significantly at higher levels. ​

## 🎯 Strategic Upgrade Recommendations

Given the escalating costs, it's advisable to prioritize your Stardust investments:​

1. **Focus on Key Immortals:** Invest in your main damage dealers or crucial support Immortals first. Enhancing their portraits to level 20 ensures you capitalize on the significant percentage bonuses.​
2. **Avoid Spreading Stardust Thin:** It's more beneficial to fully upgrade a few key Immortals than to partially upgrade many. This approach maximizes the impact of the percentage bonuses.​
3. **Plan for Milestone Levels:** Aim to reach levels 10 and 20 on selected Immortals to unlock the powerful percentage-based enhancements.​

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-1-1024x385.png)

This focused strategy ensures that your most impactful Immortals are performing at their peak, providing a competitive edge in battles.

## Acquiring Stardust

Stardust can be obtained through various in-game activities and shops:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-2.png)

- **Arena Shop:** Purchase 60 Stardust daily for 15,000 Insignias.
- **Throne of the Supreme Shop:** Acquire 250 Stardust per season for 25,000 Glory Coins.
- **Marketplace:** Buy Stardust for 120 Gems or 200 Soul Crystals each.
- **Mysterious Pearl Shop:** Obtain 20 Stardust daily for 20 Pearls.
- **Deep Sea Merchant:** Offers weekly bundles of Stardust, with escalating gem costs.
- **Events:** Participate in events like the Lucky Spin: Stardust Edition for additional Stardust rewards.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/image-3-1024x572.png)

Engaging in these activities regularly will ensure a steady supply of Stardust for your portrait upgrades.​
