The meta in Season 2 starts to look a lot more like Conquest than what you may be used to from Season 1. With **Sparks of Galaxy**, **Theodora**, **Himiko**, and **Bjorn** become much more doable for a fraction of the cost of Theias in S1 (roughly $400–$500; [**see the Sparks guide for details**](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/events/sparks-of-galaxy/sparks-of-galaxy-updated-written-2-8-7/)). So this is a **spender build** in S2, but you can still **build toward it** even if you need to keep **Zenobia instead of Theodora** and **Charles instead of Bjorn** for a while. *(Tip: in Conquest the price drops further, and multiple seasons of Battle Pass reduce the price tags considerably.)*

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-145-1024x392.png)

This isn’t a guide on obtaining these immortals. See the separate event guides for that. The point is: in Season 2 this should be thought of as a **spender build** that can compete at the top of the ladders **without using any Chaos Immortals**.

### Core Concept

The core concept relies on **massive sustain and mitigation**, while **Himiko** takes advantage of her **high mage crit chance** and **massive AoE damage and control**. (Himiko can reach that consistent crit feel because she can run **mage-crit-chance artifacts**, not just flat %damage pieces.)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-144-1024x613.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-151-1024x615.png)

Later versions of this build will build into **Fu Fei**, but for now we are assuming you do not have access to max upgraded Chaos Immortals.

## Lineup & Flex

Regardless of where you’re at, the setup and skill plan are the same (Theo or Zeno, Bjorn or Charles).

**Spending levels are different for everyone**, so here’s how to think about it:

- **Best non-Chaos setup right now:** **Theodora / Bjorn / YSS / Himiko**. This is the top version for S2 if you can afford it.
- **Growing into it:** You can use **the exact same skill setup** on **Zenobia / YSS / Charles / Himiko** while you build resources. It plays the same way — just slightly less cleanse/mitigation ceiling until you upgrade to Theo and Bjorn.
- **Big spender path (already have Fu Fei in S2):** You can still run the **same overall structure**, but **put Fighting Master on Fu Fei** instead of Himiko. The idea is the same — protect your primary controller/nuker from getting shut down by enemy control.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-152.png)

## The Setup

### Healer — Zenobia / Theodora

**Zenobia / Theodora** is your healer, offering strong sustain. **Theodora** adds the huge advantage of **removing Damage over Time (DoT)** effects like **Blade Vortex**, which is very powerful in Season 2.

- Run **Assist** to protect **Himiko’s** troop count.
- Add **Vitality Touch** for extra healing and mitigation.
- Third slot options:  - **Purification** (small heal + dispel),
- **Resist** vs heavy mage enemies, or
- **Malice** (my preferred pick in S2) because consistent crit damage can still be dampened here. *(Late in Conquest, crit scales too high for Malice to keep up, but in S2 it still does work.)*

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-146-1024x572.png)

### Carry — Himiko

**Himiko** is the core immortal. She hits very hard and brings a strong **control effect** that most S2 teams aren’t hard-countering yet (only a few whales will be running **Fu Fei** this early). Even against those, her raw damage is high, especially with elemental advantage versus anything that isn’t **Shadow** or **Holy**.

- Use **Annihilation** and **Spell Disintegration** for damage.
- **Fighting Master** is **mandatory** in S2 to avoid getting shut down by enemy control (including mirror Himikos). This is very important.

*(If you’re a big spender running **Fu Fei** this season, shift **Fighting Master** from Himiko to **Fu Fei** instead, keeping the rest of the plan the same.)*

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-148-1024x625.png)

### Tank/Aura — Bjorn / Charles

This slot gives you **survivability and mitigation** so Himiko can safely do her job. **Bjorn** is the premium pick; **Charles** is fine while you’re building.

- **Energy Shield** for steady shielding and better sustain into spikes.
- **Weakness (Aura)** because in S2 you still want a bit more help reaching that **consistent crit rate** for Himiko. Later you won’t need to burn an aura slot for it, but in S2 you usually do.
- **Weakening Curse** (ideal) to hard-counter enemy physical damage dealers, especially front-loaded lines with **Alex** and similar. If you don’t have **Weakening Curse**, slot **Oaken Guard**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-149-1024x618.png)

### Tank/Buffer — Yi Sun-sin (YSS)

**YSS** is basically mandatory because his ultimate provides **30% less damage taken** and **30% more healing received** (with his unique setup), which perfectly matches a sustain/control plan.

- **Spring of Life** for more sustain in long fights.
- **Energy Suppression**, which is great in S2 since hyper-regen skills like **Corrosive** and **Energy Master** aren’t around yet.  - If you did not slot in **Resist**on your support, you can sometimes get greater value from Resist over Energy Suppression when faced with mage-carry enemies.
- **Jade Barrier** for extra shielding and **magical mitigation**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-150-1024x620.png)

### Side notes - If You Lack Expensive Skills

If you don’t have the top-end pieces yet, **grace effects** can hold the line:

- **Blessing of Defense + Oaken Guard** works fine if you aren’t running **Weakening Curse**.
- **Rage Blessing** is also useful if you have an empty slot and no Wisdom Well options yet.

## Dragon

Run **Earth Dragon** with the **Collapse specialization** talents for extra mitigation and longer mitigation windows. As you upgrade, **prioritize Sandstorm Breath** — j**[ust stick to the basic, proven dragon upgrade principles you normally follow](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/dragon/dragon-talents-patch-2-3/best-path-to-upgrading-dragon-talents-universal-dragon/)**.

## Conclusion

For Season 2, **Earth Himiko** is a **spender-friendly, non-Chaos top option** that wins by **outlasting** and **controlling** while **Himiko crit-bombs teams**. The best version right now is **Theodora / Bjorn / YSS / Himiko**, but you can **mirror the skills** on **Zenobia / YSS / Charles / Himiko** while you grow. And if you’re already splashing on **Fu Fei** in S2, run the same idea but **move Fighting Master to Fu Fei** instead of Himiko.
**Bottom line:** build the sustain shell, protect Himiko’s troop count, keep control immunity where it matters, and let her high **mage-crit** setup do the cleanup.
