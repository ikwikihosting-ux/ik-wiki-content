## Introduction

This event follows the same structural backbone as the Infinity Kingdom seasonal cycle, but introduces one of the most impactful side systems we’ve seen in a long time: **Candy Defenders**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-40-1024x573.png)

At a glance, it looks like a standard token → grail → shop loop. In practice, the event splits into two parallel economies:

- **Traditional progression** → Tokens → Grails → Chaos / Artifacts
- **Resource acceleration** → Candy → Massive speedups piles / philosopher stones / soul crystals

Understanding how these two layers interact is what determines whether you treat this as a normal event—or extract far more value than usual.

---

## Judgment Realm (Battle Pass)

### Structure & Rewards

- Free track: **30 Tokens of Light**
- Paid track:  - +120 Tokens of Light
- 6000 Gems
- **Hall of Divine Might** skin (or upgrade)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-41-1024x533.png)

### Skin Value: Hall of Divine Might

This is where the pass becomes unusually strong.

- **Troop Attack %** (top-tier combat stat)
- **Troop Load %** (minor, but harmless)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-42-1024x514.png)

### Why Troop Attack Matters

As accounts scale, troop count increases exponentially. At that point:

- **Troop stats scale better than immortal stats**
- Attack % directly improves all formations, not just skill output

If upgraded, this skin starts competing with top-tier combat skins, especially in late game where you have scaled into very high troop counts.

## Candy Defenders (New Core System)

### Mechanics

- Earn **Candy** from:  - Glorious Challenge
- Pinnacle Trial
- Grail Shop (via gnome farming)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-46.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-45-1024x350.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-44.png)

- Spend candy to attack a boss:  - Chance to deal damage
- Chance to receive rewards

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-47-1024x543.png)

### Reward Types

- Speedups (all types)
- Philosopher’s Stones
- Soul Crystals
- Resource boxes

**[Read standalone analysis of the expected rewards as well as how much candy it takes to kill a boss, incl. cost analysis in this guide.](https://ik-wiki.com/docs/events/spring-fiesta/original-festival/candy-boss-madness-2026-original-festival-edition/)**

---

### Player Perspective

This is easily the highest raw resource value I’ve seen from a seasonal event.

What stands out is the **consistency of returns**:

- You are not chasing jackpots
- You are accumulating **bulk progression materials**

From experience, this directly impacts:

- **Tower of Knowledge progression** (stones)
- **Immortal development** (crystals & fragments)
- **Account tempo** (speedups)

Even on late-game accounts, speedups alone justify participation. On developing accounts, this system accelerates progression in multiple bottlenecks simultaneously.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-49-1024x547.png)

## Grail Shop & Chaos Economy

### Grail Costs

- Tengu: **750 grails**
- Fu Fei: **450 grails**
- Athena / Wukong / Loki / Poseidon: **250 grails (daily stock)**
- Artifacts:  - 1050 (Tengu)
- 1000 (others)
- 600 (Fu Fei)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-50-1024x561.png)

Because there is no new immortal pressure, this event is pretty much all about steady chaos immortal progression for most players.

- Daily 250-grail purchases or the 450 Fu Fei purchase are often the best long-term value for mid-spenders, etc.
- Saving for 750-tier depends on your current roster gaps but diving into this tier means expensive immortals where you should expect to also spend significantly during other events (e.g. Rune Factory).

The decision becomes account-specific rather than event-driven.

## Glorious Challenge

### Key Mechanics

- Same structure as before
- Day 4 → Hard Mode
- Currency: **Flowers (Original Festival Coins)**
- Also generates **Candy**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-51-1024x564.png)

### Strategy Update

Daily shop stock still applies, so:

### Early Days (1–3):

- Spend enough to avoid capping
- Secure daily purchases

### Day 4+:

- Push hard mode
- Optimize farming efficiency

## Pinnacle Trial

### Rewards

- Immortal fragments
- Tokens of Light
- **Large amounts of Candy (from Day 3+)**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/image-52-1024x556.png)

## Conclusion Summary

This event looks standard—but plays very differently.

### Key Takeaways

- **Token value remains stable (~4.3 grails)**
- **Battle pass is stronger than usual** due to Divine Might skin
- **No new chaos immortal** shifts focus to efficiency, not urgency
- **Candy Defenders is the real centerpiece**
