With the 5th Anniversary **Eternal Grail** event, many players are asking a straightforward question:

> Is spending Tokens of Light for Chaos Immortal fragments more cost-efficient than building them through the traditional Chaos Roulette?

This guide compares the **fragment cost efficiency** of the Eternal Grail event against the long-established Chaos Roulette system.

We are **not including Tengu** in this comparison. If you are targeting Tengu specifically, Eternal Grails are the only source and comparison is irrelevant. This guide applies to all other Chaos Immortals.

The goal here is simple: calculate the real expected cost per fragment in Eternal Grail, then compare it to the established Chaos Roulette benchmark.

## Part 2 – Cost to Fully Build a Chaos Immortal via Eternal Grail

Each 80 Chaos Immortal fragments costs **450 Grails**.

That means: 1 fragment = 5.625 Grails

---

## Unlocking Immortal Stars + Artifact Slot (1640 Fragments)

You need 1640 immortal fragments to upgrade a chaos immortal from nothing to 8 stars plus artifact slot unlock. Let's have a look at the pricing given the above calculations.

### Grails Required

```
1640 × 5.625 = 9,225 Grails
```

### Tokens Required

```
9,225 ÷ 4.425 ≈ 2,084 Tokens
```

### Cost

```
2,084 × $1.37 ≈ $2,854
```

### Eternal Grail Cost for 1640 Fragments ≈ $2,850

---

## Talents to Level 8 (Additional 2190 Fragments)

You need 2190 immortal fragments to upgrade a chaos immortal from 8 stars plus artifact slot unlock **to achieve level 8 immortal talent**. Let's have a look at the pricing given the above calculations.

### Grails Required

```
2190 × 5.625 = 12,318.75 Grails
```

### Tokens Required

```
12,318.75 ÷ 4.425 ≈ 2,783 Tokens
```

### Cost

```
2,783 × $1.37 ≈ $3,812
```

### Eternal Grail Cost for 2190 Fragments ≈ $3,810

## Chaos Roulette Benchmark

From established Chaos Roulette math:

## Upgrading stars, artifact and talents to level 8 on a chaos immortal

- You need **1640 fragments to unlock all stars and artifact slot** on a Chaos immortal.  - 1640 ÷ 131.1 ≈ **12.5 weeks**. In practice, players should plan for **13 weeks**.
- Assuming efficient GTarcade bundles at **$0.50 per Eye**: 209.9 Eyes × $0.50 ≈ **$105 per week**. **Total cost:** ≈ **$1,365**
- You need another **2190 immortal fragments to upgrade immortal talent to level 8** (all nodes level 1 for quickest progression to level 8).  - 2190 ÷ 131.1 ≈ **16,7 weeks**. In practice, players should plan for **17 weeks**.
- Assuming efficient GTarcade bundles at **$0.50 per Eye**: 209.9 Eyes × $0.50 ≈ **$105 per week** **for 17 weeks.** **Total cost:** ≈ **$1,784**

Total cost to get a Chaos from nothing to level 8 talents: **$3150**.

## Conclusion Summary

- Eternal Grail averages **4.425 Grails per token**.
- Realistic average cost per token ≈ **$1.37**.
- Full Chaos Immortal (stars + talent 8) costs **~$6,660** via Grails.
- The same progression costs **~$3,150** via Chaos Roulette.
- Eternal Grail is **more than 2× as expensive** for existing Chaos Immortals.

For practical players planning long-term investment, Chaos Roulette remains the significantly more cost-efficient method for building Chaos Immortals outside of Tengu.
