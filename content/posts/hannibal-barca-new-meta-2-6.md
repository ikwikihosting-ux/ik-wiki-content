***Hannibal Barca: The Unstoppable Force in Infinity Kingdom Battle Chess***
*Mastering the New Meta with Relentless Growth and Devastating Cleave*

In the ever-evolving world of Infinity Kingdom Battle Chess, a strategic marvel has taken center stage: Hannibal Barca. This immortal commander has become a force to be reckoned with in the new meta, where the Call of Thunder reigns supreme, decimating hordes of smaller units. Hannibal's unique abilities allow him to thrive in this chaotic battlefield, growing stronger with every fallen foe and transforming into an unstoppable juggernaut.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/image-34-1024x340.png)

### Hannibal Barca's Description:

- **HP:** 450
- **Damage:** 40
- **Attack Speed:** 0.5
- **Movement:** 0.8
- **Range:** 0.5 (melee)
- **Ability:** "Cause 35 damage to an additional enemy when applying normal attack (cleave). Every time an unit dies on the chess board, Hannibal will gain HP and Damage increase buff that can be stacked for 5 times."

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/image-32.png)

Hannibal Barca's prowess on the chessboard is nothing short of extraordinary. His base stats alone make him a formidable presence with 450 HP, 40 damage, and a respectable attack speed of 0.5. However, it's his unique ability that truly sets him apart from the rest.

Hannibal's signature move is his devastating cleave attack. With each normal attack, he inflicts 35 additional damage to an adjacent enemy, effectively making him a formidable threat to multiple adversaries at once. This cleave ability allows Hannibal to efficiently dispatch smaller units, which are prevalent in the current meta dominated by the Call of Thunder.

What truly makes Hannibal Barca exceptional, though, is his uncanny ability to grow stronger as the battle rages on. Every time a unit falls on the chessboard, Hannibal gains an HP and damage increase buff. This buff can be stacked up to five times, transforming him into an unstoppable behemoth with each fallen opponent.

In the new meta, where Call of Thunder and other area-of-effect cards are frequently deployed to eliminate swarms of smaller units, Hannibal thrives. His ability to capitalize on the constant ebb and flow of battle allows him to ascend quickly, reaching a point where he becomes virtually untouchable.

Hannibal Barca's versatility, with both a powerful cleave attack and an innate ability to grow stronger with every fallen foe, makes him a cornerstone of success in the modern Infinity Kingdom Battle Chess meta. As players adapt to the ever-shifting dynamics of the battlefield, Hannibal stands as a testament to the enduring appeal and innovation within the game.

In the hands of a skilled commander, Hannibal Barca is not just a piece on the chessboard; he's the embodiment of relentless growth and the promise of ultimate victory in the face of any challenge.
