### WINNERS OF ROUND 12 CROSS-SERVER SPIDER COMPETITION

Commanders, the arachnid battlegrounds have witnessed yet another round of fierce competition, and we're thrilled to unveil the triumphant victors of the 12th Cross-Server Spider Competition! For this new round we are finishing up the first new format with five different brackets – here are the rankings for all brackets – congratulations to all winners and everyone participating.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image.png)

*Please note that players are only allowed one reward per event, to limit the same players getting the top rewards from multiple brackets. If you e.g. came in no. 2 in one bracket and no. 4 in another, you get your no. 2 reward and is removed from the other bracket so other players can get the rewards.*
