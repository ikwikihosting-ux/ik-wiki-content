## What Is the Lightning Frame?

This newly released frame is a **visually sleek, low-profile border** that pulses subtly with a **clean lightning effect**. Unlike some previous frames that leaned heavily into ornamental and bulky effects, this one is understated—**which is exactly why it looks so good**.

It doesn’t scream for attention with oversized art or gaudy colors. Instead, it gives off a high-class, sharp aesthetic—a kind of “you know if you know” vibe that veteran players tend to appreciate.

## How to Get the Frame

Now here’s the catch: **this frame is not available in-game by default**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-17.png)

### Requirements:

- You must have spent **at least $2,000 USD total** on Infinity Kingdom (accumulated over time—not all at once).
- This is **not automatically added** to your account.
- If you are below 2K, sorry!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-19.png)

- If you are above 2K, congratulations they will send!

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-20.png)

### How to Claim It:

1. **Join the Official Infinity Kingdom Discord.**
2. Navigate to the **VIP Support** or **Spending Support** channel.
3. Open a support ticket and provide your player ID and server info.
4. Request the frame and wait for confirmation from the VIP support team.

Once approved, the frame should be **manually added to your cosmetics**—usually within a few hours.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/image-18-1024x471.png)

> 💡 *Pro Tip:* If you’re unsure whether you meet the spend threshold, you can still open a ticket and ask. The support team can confirm your total spending amount discreetly.
