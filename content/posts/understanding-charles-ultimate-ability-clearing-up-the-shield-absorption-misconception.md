### Introduction

Charles’ ultimate ability has been one of the more frequently misunderstood skills in *Infinity Kingdom*, largely due to how its second clause is worded. On the surface, the description seems straightforward:

> *“Creates a shield for all units that absorbs damage (400%). Your Shield absorption will increase by additional 150% for 6 seconds.”*

The first part is streight forward: a 400% shield applied to all allied units. The confusion starts with the second sentence. It reads like a general amplification effect, leading many to assume that *any* shield generated by Charles during those 6 seconds becomes stronger, right?

That assumption, however, does not match how the mechanic turns out to actually work. Let's dive in!

---

### What the Ability Actually Does

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-27.png)

Let’s break the skill into its real functional components:

#### Base Effect

- Charles casts his ultimate.
- All allied units (including himself) receive a shield equal to **400% of his relevant scaling stat** (Aptitude-based).

#### Additional Absorption Clause

- The “+150% shield absorption for 6 seconds”:  - **Applies only to Charles himself**
- **Applies only to the shield generated by his ultimate**
- **Does not affect any other shield sources**

In practical terms:

- Allies each receive a **400% shield**
- Charles receives:  - A **400% base shield**
- Plus an additional **150% of that base value**, resulting in a **total of 600% shield**

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-29-1012x1024.png)

This is super important to understand, because scaling shield values - if that was possible - would open up to huge synergy builds with multiple shielding effects on Charles like Energy Shield, Jade Barrier, Sturdy Barrier, etc.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-30.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-31.png)

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-32.png)

That however is **NOT**possible, contrary to what I see many thinking and building for both in theoretical forums and in-game.

---

### How the 6-Second Window Works

The timing component is equally important and often overlooked.

- The **extra 200% shield value (600% total vs. 400%) is temporary**
- It lasts for **6 seconds only**
- If that additional portion is not fully consumed within that time, **it expires**

So functionally:

- Charles has a **stronger initial shield buffer**
- But only part of it is guaranteed to remain beyond 6 seconds

This makes the effect more of a niche **front-loaded survivability boost**, not a sustained **scaling mechanic**.

---

### What It Does Not Do

This is where most misconceptions come from.

The 150% increase **does NOT**:

- Amplify **Energy Shield** (Tower of Knowledge)
- Enhance **Sturdy Barrier** (Talent specialization)
- Boost shields from other immortals

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-28-1024x836.png)

Even if Charles triggers another shield effect during that period, it behaves completely normally. This has been verified and confirmed via communication with the development team (through IKVIP discussions).

---

### Conclusion Summary

Charles’ ultimate is often misread, but its real function is quite precise:

- **All allies receive a 400% shield**
- **Charles alone receives a 600% shield (400% + 150%)**
- The extra portion:  - **Lasts 6 seconds**
- **Expires if unused**
- **No other shield effects are amplified**

Understanding this properly helps avoid building around nonexistent synergies and instead positions Charles where he performs best: as a frontline stabilizer with strong early durability.

Once you view the skill through that lens, his role in defensive compositions becomes much clearer—and much more reliable.
