## Understanding Castle Skin Combat Attributes

Before comparing individual skins, it's important to understand why some combat attributes are considerably more valuable than others.

Infinity Kingdom has several different types of combat modifiers.

Some affect the personal attributes of your Immortals, while others affect the troops commanded by those Immortals.

This distinction becomes especially important in the late game.

### Why Troop Attributes Are So Powerful

Every Immortal commands thousands of soldiers, and both the Immortal's attributes and those soldiers' attributes contribute to combat performance.

As accounts develop, their troop counts increase dramatically. At the same time, higher troop tiers provide significantly stronger base attributes.

For example, a T9 Bowman has 225 base Attack.

If an Immortal commands 18,000 Bowmen, those troops represent an aggregate base Attack value of 4,050,000.

A 5% Troop Attack modifier adds the equivalent of 202,500 Attack to that aggregate base value, before accounting for other bonuses and the actual combat damage calculation.

At 15% Troop Attack, the corresponding increase becomes 607,500.

And that's just one troop stack.

The same type of modifier can improve the soldiers commanded by all four Immortals in your march.

This is why Troop Attack becomes exceptionally attractive as players develop larger armies with stronger troop tiers.

However, these aggregate values aren't direct damage numbers. Existing attributes, other bonuses, damage formulas, and enemy defenses all affect the final result.

Also, the number of troops doesn't make the percentage bonus itself larger. Rather, the same percentage applies to a larger underlying attribute pool.

### Our Combat Attribute Ranking

When evaluating castle skins for general late-game combat, I would usually prioritize the main attributes in the following order:

**1. Troop Attack**

The strongest general-purpose offensive attribute. It improves your soldiers' damage potential, helps finish battles faster, and can indirectly improve survivability by reducing how long your army remains exposed to enemy damage.

**2. Troop Defense**

An extremely powerful defensive modifier. Particularly valuable for frontline-heavy formations, sustained combat, and compositions that benefit from surviving long enough to activate additional skills.

**3. Troop HP**

Still useful, but generally the weakest of the three main troop attributes. Additional HP improves survivability without directly increasing damage output or reducing incoming damage per hit.

**4. Immortal Attributes**

Immortal Physical Attack, Magical Attack, and Defense can all be useful, but they affect a different part of the combat calculation and generally offer less broad scaling than troop-based attributes in highly developed marches.

This is a practical ranking rather than an absolute rule. Certain Immortal builds, defensive mechanics, and damage formulas can change the relative value of individual attributes.

Nevertheless, Troop Attack and Troop Defense are the two modifiers I would prioritize most heavily when choosing a combat castle skin.

## S+ Tier – Abyssal Temple: The Best Overall Combat Skin

Abyssal Temple remains my number-one recommendation for players who want the strongest all-around PvP castle skin.

Its main advantage is straightforward: it provides one of the best combat modifiers in Infinity Kingdom, alongside an additional benefit that makes prolonged warfare more sustainable.

### Abyssal Temple Upgrade Bonuses

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-115831-23tykq.png)

Each upgrade increases Troop Attack by one percentage point, eventually reaching +15%.

Meanwhile, Death Rate reduction improves from −3% to −6%, increasing by 0.3 percentage points per upgrade.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120341-b97m5c.png)

These bonuses make Abyssal Temple useful in almost every offensive combat situation.

The Troop Attack modifier improves the offensive capabilities of your entire march, regardless of which element or Immortal composition you're using.

This means it remains relevant whether you're fighting with Earth, Fire, Lightning, Holy, Shadow, or another formation.

### Why Abyssal Temple Takes First Place

Abyssal Temple doesn't provide more Troop Attack than Throne of Blood or Hall of Divine Might when all three are equally upgraded.

All three reach the same maximum of +15%.

The difference is the secondary attribute.

Abyssal Temple reduces Soldier Death Rate, while the other two skins provide development or gathering-related bonuses.

Death Rate reduction doesn't directly make your army deal more damage or improve its immediate defensive attributes.

However, it reduces permanent troop losses during battles where the modifier applies.

During prolonged PvP conflicts, where players repeatedly attack, defend, heal, and rebuild their armies, this becomes extremely valuable.

Even a relatively small reduction in permanent troop deaths can save substantial training resources and speedups across an entire KvK season.

And because the skin also provides maximum Troop Attack, you don't have to sacrifice your strongest offensive modifier to obtain that benefit.

This combination is what makes Abyssal Temple our S+ choice.

**Verdict: S+ Tier**

The best overall combat skin for competitive players. It matches the highest available Troop Attack bonus while also improving troop conservation during sustained warfare.

If I could choose only one fully upgraded skin for ordinary PvP, this would be it.

## S Tier – Throne of Blood: Troop Attack and Technology Progression

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-115844-b3th47.png)

Throne of Blood was introduced during the May 2026 Hunting Festival and immediately became one of the strongest offensive castle skins available.

It provides the same Troop Attack progression as Abyssal Temple, but replaces Death Rate reduction with Technology Upgrade Speed.

### Throne of Blood Upgrade Bonuses

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120355-9l4o9r.png)

The Troop Attack bonus increases by one percentage point per upgrade.

Technology Upgrade Speed increases more gradually, gaining 0.5 percentage points per upgrade until it reaches +10%.

### Why Throne of Blood Is So Strong

For direct offensive combat, Throne of Blood is effectively identical to Abyssal Temple at the same upgrade level.

Both provide the same Troop Attack modifier.

That makes Throne of Blood an excellent choice for players who want powerful combat attributes but don't necessarily need Death Rate reduction.

Its secondary bonus is also one of the better development attributes available on castle skins.

Technology is a major progression bottleneck in Infinity Kingdom, especially during Conquest and Legendary Season.

Players frequently complete their most important buildings long before finishing the enormous research requirements associated with advanced account development.

Additional Technology Speed therefore remains useful for a very long time.

Of course, this attribute doesn't provide an immediate advantage during battle.

But by allowing you to progress research more quickly, it can contribute to stronger combat capabilities over time.

### Abyssal Temple vs. Throne of Blood

This is one of the easiest comparisons in the guide.

At equal upgrade levels:

* Both provide identical Troop Attack.
* Abyssal Temple provides fewer permanent soldier deaths.
* Throne of Blood provides faster technology research.

For players fighting constantly in KvK, Abyssal Temple is my preferred option.

For accounts still developing their technology, Throne of Blood may actually provide greater overall account value.

It deserves to stand alongside Abyssal Temple for raw offensive strength, with Abyssal retaining the overall PvP advantage through its Death Rate reduction.

## S Tier – Hall of Divine Might: Another Top Troop Attack Skin

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-115941-c1rr1n.png)

Hall of Divine Might is another extremely powerful offensive castle skin.

Like Abyssal Temple and Throne of Blood, it provides up to +15% Troop Attack.

Its secondary bonus increases Troop Load rather than reducing deaths or improving research.

### Hall of Divine Might Upgrade Bonuses

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120257-wnenvx.png)

Again, Troop Attack increases by one percentage point per upgrade, while the secondary attribute increases by 0.5 percentage points.

### How Good Is Hall of Divine Might?

From a pure offensive perspective, Hall of Divine Might is just as strong as Abyssal Temple and Throne of Blood when upgraded to the same level.

This is the important distinction.

**You are not sacrificing any raw Troop Attack simply because you're using Hall of Divine Might instead of Abyssal Temple.**

The difference is what happens outside the direct damage calculation.

Troop Load determines how much resources your soldiers can transport.

This can be useful for resource collection and other logistical activities, but it doesn't improve damage output, durability, or combat survivability.

Consequently, Hall of Divine Might offers less overall PvP utility than Abyssal Temple.

It also lacks the development advantages provided by Throne of Blood.

However, that doesn't make it a weaker offensive combat skin.

For a player who already owns an upgraded Hall of Divine Might, there's little reason to spend heavily chasing an equally upgraded Throne of Blood purely for combat damage. Both provide the same Troop Attack.

**Verdict: S Tier**

One of the strongest offensive castle skins in Infinity Kingdom.

Its secondary bonus is less attractive than those of Abyssal Temple and Throne of Blood, but its maximum Troop Attack makes it an excellent choice for direct combat.

## A Tier – Lunar Grace Pavilion: The Defensive Powerhouse

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120001-blzkma.png)

Lunar Grace Pavilion is particularly interesting because it offers something different from the three offensive skins discussed above.

Instead of Troop Attack, it provides Troop Defense.

And not just a small amount.

At maximum level, Lunar Grace Pavilion provides **+15% Troop Defense**, making it one of the strongest defensive castle skins available.

### Lunar Grace Pavilion Upgrade Bonuses

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120421-83472l.png)

Troop Defense follows the same progression as Troop Attack on the premium offensive skins, increasing by one percentage point per upgrade.

Resource Gathering Speed improves by 0.5 percentage points per upgrade.

### Why Troop Defense Is So Valuable

Troop Defense is one of the most important combat attributes available in Infinity Kingdom.

Like Troop Attack, it affects your soldiers rather than only your Immortals.

This makes it particularly attractive at higher troop tiers, where the underlying Defense attributes of individual soldiers are already substantial.

For example, T9 Shieldmen have 213 base Defense.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120026-tg79xx.png)

A 15% Troop Defense modifier provides the equivalent of an additional 31.95 base Defense per Shieldman, before accounting for other bonuses and the full combat calculation.

Across thousands of soldiers, that represents a meaningful defensive improvement.

More importantly, additional Defense can help your formation survive long enough to activate more skills and maintain its damage output.

For certain builds, this can be extremely valuable.

### When Lunar Grace Can Outperform Troop Attack

Although I generally favor Troop Attack over Troop Defense, there are situations where Lunar Grace Pavilion becomes especially attractive.

The most obvious is a defensive formation designed to survive rather than defeat its opponent as quickly as possible.

Certain compositions rely heavily on healing, shields, damage reduction, Energy manipulation, and other defensive mechanics.

Their objective may be to survive until a powerful late-battle effect activates or simply to remain alive until the battle timer expires.

For these formations, additional Troop Defense can be exceptionally useful.

The same applies to defensive ramp-up builds that grow stronger as the battle continues.

If your formation needs to survive a prolonged opening period before becoming effective, increasing Troop Defense might contribute more to its success than adding another offensive modifier.

That is why Lunar Grace Pavilion deserves special attention for timeout-oriented and defensive builds.

### Why It Ranks Below the S-Tier Skins

For most ordinary PvP compositions, I still prefer Troop Attack.

Additional Attack helps defeat enemies more quickly, potentially reducing incoming damage by shortening the battle.

Troop Defense improves survivability but doesn't directly increase your damage output.

And while Lunar Grace Pavilion's Gathering Speed bonus is useful, it generally offers less long-term progression value than Throne of Blood's Technology Speed.

**Verdict: A Tier**

An exceptionally strong defensive skin and potentially an S-tier choice for specific timeout, tank, and defensive scaling formations.

For general-purpose PvP, however, I would prioritize the Troop Attack skins first.

## B Tier – Sun Chariot: Powerful Mobility, Limited Raw Combat Strength

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120056-2pgm2x.png)

Sun Chariot is probably the most interesting specialized castle skin on this list.

Unlike the others, its main attraction isn't increasing Troop Attack or Defense.

Instead, it provides substantial Marching Speed alongside bonuses to Immortal Physical and Magical Defense.

### Sun Chariot Upgrade Bonuses

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120434-w4ag2p.png)

All three attributes increase by one percentage point per upgrade.

At maximum level, +15% Marching Speed is a significant mobility bonus.

However, the distinction between Immortal Defense and Troop Defense is extremely important.

Sun Chariot improves the personal defensive attributes of your Immortals. It doesn't provide the same broad Troop Defense modifier as Lunar Grace Pavilion.

That makes it considerably less attractive for maximizing raw defensive combat strength.

### Why Marching Speed Can Be Extremely Useful

While Marching Speed doesn't directly improve your damage or durability once battle begins, it can be incredibly valuable during open-world PvP.

Moving faster creates opportunities that slower opponents cannot always respond to.

For example, you may be able to attack an enemy march and recall before their reinforcement arrives.

You can also intercept enemy movements, reinforce objectives more quickly, and reposition between engagements with greater flexibility.

This is particularly useful when you're fighting with your main march and need to balance offensive pressure with defensive responsibilities.

A faster march can attack an enemy and return to reinforce an important position sooner than a slower one.

During KvK, Norheim PvP, and other map-based activities, that kind of mobility can be a meaningful tactical advantage.

### When Sun Chariot Is a Bad Choice

The problem is that Marching Speed only matters when movement is relevant.

In Arena, or in other combat situations where both armies simply fight until one is defeated, the speed bonus does virtually nothing.

The same applies to many rallies and direct battles where the outcome is determined primarily by combat strength rather than positioning.

In those situations, Sun Chariot's Immortal Defense bonuses are significantly less attractive than Troop Attack or Troop Defense.

That means you could be sacrificing considerable combat strength in exchange for mobility you aren't actually using.

**Verdict: B Tier**

A specialized utility skin.

When you need speed, Sun Chariot can be excellent. When you don't need speed, it's generally a poor alternative to the premium Troop Attack and Defense skins.

I would obtain it for tactical flexibility, but I wouldn't prioritize expensive upgrades over improving Abyssal Temple, Throne of Blood, Hall of Divine Might, or Lunar Grace Pavilion.

## What About the Older Combat Skins?

Before these powerful upgradeable skins were introduced, most of the best combat-oriented options offered relatively small bonuses to Troop HP, Immortal attributes, Marching Speed, or other utility effects.

Several remain useful, particularly for players who haven't unlocked newer premium skins.

### Skyriver Palace

Skyriver Palace provides upgradeable Immortal Physical Attack and Magical Attack bonuses, alongside Death Rate reduction.

At maximum level, its offensive modifiers reach +15%.

This makes it useful for improving Immortal damage, but generally less attractive than the same percentage of Troop Attack in highly developed late-game marches.

It remains a reasonable alternative when a player doesn't own one of the stronger troop-based offensive skins.

### Mt. Paradise

Mt. Paradise provides +2% Troop HP and +5% Marching Speed.

It's a useful alternative to Sun Chariot for players who want additional mobility without investing in an expensive premium skin.

The Troop HP bonus is modest, but the ability to move more quickly can still provide tactical advantages.

### Goddess of Victory

Goddess of Victory provides +2% Troop HP and −3% Death Rate.

Its main value is reducing permanent troop losses during repeated combat.

It doesn't compete with a developed Abyssal Temple in raw fighting power, but can remain useful for troop conservation when stronger alternatives aren't available.

### City of Glory and Corridor of Ghostflame

These skins provide Troop HP alongside bonuses to Honor Gain, making them useful when farming Honor and progressing through Nobility ranks.

However, Honor Gain doesn't directly improve the strength of your army during a battle.

They're better understood as progression and farming skins rather than competitive combat skins.

### Colosseum

Colosseum provides Marching Speed and Honor Gain.

It can be useful for mobile Honor farming, but offers no direct Troop Attack, Defense, or HP bonus.

For competitive combat, it falls behind the more specialized options.

You might use Abyssal Temple for competitive PvP, Throne of Blood while developing research, and Sun Chariot for situations where movement speed allows you to outmaneuver an opponent.

The most important thing is understanding why you're switching.

## Should You Upgrade One Skin or Unlock Several?

Upgradeable castle skins have become some of the most powerful premium bonuses available in Infinity Kingdom.

However, developing them can be extremely expensive.

The difference between the starting +5% Troop Attack and the maximum +15% is substantial.

But obtaining the additional skin fragments required for those upgrades can involve major spending or extensive participation in limited-time events.

This creates an important decision for players.

Should you focus on maximizing one powerful skin, or unlock several different skins for flexibility?

### For Low and Moderate Spenders

I would generally prioritize obtaining one good Troop Attack skin.

Even at its starting level, +5% Troop Attack is an excellent bonus.

If you already have Throne of Blood or Hall of Divine Might, there's little reason to purchase a level 1 Abyssal Temple purely because of its S+ ranking.

The offensive bonuses are identical at the same upgrade level.

Instead, evaluate which skin is easiest and cheapest for you to develop further.

You should also consider whether your resources would provide greater value through Immortal progression, artifacts, Dragon upgrades, or Tower of Knowledge skills.

### For Competitive Spenders

Players with highly developed accounts should consider prioritizing one strong Troop Attack skin toward its maximum level.

Abyssal Temple is the most attractive overall choice due to its combination of combat strength and Death Rate reduction.

However, if you've already developed Throne of Blood or Hall of Divine Might significantly further, switching to an underdeveloped Abyssal Temple may be a downgrade in immediate offensive power.

A fully upgraded Hall of Divine Might provides considerably more Troop Attack than a newly unlocked Abyssal Temple.

After developing a strong offensive skin, Lunar Grace Pavilion becomes a particularly interesting second investment for players who use defensive formations.

Sun Chariot is worth considering for players who frequently engage in tactical open-world PvP, although upgrading it should generally remain a lower priority.

### Compare Your Actual Bonuses, Not Just the Tier List

Our rankings assume equal development levels.

In practice, players may own skins at very different upgrade stages.

For example:

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261104-120509-mzezqz.png)

In this situation, Hall of Divine Might provides the strongest direct offensive modifier, even though Abyssal Temple is ranked higher overall.

The secondary bonuses don't change that basic difference in Troop Attack.

This is why you should always evaluate the actual bonuses displayed on your skins before choosing which one to apply.

## Final Thoughts – The Best Castle Skins in 2026

Infinity Kingdom's castle skin system has changed considerably over the years.

The older generation of skins offered modest bonuses to Troop HP, Immortal attributes, Marching Speed, and resource efficiency.

Today, several premium skins can reach +15% Troop Attack or Troop Defense, making them significantly more impactful in competitive combat.

Our ranking places **Abyssal Temple in S+ Tier**, thanks to its combination of maximum Troop Attack and reduced permanent troop losses.

**Throne of Blood and Hall of Divine Might belong in S Tier**, providing exactly the same Troop Attack scaling with different secondary bonuses.

**Lunar Grace Pavilion earns A Tier** because Troop Defense is an exceptionally powerful attribute, particularly for defensive, timeout-oriented, and scaling formations.

Finally, **Sun Chariot belongs in B Tier**, not because Marching Speed is useless, but because it only becomes valuable when you can take advantage of movement and positioning.

For most players looking to maximize raw combat performance, the decision is straightforward.

Prioritize Troop Attack, consider Troop Defense for specialized compositions, and treat Marching Speed as a tactical tool rather than a replacement for combat strength.

And most importantly, don't assume that a skin is automatically stronger simply because it belongs to a higher tier in our rankings.

A +15% Troop Attack skin is stronger offensively than a +5% Troop Attack skin, regardless of its secondary bonus.

**My recommendation: Develop one powerful Troop Attack skin as your main combat option, preferably Abyssal Temple for sustained PvP, and use specialized alternatives only when their bonuses directly support your strategy.**
