### Everything You Need to Know About the New Outfit System

The Immortal Outfit system introduces an entirely new layer of progression to Infinity Kingdom. While outfits may initially look like cosmetic skins, they are actually permanent upgrades that provide additional stats, unlock exclusive passive effects, and replace an Immortal's Ultimate Skill.

At launch, only Alexander and Athena have outfits available, but the system has clearly been designed with future expansions in mind. As more outfits are released, they are likely to become an important part of long-term account progression alongside Artifacts, Specializations, and Mythic Equipment.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-1-1024x547.png)

---

## How the Outfit System Works

Every outfit is exclusive to a single Immortal. Once unlocked, it becomes permanently attached to that hero and cannot be transferred to another Immortal.

Outfits are unlocked by collecting Outfit Fragments during Festival Events. After obtaining enough fragments, the outfit becomes permanently available and remains unlocked even if the Immortal is Rebirthed. In that sense, the system works much like Mythic Equipment. Rather than being an item you freely equip or remove, an outfit becomes a permanent upgrade for that specific Immortal.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-3.png)

Unlocking an outfit also activates that Immortal's Outfit Insignia, adding another layer to the progression system.

---

## Upgrading Outfits

Unlocking an outfit is only the first step.

Outfits can also be upgraded using additional Outfit Fragments, increasing their effectiveness over time. Although Lilith has not published the complete upgrade requirements, the interface strongly suggests a progression system similar to Immortal Star upgrades which improves ultimate as well as immortal attribute stats.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/image-4.png)

Players should therefore expect outfits to be a long-term investment rather than something that can be completed immediately.

---

## More Than Cosmetic Skins

The Outfit system fundamentally changes how certain Immortals perform in battle.

Each outfit currently provides three major benefits:

- Permanent attribute increases.
- Exclusive passive effects.
- A completely different Ultimate Skill.

Together, these upgrades make outfits a gameplay feature rather than a cosmetic one.

### Permanent Stats

Every outfit grants additional attributes while equipped. Although the exact values depend on the outfit's upgrade level, these bonuses permanently increase the Immortal's combat performance and contribute directly to overall account power.

### Exclusive Passive Effects

Each outfit also introduces its own passive ability. Unlike normal skills, these passives are unique to the outfit and can alter how an Immortal functions during combat. Alexander's outfit, for example, adds a new interaction with his Unique Artifact, while Athena's outfit focuses on increasing Energy Recovery to improve Ultimate Skill uptime. Rather than simply increasing existing numbers, these effects can noticeably change an Immortal's strengths and playstyle.

### Alternate Ultimate Skills

The defining feature of the Outfit system is the replacement Ultimate Skill. Changing an outfit doesn't only change an Immortal's appearance—it replaces their Ultimate with a different version designed specifically for that outfit. Some changes are relatively modest, while others have the potential to significantly alter an Immortal's role or preferred team composition. In practice, outfits function almost like alternate versions of existing Immortals rather than simple skins.

---

## Alexander's Summer Passion Outfit

Alexander was already widely considered one of the strongest Immortals in Infinity Kingdom before the Outfit system arrived, making him a surprising choice for one of the first releases.

His Summer Passion Outfit grants additional stats, replaces his Ultimate Skill, and introduces a passive effect that interacts directly with his Unique Artifact. The mechanic drawing the most attention is the removal of Alexander's damage and attack speed caps while his Unique Artifact is equipped.

Exactly how this interaction functions still requires practical testing. Early descriptions suggest it could allow Alexander to retain the higher damage values from his original Ultimate while benefiting from the outfit's additional effects, but this has not yet been fully confirmed. If that interpretation proves correct, the outfit could represent one of the largest individual power increases currently available for any Immortal. Until more testing is completed, however, it's best to treat this as a strong possibility rather than an established mechanic.

---

## Athena's Summer Passion Outfit

Athena's outfit takes a different approach. Instead of focusing purely on survivability, it appears to improve her consistency by significantly increasing Energy Recovery Speed. Faster Energy generation allows Athena to cast her Ultimate more frequently, improving both her reliability and overall impact during extended battles. To balance this, part of her original damage reduction has been reduced.

Based on the available information, the trade-off appears reasonable. Athena sacrifices a portion of her durability in exchange for considerably faster Ultimate rotations, which could prove valuable in longer encounters where repeated skill usage has a greater impact than raw mitigation. Whether this ultimately becomes a net improvement will depend on practical testing, but the early outlook is encouraging.

---

## Are Outfits Worth Investing In?

Based on everything currently available, the answer is yes.

Even before considering the alternate Ultimate Skills, outfits provide permanent stat increases, unique passive abilities, and lasting account progression that cannot be lost through Rebirth. The system feels much closer to Artifacts or Specializations than traditional cosmetic skins. The biggest unanswered question is accessibility. Outfit Fragments are currently obtained through Festival Events, but it remains unclear how quickly free-to-play players will be able to unlock and upgrade future outfits compared to players who spend heavily during those events. That question will become much more important as additional outfits are released.

---

## Final Thoughts

The Immortal Outfit system is one of the most significant progression features added to Infinity Kingdom in recent updates. Rather than introducing optional cosmetics, Lilith has created a system that directly affects combat through additional stats, exclusive passive abilities, and alternate Ultimate Skills. At launch, only Alexander and Athena have received outfits, but the framework is clearly intended to expand across many more Immortals over time.

The long-term success of the system will ultimately depend on two factors: balance and accessibility. If future outfits offer meaningful gameplay variety without becoming mandatory upgrades locked behind heavy spending, they could add an interesting new layer of strategy. If every outfit simply becomes a straight power increase, they risk becoming another required progression system for competitive play.

For now, one conclusion is already clear: Immortal Outfits are not cosmetic skins. They are a permanent power system that will likely play an increasingly important role in Infinity Kingdom as new outfits continue to arrive.
