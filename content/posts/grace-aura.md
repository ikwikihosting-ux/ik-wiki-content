### Introduction

Grace is one of the newest additions to the game and one that is extreemly interesting given the current crit-based meta. As such it is perhaps not surprisingly a prominent skill in most top tier builds once you hit the later legendary seasons and it is made available. Like most of the Tower of Knowledge aura skills Grace is quite simple, yet hugely effective! It reduces incoming critical damage, which directly targets one of the strongest and most common strategies for build-setups in the current meta.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-22.png)

A lot of popular builds rely heavily on crit-based burst—Khan, Loki, and similar setups—so a skill that weakens crit damage changes how those fights play out. It’s not flashy, but it affects your defensive capabilities in a massive way.

---

### How the Skill Works

Grace is an **epic aura skill**, which means its effect is always active from the moment the battle starts. There’s no trigger condition, no cooldown, and no RNG involved. As soon as the fight begins, all allies benefit from reduced critical damage taken.

The effect itself is simple: it reduces critical damage received by up to 50%.

It’s important to understand that this **does not prevent crits from happening**. Enemies will still land critical hits, **but the damage multiplier** on those hits is significantly reduced.

In practice, this turns large burst hits into something much more manageable. A hit that would normally chunk a unit heavily instead becomes something that can be healed through or absorbed by shielding effects much better. Overall that means enemies are not going to be able to rely on huge burst hits to settle the fight, instead Grace ensures a much longer fight as incomming damage is smoothed.

Because it’s an aura skill, it also comes with a limitation: each immortal can only equip one aura. So running Grace means you’re giving up another aura option, which is an important trade-off to consider when building your setup. That being said, this is an absolute S-tier skill and should be a priority.

---

### Availability and Upgrading

Unlike legendary Chaos skills, Grace is relatively accessible. You unlock it through the **Contract Shop during Legendary Season**, I believe starting at Round 3. This means it is available to a much wider range of players than many other top tier skills (namely legendaries), and is available also to free-to-play.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-23-1024x435.png)

After unlocking it with skill stones, upgrades are done using **Soul Crystals**, which is a more standard and manageable resource compared to Chaos skill upgrades. Because of this and how powerful the effect is, you can expect Grace to become very common across all levels of play once players reach that stage of progression.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-24-1024x512.png)

That accessibility is a big part of why this skill is so interesting —it’s not just strong, it’s widely usable.

---

### What It Changes in Combat

The most immediate effect of Grace is that it reduces the impact of burst damage. In the current meta burst crits can be massive when players build out strong artifacts, etc. and that means fights tend to go quite short. For example below, you see one Corona Godstrike eliminating (almost) an entire team on a single critical hit.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-25-1024x498.png)

Critical hits are still there, but they no longer decide fights on their own. Where you would normally see a unit lose a huge chunk of HP from a single crit, you now see a more controlled drop in health.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/image-26-1024x377.png)

Naturally the above is me finding extreemes to prove a point, but in general critical hits are huge in Infinity Kingdom and reducing their damage by a whopping 50% is absolutely massive!

This has a ripple effect on the entire fight. Battles last longer because units aren’t being deleted as quickly. Healing becomes more effective because it has time to work between hits. Instead of fights being decided in short burst windows, they stretch out into longer exchanges where sustain and consistency matter more.

Over time, this leads to more fights approaching the time limit. In PvP especially, that changes how wins are secured. Instead of overwhelming your opponent quickly, you often win by simply surviving longer.

---

### Impact on Team Building and Meta

Grace naturally shifts the game toward more defensive and sustain-oriented setups. Tanks, shields and healers gain value because they benefit directly from reduced burst damage. Double tank compositions, which can struggle against high burst, start to feel more stable again.

At the same time, crit-reliant builds lose some of their edge. They still deal good damage, but they no longer have the same ability to quickly eliminate targets. This forces those builds to rely more on sustained damage rather than single big hits.

Another important point is that Grace is often stronger in defense than offense. When you’re attacking, you usually need to secure damage to either win or atleast make a huge impact in a rally. When you’re defending, simply surviving long enough can be enough—especially if the fight times out. Grace supports that defensive playstyle very well.

---

### Player Perspective

From experience, Grace doesn’t feel like a power spike in the traditional sense. It doesn’t increase your numbers or make your damage look better. Instead, it limits how much your opponent can do to you.

The difference becomes obvious in close fights. Situations where a couple of crits would normally decide the outcome instead turn into longer trades. Units that would have died survive just long enough to be healed, and that often changes the entire flow of the fight.

It also changes how you approach matchups. Against strong burst players, Grace gives you a layer of consistency that makes fights feel less volatile. You’re less dependent on avoiding bad RNG from enemy crits, and more able to play around sustained pressure.

---

### Conclusion Summary

Grace is an epic aura skill that reduces critical damage taken by up to 50%, and while the effect is simple, its impact is significant. It slows fights down, weakens burst-heavy strategies, and makes sustain-based gameplay more viable.

Because it’s always active and relatively easy to obtain, it’s likely to become a standard part of many builds. It won’t help you win faster, but it will make it much harder for your opponent to win quickly—and in the current meta, that alone makes it a very valuable tool.
