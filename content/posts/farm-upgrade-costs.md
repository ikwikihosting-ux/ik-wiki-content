Farms can produce Food, and Food is often used to train troops or as research materials. Upgrading the Farm level will increase its production and storage capacity. The produced Food must be collected manually. And when the Farm reaches its storage capacity, the production will be paused. So make sure to collect the produced resources in time!

![](https://oss.gtarcade.com/forum/jpg/2020-08-28/25_94790cc2-8d84-4100-beef-7a3711e827b1_193119.jpg)
