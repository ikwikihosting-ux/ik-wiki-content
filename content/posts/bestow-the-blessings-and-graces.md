### Bestow the Blessings: Empowering Immortals in Infinity Kingdom

In the dynamic realm of Infinity Kingdom, blessings have the power to elevate your immortals and turn the tide of battle in your favor. Understanding the blessings that can empower your heroes is essential for crafting a formidable strategy. In this guide, we'll explore the blessings designed to enhance your immortals and the heroes who can best wield their effects to become unstoppable forces on the battlefield.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-45-1024x521.png)

### 1. Oaken Guard, Resist, and Defense Blessing

Oaken Guard, Resist, and Defense Blessing are blessings that shine when bestowed upon support immortals or heroes who don't rely on HP to be effective in battle.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-46.png)

Immortals like Trajan, Qin, and Dido can benefit greatly from these blessings, even if they are allowed to drop and face defeat quickly. These blessings enhance their survivability and overall resilience on the battlefield.

### 2. Rage Blessing

Rage Blessing is a blessing that thrives when assigned to support immortals or heroes who don't depend on HP for their effectiveness, much like Trajan, Qin, and Dido.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-47.png)

This blessing is especially effective when used in marches featuring immortals who require a significant amount of energy or can burst before the first dragon skill is activated. Heroes like Hippolyta, Wu (sometimes), or Kubilai (with Adrenaline Rush) can make the most of Rage Blessing to ensure their abilities are ready when needed most.

### 3. Energy Suppression

Energy Suppression is a blessing that excels when assigned to support immortals or heroes who can be effective without relying on HP, similar to Richard, Bjorn, Trajan, Dido, and Qin. Its effectiveness varies depending on the battlefield:

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-48.png)

- In water (and wind), it ensures healing occurs before the Wound status is triggered, keeping your immortals in top condition.
- In lightning, it delays the opponent's first burst until after your dragon's nuke, reducing the damage your opponent can inflict due to lost troop HP.
- In shadow, it delays the opponent's first burst, often causing it to overlap with your dragon's silence, further delaying or canceling the first ultimate of your opponent.

### 4. War Blessing and Speed Blessing

War Blessing and Speed Blessing are blessings tailored for support immortals like Qin, Cyrus, and others. These blessings shine in marches featuring immortals who deal damage early on, such as Alex and Hannibal.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/image-49.png)

When combined, they create a powerful synergy that not only boosts your immortals' attack capabilities but also enhances their speed, ensuring they reach their full potential quickly.

In conclusion, blessings are a critical component of your strategy in Infinity Kingdom, offering various ways to empower your immortals. Whether you're enhancing survivability with Oaken Guard, Resist, and Defense Blessing, unleashing devastating attacks with Rage Blessing, manipulating energy with Energy Suppression, or boosting both offense and speed with War Blessing and Speed Blessing, these blessings can turn your heroes into unstoppable forces on the battlefield.

So, embrace the power of blessings, refine your tactics, and watch as your immortals become the champions who lead your army to victory in the world of Infinity Kingdom.
