***Announcing the Revamped Cross-Server Spider Competition in Infinity Kingdom!***

Attention, Lords and Ladies of Infinity Kingdom! Get ready to embark on a thrilling new chapter in one of our most beloved events—the Cross-Server Spider Competition! After almost a month of downtime, we have gotten permission for another round and with new changes to the bracket system.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/image-9.png)

## What's New?

This time around, we will be **removing bracket D** from the last event given there was not competition here, instead what we are going to do is increase the rewards in the remaining brackets hopefully making it more competitive and interesting to battle it out!

Here are our **three main brackets** based on your march's Strategic Troop Power (STP). This innovative change aims to level the playing field, ensuring that battles are matched more fairly and focus on player strategies and builds. Say goodbye to the days of David versus Goliath matchups and hello to competitions where tactics truly shine!

### Bracket Breakdown:

- **Bracket A:** 500K+ STP
- **Bracket B:** 400K-500K STP
- **Bracket C:** 300K-400K STP

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-146-1024x475.png)

Your placement in these brackets will be determined by your march's STP, verified through screenshots submitted via our official submissions channel. Note that players can send screenshots for multiple brackets to enter the competition, however only your BEST ranking will count. For example, if you rank fourth in one bracket and 8th in another, you will get rewards as rank four and your 8th placement will be removed allocating space for other players there.

## Exciting Rewards Await!

Competing in these brackets not only brings glory but also gems! Here's what's up for grabs in each bracket:

- **1st Place:** 1200 gems
- **2nd Place:** 1000 gems
- **3rd Place:** 800 gems
- **4th Places:** 500 gems each
- **5th Places:**400 gems each
- **6th - 10th Places:** 200 gems each

## Bonus mini-event for new/low pop. servers

**And for our aspiring conquerors below level 100, a special lower bracket ensures everyone gets a slice of the action:**

- **1st Place:** 500 gems
- **2nd**: 400 gems
- **3rd - 5th Places:** 200 gems each

### Important to Note:

For this inaugural revamped competition, players are allowed to earn rewards from **one bracket only**. Keep your eyes peeled, though, as this may evolve in future competitions.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-144-1024x484.png)

You can compete in multiple brackets, but rewards are limited to your best entry. Use the bot on Discord to submit - maybe you can beat last rounds winners?

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-145.png)

## Mark Your Calendars!

The 13th round of the Spider Competition kicks off on **Sunday, 28th of April at 00:01 UTC**, running through to the **13th of May at 23:59 UTC**.

![](https://ik-wiki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/image-142-1024x530.png)
