If you've participated in the previous seasonal festivals, most of the activities will already be familiar. Lucky Koi, the festival Battle Pass, Grail Shop, Pinnacle Trial, and Glorious Challenge all return, while Enchanted Forest continues the cooperative reward-collection gameplay introduced during Ghost Fiesta.

However, there is one notable change.

**Dazzling Wish has been replaced by Touch of Surprise, a new scratch-card lottery offering Immortal outfit fragments and Eternal Grails.**

Unfortunately, the new system doesn't appear to offer particularly attractive value for players simply looking to collect Eternal Grails. As with Dazzling Wish before it, we generally recommend treating it as an optional activity for players specifically pursuing outfits.

Let's go through the festival, what's new, and where players should focus their time and resources.

The Thanksgiving Festival runs from **November 16 to November 26, 2026**, with some shop redemption timers extending beyond the main activities.

## TL;DR – What Should You Focus On?

The event is largely business as usual, with several familiar opportunities to collect valuable rewards.

* **Lucky Koi:** Collect your daily free Token of Light. For spenders, the five-purchase consolation reward remains one of the best sources of Eternal Grails.
* **Pet Paradise: Ginger Cat:** The $49.99 Battle Pass provides 120 additional Tokens of Light, 60 Ginger Cat decoration fragments, 60 selectable Immortal outfit fragments, and 5,000 Magic Wands.
* **Enchanted Forest:** Collect food, complete progression milestones, and obtain additional outfit fragments. It follows a similar reward structure to Witch Forest.
* **Touch of Surprise:** The new scratch-card lottery replacing Dazzling Wish. Interesting for outfit collectors, but generally poor value if your main objective is Eternal Grails.
* **Fruit Frenzy:** The returning 2048-style puzzle. Complete its tasks for Magic Wands and additional progression rewards.
* **Grail Shop:** Horus returns alongside valuable Chaos Immortal and artifact selection chests.
* **Pinnacle Trial and Glorious Challenge:** Complete as many stages as possible for free event currencies, Magic Wands, and additional rewards.

For most players, the best strategy remains collecting every free reward, investing in the Battle Pass only if its contents are useful to your account, and prioritizing **Wish Grail over Touch of Surprise when converting Tokens of Light into Eternal Grails**.

## Lucky Koi – Still One of the Best Purchases

Lucky Koi returns with exactly the kind of rewards we've come to expect from seasonal events.

Every player can claim a free daily reward, including a Token of Light. Over the festival, this provides up to ten free Tokens of Light simply for remembering to visit the event page.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-152038-gdmgiu.png)

These tokens can be saved for future seasonal activities, so even players who don't intend to spend anything should collect them.

For spenders, the familiar $4.99 Koi Gift Pack is once again available.

Each purchase provides:

* 5 Tokens of Light
* 1,500 Magic Wands
* Entry into the Lucky Koi lottery

The lottery takes place every two days, with one lucky player receiving 4,250 Eternal Grails and an exclusive insignia.

However, the grand prize isn't the main reason we recommend Lucky Koi.

Players who participate in all five rounds without winning receive a guaranteed consolation reward of **850 Eternal Grails**, alongside a special insignia.

Buying all five $4.99 packs costs approximately $24.95.

That's already an excellent source of Eternal Grails, even before accounting for the 25 Tokens of Light and 7,500 Magic Wands included with those purchases.

For players planning to spend money on Chaos Immortals or artifact progression, this continues to be one of the most attractive purchases available during seasonal events.

**Our recommendation:** Collect the free rewards every day. For moderate spenders, completing all five Lucky Koi purchases remains a very high priority, provided you participate in every required round.

## Pet Paradise: Ginger Cat – The $50 Festival Battle Pass

The festival Battle Pass returns with its usual 30-level progression system.

Players complete daily objectives to earn EXP and unlock rewards across the free and premium tracks.

This month's featured reward is **Pet Paradise: Ginger Cat**, a new decoration featuring an orange cat relaxing on a colorful miniature castle.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-152055-vtqut8.png)

The premium Battle Pass costs **$49.99**, equivalent to the usual $50 seasonal pass.

### What Do You Get?

The complete Battle Pass provides:

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-151723-ffebfp.png)

The premium pass also includes additional progression rewards distributed across its 30 levels.

The Immortal Outfit Fragment Selection Chests are particularly interesting because they allow players to choose between:

* Louis IX – Night of Terror
* Alexander the Great – Summer Passion

This means players can continue progressing outfits introduced during previous seasonal events rather than being forced to collect only the latest reward.

Sixty fragments are enough to unlock an outfit requiring 60 fragments, provided you've selected the corresponding reward throughout the pass.

The Ginger Cat decoration is the other main collectible. Its fragments are available through the premium track, making it primarily a reward for paying participants.

### Is the Battle Pass Worth $50?

As usual, this depends on what you actually want.

The 120 additional Tokens of Light are valuable because they can be converted into Eternal Grails through Wish Grail, and the 60 outfit fragments are particularly useful if you're working toward Alexander's or Louis IX's appearance.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-151906-voos51.png)

The 5,000 Magic Wands also provide additional progression through Enchanted Forest.

However, I wouldn't purchase the Battle Pass simply because it's available.

If you don't care about the outfits or Ginger Cat decoration, much of the additional value comes down to whether the Tokens of Light justify the cost compared with other available bundles.

**Our recommendation:** A reasonable purchase for players actively collecting outfits or seeking a combination of seasonal currencies and collectible rewards. Not necessarily the most efficient purchase if you only want Eternal Grails.

## Enchanted Forest – The Return of Cooperative Reward Collection

Enchanted Forest is this month's version of the cooperative collection event introduced through Witch Forest during Ghost Fiesta.

The Halloween setting has been replaced by a much more cheerful Thanksgiving theme, featuring a sleeping orange cat surrounded by delicious food.

The underlying concept is familiar.

Players spend Magic Wands to activate spaces around the event board and collect food, resources, and additional rewards.

The food contributes points toward daily progression chests, while special spaces can provide additional multipliers or rewards.

### How the Event Works

The main board contains several types of spaces, including reward chests, special events, and Magic Wand reward tiles.

Players can select different spending multipliers before activating the board, allowing them to consume larger amounts of Magic Wands in exchange for proportionally larger rewards.

The event includes cooperative participation, with players able to team up and contribute toward shared progression.

As with the Halloween version, the daily progression rewards and ranking systems provide additional incentives to participate regularly.

The main difference is the seasonal presentation and the items being collected.

Instead of collecting Halloween candy, players now gather Thanksgiving-themed food such as Savory Drumsticks, Fruit Pies, and Savory Cheese.

These contribute different point values toward the event's milestones.

### Outfit Fragments and My Food

An important feature is the **My Food** collection system.

Players collect specific foods to progress through separate milestone rewards, including Immortal Outfit Fragment Selection Chests.

The visible milestones include:

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-151737-zlte99.png)

The selection chests provide another opportunity to progress the returning outfits without relying entirely on the premium Battle Pass.

However, reaching the higher milestones can require considerable participation and potentially significant spending on additional Magic Wands.

Like Fishing Contest and Witch Forest before it, this is an activity where chasing the final rewards can become expensive.

**Our recommendation:** Collect all free Magic Wands from the Battle Pass, Fruit Frenzy, and combat challenges before purchasing additional ones. If you're pursuing an outfit, calculate how far you need to progress toward its next milestone rather than spending blindly.

## Touch of Surprise – The New Scratch-Card Lottery

The biggest new feature of November's seasonal event is **Touch of Surprise**, which replaces Dazzling Wish.

Instead of spinning a wheel, players use Tokens of Light to purchase Wonder Cards.

Each Wonder Card can then be scratched to reveal symbols and determine the reward.

The possible rewards include Eternal Grails and Immortal outfit fragments, with larger rewards requiring specific combinations of matching symbols.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-152113-novacx.png)

There's also an **UP mechanic**, where collecting five UP symbols increases the level of the event and improves future winning probabilities.

### How Good Are the Rewards?

At first glance, the new lottery is entertaining enough. But when you examine the probabilities, the familiar problem with Dazzling Wish becomes apparent.

At the starting level, some of the rarer rewards shown are:

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-151746-jdocwd.png)

These are the starting-level probabilities displayed in-game. The full reward distribution changes as the UP system progresses, so they shouldn't be treated as the average probabilities for an entire spending session.

The event also displays a grand-prize guarantee counter. In our recording, it indicated that approximately 1,200 scratches were required to reach the guaranteed grand prize from a fresh starting point.

That's a very large number of Tokens of Light.

Even though the UP progression improves the lottery as you continue, the basic problem remains: players must commit substantial resources to a system where the most attractive outfit rewards are rare.

### Is Touch of Surprise Worth Spending On?

For players specifically pursuing the available Immortal outfits, Touch of Surprise may be worth considering.

After all, outfit fragments are the primary reason this event exists.

However, if your main objective is obtaining Eternal Grails, I would strongly recommend considering the alternative.

**Wish Grail remains the better general-purpose destination for Tokens of Light when you're trying to maximize Eternal Grail income.**

Unlike Touch of Surprise, the dedicated Wish Grail wheel focuses on Grail rewards, cumulative milestones, and a grand-prize system that can be evaluated more directly.

Touch of Surprise instead mixes Grails with outfit fragments and low-probability combinations, making it a much less attractive option for players who simply want a predictable source of Grails.

We haven't yet calculated the complete expected return across every UP level, so this is a practical recommendation rather than a finalized mathematical comparison.

But for players primarily interested in long-term Chaos Immortal progression, the choice is straightforward.

**Our recommendation:** Only invest heavily in Touch of Surprise if you're specifically chasing the outfit fragments. Otherwise, save your Tokens of Light and use Wish Grail for Eternal Grails.

## Fruit Frenzy – The 2048 Puzzle Returns

Fruit Frenzy returns from previous seasonal events with its familiar 2048-style gameplay.

Players combine matching fruits on a board, gradually creating higher-level fruits and completing progression objectives.

The event includes several separate tasks, with rewards for merging specific fruits and reaching different milestones.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-152124-iqccbf.png)

The main reason to participate is the opportunity to collect additional Magic Wands and other seasonal resources.

These can then be used in Enchanted Forest without purchasing extra event bundles.

Fruit Frenzy is also a welcome change of pace from the recurring combat activities, offering players a puzzle-based alternative.

For most players, there isn't much reason to spend additional money here. Simply complete the available free attempts and tasks whenever possible.

**Our recommendation:** Treat Fruit Frenzy as a source of free Enchanted Forest progression. Collect its rewards before spending anything on additional Magic Wands.

The Grail Shop returns with another familiar selection of Immortal fragments, artifact progression materials, and other valuable rewards.

Horus remains one of the main featured Immortals, alongside his exclusive artifact schematic.

The shop also includes several discounted selection chests that deserve serious attention, particularly for players still progressing their Chaos Immortals.

Among the more interesting rewards are:

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-151758-j9h919.png)

### Where Is the Best Value?

For most players, I'd recommend checking the discounted Chaos Immortal selection chests before committing to Horus.

The Fate of Chaos Selection Chest VIII is particularly attractive because it offers valuable Immortal fragments from a selection of powerful Chaos Immortals.

At 250 Eternal Grails for 80 fragments, it can represent considerably better practical progression value than purchasing more expensive fragments simply because they're featured.

The artifact schematic selection chests also offer useful progression opportunities, particularly for accounts that already have their priority Immortals unlocked.

Meanwhile, the Shadow and Light selection chests are worth checking for players working on premium Holy and Shadow formations.

The best purchase ultimately depends on which Immortals and artifacts your account is developing.

### Challenge Shop

The event also has a separate Challenge Shop where players can exchange festival currency for additional rewards.

These include Magic Wands, artifacts, decorations, and progression materials.

The shop has daily purchase limits on some items, so check it regularly and avoid leaving useful currency unspent.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-152150-ec81xm.png)

**Our recommendation:** Don't assume Horus is automatically the best choice simply because he's the featured Immortal. Compare the discounted selection chests against your current account needs.

## Pinnacle Trial – Complete the Available Stages

Pinnacle Trial returns with its usual series of increasingly challenging battles.

Players progress through stages, earn event rewards, and eventually unlock additional ranking-related challenges.

Rewards include Tokens of Light, Immortal fragments, and seasonal progression materials.

The higher stages become available as the festival progresses, so you won't necessarily be able to complete everything immediately.

As usual, progress as far as your account allows, then return when additional stages unlock.

The event provides valuable free rewards without requiring any direct spending.

**Our recommendation:** Complete every stage you can reasonably clear, particularly when it awards Tokens of Light or materials used elsewhere in the festival.

## Glorious Challenge – Free Festival Currency and Magic Wands

Glorious Challenge also returns in its familiar format.

Players battle through increasingly difficult encounters to earn festival currency, Magic Wands, and other rewards.

Normal difficulty is available from the beginning, while harder stages unlock later in the festival.

The daily challenge system also allows players to repeat previously completed stages through the raid feature.

For many players, this remains one of the most important free sources of seasonal currency.

The festival currency can be spent in the Challenge Shop, while Magic Wands contribute to Enchanted Forest progression.

Even if your account cannot clear the highest difficulty stages, there's still meaningful value in completing the content available to you.

**Our recommendation:** Use your free challenge attempts consistently, clear new stages as they unlock, and make sure to spend your earned event currency before the shop closes.

## Where Is the Best Value During Thanksgiving Festival?

The festival offers several different spending opportunities, but their value varies significantly depending on what you're trying to obtain.

For most players, I would prioritize the event as follows.

![Screenshot](/uploads/screenshot-20261121-151812-fpgrth.png)

### Free-to-Play Players

The priorities are straightforward.

Collect your daily Lucky Koi reward, complete the free Battle Pass objectives, progress through Fruit Frenzy, and clear the available combat challenges.

Use the Magic Wands earned through these activities in Enchanted Forest to collect as many free rewards as possible.

Save your Tokens of Light for activities where they'll provide the best long-term progression value.

You don't need to participate in every paid lottery or chase every outfit to get worthwhile rewards from the festival.

### Low and Moderate Spenders

For players spending modest amounts, Lucky Koi remains the standout purchase.

Completing all five $4.99 purchases provides an excellent consolation reward alongside useful Tokens of Light and Magic Wands.

After that, consider whether the $49.99 Battle Pass offers enough value for your particular account.

If you're actively progressing the Alexander or Louis IX outfits, the 60 selectable outfit fragments can be particularly attractive.

If you don't care about those outfits, I wouldn't automatically recommend buying the pass simply for its seasonal currencies.

### Heavy Spenders and Outfit Collectors

For players collecting every outfit, Touch of Surprise and Enchanted Forest may be unavoidable.

However, the important consideration is identifying how much you're actually prepared to spend to reach the desired outfit reward.

The scratch-card probabilities are extremely low at the starting level, and progressing through the UP system can require substantial investment.

Before committing, make sure you're comfortable with the possibility of needing a very large number of scratches.

For players primarily interested in competitive combat progression, however, I would generally favor acquiring Eternal Grails and spending them on important Immortals and artifacts rather than chasing cosmetic collectibles with uncertain returns.

## Final Thoughts – A Familiar Festival With a New Lottery

The Thanksgiving Festival is another traditional Infinity Kingdom seasonal event, with most activities returning in familiar forms.

Lucky Koi remains an excellent purchase for players collecting Eternal Grails, while the $49.99 Battle Pass offers a substantial collection of Tokens of Light, outfit fragments, and the new Ginger Cat decoration.

Enchanted Forest continues the cooperative progression introduced during Halloween, with additional outfit fragments available through its collection milestones.

Fruit Frenzy, Pinnacle Trial, and Glorious Challenge provide several useful opportunities to collect free resources throughout the event.

The main novelty is Touch of Surprise.

Although the scratch-card format is new, its practical role is very similar to Dazzling Wish: an expensive, luck-dependent activity aimed primarily at players collecting limited Immortal outfits.

For most players, that doesn't change where the best value lies.

**Our recommendation:** Collect every free reward, consider Lucky Koi if you're spending, and prioritize Wish Grail when converting Tokens of Light into Eternal Grails. Only commit heavily to Touch of Surprise or Enchanted Forest if you're specifically pursuing the outfit rewards.

The Thanksgiving theme might be new, but the most efficient strategy remains the same: identify which rewards actually improve your account, collect everything available for free, and avoid spending heavily on lotteries unless their exclusive rewards are genuinely important to you.
